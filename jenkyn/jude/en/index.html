About this text. EEBO-TCP A87554 (Part I, London 1652; Thomason copy) and A87557 (Part II, London 1654), keyed by hand from the microfilm; CC0. Original spelling and italics are retained; the long s is printed as s. Page numbers of the original are kept as in-page anchors (each part is paged from 1). Manuscript notes on the Thomason copy (dates) are omitted.

Illegible places. The keyers marked 1,557 places they could not read, most of them Greek and Hebrew words, which TCP does not transcribe. English words were supplied from the same place in the 1865 Edinburgh reprint (Nichol), keeping the original spelling, and checked against page images; Greek, Hebrew and the marginal notes (dropped from the 1865 reprint) were read from the 1652 microfilm for Part I and the 1656 second edition for Part II. places still undecided remain marked in red (410).

文本说明。本书据 EEBO-TCP 人工录入本 A87554（第一部分，伦敦 1652，Thomason 藏本）与 A87557（第二部分，伦敦 1654），保留原书拼写与斜体，长 ſ 作 s；原书页码保留为页内锚点（两部分各自从 1 起）；Thomason 藏本扉页上的手写日期不录。录入本标出 1,557 处读不出的字，大半是录入本照例不录的希腊文、希伯来文：英文词按 1865 年爱丁堡重印本（Nichol）同一位置读出、看图核对，拼法照原书；希腊文、希伯来文与页边旁注（1865 本把旁注删了）对照原书胶片判读——第一部分用 1652 同版胶片，第二部分用 1656 年第二版胶片（1654 初版网上无扫描）。仍未定的标红留在明面（410 处）。逐条依据见 jenkyn_raw/jude/PROVENANCE.md。
