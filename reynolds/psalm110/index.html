About this text. Reynolds preached these sermons on Psalm 110 as preacher at Lincoln's Inn and published them in 1632. Each verse of the psalm is one part of the book; the long expositions of verses 1–4 are here divided into pages of about thirty printed pages each, and each page is headed by the entries of Reynolds's own table of contents that fall within it. The text is the keyed transcription of the 1632 edition (EEBO-TCP A10650; released under CC0), with the spelling of the original; only the long s is printed as s. Places the transcribers could not read are shown in red (461 letters, 19 words, 11 longer passages, 153 Greek or Hebrew passages left untranscribed). The index of Scripture places is not included.
