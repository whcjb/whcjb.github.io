The Fortieth Psalm, as we have seen (vol. 1. p. 333), consists of a thanksgiving for deliverances experienced already, vs. 2—14 (1—13), and of a prayer for fresh occasion of thanksgiving, vs. 15—18 (14—17.) The latter portion is here repeated by itself, as a kind of appendix to the Sixty-ninth and preface to the Seventy-first, with both which it has several points of contact and resemblance. The mutual relation of the two editions is the same as that between the Fourteenth and the Fifty-third. The supposition of an erroneous copy or an accidental repetition is forbidden by the fact that both are left on record, and by the appearance of an uniform design in the variations. In this case, as in that of the Fifty-third Psalm, no comments will be made upon those expressions which are common to both forms and have therefore been explained already.
