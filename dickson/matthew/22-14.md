<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Ver. 14.* For many are called, but few are chosen.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-22-14"></div>

The reason of this different manner of receiving the offer of the gospel is marked. *Doct.* The cause why some receive the grace of Christ offered in the Gospel, in a better manner then the multitude of others do, and do adorne the gospel by a holy conversation, flowing from faith and love to Jesus Christ, as the multitude of others do not, is not in the men themselves, but in the free grace and love of God electing some unto grace and glory, and leaving others to answer for their meeting given to this call; for, *Many are called,* saith he, *but few are chosen.*
