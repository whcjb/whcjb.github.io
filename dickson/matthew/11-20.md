<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Verse 20.* Then began he to upbraid the cities wherein most of his mighty works were done, because they repented not.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-11-20"></div>

Seeing the main scope of the following speech is expresly set down to the upbraiding of these cities, and the ag<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>eging of their sin, it is without ground <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>o argue hence, the ability of man to repent and beleeve, without the speciall operation of Gods grace, and, it is an idle and prophane deed, to move question, why God did not grant such meanes to Tyrus and <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>idon, upon such and such supposition: for it is sufficient unto Christs purpose, that *Aborazin and Bethsaida,* had so abused the meanes of grace, as they had hardened themselves in the sinfulnesse of nature, above Tyrus and Sidon: so that more might have been expected in the matter of repentance from Tyrus and Sidon, and from Sodom and Gomorrah, even according to the principles of <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">〈◊〉</span> , then was found in these cities; for is so much paines had been taken on them, as was taken on Capernaum, &c. in much appearance of reason it might have been expected, that they should have repented <span class="dk-pb" id="p132" data-p="132" data-facs="72" title="Page 132 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 132 页起)"></span> in some sort, and have given such signes of repentance, at least, as with Ahab, and Niniveh, they might have escaped temporal Judgments. This being the scope, we should not stretch the words beyond, and contrary to it: Hence learn, 1. That the miracles of Christ were done by his own power<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">▪</span> for here they are called *His* mighty works, in a proper sense, and so they do prove him to be true God. 2. The use which should be made of the miracles of Christ, and mighty works done by him, is repentance; for that which is found fault with here, is, they repented not. 3. Whosoever will not repent, when God calls for repentance, and will not give glory to God when he calls for it, he will pour shame on them; for here he upbraids these cities, which did not honour him.
