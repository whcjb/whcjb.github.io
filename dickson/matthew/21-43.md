<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Vers. 43.* Therefore say I unto you, The Kingdom of God shall be taken from you, and given to a Nation bringing forth the fruits thereof.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-21-43"></div>

Here Christ applieth the last Parable, and the Psalme last cited to the Scribes and Rulers, and to the rest of the rotten members of that Church. *Doct.* 1. The Gospel or the means of Grace in a visible face of a Church, is Gods Kingdom on earth, and the greatest benefit that can be bestowed on a Land; for so it is called here. 2. The Nation which doth not bring forth the fruits of the Gospell, may justly be deprived of that priviledge, as here is threatned, *The Kingdom of God shall be taken from you.* 3. The Church m<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>y be flitted from one Nation to another, but shal not cease to be among some people; for, *It shall be given to another,* saith Christ; Thus he foretelleth <span class="dk-pb" id="p248" data-p="248" data-facs="130" title="Page 248 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 248 页起)"></span> them of the rejection of the Jews, and the in-bringing of the Gentiles.
