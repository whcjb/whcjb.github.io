<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Verse 18.* And Iesus rebuked the divell, and he departed <span class="dk-pb" id="p205" data-p="205" data-facs="108" title="Page 205 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 205 页起)"></span> out of him: and the child was cured from that very hour.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-17-18"></div>

At a word our Lord delivereth this distressed soul. *Doct.* 1. How strong soever, and how long soever Satans possession be in a soul, Christ can cast him out quickly; as here, *Iesus rebuked the divel.* 2. Christ is a perfect Physician, he can so cast out Satan, and remove a plague, that the party afflicted shall be no more molested, as here, The *divel departed out of him, and the child was cured from that very hour.*
