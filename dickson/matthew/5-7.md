*Verse 7.* Blessed are the mer<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>ifull, for they shall obtain mercy.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-5-7"></div>

The fifth mark of true Disciples, is mercifulness, whereby Christians have such a holy compassion of the miseries of other mens bodies and soules, as doth make them actually to do them good, as they are called unto their relief. *Doct.* 1. The outletting of bowels of compassion to others, who are lying under the burden of sin and misery, is the sure evidence of a blessed man; for Christ of such doth say, *Blessed are the mercifull.* 2. The Christian his shewing of mercy un<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>o others, is a fo<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>erunner of the renewed, and fresh sense of Gods mercy to himself, for this is imported in the proof of such mens blessedness; for, *they shall find mercy:* now certain it is, that such men have already obtained mercy, else they could not be mercifull, else they could not be blessed; but it is possible that they do not feel mercy, but rather for the present feel sad w<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>ath to their estimation, yet the promise is, *They shall find mercy* to their own discerning sensibly. 3. When God maketh his children find the fruits of their mercifulness, it doth not puff them up, as if they merited or deserved it, for it is not said here, they shall merit, but *They shall find mercy.*
