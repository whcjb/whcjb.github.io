*Ver. 59.* Now the chief Priests and Elders, and all the councel, sought false witness against Iesus to put him to death,

60\. But found none:yea, though many false witnesses came, yet found they none. At last came two false witnesses.

61\. And said, This fellow said, I am able to destroy the temple of God, and to build it in three days.

This corrupt church-assembly is resolute to have his life, yet will they seek to keep a form of Justice, and to lead Witnesses. *Doct.* 1. Malice against the Innocent is so shameful <span class="dk-pb" id="p321" data-p="321" data-facs="167" title="Page 321 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 321 页起)"></span> <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>thing, that such as are most guilty of it, shun to seem guilty; <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">〈◊〉</span> these men seek to have some appearance of Witnesses <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>gainst Christ; and when they have found disagreeing false Witnesses, they do reject them, till they find such false Witnesses <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">〈◊〉</span> might agree in one, and seem true. 2. Christ his greatest E<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>emies could find no fault in him, but were forced to make that <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>lau<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>t which was none, to wit, His foretelling of his own death and resurrection, which was the matter of his glory and our comfort, *Destroy this Temple, and I will build it in three dayes,* saith Christ; that is, Do your worst against me, in destroying the Temple of this Body of mine, and I will raise it up again the third day; which words the Witnesses do alter, and repeat them, as if he had promised to destroy the Temple of Jerusalem, and build it again in three dayes; and therefore are these men justly called here *False Witnesses.*
