<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Ver. 7.* And brought the asse, and the colt, and put on them their cloaths, and they set him theron.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-21-7"></div>

The disciples obedience and success is observed: Hence learn, 1. When we have a clear call, ready obedience is our part, without troubling our selves what may be the successe; for, *The disciples went and did as he commanded them.* 2. It is our pa<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>t to honour Christ, so farre as we can, and to lay aside our ornaments to glorifie him; for, *They put their cloathes on the asse and the colt.* 3. Christ contenteth himself for any state which is to keep in his Kingdom here on earth, with what his disciples can furnish him, as here; for, *They set him theron,* and so he rode.
