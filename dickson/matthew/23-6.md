*Ver. 6.* And love the uppermost rooms at feasts, and the chief seats in the Synagogues.

7\. And greetings in the markets, and to be called of men, Rabbi, Rabbi.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-23-6"></div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-23-7"></div>

Their second fault, is, vain affectation of preheminence, and respect in all things above other people, in all sort of conventions within doores, and without, at feasts, in churches, in mercats, and to have stately stiles of *Rabbi, Rabbi. Doct.* 1. Albeit the Lord doth not condemne respects and reverence due to men, according to their callings and places, yet he condemneth love, and ambitious affectation of these respects, saying, *They love the uppermost roomes.* 2. Hypocrites and vain men, least worthy of respect or honour, are most ambitious and desirous to have respect; for, *They love the first seats,* the first *Salutation,* and the stile of *Rabbi, Rabbi.*
