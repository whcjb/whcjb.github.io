<span class="dk-pb" id="p289" data-p="289" data-facs="150" title="Page 289 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 289 页起)"></span>The seventh and last motive, looking specially unto Ministers. If any man, and especially a Minister, do not watch adoe the worke intrusted unto him, he shall be destroyed <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">••</span>rfully at the Lords coming: Therefore it is necessary a watch for the Lords coming. And here we have the portrait of an evill servant, and namely of an unfaithfull Minister: first, he hath an evill heart of mis-beliefe, he beleeveth not Christs coming unto Judgement, nor the Truth which he preacher unto others, *He saith in his heart, <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>y Master delayeth his coming,* that is, He looketh not for his coming. 2. He will not fail to maligne, envy, traduce and injure to his power, the more painfull and faithfull his fellow-servants be; for, *He smileth his fellow servants.* 3. One way or other he strengtheneth i<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>odity, and the hands of the wicked, that they should not repent, nor turne from their iniquity; for, *<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">〈◊〉</span> and drinketh <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">〈◊〉</span> the drunken.*
