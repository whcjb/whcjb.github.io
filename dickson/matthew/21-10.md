<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Ver. 10.* And when he was come into Ierusalem, al the city was moved, saying, Who is this?

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-21-10"></div>

Thus he entreth openly, in State, into Jerusalem: Hence learn, 1. When it pleaseth Christ to take unto him his kingdome, he will avow himselfe king in the midst of his enemies; <span class="dk-pb" id="p238" data-p="238" data-facs="125" title="Page 238 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 238 页起)"></span> as now he rideth in this glory into Jerusalem. 2. Where greatest shew of Religion is, it is no new thing to see Christ to be least known; for, *Who is this?* say they. 3. Where he hath a mind to honour himselfe in suffering, he will shew himselfe so evidently, as he may be taken notice of by his enemies, as here, *All the city is moved, saying, Who is this?*
