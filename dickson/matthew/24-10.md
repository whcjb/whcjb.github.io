<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-24-10"></div>

The fourth temptation, or fourth sort of exercise of Christ's Disciples, is the apostasie of professours, when persecution ariseth for the Gospel. *Doct.* 1. This is a narrow trial, when many who ran fast in profession with prosp<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>rity, do turn their back on Christ when trouble cometh; yet it must come to passe; for, *Then,* saith he, *shall ma<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>y be offended*; that is, Stumble at Christ's Crosse, and forsake the true Religion. 2. Such professours as make apostasie from true Religion, shall be the chiefe adversaries of the true members of the Church, Traitours and Enemies; <span class="dk-pb" id="p277" data-p="277" data-facs="144" title="Page 277 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 277 页起)"></span> for of Professours it is said, *They shall betray and hate one another.*
