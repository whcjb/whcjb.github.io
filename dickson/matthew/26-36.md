<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-26-36"></div>

Our Lordslast sufferings may be for memories cause distinguished by the places wherin He suffered. First, In the Garden on moun<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span> Olives, to *ver.* 57. and next in C<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>iphas hall, to the end of this chapter: Our Lord taketh his eleven trusty Disciples with him into the Garden:where he <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>new the traitour with the enemies should shortly come to apprehend him, and acquainteth his Disciples with his pu<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>pose of going apart to prepare himself by prayer, for suffering. *Doc.* 1. As the truth of the Gospel, so the right way of suffering for the truth must be learned from Christ; Therfore our Lord tak<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>th with him his disciples unto Gethsemane, a Garden and place where he is to begin his last sufferings, that they might see how voluntarily and holily he addressed himself unto that service. 2. As we should not make oftentation of going to private prayer, so neither need we scrupulously to conceal our purpose when it may edifie; for Christ saith here, *Sit you here, till I go and pray yonder.*
