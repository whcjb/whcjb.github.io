<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Ver. 42.* Give to him that asketh thee, and from him that would borrow of thee, turn not thou away.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-5-42"></div>

Last of al, to teach us not to be weary of weldoing, even then when we think we have many reasons, which may hinder us to bestow upon such as do either beg or borrow from us oftner, and more (possibly) then we can well endure; our Lord commandeth *to give almes,* and *to lend the needy,* albeit not all that is craved, yet what we may spare, and the parties present need requireth.
