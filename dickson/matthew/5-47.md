<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Ver. 47.* And if ye salute your Brethren only, what do ye more *then others?* do not even the Publicans so?

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-5-47"></div>

A Fourth motive is, there must be more in you then civility, cou<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>tesie, and humanity, moving you to give expressions of love only to your friends; therefore love also your enemies. *Doct.* 1. There must be more in a christian then can be in these that are not renewed: for, *What do you more then others,* importeth, that we should do more; seeing we are born of God, better furnished with his spirit, & more ingaged by special obligation to God then others, and therefore must not carry our <span class="dk-pb" id="p59" data-p="59" data-facs="35" title="Page 59 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 59 页起)"></span> selves so, as it may be said unto us, *what do you more then others?* 2. The most hated sinners may equall the holiness of them who make not conscience to have this commanded love unto their enemies in them; for, *Do not the Publicans so,* importeth so much.
