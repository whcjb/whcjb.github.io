<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Ver. 42.* Watch therefore, for ye know not what hour your Lord doth come.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-24-42"></div>

The third motive teaching us, That the Lord hath of set purpose kept from our knowledge the time when Christ shall come; Therfore all should watch and be ready, come when he will.
