<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Ver. 3.* His countenance was like lightning, and his raiment white as snow.

4\. And for fear of him the keepers did shake, and became as dead men.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-28-3"></div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-28-4"></div>

In the description of the Angel, learn, 1. That Angels take upon them bodily shapes, when their commission to speak to men, and to be seen of men requireth so; as here this Angels countenance, and his garments are observed. 2. Great <span class="dk-pb" id="p349" data-p="349" data-facs="181" title="Page 349 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 349 页起)"></span> must be the Majesty of our Lord Jesus, when his servants countenance is *like lightning, and his garment white as snow.* 3. The plots of Christ's adversaries are turned about, as engines of war against themselves; for these keepers are sent forth to keep Christ within the tombe, and now they are made, nill they will they, witnesses of his resurrection. 4. Such as are most stout against Christ, shall be most afraid and astonished when he sheweth his Glory; for, *The keepers now do shake, and become as dead men.*
