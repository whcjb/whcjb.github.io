<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Ver. 44.* Again, the Kingdome of heaven is like unto a treasure bid in a field: the which when a man hath found he hideth, and for joy thereof, goeth and selleth all that he hath, and buyeth that field.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-13-44"></div>

Another parable, teaching us, that the church, in regard of the precious doctrine of grace and salvation to be had by Christ in it, is a rich treasure, able to relieve and supply all wants and necessities, therefore called an *hid Treasure,* which the misbeleevers, how wise soever in the world, cannot perceive.

2\. The beleever who findeth it, will make no reckoning of the worth of any ear<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>hly thing, in comparison of it, but will part with what soever is pleasant or profitable unto him in this life, rather then be deprived of this grace; for, *He selleth all to buy it.*

3\. As he laboureth to have this treasure, so he hath a care to keep it, that neither by violence nor deceit it be taken from <span class="dk-pb" id="p168" data-p="168" data-facs="90" title="Page 168 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 168 页起)"></span> him; for, *He hideth it,* and *selleth all* to have the means of enjoying of it, and to have right to the field of the visible Church, where this treasure is to be found.
