<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Ver. 13.* Then the Disciples understood that he spake unto them of Iohn the Baptist.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-17-13"></div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-17-14"></div>

By this speech the Apostles take satisfaction, and stumble no more at this doubt. *Doct.* Albeit our Lord do not answer mens doubts and questions in so many letters and syllables, yet he will make his own disciples understand the solution of their questions, and what is his meaning; His inquisitive Scholers shall be taught what is needful for them to know, as here Christs speech doth satisfie his Disciples; for it is said, *Then they understood,* &c.
