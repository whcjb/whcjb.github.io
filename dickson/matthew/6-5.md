*Ver. 5.* And when thou prayest, thou shalt not be as the hypocrites are: for they love to pray standing in the synagogues, and in the corners of the streets, that they may be seen of men. Verily, I say unto you, they have their reward.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-6-5"></div>

The next Doctrine concerns the right manner of prayer, *Doct.* 1. In prayer, all hypocriticall oftentation must be eschewed, such as the Pharisees used, who in the congregation, and in the Street carried themselves, so as they might be taken notice of, and accounted to be devout men: for it is reproved here, *They love to pray standing, that they may be seen of men.* 2. Persons who are vain glorious in their prayers, have no profit of their prayers, save the wind of some witlesse mens approbations; for, *They have their reward.* 3. Howsoever men may think it hard, that prayers for this fault should be lost, yet so it shall be found; for Christ saith, *Verily I say unto you, they have their reward.*
