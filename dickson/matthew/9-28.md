<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Ver. 28.* And when he was come into the house, the blind men came to him, and Iesus saith unto them, Beleeve ye that I am able to do this? they said unto him, Yea, Lord.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-9-28"></div>

Christ suffereth them to cry on, till he lead them unto his lodging. *Doct.* 1. The Lord will seem not to regard the prayer which he mindeth to grant, and so will traine on the Supplicant patiently to pursue his request: for no answer is given, till the blind men come to his lodging, and do follow him within doors. 2. Whoso love to have any thing from Christ, had need to have a right estimation of his power, and to have their faith fixed; therefore, *Beleeve ye,* saith Christ, *that I am able to do this?* 3. In things belonging to this life, it is sufficient to beleeve his *Power,* leaving the matter of his *Wil* to himself. Therfore here it is asked only, Beleeve <span class="dk-pb" id="p104" data-p="104" data-facs="58" title="Page 104 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 104 页起)"></span> ye that I am *able?* And they answer, *Yea Lord,* and no more.
