One of the Disciples goeth about by the sword to rescue Christ from suffering, and is <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>ep<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>oved, 1. For doing so without a calling. 2. Because Christ could have helped <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>h<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span> matter another way, if he had pleased. 3. Because the Scripture behoved to be fulfilled, which foretold of his sufferings. *Doct.* 1. Rash zeal is dangerous in Christ's matters, and glosse miscarrieth <span class="dk-pb" id="p319" data-p="319" data-facs="166" title="Page 319 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 319 页起)"></span> men where it is followed, as here is to be seen, *One of Christ's followers ingageth the rest to resist* the Magistrate, with the present hazard of their life, and draweth on the blot of sedition upon Religion. 2. No man shall have thanks to fight for Christ without a warrant; for, to *Peter* Christ saith, *Put up thy Sword.* 3. They deserve to perish by the sword whosoever draws the sword, under whatsoever pretence, without Gods warrant; for it is said, *They that take the sword, shall perish with the sword.* 4. They do entertain wrong thoughts of God, whosoever go about to help their cause by unlawfull means, *Thinkest thou that I cannot help my selfe?* saith Christ. 5. When the Lord seeth it fit to glorifie himselfe, rather by the deliverance, than by the sufferings of his servants, he can finde means to save them, and hath Angels in abundance ready, if men should fail; for he saith, *Cannot I pray for legions of Angels?* 6. Our Lord suffered most willingly, with express<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span> purpose to fulfill the Scriptures, which do speak of the way of our redemption; for, *How,* saith he, *shall the Scripture be fulfilled, that thus it must be?*
