<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Ver. 4.* And Iesus answered and said unto them, Take heed that no man deceive you.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-24-4"></div>

Our Lord answereth so, as his disciples might be best edi<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">••</span>ed. *Doct.* 1. The Lords words serve not to satifie his peoples <span class="dk-pb" id="p275" data-p="275" data-facs="143" title="Page 275 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 275 页起)"></span> curiosity, but for their instruction and utility, as here we see. 2. It is better for us to guard against the hazard wherein we may be, before the worlds end, than to be curious about the time when it shall end: therefore saith Christ, *Take heed that no man deceive you.* 3. The speciall danger of the church is from errours, and false doctrine, whereby their faith may be hurt; against this should we mainely watch, as Christ here wa<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>neth, *Take heed that no man deceive you.*
