<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Ver. 48.* Be ye therfore perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-5-48"></div>

A fifth motive is, Gods children must aime at the perfection of all vertues, and therefore at the perfection of Love, extended to their enemies. *Doct.* 1. Christians are called unto perfection; and albeit they cannot fully attain unto it in this life, yet must they aime at it, to come more and more near unto it; for it is said, *Be you therefore perfect.* 2. God only is the pattern of perfection to be set before our eyes, as he holdeth forth himselfe unto us in his word, and in his Son Jesus Christ, the expresse image of his Person, to be imitate by us; therefore it is said, *Be ye perfect, as your heavenly Father is perfect.*
