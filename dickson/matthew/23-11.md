<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Ver. 11.* But he that is greatest among you, shall be your servant.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-23-11"></div>

Here he teacheth the right strife for greatnesse, and dischargeth prelacy. *Doct.* The Majority of the Ministers in the Church, standeth not in the exalting of any man with higher power over the rest, but in true humility and subjection of a man unto his brethren, and being ready to serve them, for promoving the work of God in their hands; therefore saith he, *He that is greatest among you, let him be your servant.*
