<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Ver. 26.* Behold the souls of the a<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>r, for they sow not, neither do they reap, nor gather into barns; yet your heavenly Father feede<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>h them. Are ye not much better then they?

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-6-26"></div>

The second reason: God who provide<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span> food for birds and fowls, will certainly provide for his own children; Therefore <span class="dk-pb" id="p72" data-p="72" data-facs="42" title="Page 72 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 72 页起)"></span> anxious and distrustfull cares for food and raiment, in Christ Disciples is unlawfull; for, *Behold the fowles of the air,* &c. This reason is strengthened, 1. By insinuation, that men have the ordinary meanes of some lawfull calling (comprehended under sowing and reaping,) which sow<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>s have not; and therefore anxious and distrustfull care for food and raiment is so much more unlawfull in them, as is imported in these words, *neither do they reap:* again, this reason is strengthened, by comparison of us with birds, and preferring of us in Gods estimation, *are ye not much better then they?* saith he.
