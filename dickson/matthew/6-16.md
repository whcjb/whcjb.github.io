*Verse 16.* Moreover, when ye fast, be not as the hypocrites, of a sad countenance: for they disfigure their faces, that they may appear to men to fast. Verily I say unto you, they have their reward.

17\. But thou when thou fastest, anoint thine head, and wash thy face.

18\. That thou appear not unto men to fast, but unto thy Father which is in secret: and thy Father which seeth in secret, shall reward thee openly.

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-6-18"></div>

The third head of Doctrine teaching us in our religious fasting, to beware of vain oftentation, such as the Pharisees used: It is not Christs meaning here, that men should refuse publick Fasts, or therein carry themselves as in a day of feasting, annoynting their head, and washing their face; for that is not lawfull: neither is it his meaning, that in secret Fasts, men must either carry the matter so closely, that none of the Family should know it, or else not fast at all; for that is hardly possible: But the meaning is, That when God calleth unto religious humiliation in prayer and fasting, that wee should be as far from seeking vain applause of men, as if we <span class="dk-pb" id="p68" data-p="68" data-facs="40" title="Page 68 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 68 页起)"></span> were not at all about such a business. *Doct.* 1. Causes of fa<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>ing and humiliation do attend Christs Disciples; for this <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">〈◊〉</span> Lord doth insinuate, while he saith, *When ye fast.* 2. Such <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span> in their fasting do seek mens approbation, do lose their labo<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span> at Gods hand; and at the farthest, do gain no more but <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">〈◊〉</span> applause of men; for of such Christ saith, *Verily, they have <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">〈◊〉</span> reward.* 3. Hypocrits do religious actions for worldly respects; for of Hypocrites saith Christ, *They do seek that <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">〈◊〉</span> may appear to men to fast.* 4. That in all our religious actio<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">••</span> we should be as far from seeking mens applause, as if we <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">〈◊〉</span> not at all about any such businesse; and should so study to approve the secret sincerity of our hearts unto God, as we loo<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span> not toward any applause of men; for Christ commandeth <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">〈◊〉</span> to fast, *that you appear not to men, &c.* 5. The secret sincerity of Gods service is followed with the open blessing of God; <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">〈◊〉</span> he *doth reward such openly.*
