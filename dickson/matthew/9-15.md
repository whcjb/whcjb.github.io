*Ver. 15.* And Iesus said unto them, can the children of the bridechamber mourne, as long as the bridegroom is with them? but the dayes will come when the bridegroom shall be taken from them, and then shall they fast.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-9-15"></div>

Christ justifieth his Disciples by two reasons, in which he dealeth with Johns Disciples as with friends, more mildly then with the Pharisees. The first reason is, so long as I am in my Disciples company, it is the time of joy unto them; Therfore <span class="dk-pb" id="p100" data-p="100" data-facs="56" title="Page 100 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 100 页起)"></span> to injoyn them fasting, were untimeous: Hence learn, 1. That our Lord Jesus is Bridegroom, and the church is his Bride, which he espouseth to himselfe to be partaker of all the riches of his grace; and Christs Ministers are the Bridegrooms men, for this the comparison doth import. 2. The Disciples of Christ so long as Christ was bodily present among them, had days of great joy, as children of the Bride-chamber, daily beholding his glory and grace, in which condition they were not called unto fasting: Therfore saith he, *The children of the Bridechamber cannot mourn, as long as the Bridegroom is with them.* 3. When the Lord doth with-draw his wonted presence, and usuall comforts from us, we are called to fasting and mourning; for *The days shall come, wherein the bridegroom shal be taken from them, and then shall they fast,* saith he.
