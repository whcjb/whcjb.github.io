<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Ver. 33.* And when the multitude heard this, they were astonished at his doctrine.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-22-33"></div>

This is the issue of the Controversie, *Doct.* The end of disputation against the truth, is a further clearing of it, and glorifying of Christ; as here, *The multitude were astonished at his Doctrine.*
