*Ver. 23.* But he turned and said unto Peter, Get thee behind <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>e Satan, thou art an offence unto me: for thou savourest not the thing<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span> that be of God, but those that be of man.

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-16-23"></div>

In Christs reproof given to Peter, learn, 1. Our Lord so loved to work out our redemption, that he could not endure to be any way hindred; therefore saith he, *Get thee behind me.* 2. What Satan cannot do immediately, he will assay to do it by instruments, Christ findeth him out here, saying, *Get thee behinde me Satan.* 3. Naturally a man savoureth not thing<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span> spirituall, neither knoweth them, nor loveth them, if they be told him; Therefore saith he, *Thou savourest not the things of Go<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>.* 4. We should in temptations beware of Satan, as our pany, whoever be the instrument, and the more impudently we be tempted unto sin, wo should the more stoutly, and peremptorily resist it, as Christ did; saying, *Get thee behinde me Satan.* 5. After a man hath been much lifted up in consolation, be may readily miscarry and fall in some offence, as Peters case is here, compared with *ver.* 16.6 A man may be a stumbling block unto others, albeit he do not intend it; for to Peter it is said, *Thou art an offence.* 7. Apparent good counsell from a carnall friend, may readily carry some temptation in the bosome of it, as Peters counsell here doth; and sin will serve Satans tur<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">••</span>, where ever he find it, whether in the godly or wicked, for Peters corruption here is Satans instrument fit enough for the time.
