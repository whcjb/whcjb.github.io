<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-8-9"></div>

In the Centurions answer learn, 1. That Faith hath a high esteem of Christ, and a low esteem of it selfe, as is seen in the Centurions saying, *I am not worthy that thou shouldest come under my roof.* 2. That faith is content with Christs word, without bodily presence, and looks for no lesse effect from his word then from his presence; for, *Speake* saith he, *the word only, and my servant shall be made whole.* 3. Th<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">••</span> faith acknowledgeth all power and authority over all things, to be eminently in Christ; and in speciall, that all sicknesses are Christs souldiers and servants, to go or stay as he command<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>, in a far more excellent way then a centurion can command his souldiers; for, saith he, *I am a man under authority,* &c. therefore thou who art supream in authority overall, may it do more then I.
