<span class="dk-pb" id="p278" data-p="278" data-facs="145" title="Page 278 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 278 页起)"></span> *Ver. 13.* But he that shall endure to the end, the same shall he saved.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-24-13"></div>

After these temptations thus in order set before us, our Lord requireth constancy in the Faith, notwithstanding of them all. *Doct.* 1. All these temptations must be striven against, and overcome by them who purpose to go to Heaven; for, *He that endureth to the end, shall be saved.* 2. Whosoever for any of these temptations, or any other like, shall fall off from the Faith, and following of Jesus, he cannot be saved; for, *Onely he that endureth to the end, shall be saved*; no other hath the promise.
