<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Ver. 12.* Then came his disciples and said unto him, Knowest thou that the Pharisees were offended after they heard this saying?

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-15-12"></div>

The Disciples do wonder that Christ did regard so little to displease the Pharisees: Hence learn, 1. Truth must be taught, whosoever may be displeased; for so did Christ preach, albeit the Pharisees were offended. 2. Nothing will vexe false <span class="dk-pb" id="p182" data-p="182" data-facs="97" title="Page 182 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 182 页起)"></span> teachers more, then the discovery of their false doctrine before the multitude, as the Pharisees for this *Cause were offended.* 3. Men are ready to stumble and offend at the teaching of Truth, where no stumbling block is laid before them, as at Christs doctrine, *The Pharisees were offended after they heard this saying.*
