<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Vers. 15.* And Iesus answering, said unto him, suffer it to be so now, for thus it becometh us to fulfill all righteousness. Then he suffered him.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-3-15"></div>

Christ will have John to consider the time of his Lords humiliation, and what belonged to them both in regard of their office. *Doct.* 1. Some things were necessary to be done by *Christ* in the time of his humiliation, which otherwise was unbeseeming the dignity of his person; *Therefore,* saith Christ, *suffer it be so now.* 2. It is a thing both right and comely for each man to do what his calling requireth; for, *It becometh us to fulfill all righteousness,* saith Christ. 3. When the Lord maketh his will clear unto us, we should renounce our wil, and follow his, *whatsoever* blind zeal, or carnal humility shall speak to the contrary; for when John was informed of Christs mind, *then he suffered him.*
