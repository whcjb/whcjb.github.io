This is the judgement that shall come upon all wicked servants. *Doct.* 1. Such as do not make them ready for our Lords coming, but do follow their owne will and lusts, shall find themselves miserably mis<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•••</span>en; for the good which they injoyed or loved to have, shall be taken from them; and the evill which they feared not, shall come upon them to the full, when they least expect it; for, *It shall be in <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">〈◊〉</span> hour that be is not <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">〈◊〉</span> of.* 2. Unfaithfull Ministers who pretend to take charge of soules, and are not faithfull in that service, a<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>e ranked among the worst sort of men, that is *hypocrites,* and shall be of all men most severely punished; for, *He shall cut them a<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">••</span>nder,* saith he, *and give them their portion with hypocrites.*
