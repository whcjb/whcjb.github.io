<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Verse 4.* Whosoever therfore shall humble himself as this little childe, the same is the greatest in the kingdome of heaven.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-18-4"></div>

A second reason, The man furthest from affectation of preheminence, is the man worthy of most estimation before men; therfore beware of looking toward prelaticall preheminence of one over another. *Doct.* True greatness of Ministers stands not in being one over another in majority of power, but in humility and farnesse from seeking a prelacy or preheminence over their brethren; for, *Whosoever shall humble himself as this little child,* saith he, *is the greatest in the kingdome of heaven.*
