<div class="dk-text" markdown="1">

*Ver. 22.* When they had heard these words, they marvelled, and left him, and went their way.

</div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="matthew-22-22"></div>

They find themselves disappointed, and with a fruitlesse admiration of Christs wisdom, do depart. *Doct.* Christs adversaries in the end will find themselves mistaken in their vain hopes, which they have had to ensnare him, or his: and all that open the mouth against him shal be ashamed, as, *These men marvelled, and went away.*
