A sixth motive, especially sorting to stir up Ministers to be watchfull in their calling, as every man in his calling, so namely a Minister, (who is sa<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">••</span>-over the Lords people as a Steward to give the children the bread of life in due season) shal be blessed, if he be faithful, and found at his work, when Christ cometh: Therefore should every one watch, and the Minister in speciall, that he may be found diligence in his calling, when his Master cometh. *Doct.* 1. The greatest honour which a man can do to his servant in this world, is but a shadow, to shew forth what the Lord will bestow on his faithfull servants in the world to come; this is it which he saith, *He shall make him ruler over all his goods*; that is, He shall put honour upon him. 2. The so<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">••</span> of; service and trust which is put upon a Minister of the Gospel is very high, and the happinesse of a faithfull and <span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>i<span class="dk-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>e Minister is very great; for, *Who then is a faithfull and wise servant,* &c. saith he.
