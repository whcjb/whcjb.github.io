About this text. EEBO-TCP A35949 (the third edition, London: for Ralph Smith, 1651; Wing D1400), keyed by hand from the EEBO microfilm; CC0. Original spelling and italics are retained; the long s is printed as s. Page numbers of the original are kept as in-page anchors. Verse headings misprinted in the source (10:19 as 29; 12:8–9 as 18 and 8; 12:27 as 17) are left as printed; pages are named by the true verse. Pages 52–53 (on Matthew 5:24–30) are missing from the transcription.

Illegible places. The keyers marked 573 places they could not read from the film images. 0 have been read from the page images so far; 0 are editorial conjectures shown [in brackets]. The rest remain marked in red.

文本说明。本书据 EEBO-TCP 人工录入本 A35949（第三版，伦敦 1651 年，Wing D1400，EEBO 缩微胶片）。保留原书拼写与斜体，长 ſ 作 s；印本页码保留为页内锚点。原书有几处经文头节号印错（10:19 印作 29；12:8、9 印作 18、8；12:27 印作 17），正文照录，页名按真节号。录入本缺原书第 52–53 页（马太福音 5:24–30 的讲解），待据 1647 年格拉斯哥初版的胶片影像补录。录入本有 573 处读不出的字，目前已看图读出 0 处；0 处为编者推补，以 [方括号] 另色标出；其余标红留在明面，待对照 Internet Archive 所藏 1651、1647 两版胶片灰度扫描逐处判读。判读记录见 dickson_raw/matthew/。
