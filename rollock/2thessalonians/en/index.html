About this text. EEBO-TCP A11010 (Edinburgh: Robert Charteris, 1606; STC 21281), keyed by hand from the EEBO microfilm; CC0. The lectures on both epistles were printed in one volume; the lectures on 1 Thessalonians are here. Original spelling and italics are retained; the long s is printed as s. Page numbers of the original are kept as in-page anchors.

Illegible places. The keyers marked 1,202 places (in both epistles) they could not read from the black-and-white film images. 617 were read from the grayscale scan of the same film (Internet Archive), place by place against the page image. Places still undecided in this epistle remain marked in red (323).

文本说明。本书据 EEBO-TCP 人工录入本 A11010（爱丁堡 1606，STC 21281，EEBO 缩微胶片），帖前、帖后两部讲章原为一册。保留原书拼写与斜体，长 ſ 作 s；印本页码保留为页内锚点。录入本有 1,202 处读不出的字（两卷合计），617 处对照同一卷胶片的灰度扫描（Internet Archive）逐处看图读出；本卷仍未定的标红留在明面（323 处）。逐条依据见 rollock_raw/thessalonians/。
