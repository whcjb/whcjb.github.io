About this text. EEBO-TCP A13535 (Cambridge: Cantrell Legge for L. Greene, 1612; STC 23825), keyed by hand from the EEBO microfilm; CC0. The commentary was preached in Cambridge; this is the first edition. Original spelling and italics are retained; the long s is printed as s. Page numbers of the original are kept as in-page anchors.

Illegible places. The keyers marked 1,067 places they could not read from the black-and-white film images. 966 were read from the grayscale scans of two copies of the 1612 edition (Internet Archive), place by place against the page image. Places still unread remain marked in red (117).
