About this text. Taylor, "late fellow of Christs Colledge in Cambridge" as the title page calls him, preached this commentary on Psalm 32 and published it in 1617. Each verse is one page here; the long expositions of verses 2, 5–6 and 7 are divided by the printed pages into parts of about thirty pages. The text is the keyed transcription of the 1617 edition (EEBO-TCP A13538; released under CC0), with the spelling of the original; only the long s is printed as s. Places the transcribers could not read are shown in red (12 letters, 1 words, 1 longer passages, 14 Greek or Hebrew passages left untranscribed). The folding table of the method of the whole psalm and the alphabetical table of chief matters are not included.

文本说明。泰勒（剑桥基督学院前院士）讲诗篇 32 篇，1617 年出版。本站每节一页；第 2 节、第 5–6 节、第 7 节讲得长，按原书页码约三十页分一页。本站正文据 1617 年版的人工录入本（EEBO-TCP A13538，CC0 公共领域授权），拼法照录原书，只把长 s（ſ）印作 s。录入者读不出的地方以红色标出：缺字母 12 个、缺整词 1 处、缺一段以上 1 处，另有希腊文、希伯来文 14 处未录。卷首的全篇纲目折页表与卷末主题索引未收。
