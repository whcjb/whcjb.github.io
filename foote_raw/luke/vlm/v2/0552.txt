"See that you lose not the things which you have wrought, but receive a full reward." In the meantime, remember that you are the salt of the earth. That you may answer this honourable and useful purpose, "Have salt in yourselves;"—"let your speech be always with grace, seasoned with salt;" and do all you can to prevent the progress of sin, and to promote the cause of religion in society. Thus shall you fulfil the chief end of your being on earth, and, in due time, pass into the abodes of perfect purity, and endless peace.
