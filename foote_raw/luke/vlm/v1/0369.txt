We have found him whom Adam lost, whom Eve injured, whom the clouds of sin have hidden from us, and whom our transgressions had hitherto made a stranger to us." Andrew found him as bearing the general character of the expected Messiah, and he found him in that sense, in which happy shall we be too, my friends, if we find him—he found him for himself; he found him, as the one all-sufficient object of his trust and of his affection: he won Christ, and was found in him; he knew him and the power of his resurrection, and the fellowship of his sufferings, being made conformable unto his death. And after living, and labouring, and dying for his name, he left the proconsul, and the lictors, and all his sorrows behind him; and in the open vision and full enjoyment of heaven, he found him whom His soul loved—and so shall he ever be with the Lord.
