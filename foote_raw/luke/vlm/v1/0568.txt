Speaking, finally, to those of you who are like the good-ground hearers, how can we but rejoice before God on your account? "For this cause thank we God without ceasing, because, when ye received the word of God which ye heard of us, ye received it, not as the word of men, but (as it is in truth) the word of God, which effectually worketh also in you that believe." Hold fast the faithful word, as ye have been taught. Rest not satisfied with small degrees of fruitfulness; but continue to improve, knowing that according to your progress, so will be your gracious reward. "And this I pray, that your love may abound yet more and more in knowledge and in all judgment; that ye may approve things that are excellent; that ye may be sincere, and without offence, till the day of Christ; being filled with all the fruits of righteousness, which are by Jesus Christ unto the glory and praise of God."
