With this view, while you carefully attend to your frame of mind, recollect your own insufficiency, and the deceitfulness and desperate wickedness of your own hearts, and the difficulty of knowing them; and recollecting all this, be earnest in prayer to God, that he would be graciously pleased to reveal to you your true character, and enable you to proceed accordingly. Say to him: "Search me, O God, and know my heart: try me, and know my thoughts; and see if there be any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting." The Lord grant that the weapons of divine truth may, in all of you, prove "mighty through God, to the pulling down of strongholds, casting down imaginations, and every high thing that exalteth itself against the knowledge of God, and bringing into captivity every thought to the obedience of Christ."
