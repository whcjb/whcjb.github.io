their head. Let us ever remember the real nature of
Christ's kingdom, or reign. The kingdom of God is within
men. “The kingdom of God is not meat and drink,” or
anything external, “ but righteousness, and peace, and joy
in the Holy Ghost.” It has its seat in the individual
heart, and thus, it is true, ultimately influences society.
