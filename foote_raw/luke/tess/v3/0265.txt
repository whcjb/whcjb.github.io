of him who, in the same spirit, do what they can for his
people and his cause. Most instructive to all classes, but
especially to the lower, are the words of the apostle to the
Corinthians:t ‘“‘ Moreover, brethren, we do you to wit of
the grace of God bestowed on the Churches of Macedonia;

their liberality. For, to their power, I bear record, yea,
and beyond their power, they were willing of themselves,
praying us with much entreaty that we would receive the
gift, and take upon us the fellowship of the ministering to
