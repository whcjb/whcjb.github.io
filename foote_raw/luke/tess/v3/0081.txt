How preposterous the declaration of the young ruler in
reference to the commandments which our Lord enume-
rated! He said, ‘ Adl these have I kept from my youth up.”
The word “ youth,” as well as that in the original f for
which it is used, is somewhat indefinite, and must here stand
for early youth, or childhood, as this ruler was yet but “a
young man.” It is agreeable to the whole circumstances of
his case to suppose, as well as implied in his own assertion,
that whatever radical defects might be discovered in his
state and character on a close inspection, his outward de-
meanour had always been steady, decent, and moral. This
