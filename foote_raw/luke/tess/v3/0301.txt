duty to resist foreign power; and there is a time to submit.
Resistance is folly, when it cannot succeed. When the will
of God was made known to Jeremiah,t he exhorted the
Jews to submit to the Chaldeans; but they would not, and
the consequences were most fatal. From the place where
dehovah has declared that he will pour out his wrath to the
uttermost, the faith of his people will urge them to fly.
Thus Lot left Sodom, and the Israelites departed from the
tents of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram. As the Jews would
not yield to the Romans, nothing remained for the Chris-
tians, who had firm faith in the divine predictions, but to
save themselves by flight.
