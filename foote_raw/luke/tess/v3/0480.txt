Being refuted, the undefined charge of stirring up and per-
verting the nation need not be noticed. How base the
duplicity here manifested by these persecutors! In order to
serve their malicious purpose, they pretended a great zeal
for the Roman government, though they hated it most
heartily, and would gladly have thrown it off if they could.
They seem to have expected an immediate sentence against
Christ, on the ground of sedition; but Pilate so far fol-
lowed the forms of justice, as to resolve to inquire for him-
self.
