BLATCH-—Lesseons for the Living from the Experience ef

the Dying. By the Rev. W. Blatch. Second edition, 18mo, cloth,

** A neat small volume, written by the pious author with the view of
enforcing on the mind of his reader the necessity for present decision for
God, and entire devotedness to Him. It records the dying words of man
eminent saints, showing their hope in death, and the power and excel-
lence of religion to soothe, to comfort, and to exhilarate its possessor in
the hour of death; and paints the death-bed scene of some notorious in-
fidels, whose death without hope, exhibited the most palpable evidence
of the utter inefficiency of their principles to impart, not to say the
slightest 1 ray of comfort, but even the meanest degree of moral courage.”
