offerings at a cheaper rate.

Such was the disease of leprosy, and such the ordinances
with regard to it: these being kept in remembrance, we
shall be the more able to enter into the case which this part
of the Gospel history places before us, and to perceive how
naturally it suggests considerations of the highest impor-
tance to our own spiritual welfare.
