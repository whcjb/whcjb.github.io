for “he had his raiment of camel's hair, and a leathern
girdle about his loins; and his meat was locusts ang wild
honey.” And this was the second part of the commenda-
tion our Lord bestowed on John. He was a man of self-
denied habits. Such habits well befitted the character in
which he appeared, namely, that of a preacher of repent-
ance. And, my brethren, though John is not now to he imi-
tated in this to the very letter, yet we ought all to imitate
him in his spirit. We must mortify the deeds of the body,
learn to endure hardness, as good soldiers of Jesus Christ,
and not be entangled with the affairs of this life.
