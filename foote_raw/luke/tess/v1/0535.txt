“ Mine head with oil thou didst not anoint.” The use
of oil (generally olive oil, as the original word here primi-
tively signifies), to anoint the body, and particularly the hair,
was very common in these countries, as it is still; and it
appears to have been often adopted at feasts. Thus the
Psalmist, in the 23d Psalm, describes the goodness of God
