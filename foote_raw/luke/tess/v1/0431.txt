Our Lord goes on to inculcate another branch of Chris-
tian benevolence, in the 38th verse: “ Give, and it shall
be given unto you; good measure, pressed down, and shaken
together, and running over, shall men give into your bosom.
or with the same measure that ye mete withal tt shall
be measured to you again.” He here exhorts us to give of
our substance, and that, too, liberally, to those who may
stand inneed. This description of the requital of liberality
is taken from a person measuring corn; it being well known
that he who, in that action, presses down the corn, and
shakes it, and causes it to run over, gives large measure.
