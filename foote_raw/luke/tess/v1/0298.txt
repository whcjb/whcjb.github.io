With Pharisees are here joined doctors of the law. These
were the same who were also called scribes and lawyers.*

readily; and as this art appears in ancient times to have
been possessed but by few, the employment of a writer, or
scribe, or secretary, was one of great trust and importance,
both in civil and in ecclesiastical affairs. The scribes were

of the tribe of Levi. They were educated in the school of
