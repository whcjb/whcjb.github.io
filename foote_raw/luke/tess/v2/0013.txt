the miracles they performed; and their miracles were so
ordered as to lead the beholder to think of a far higher
power than theirs. In dividing the sea, and in the other
miracles of Moses, the Lord directed him how to proceed,
and bade him take the rod in his hand, so that it was evi-
dent he was merely an instrument. The apostles speak of
their miracles being done in the name of Christ, and by
