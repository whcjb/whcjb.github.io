In the other sense, however, the sense of trials, temptations
may be considered as sent by God. Thus God “tempted,”
or tried Abraham, in commanding him to offer up his son
Isaac. No doubt, trials are often blessed to God's people;
they are blessed, when rightly borne and improved. The
apostle James says, “ Blessed is the man that endureth
temptation: for when he is tried, he shall receive the crown
of life, which the Lord hath promised to them that love
him.” But, when we consider our own great weakness, it
would be wrong for us to ask that we should be subjected
to any very severe trials; nay, we feel inclined to ask, and
we are here encouraged to ask, that God either would not
subject us to such as might endanger our stability, or, if he
should, that he would support us under them. Now, we
