On the former occasion, as related by Matthew,} our Lord
said, ‘‘Are not two sparrows sold for a farthing?”—and
here he says, ‘“‘ Are not five sparrows sold for two farthings ?”
—a proportion which is often observed in selling articles,
according to the rule of some advantage being allowed when
@ greater number are taken. These birds seem to have been
used for food. Of small value as they are, “not one of
them is forgotten before God.” They are not forgotten, for
they are cared for, and provided for, during their life:
“Our heavenly Father feedeth them.” Nor are they for-
gotten in their death, seeing not one of them dies, or is
killed, but by his appointment: as it is in Matthew, ‘“ One
of them shall not fall on the ground without your Father.”
