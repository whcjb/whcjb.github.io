for you exceedingly abundantly above all that you ask or
think. From listening to his wisdom, and enjoying his
fellowship in his house below, return with cheerfulness and
increased devotedness to serve him in your own place in
society; and look forward to the time when you shall be
admitted to dwell with him at his court above, and find,
more than ever, that the report of his glory was a true report,
nay, that the half had not been told you.

is too plain to require any illustration. The words, how-
ever, appear to be introduced with a different view on this
occasion; for, whereas they were plainly spoken formerly in
explanation of the conduct Christ expected of his followers,
