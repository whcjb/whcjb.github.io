Ir is probable that Satan still exerts a baneful influence on
men’s bodies, though not in such a way and to such degree
as to enable us to distinguish what is attributable to his
operation from common disease: at all events, it is certain,
from the general tenor of Scripture, that his influence over
the minds of men is still very great, though it is but little
thought of by many. Now, the extraordinary and strikingly
obvious way in which we read of his possessing the bodies
of men, during the time our Lord sojourned on earth, is well
calculated to bring home to us the conviction of the truth
and power of invisible, satanic agency on the mind, as is
the account we have of our Lord’s dispossessing him from
the bodies of men, to convince us of our Lord’s ability and
willingness to deliver our souls from spiritual bondage; but
