About this text. Burroughs preached through Hosea in weekly lectures at St Michael’s, Cornhill, London, from 1642 until his death in November 1646. The lectures were published in four volumes: chapters 1–3 in his lifetime (here in the collected edition of 1652), chapters 4–13 after his death, edited by Thomas Goodwin, William Greenhill, Sidrach Simpson, William Bridge and others. He reached Hosea 13:11; the rest of the prophecy was completed by others (Thomas Hall on 13:12–16, Edward Reynolds on chapter 14) and is not included here. The text is the keyed transcription made by the Text Creation Partnership from the EEBO microfilms (Vol. 1 (ch. 1–3, 1652): A30572; Vol. 2 (ch. 4–7, 1650): A77979; Vol. 3 (ch. 8–10, 1650): A30574; Vol. 4 (ch. 11–13, 1651): A30575), released under CC0. Original spelling and italics are retained; the long s is printed as s. Page numbers of the original are kept as in-page anchors; each volume is paged from 1. Chapters 1–3 are given one lecture to a page, with the verses each lecture takes up (a lecture often resumes in the middle of the verse where the last one stopped); chapters 4–13 are divided into passages of a few verses, following the sections of the original. Marginal notes are printed as footnotes. The tables of contents, indexes and booksellers’ advertisements are not included.

Illegible places. The transcription is incomplete at 2,866 places: letters or words the keyers could not read from the film images, and the Greek and Hebrew, which they did not transcribe. They are being filled from scans of actual copies on the Internet Archive (Duke University Library, and for chapters 8–10 also the West Virginia Wesleyan copy): 339 places where the OCR of a copy of the same edition reads, at the same place, a word that agrees letter for letter with what the keyers did transcribe (checked against the page images by sample); 883 places read directly from the page images of the same edition. These are printed as plain text. For chapters 1–3 no scan of the 1652 edition is available; 159 places are supplied from the first edition (1643) at the same place and shown in [brackets]. 39 places are damaged in the scanned copy as well and remain open; the rest have not yet been checked and are shown in red.

文本说明。巴罗夫自 1642 年起在伦敦康希尔圣米迦勒堂（St Michael’s, Cornhill）的周间讲座上逐节讲解何西阿书，直到 1646 年 11 月去世。讲稿分四卷出版：第 1–3 章生前即出（此处用 1652 年合订本），第 4–13 章由古德温（Thomas Goodwin）、格林希尔（William Greenhill）、辛普森（Sidrach Simpson）、布里奇（William Bridge）等人在他身后整理刊行。他讲到 13:11 为止，其余由别人续完（13:12–16 霍尔 Thomas Hall，第 14 章雷诺兹 Edward Reynolds），本站未收。正文据 EEBO-TCP 对这四卷的人工录入本（A30572、A77979、A30574、A30575，CC0 公共领域授权），保留原书拼写与斜体，长 ſ 作 s；印本页码保留为页内锚点，各卷页码自 1 起。第 1–3 章一讲一页，标出每讲所讲的经节（常从上一讲讲到的那节中间接着讲，故节段前后相叠）；第 4–13 章照原书分段，每页一段经文。页边旁注排成页底脚注。各卷目录、索引与书商广告未收。

录入本不完整之处共 2,866 处，包括录入者读不出的字母和词，以及没有录入的希腊文、希伯来文。正逐处对照 Internet Archive 上的原书实物扫描补出（杜克大学藏本；第 8–10 章另有西弗吉尼亚卫斯理学院藏本）：同版扫描的 OCR 在同一处读出、且与录入本已录字母逐字相合的 339 处（抽样对图核过）；直接看同版影像读出的 883 处。这两类直接补成正文，不加标记。第 1–3 章找不到 1652 年版的扫描，159 处据 1643 年初版同处补出，正文以[方括号]另色标出。扫描本同处也残缺、读不出的 39 处列在文末；其余尚未核对，正文中标红留在明面。
