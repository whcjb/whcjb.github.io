About this text. Burroughs preached through Hosea in weekly lectures at St Michael’s, Cornhill, London, from 1642 until his death in November 1646. The lectures were published in four volumes: chapters 1–3 in his lifetime (here in the collected edition of 1652), chapters 4–13 after his death, edited by Thomas Goodwin, William Greenhill, Sidrach Simpson, William Bridge and others. He reached Hosea 13:11; the rest of the prophecy was completed by others (Thomas Hall on 13:12–16, Edward Reynolds on chapter 14) and is not included here. The text is the keyed transcription made by the Text Creation Partnership from the EEBO microfilms (Vol. 1 (ch. 1–3, 1652): A30572; Vol. 2 (ch. 4–7, 1650): A77979; Vol. 3 (ch. 8–10, 1650): A30574; Vol. 4 (ch. 11–13, 1651): A30575), released under CC0. Original spelling and italics are retained; the long s is printed as s. Page numbers of the original are kept as in-page anchors; each volume is paged from 1. Chapters 1–3 are given one lecture to a page, with the verses each lecture takes up (a lecture often resumes in the middle of the verse where the last one stopped); chapters 4–13 are divided into passages of a few verses, following the sections of the original. Marginal notes are printed as footnotes. The tables of contents, indexes and booksellers’ advertisements are not included.

Illegible places. The transcription is incomplete at 2,866 places: letters or words the keyers could not read from the film images, and the Greek and Hebrew, which they did not transcribe.
