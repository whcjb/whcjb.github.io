About this edition. The text and its typography (colours, small capitals, alignment) follow the AGES Digital Library edition (AGES Software, 1998; HAL_ROMA.PDF). Paragraph breaks that the AGES typesetting lost in the later chapters are restored from the BibleSupport.com transcription, which descends from the same electronic text. The modern foreword printed in the AGES edition is not reproduced.

Not in the AGES edition and not yet given here: the two dissertations added to the later editions, “The State of the Heathen World Destitute of the Gospel” (with “The Case of Cornelius”) and “The Sanctification of the Sabbath”, and the Appendix (letter to the Rev. Edward Bickersteth; remarks on Mr. Stuart, Dr. Macknight and Mr. Tholuck), pp. 653–702 and 713–730 of the ninth edition (Edinburgh, 1874).

Editorial corrections: Greek words, which the AGES edition gives in its own transliteration code, are given in Greek type; Romans 8:5 (printed “Ver. 6.” in the AGES edition); Romans 8:24 “heaven.” (AGES: comma); Romans 15:5 “received.” (AGES omits the full stop); Romans 5:18 δικαιώματος (AGES omits the μ).
