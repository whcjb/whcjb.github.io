About this edition. The text is the transcription distributed by BibleSupport.com (public domain), taken from StudyLight.org through the Internet Archive. It was checked passage by passage against a scan of the ninth edition (Edinburgh: William Oliphant, 1874; Internet Archive expositionofthee00halduoft): for chapters 1–16 no passage of the commentary is missing.

The transcription contains the exposition of chapters 1–16 only. The following parts of the original edition are not in it and are not yet given here: the Preface and Introduction (pp. 3–18 of the 1874 edition); the two dissertations at the end, “The State of the Heathen World Destitute of the Gospel” (with “The Case of Cornelius”) and “The Sanctification of the Sabbath” (pp. 653–702); the Conclusion (pp. 703–712); and the Appendix, with the letter to the Rev. Edward Bickersteth and the remarks on Mr. Stuart, Dr. Macknight and Mr. Tholuck (pp. 713–730).

Seven footnotes (nos. 22, 23, 34, 38, 56, 57, 58) are referred to in the text but their wording is wanting in the transcription; they are marked as such where they occur.
