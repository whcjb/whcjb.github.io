About this text. EEBO-TCP A22481 (London: William Iaggard, 1612; STC 890), keyed by hand from the EEBO microfilm; CC0. This is the first edition; a second, corrected and enlarged, followed in 1633. Original spelling and italics are retained; the long s is printed as s. Page numbers of the original are kept as in-page anchors.

Illegible places. The keyers marked 155 places they could not read from the black-and-white film images. 131 were read from the grayscale scan of the 1612 microfilm (Internet Archive), place by place against the page image. 12 places still unread remain marked in red; all are listed below.
