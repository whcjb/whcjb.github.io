<p class="at-lhead" markdown="1">Out of the Praeface.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1"><span class="at-dropcap">T</span>He course of the Gospell cannot bee stopped, but will haue his passage in the world. page 1.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">The Argument and occasion of the Epistle, together with the vses thereof. page 7.</p>

<p class="at-lhead" markdown="1">Verse 1, and 2.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 1. Good things must be followed and sought after earnestly and feruently, not coldly and carelesly. pag. 9</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 2. It is no disgrace or reproch to the Seruants of God, to bee cast into prison for the Gospels sake. pag: 12.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 3. The persecutions of all true Christians, are the persecutions of Christ Iesus, when they are imprisoned for Christs sake. page 15.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 4. All good duties to God or man, are better doone by the helpe of others, then alone by our selues. pa: 21</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 5. A christian friend wil performe any christian duty to his friend. page 25.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 6. Christian women should be helpers vnto their husbandes, as heires together of the grace of life, page 29</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 7. The calling of a Minister, is a painfull and laborious, a needefull and troublesome calling. page 33.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 8. It is the duty of all house-holders, to teach and instruct their families that belong vnto them. page 38</p>

<p class="at-lhead" markdown="1">Verse 3.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 1. The free fauour and mercy of God in Christ Iesus is first and aboue all other things to be desired and prayed for. page 48</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 2. Such as are in Gods fauor, haue his blessings flowing vnto them, and following them. page 55</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 3. All blessings temporall and eternall, are to be craued from God alone in Christ Iesus. page 61</p>

<p class="at-lhead" markdown="1">Verse 4, and 5.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 1. Men ought to take cause <span class="at-pb" data-facs="11"></span> of great ioy, to see others growe and proceed in good things. page 68</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 2. It is the nature of faith to apply the mercies and promises of God to our owne selues. page 76</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 3. It is the duty of the faithfull, to pray not onely for themselues, but also for others. page 82</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 4. True Religion must not onely bee inwardly beleeued, but also outwardly confessed, and openly professed. page 86</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 5. Faith and Loue, are the cheefest thinges that commend a man to God and his Church. page 95</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 6. Faith in Christ, and Loue to the Saints, do alwaies go together in all the seruants of God. pag. 99.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 7. Christ must be the Obiect of our faith, we must looke vnto him, and depend vpon him. page 106</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 8. The workes of mercy, are especially, and aboue, & before others to be shewed to the poore Saints that are godly. 111</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 9. Such as truely beleeue in Christ, and belong to him, are Saints. page 117</p>

<p class="at-lhead" markdown="1">Verse 6, and 7.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 1. It is our duty to stir vp our selues and others to encrease in good things. page 126</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 2. The guifts which we haue receiued, must not lye hid in vs, but be employed to the good of others. 140</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 3. The goodnesse of God bestowed vpon our selus or others, must be published abroad, and made known to others. page 142</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 4. The spiritual graces of God bestowed vpon others, doe giue occasion of ioy to the Saints. pa. 147</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 5. The workes of mercie are to bee shewed to the poore distressed Saints. page 154</p>

<p class="at-lhead" markdown="1">Verse 8, and 9.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 1. The Office of the Pastour and Minister of God, is an Office of power and authority vnder christ. 163</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 2. Gentle meanes are to bee vsed rather then seuere, to perswade men to holy duties. page 172</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 3. Superiors in guifts, or age, or both, are to bee reuerenced and regarded aboue others. pa. 177.</p>

<p class="at-lhead" markdown="1">Verse 10.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 1. The least and lowest member conuerted to Christ, must not bee contemned or condemned. page 184</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 2. The same affection that is betweene the Father and the Sonne, ought to be betweene the Minister & the people committed vnto him. 189</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 3. The preaching of the word is the ordinary meanes and instrument of our conuersion and regeneration, page 205</p>

<p class="at-lhead" markdown="1">Verse 11. and 12.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 1. Christian religion maketh a man profitable and helpfull vnto others, that before hath been iniurious and hurtfull. page 227.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 2. In godly, religious, and reformed families, are many times vngodly, obstinate, and vnreformed persons, both Children and Seruaunts. page 237</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 3. Former Offences (albeit great and heynous) vppon true repentance, are to bee forgiuen and forgotten. page 243</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 4. Our loue to al the Saints, especialy such as haue bin conuerted by <span class="at-pb" data-facs="11"></span> vs, ought to bee deare and feruent, page 254</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 5. The Gospell doeth not abolish or diminish ciuill ordinances, & distinct degrees among men. pag: 262</p>

<p class="at-lhead" markdown="1">Verse 13. and 14.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 1. Euery Christian is bound to serue the common good of the church, by what meanes soeuer GOD hath enabled him thereunto. pag: 272</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 2. All christian duties done to God or man, must be done willingly, and chearefully performed, pa: 282</p>

<p class="at-lhead" markdown="1">Verse 15, and 16.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 1. All thinges (euen sinne it selfe) are ordred and turned by the prouidence of God, to the good of the elect. page 295</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 2. God oftentimes taketh from his seruants outward commodities, to bestow vppon them greater, page 305</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 3. The fals and sinnes of our brethren, wherof they haue repented, are not to be encreased and amplified with odious and extreame words, but rather to be buried and forgotten, page 311</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 4. The more grace apeareth in any, the more should they be tendered and regarded of vs. page 322</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 5. Although christian religion do not take away the degrees of persons, yet it maketh vs al equall, and brethren in Christ, page 330</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 6. The more bandes and reasons are giuen vs to care for any, the more wee are bound to care for him, page 337.</p>

<p class="at-lhead" markdown="1">Verse 17.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 1. The consideration of our communion one with another, ought to moue vs to regard one another, and to do all good one to another. pa: 348</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 2. Among Christian friends all things are common. page 350</p>

<p class="at-lhead" markdown="1">Verse 18, and 19.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 1. The communion of Saints doth not take away any mans right & interest in his priuate possessions, and things of this life. page 365</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 2. It is lawfull for one man to become surety for another, and to engage himselfe and his credit. pa: 373</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 3. Couenants in Writing, for debts, bargaines, and sales, are honest and lawfull. page 385</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 4. Such as haue gayned vs vnto God, ought aboue all others to be most deare vnto vs. page 394</p>

<p class="at-lhead" markdown="1">Verse 20, and 21.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 1. No man ought to be eager and extreame in exacting and requiring their debts, dues, and demandes, from the poore and needy, page 407</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 2. Whatsoeuer wee desire, prouoke, and perswade others to doe, must be in the Lord. page 415</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct. 3, Men ought greatly to reioyce at the good and benefit of their brethren, in temporall & eternal blessings, which they see to befall them, page 421</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 4. Men ought alwayes to hope well, and to thinke the best of their brethren, not to suspect the worst of them. page 426</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 5. The faithfull being moued to christian duties, haue yeilded more then hath bin required at their hands. page 431</p>

<p class="at-lhead" markdown="1"><span class="at-pb" data-facs="12"></span>Verse 22.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 1. Hospitality, that is, the ioyfull and courteous entertainment of distressed strangers for the truths sake, is to be vsed and practised of al the seruants of God. page 440</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 2. The prayers of the faithfull are auayleable for themselues and others, both to obtaine blessinges to them, and to remoue iudgments from them. page 447</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 3. The guifts of God bestowed vpon his Seruants, come from his free grace, not from our free will or deserts. page 457</p>

<p class="at-lhead" markdown="1">Verse 23, & 24.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 1. Courteous speeches, and louing Salutations, are beseeming the Seruants of God. page 471</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 2. We must not vtterly cast off the weake, but shew our compassion toward them. page 478</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 3. Many that seeme forward in the profession, do afterwardes fall backe. page 484</p>

<p class="at-lhead" markdown="1">Verse 25.</p>

<p class="at-item" markdown="1">Doct: 1. Spirituall thinges are to bee prayed for, and preferred before earthly things. page 500</p>
