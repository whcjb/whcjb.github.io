About this text. Pierson's expositions of Psalms 27, 84, 85 and 87 were published after his death by Christopher Harvey as Excellent Encouragements against Afflictions (1647), each with its own title page and pagination. The keyed transcription has only the first, on Psalm 27, with the sermon on Psalm 40:1–2 that follows it; the other three are given on their own pages from the 1868 Edinburgh reprint. Each verse is one page here. The text is the keyed transcription of the 1647 edition (EEBO-TCP A54870; released under CC0), with the spelling of the original; only the long s is printed as s. Places the transcribers could not read are shown in red (76 letters, 2 words, 0 longer passages, 15 Greek or Hebrew passages left untranscribed). The publisher's postscript belongs to the exposition of Psalm 84 and is given there.

文本说明。皮尔森讲诗篇 27、84、85、87 篇的四篇讲解，在他身后由哈维编成《苦难中的大鼓励》（1647）出版，四篇各有扉页、各自编页。录入本只有第一篇诗篇 27 篇，连同其后一篇讲诗篇 40:1–2 的讲章；其余三篇另据 1868 年爱丁堡重印本各成一书。本站每节一页。本站正文据 1647 年版的人工录入本（EEBO-TCP A54870，CC0 公共领域授权），拼法照录原书，只把长 s（ſ）印作 s。录入者读不出的地方以红色标出：缺字母 76 个、缺整词 2 处、缺一段以上 0 处，另有希腊文、希伯来文 15 处未录。出版者附言属于诗篇 84 篇那一篇，收在那里。
