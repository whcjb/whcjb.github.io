About this text. Pierson's expositions of Psalms 27, 84, 85 and 87 were published after his death by Christopher Harvey as Excellent Encouragements against Afflictions (London, 1647), each with its own title page and pagination. Only the first, on Psalm 27, survives in a keyed transcription (EEBO-TCP); this one is given from the Edinburgh reprint of 1868 (Nichol's Series of Commentaries), read by OCR from the Princeton copy (Internet Archive davidsblessedman00smit) and checked against a second, independent OCR. Each verse, or group of verses as Pierson handles them, is one page; the notes at the foot of the 1868 pages are collected at the end of each page, and its page numbers are marked in the text. Hebrew and Greek words, which OCR cannot read, are marked in red. OCR errors still remain, chiefly in italic passages; they are being corrected against the page images.

文本说明。皮尔森讲诗篇 27、84、85、87 篇的四篇讲解，在他身后由哈维（Christopher Harvey）编成《苦难中的大鼓励》（伦敦 1647）出版，四篇各有扉页、各自编页。只有第一篇（诗篇 27 篇）有 EEBO-TCP 录入本；本篇据 1868 年爱丁堡 Nichol《清教徒注释丛书》重印本，用普林斯顿藏本扫描件（IA davidsblessedman00smit）OCR 识读，并与另一份独立 OCR 对照校过。按皮尔森的讲法，每节（或几节合讲）一页；1868 年版页脚的注收在各页之末，文中标出该版页码。希伯来文、希腊文 OCR 读不出，以红字标出。目前仍有 OCR 错误，主要在斜体段落，正在对照页面影像逐页校正。
