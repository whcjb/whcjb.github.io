全节可如此翻译：—— <span style="color:#000080">*For doing evil*</span> <span style="color:#000080">are</span> <span style="color:#000080">*their hands, to execute it fully:*</span> <span style="color:#000080">*The prince asks, and the judge*</span> <span style="color:#000080">also,</span> <span style="color:#000080">*for reward;*</span> <span style="color:#000080">*When the great man speaks of oppression,*</span> <span style="color:#000080">That</span> <span style="color:#000080">*it*</span> <span style="color:#000080">is</span> <span style="color:#000080">*his desire, then they contrive it together,*</span> <span style="color:#000080">或，按字面作，</span> <span style="color:#000080">*entwine it.*</span>（他们的手是为行恶，务要行到尽头：首领索取赏赐，审判官也是如此；大人物说到欺压，说那是他所愿的，他们便一同谋划，或，字面作，交织成之。）像 *Newcome* 那样将 <span style="color:#008080">הות נפשו הוא</span> 译作「心中的邪恶」，是把末一字完全省略了；*Henderson* 也是如此。*Piscator* 给出这些字的形式为：「aerumnam, quam expetit——他所渴求的祸害。」末二字按字面乃是「乃是他所愿的」。—*Ed.*
