<p style="text-align:right;" markdown="1">24. In manu servorum tuorum exprobrasti Domino ac dixisti: Ego in multitudine curruum meorum ascendam in excelsa montium, ad latera Libani, succidam summa cedrorum ejus, electas ejus abietes; tum veniam ad summum extremitatis, sed sylvam ejus campestrem, *(vel, planitiei ejus.)*（你藉你仆人的手辱骂主，说：我要用我众多的车辆上山顶，到黎巴嫩的极深之处；我要砍伐其中高大的香柏树和佳美的松树；我必上极高之处，进入它平原的树林，*（或作：它平地的树林。）*）</p>
