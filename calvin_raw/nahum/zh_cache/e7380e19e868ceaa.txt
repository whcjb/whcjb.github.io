<p style="text-align:right;" markdown="1">15. Illic vorabit te ignis, exterminabit te gladius, comedet te quasi bruchus (alii vertunt, quasi bruchum;) multiplicari (ad verbum, vel, multiplicando; est <span style="color:#008080">כירק</span> quasi bruchus,) multiplicare quasi locusta.（在那里，火必吞灭你，刀剑必剪除你，吞灭你如同蝻子〔别的译作：如同吞灭蝻子〕；增多〔按字面作：藉着增多；<span style="color:#008080">כירק</span> 意即如同蝻子〕，增多如蝗虫。）</p>
