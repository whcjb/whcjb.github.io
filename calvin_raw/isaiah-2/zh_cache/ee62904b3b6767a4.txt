<p style="text-align:right;" markdown="1">22. At hic populus direptus est ac conculcatus; illaqueandi in carceribus omnes, et in speluncis abscondentur. Erunt in direptionem, nec erit qui liberet; in praedam, nee quisquam dicet, Redde.（然而这百姓被抢夺、被践踏；众人都必被囚于狱中，藏于洞穴之内。他们必成为掠物，无人施行拯救；成为掠夺之物，无人说：「归还！」）</p>
