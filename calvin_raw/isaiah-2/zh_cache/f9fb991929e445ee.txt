<p style="margin-left:2em;" markdown="1">**2.** 报告耶和华的恩年，和我们神报仇的日子；安慰一切悲哀的人。3. Ad constituendum lugentibus in Sion, ut dem illis decorem pro cinere, oleum gaudii pro luctu, pallium laetitiae pro spiritu angusto, ad vocandum eos arbores justitiae, plantationem Iehovae ad glorificandum.（为锡安悲哀的人所定的，赐他们华冠代替灰尘，喜乐油代替悲哀，欢乐的外袍代替忧伤的灵，称他们为义树，是耶和华所栽的，为要得着荣耀。）</p>
