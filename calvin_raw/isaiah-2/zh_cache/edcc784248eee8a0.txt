<p style="text-align:right;" markdown="1">2. Cur veni, et nemo (occurrit?) vocavi, et nemo respondit? An abbreviando abbreviavit se manus mea, ut non redimat? Annon in me virtus ad liberandum? Ecce increpatione mea exsicco mare; pono flumina in desertum, ut putrescant（我来的时候，为何无人迎接？我呼唤，为何无人答应？我的手岂是缩短了，以致不能救赎？我岂无拯救之能？看哪，我一斥责就使海干涸，使江河变为旷野，以致）</p> <!-- PAGE 827 --> <p style="margin-left:2em;" markdown="1">干渴而死。</p>
