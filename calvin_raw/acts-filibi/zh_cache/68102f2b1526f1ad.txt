<strong>25.</strong> For David saith of him, I saw the Lord before me always, because he is on my right hand; lest I should be moved. <strong>26.</strong> For this my heart rejoiced, and my tongue was glad; moreover, my flesh shall rest in hope: <strong>27.</strong> Because thou wilt not leave my soul in hell, neither wilt thou suffer thy Holy One to see corruption. <strong>28.</strong> Thou hast made known unto me the ways of life, thou shalt fill me with gladness with thy face. <strong>29.</strong> Men and brethren, seeing that I ,nay boldly speak unto you of the patriarch David, because he is both dead and buried, and his sepulcher remaineth with us until this day. <strong>30.</strong> Therefore, seeing he was a prophet, and did know that God had sworn unto him with an oath that it should come to pass, that Christ should rise of the fruits of his loins, concerning the flesh, who should sit upon his throne; <strong>31.</strong> He knowing this before, spoke of the resurrection of Christ, that his soul was not left in hell, neither did his flesh see corruption.
