英文《公祷书》将第 5、6 节译作：“He that hewed timber afore out of the thick trees was known to bring it to an excellent work. But now they break down all the carved work thereof with axes and hammers.”（昔日在密林中伐木者，素以能成精工著称。如今他们却用斧子锤子打坏其中一切雕刻的物件。）尼科尔斯博士对此的意译如下：“It is well known from the sacred records of our nation to what admirable beauty the skillful hand of the artificers brought the rough cedar trees, which were cut down by the hatchets of Hiram's woodmen in the thick Tyrian forests. But now they tear down all the curious carvings, that cost so much time and exquisite labor, with axes and hammers, and other rude instruments of iron.”（从我国的圣典可知，希兰的伐木人在提尔的密林中用斧子砍下的粗糙香柏木，经工匠巧手打造出何等可赞的华美。如今他们却用斧子、锤子和别的粗笨铁器，拆毁那一切耗费许多时日与精工细作的奇巧雕刻。）曼特说：“这是对该处经文清楚而前后一贯的理解，且提供了鲜明而构思精巧的对照。”
