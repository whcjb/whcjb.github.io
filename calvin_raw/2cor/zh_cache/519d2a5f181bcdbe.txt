<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#000080">*Quid facerem? quæ nos tibi consecrauimus olim,*（我当如何？昔日我所献与你者，）</span> <span style="color:#000080">*Eripere haec eadem quo tibi iure queam?*（今以何理可从你手中夺回？）</span> <span style="color:#000080">*Adde, quod ipse tuus quum sit quoque muneris auctor*（更何况，所赐之礼的本源亦在于你，）</span> <span style="color:#000080">*Haec quum dona petis, tu tua dona petis.*（你所求之礼，乃是你自己之礼。）</span> <span style="color:#000080">*Fama igitur valeat, nos iam nil fama moratur*（声名罢了，我已不为声名所留；）</span> <span style="color:#000080">*Fas, tibi quo placeam, displicuisse mihi.”*（为使你喜悦，于己不悦，乃是合宜。）</span></p>
