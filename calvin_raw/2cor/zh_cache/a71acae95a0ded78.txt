"Quaerenda omnino <span style="color:#0000d4">ἰσότης</span> est, sed *analogica* qualis est membrorum in corpore humano, qua quidem non omnia in eodem pretio et dignitate habentur, sed omnia tamen, quae ornamento vel integumento indigent, ornantur et teguntur;"（务必追求*均平*，但乃是*按比例的*均平，正如人体肢体之间所有的那样——肢体诚然并非都被视为同等贵重和尊荣，然而凡需要装饰或遮盖的，都得着装饰和*遮盖*。）——"*Equality* must by all means be aimed at, but *proportional,* such as subsists among the members of the human body, according to which they are not, indeed, all held in the same estimation and dignity, but all of them notwithstanding, that require ornament or clothing, are adorned and *clothed.*"*——海德格尔（Heideggerus）。——编者注。*
