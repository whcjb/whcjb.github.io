<p style="margin-left:2em;" markdown="1">**2.** Nam miserat in domum Dei (hoc est, Templum) Sareezer et Regem-melech et viros ejus ad deprecandam faciem Iehovae (aut, Miserat in domum Dei Sareezer et Regem-melech, in nominativo casu, et viri ejus ad deprecandam faciem Iehovae:) 3. 并问万军之耶和华殿中的祭司和先知说：「我历年以来，在五月间哭泣斋戒，现在还当这样行吗？」</p>
