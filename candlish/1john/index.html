About this text. The text follows the Monergism Books e-text (2020), a modern keyed transcription in which Scripture references are given in modern form (1 John 2:3) and the original italics are not marked. That e-text omits the author's footnotes, leaving only the asterisks; their wording has been supplied from a second, independent transcription (the e-Sword / BibleSupport module, from William A. Anderson's 2008 Libronix edition), cleared of that module's conversion damage. Where the second transcription supplies a word or a passage the first has dropped or misspelled, the reading has been adopted. Obvious misprints shared by both, and Scripture references lost from both, have been corrected or supplied by the editor (supplied references are shown [in brackets]). Every change is listed in the table below.
