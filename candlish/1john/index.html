About this text. The text follows the Monergism Books e-text (2020), a modern keyed transcription in which Scripture references are given in modern form (1 John 2:3) and the original italics are not marked. That e-text omits the author's footnotes, leaving only the asterisks; their wording has been supplied from a second, independent transcription (the e-Sword / BibleSupport module, from William A. Anderson's 2008 Libronix edition), cleared of that module's conversion damage. Where the second transcription supplies a word or a passage the first has dropped or misspelled, the reading has been adopted (7 places). Places wanting in both are marked ….
