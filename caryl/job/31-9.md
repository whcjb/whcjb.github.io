<p class="cy-vhead" markdown="1">Vers. 9. If my heart hath been deceived by a woman, &c.</p>

In this verse Job offers two things to prove his clearenesse from that sin.

First, That he had kept a faithfull watch at all times upon his heart, least it should goe vainely out after, or be enticed by women.

Secondly, He had been carefull to avoyd ( which is the custome of uncleane persons to seeke) all opportunities of running into that sin. As he watcht his heart at all times, so he never watcht his time at his neighbours doore, to injoy those forbidden pleasures.

If my heart hath been deceived by a woman.
