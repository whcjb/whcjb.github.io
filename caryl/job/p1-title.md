<span class="cy-pb" data-facs="1"></span><span class="cy-pb" data-facs="1"></span>AN EXPOSITION WITH PRACTICAL OBSERVATIONS Vpon the three first CHAPTERS of the Booke of *IOB.*

Delivered in XXI. *Lectures,* at MAGNUS neare the Bridge, *London.*

By JOSEPH CARYL, Preacher to the Honourable Society of *Lincolnes-Inne.*

<p class="cy-quote" markdown="1">JAMES 5. ver. 10, 11.</p>

<p class="cy-quote" markdown="1">Take my bretheren the Prophets, who have spoken in the Name of the Lord, for an example of suffering affliction, and of patience.</p>

<p class="cy-quote" markdown="1">Behold we count them happy which endure. Yee have heard of the patience of *Job,* and have seene the end of the Lord: that the Lord is very pitifull, and of tender mercy.</p>

LONDON, Printed by *G. Miller,* for *Henry Overton* in Popes-head-Alley, and *Luke Fawne,* and *Iohn Rothwell* in *Pauls* Church-yard. M.DC.XLIII.
