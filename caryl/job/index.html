About this text. Caryl expounded Job in a long course of lectures at St Magnus the Martyr, near London Bridge, and published them in twelve quarto parts as the course went on, from 1643 to 1666 (collected in two folio volumes in 1676–77). The eight parts given here contain 284 lectures by the count on their title pages. The text here is the keyed transcription of 8 of those parts made by the Text Creation Partnership (EEBO-TCP A35389, A35438, A35439, A35473, A81199, A35535, A35537, A35538; released under CC0), each from the edition named on its title page; part 2 is the reissue of 1656. Spelling is that of the original; only the long s is printed as s. Each page is one section of the exposition as Caryl divided it, headed by the verses he quotes; the page numbers of the printed part are shown in the text. Places the transcribers could not read are shown in red (7,381 letters, 139 words, 27 longer passages, and 2,260 Greek or Hebrew words left untranscribed). Part 4 (chapters 11–14), Part 6 (chapters 18–21), Part 8 (chapters 27–29), Part 9 (chapters 30–31) were never transcribed by EEBO-TCP and will be added from page images. The subject and Scripture indexes at the end of each part are not included.

文本说明。卡里尔在伦敦桥边圣马格努斯堂逐节讲约伯记，边讲边分 12 部（四开本）出版， 1643 年出第一部，1666 年出完；1676–77 年合为对开两卷。本站所收 8 部，按各部扉页所记共 284 讲。本站正文据 EEBO-TCP 人工录入本（A35389, A35438, A35439, A35473, A81199, A35535, A35537, A35538，CC0 公共领域授权）， 共 8 部，各据扉页所印之版；第二部是 1656 年再版。拼法照录原书，只把长 s（ſ）印作 s。一页是卡里尔所分的一段，段首是他所引经文， 正文中标出原书页码。录入者读不出的地方以红色标出：缺字母 7,381 个、缺整词 139 处、缺一段以上 27 处， 另有希腊文、希伯来文 2,260 处未录。第四部（11–14 章）、第六部（18–21 章）、第八部（27–29 章）、第九部（30–31 章）EEBO-TCP 未录入，待据原书影像补录。各部卷末的主题索引与经文索引未收。
