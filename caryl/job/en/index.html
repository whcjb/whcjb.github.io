About this text. Caryl expounded Job in a long course of lectures at St Magnus the Martyr, near London Bridge, and published them in twelve quarto parts as the course went on, from 1643 to 1666 (collected in two folio volumes in 1676–77). The eight parts given here contain 284 lectures by the count on their title pages. The text here is the keyed transcription of 8 of those parts made by the Text Creation Partnership (EEBO-TCP A35389, A35438, A35439, A35473, A81199, A35535, A35537, A35538; released under CC0), each from the edition named on its title page; part 2 is the reissue of 1656. Spelling is that of the original; only the long s is printed as s. Each page is one section of the exposition as Caryl divided it, headed by the verses he quotes; the page numbers of the printed part are shown in the text. Places the transcribers could not read are shown in red (7,381 letters, 139 words, 27 longer passages, and 2,260 Greek or Hebrew words left untranscribed). Part 4 (chapters 11–14), Part 6 (chapters 18–21), Part 9 (chapters 30–31) were never transcribed by EEBO-TCP; their text here is machine-read (OCR, kraken with the CATMuS-Print model) from page images of the printed parts, then corrected word by word against a second copy wherever the two could be aligned. It has not been proofread by eye: words neither copy could settle are underlined with dots, and some misreadings that happen to form real words remain. Italics are not reproduced in these parts. Part 8 (chapters 27–29) was never transcribed by EEBO-TCP and will be added from page images. The subject and Scripture indexes at the end of each part are not included.
