About this text. The text is the keyed transcription of the 1574 London edition (EEBO-TCP A69056, STC 4445; released under CC0), in Arthur Golding's English translation from the French. The spelling is that of the original; only the long s has been printed as s. Places the transcribers could not read on the microfilm are shown in red: 524 letters, 8 words and 110 longer passages. Two headings are misnumbered in the original (Sermon 78 printed lxxvij., Sermon 112 printed Cij.) and the heading of Sermon 124 gives chapter xxxiiij. for xxxiij.; they are printed as they stand, and the sermons are numbered and grouped by their place in the book. The indexes at the end of the volume are not yet included.

文本说明。本书正文据 1574 年伦敦版的人工录入本（EEBO-TCP A69056，STC 4445，CC0 公共领域授权），即戈尔丁（Arthur Golding）自法文译出的英译本。拼法照录原书，只把长 s（ſ）印作 s。录入者在胶片上读不出的地方以红色记号标出：缺字母 524 个、缺整词 8 处、缺一段以上 110 处；尚未据其他扫描本补全。原书有几处标题印错：第 78 篇印作 lxxvij.、第 112 篇印作 Cij.，第 124 篇把第 33 章印成 xxxiiij.；标题照录，篇次与归章按书中实际次序。卷末索引暂未收录。
