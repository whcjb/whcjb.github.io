<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 75</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. Destroy not. A Psalm of Asaph. A song*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> We will celebrate thee, O God! we will celebrate *thee*; And near is thy name: they will declare thy wonders.

<sup>2</sup> When I shall have taken the congregation, I in uprightness will judge.

<sup>3</sup> Dissolved is the earth, and all the inhabitants thereof: will establish the pillars of it. Selah.

<sup>4</sup> I said to the fools, “Act not foolishly; “ And to the ungodly, “Raise not up the horn.”

<!-- PAGE 941 -->

<sup>5</sup> Raise not up on high your horn; And speak not with a stiff neck. g. For neither from the east, nor from the west, Nor from the wilderness are exaltations.

<sup>7</sup> For God is judge: tie bringeth low, and he setteth up.

<sup>8</sup> For a cup *is* in the hand of Jehovah, and the wine is turbid; It is full of mixture, and he shall pour forth of it. Surely the dregs thereof they shall wring out;. And drink *of it* shall all the wicked of the earth.

<sup>9</sup> But as for me I will publish for ever, I will sing psalms to the God of Jacob.

<sup>10</sup> And all the horns of the ungodly I will break; But exalted shall be the horns of the righteous.
