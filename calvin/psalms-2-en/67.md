<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 67</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician on Neginoth. A Psalm [*</span><span style="color:#000080">or</span><span style="color:#000080">*] Song*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> May God have compassion upon us, and bless us; May he cause his countenance to shine upon us. Selah.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>2</sup> That thy way may be known upon the earth, Among all nations thy salvation.</p>

<sup>3</sup> Let the peoples praise thee, O God! Let all the peoples celebrate thee.

<!-- PAGE 927 -->

<sup>4</sup> Let the peoples rejoice and exult, For he shall judge the peoples with rectitude, And the nations upon earth thou shalt guide. Selah.

<sup>5</sup> Let the peoples celebrate thee, O God! Let all the peoples celebrate thee:

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>6</sup> The earth hath given *her* increase; God [*even*] our own God will bless us.</p>

<sup>7</sup> God will bless us; And fear him shall all the ends of the earth.
