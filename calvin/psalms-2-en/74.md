<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 74</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*An Instruction of Asaph*</span></p>

<!-- PAGE 939 -->

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> Wherefore, O God! hast thou cast us off for ever? [*Why*] smoketh thy wrath against the flock of thy pastures?

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> Remember thy congregation which thou hast possessed of old; <span style="color:#800000">ff97</span> *Which* thou hast redeemed, the rod of thy inheritance, This Mount Zion in which thou hast dwelt.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> Lift up thy strokes to desolations perpetual, Against every enemy who doeth mischief to the sanctuary.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Roared *like lions* have thy adversaries in the midst of thy sanctuaries; They have set up their signs *for* signs.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> He was renowned as doing an excellent work Who lifted up the axes upon the thick trees. <span style="color:#800000">ff98</span> <span style="color:#800000">6</span> And now the carved works thereof together, With the hatchet and with hammers they break in pieces.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> They have set on fire thy sanctuaries; Leveling it with the ground, they have polluted the dwelling-place of thy name.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> They have said in their heart, “Let us destroy them altogether.” They have burned all the tabernacles of God in the land.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> Our signs we see not; There is no longer a prophet, Nor is there with us one who knoweth how long! — <span style="color:#800000">10</span> How long, O God! shall the adversary reproach? Shall the enemy blaspheme thy name for ever?

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> How long wilt thou withdraw thy hand, and thy right hand? In the midst, of thy bosom consume them.

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> But God *is* my king from the beginning, Working salvations in the midst of the earth.

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> Thou didst divide  <span style="color:#800000">ff99</span> by thy power the sea; Thou didst break the heads of dragons in the waters.

<span style="color:#800000">14</span> Thou didst break the head of the leviathan; Thou gavest him *for* food to *thy* people in the wilderness.

<!-- PAGE 940 -->

<span style="color:#800000">15</span> Thou didst cleave the fountain and the torrent: Thou didst dry up mighty rivers.

<span style="color:#800000">16</span> Thine is the day; thine also is the night: Thou hast ordained the light and the sun.

<span style="color:#800000">17</span> Thou hast fixed all the boundaries of the earth: The summer and the winter thou hast made.

<span style="color:#800000">18</span> Remember this: the enemy hath reproached Jehovah; And a worthless people hath done despite to thy name.

<span style="color:#800000">19</span> Give not to the beast the soul of thy turtle dove: The congregation of thy poor ones forget not for ever.

<span style="color:#800000">20</span> Have regard to *thy* covenant; For filled are the dark places of the earth with the abodes of violence.

<span style="color:#800000">21</span> Let not the afflicted one return ashamed; Let the poor and needy one praise thy name.

<span style="color:#800000">22</span> Arise, O God! plead thy own cause: Remember thy reproach, *which is done thee* by the foolish man daily.

<span style="color:#800000">23</span> Forget not the voice of thy adversaries: The tumult of those who rise up against thee ascendeth continually.
