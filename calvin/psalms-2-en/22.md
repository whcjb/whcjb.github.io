<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 22</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. Upon the hind of the morning. A Psalm Of David*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> My God! my God! why hast thou forsaken me? [*Why art thou*] far from my help, from the words of my roaring?

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> O my God! I cry by day, and thou dost not hear: And in the night, and am not silent.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> Yet thou art holy, Inhabiting the praises of Israel.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> In thee trust did our fathers: They trusted, and thou didst deliver them.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> To thee they cried, and were saved: In thee they trusted, and were not put to shame.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> But I *am* a worm, and not a man; The reproach of men, and despised of the people.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> All who see me scoff at me: They thrust out the lip, they shake the head.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> “He hath devolved,” *say they*, “upon Jehovah, *his* cause, let him rescue him, “Let him deliver him, since he hath a favor for him.”

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> Surely it is thou who didst take me out of the womb, Causing me to confide upon the breasts of my mother.

<!-- PAGE 871 -->

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> Upon thee I was cast from the womb: From the belly of my mother my God art thou.

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> Depart not far from me, for trouble is near, For there is no helper.

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> Encompassed me have bulls [*that are*] strong, The bulls of Bashan have beset me.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">13</span> They have opened upon me their mouth, As a lion ravening and roaring.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">14</span> Like water I have flowed, <span style="color:#800000">ff66</span> And disjointed have been  <span style="color:#800000">ff67</span> all my bones: My heart hath been  <span style="color:#800000">ff68</span> like wax, It hath been  <span style="color:#800000">ff69</span>

<p style="margin-left:2em;" markdown="1">melted in the midst of my bowels.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">15</span> Dried up as a potsherd hath been  <span style="color:#800000">ff70</span> my strength, And my tongue cleaveth to my jaws; And to the dust of death thou hast brought me.

<span style="color:#800000">16</span> For encompassed me have dogs; The assembly of the wicked have surrounded me: They have pierced my hands and my feet.

<span style="color:#800000">17</span> I will count all my bones; As for them they look and gaze upon me.

<span style="color:#800000">18</span> They divide my garments among them; Upon my vesture they cast the lot.

<span style="color:#800000">19</span> And thou, O Jehovah! be not far from me; *Thou who art* my strength, to my aid hasten.

<span style="color:#800000">20</span> Rescue from the sword my soul; From the hand of the dog my only one.

<span style="color:#800000">21</span> Save me from the mouth of the lion, And from the horns of unicorns do thou hear me.

<span style="color:#800000">22</span> I will declare thy name to my brethren; In the midst of the assembly will I praise thee.

<!-- PAGE 872 -->

<span style="color:#800000">23</span> *Saying*, “Ye who fear Jehovah praise him: “All ye the seed of Jacob glorify him: “And stand in awe of him, all ye the seed of Israel.

<span style="color:#800000">24</span> “For he hath not despised nor disdained the poor; “Nor hath he hidden his face from him; “And when he cried to him, he heard him.”

<span style="color:#800000">25</span> From thee shall proceed my praise in the congregation great; My vows will I pay before them that fear him.

<span style="color:#800000">26</span> Eat shall the poor, and be satisfied; They shall praise Jehovah who seek him; Your heart shall live for ever.

<span style="color:#800000">27</span> Remember and turn to Jehovah shall all the ends of the earth; And prostrate themselves before his face shall all the tribes of the Gentiles.

<span style="color:#800000">28</span> For Jehovah’s is the kingdom, That he may have dominion among the Gentiles.

<span style="color:#800000">29</span> Eat and worship shall all the fat ones of the earth: Before his face shall bow all who are going down to the dust; And he who his own soul doth not quicken.

<span style="color:#800000">30</span> *Their* seed shall serve him, It shall be registered to the Lord for a generation.
