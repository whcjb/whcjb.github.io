<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 43</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> Judge me, O God! and plead my cause: From a people without mercy, from a man deceitful and wicked deliver me.

<sup>2</sup> For thou *art* the God of my strength; Why art thou estranged from me? Why mourning do I go because of the oppression of the enemy?

<sup>3</sup> Send forth thy light and thy truth; Let them direct me, let them conduct me, To the mountain of thy holiness, and to thy tabernacles.

<sup>4</sup> And I will go to the altar of God, To the God of the joy of my rejoicing; And I will celebrate thee upon the harp, O God, my God!

<!-- PAGE 900 -->

<sup>5</sup> Why art thou cast down, O my soul! And why art thou disquieted within me? Hope in God, for I shall yet celebrate him, *Who is* the salvation of my countenance and my God.
