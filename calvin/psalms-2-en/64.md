<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 64</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. A Psalm of David*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> Hear, O God! my voice in my prayer: From fear of the enemy preserve my life.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> Hide me from the counsel of malignant men — From the assembly of tire workers of iniquity.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> For they have sharpened as a sword their tongue; They have directed for their arrow a bitter word.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> To shoot in secret at the innocent man: Suddenly they will shoot and not fear.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> They encourage themselves in an impious action; They talk together of secretly laying snares; They say, “Who shall see them?”

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> They have searched out iniquities, They have accomplished a searching search: And the inward part of each of them and the heart [*is*] deep.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> But God shall shoot at them an arrow; Suddenly shall they be wounded.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> And they shall make their own tongue fall upon themselves, And flee away shall all who see them.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> And see shall all men, and shall declare the work of God, And his doing they shall understand.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> Rejoice shall the righteous in Jehovah, and they shall hope in him; And glory shall all the upright in heart.
