<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 17</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*A Prayer of David*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> Hear, O Jehovah! my righteousness; Attend to my cry; Hearken to my prayer, which is not in lips of deceit.

<sup>2</sup> From the presence of thy countenance let my judgment go forth Let thine eyes behold uprightness.

<sup>3</sup> Thou hast proved my heart; Thou hast visited *it* by night; Thou hast examined *it*, thou shalt not find *any thing in it*; My thought shall not pass beyond my mouth.

<sup>4</sup> As for the works of men, by the word of thy lips I have taken heed of the ways of the destroyer.

<sup>5</sup> Uphold my steps in thy paths, That my footsteps may not slide.

<sup>6</sup> I have called upon thee, surely thou wilt hear me, O God! Incline thy ear to me, and hear my speech.

<sup>7</sup> Make marvelous thy mercies, O thou Preserver of those who trust [*in thee*,] From those that exalt themselves against thy right hand.

<sup>8</sup> Keep me as the apple, the daughter of the eye; In the shadow of thy wings hide me, <sup>9</sup> From the face of the ungodly who endeavor to destroy me — Of my enemies who in my soul besiege me.

<sup>10</sup> In their own fat they have enclosed themselves; With their mouth they have spoken haughtily.

<!-- PAGE 863 -->

<sup>11</sup> In our steps they have now encompassed me; eyes they have set to east down to the ground.

<sup>12</sup> He is like a lion, he desireth to seize his prey, And like a lion’s whelp which lurketh in secret places.

<sup>13</sup> Arise, O Jehovah! confront him, lay him prostrate; Deliver my soul from the ungodly man by thy sword <sup>14</sup> From men by thy hand, O Jehovah! — From men who are of long duration, Whose portion is in life, Whose belly thou fillets with thy secret, goods: Filled to the full are their children with them, And their residue they leave to their babes.

<sup>15</sup> As for me in righteousness I shall behold thy face; I shall be satisfied when I shall awake with thy likeness.
