<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 28</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*Of David*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> Unto thee, O Jehovah! will I cry; O my strength! hold not thy peace from me Lest if thou shouldst be silent to me, I then become like those who descend into the grave.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> Hear the voice of my prayers when I cry to thee, When I lift up my hands to the sanctuary of thy holiness. 3, Draw me not away with ungodly men, And with the workers of iniquity, Who speak peace  <span style="color:#800000">ff72</span> with their neighbors, When in their hearts is malice.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Give them according to their works, And according to the wickedness of their doings: According to the work of their hands give them, Render their reward to them.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> Because they do not consider the doing of Jehovah, Nor the work of his hands, Let him destroy them and not build them up.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> Blessed *be* Jehovah! For he hath heard the voice of my supplications.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> Jehovah *is* my strength and my shield; In him trusted hath my heart, and I have been helped: Therefore exult shall my heart, And with my song will I celebrate him.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> Jehovah *is* strength to them, And the strength of the salvations of his Anointed *is* He.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> Save thy people, and bless thy inheritance; Feed them and exalt them for ever.
