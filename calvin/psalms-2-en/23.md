<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 23</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#000080">*A Psalm of David*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> Jehovah is my shepherd, therefore I shall not want any thing.

<sup>2</sup> In pastures of grass he maketh me to lie down; To waters gently flowing he leadeth me.

<!-- PAGE 873 -->

<sup>3</sup> My soul he restoreth: He leadeth me by the paths of righteousness for his name’s sake.

<sup>4</sup> Though I should walk in the valley of the shadow of death, I will not fear evil, because thou art with me; Thy staff and thy crook they comfort me.

<sup>5</sup> Thou wilt prepare before me a table in the presence of my persecutors; Thou wilt anoint with oil my head; My cup overfloweth.

<sup>6</sup> Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life, And I shall dwell in the house of Jehovah for a length of days.
