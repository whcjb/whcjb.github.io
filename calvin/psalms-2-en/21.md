<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 21</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. A Psalm Of David*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> O Jehovah! in thy strength rejoice shall the king, And in thy salvation how greatly shall he exult!

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> The desire of his heart thou hast given him, And what he uttered with his lips, thou hast not denied *him*. Selah.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> For thou shalt prevent him with blessings of good; Thou shalt place upon his head a crown of gold.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Life he asked from thee; Thou hast given him length of days for ever and ever.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> Great *is* his glory in thy salvation: Splendor and beauty thou hast put upon him.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> For thou hast set him [*to be*] blessings for ever: Thou hast gladdened him with joy before thy countenance.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> For the king trusteth in Jehovah; And through the goodness of the Most High, he shall not be moved <span style="color:#800000">8</span> Find out shall thy hand all thy enemies; Thy right hand shall find out thy haters.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> Thou shalt put them as in a furnace of fire in the time of thy wrath, O Jehova! In his wrath he shall overwhelm them, And consume them shall the fire.

<!-- PAGE 870 -->

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> Their fruit from the earth thou wilt destroy, And their seed from among the sons of men. 1l. For they have spread out against thee evil; They have devised against thee a stratagem which they could not accomplish.

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> For thou wilt set them as a butt; On thy strings thou shalt make ready *thy arrows* against their faces.

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> Be thou exalted, O Jehovah! in thy strength, *Then* we will sing and celebrate in psalms thy power.
