<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 72</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#000080">*Of Solomon*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> O God! thy judgments give to the King, And thy righteousness to the son of the King <sup>2</sup> He shall judge thy people in righteousness, And thy poor ones in judgment.

<sup>3</sup> Bring forth shall the mountains peace to the people, And the hills in righteousness.

<sup>4</sup> He shall judge the poor ones of the people; He shall save the children of the afflicted; And shall break in pieces the calumniator.

<sup>5</sup> They shall fear thee with the sun; And before the moon the generation of generations shall fear thee.

<sup>6</sup> He shall descend as rain upon the mown grass; As the showers which water the earth.

<sup>7</sup> Flourish in his days shall the righteous; And abundance of peace until there be no more moon.

<sup>8</sup> And he shall have dominion from sea to sea, And from the river even to the ends of the earth.

<sup>9</sup> Before him shall bow the inhabitants of the desert, And his enemies shall lick the dust.

<!-- PAGE 936 -->

<sup>10</sup> The kings of Tarshish and of the isles a present shall bring: The kings of Sheba and Seba a gift shall offer *to him*.

<sup>11</sup> And all kings shall prostrate themselves before him; All nations shall serve him.

<sup>12</sup> For he shall deliver the poor who crieth *to him*, And the needy who hath no helper.

<sup>13</sup> He will have compassion upon the indigent and the poor one, And the souls of the poor ones he will save.

<sup>14</sup> From fraud and violence he will redeem their souls, And precious shall be their blood in his eyes.

<sup>15</sup> And he shall live, and there shall be given to him of the gold of Sheba, And prayer shall be made for him continually; Daily shall he be blessed.

<sup>16</sup> There shall be *sown* an handful of corn in the earth on the top of the mountains; The fruit thereof shall be shaken like [*that*] of Lebanon. And they shall go forth from the city as a plant of the earth.

<sup>17</sup> His name shall be for ever; In presence of the sun propagated shall be his name: And bless themselves in him shall all nations, And blessed they shall call him.

<sup>18</sup> Blessed be Jehovah God! the God of Israel! <span style="color:#800000">ff95</span> Who doeth wonderful things alone.

<sup>19</sup> And blessed be his glorious name for ever; And let all the earth be filled with his glory. Amen and Amen.

<sup>20</sup> Finished are the prayers of David, the son of Jesse.
