<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 12</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician upon the Eighth. A Psalm of David*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> Save me, O Jehovah! for failed hath the merciful man, For wasted away are the faithful from among the children of men.

<sup>2</sup> Deceit doth every one speak with his neighbor; With lips of flatteries, with a double heart do they speak.

<!-- PAGE 859 -->

<sup>3</sup> Let Jehovah cut off all lips of flatteries, The tongue that great things doth speak:

<sup>4</sup> Those who have said, “By our tongues we will be strengthened; “Our lips are our own; who is lord over us?”

<sup>5</sup> “Because of the spoiling of the needy, “Because of the groaning of the poor, “Now will I arise,” say will Jehovah, “I will set in safety him for whom *the wicked man* layeth snares.”

<sup>6</sup> The words of Jehovah are pure words; Silver melted in an excellent crucible of earth, purified seven times.

<sup>7</sup> Thou, O Jehovah! wilt keep them; Thou wilt preserve him from this generation for ever.

<sup>8</sup> On every side the ungodly walk; When they are exalted, reproach is to the children of men.
