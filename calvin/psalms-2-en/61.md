<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 61</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician upon Neginoth. A Psalm of David*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> Hear, O God! my cry, Attend to my prayer.

<sup>2</sup> From the end of the earth to thee will I cry, when vexed is my heart: To the rock which is higher than I thou shalt lead me.

<sup>3</sup> For thou hast been a hope to me, A tower of strength from the face of the enemy.

<sup>4</sup> I will dwell in thy tabernacle for ever; I will be safe under the covert of thy wings. Selah.

<sup>5</sup> For thou, O God! hast heard my vows: Thou hast given an inheritance to those who fear thy name.

<!-- PAGE 921 -->

<sup>6</sup> Days upon days to the king thou shalt add, His years *shall be* as many generations.

<sup>7</sup> He shall dwell for ever in the presence of God: Mercy and truth do thou prepare: thou shalt preserve him.

<sup>8</sup> So will I sing unto thy name for ever, That I may perform my vows every day.
