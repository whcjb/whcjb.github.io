<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 29</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#000080">*A Psalm of David*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> Ascribe to Jehovah: ye sons of the mighty — Ascribe to Jehovah glory and strength.

<!-- PAGE 879 -->

<sup>2</sup> Ascribe to Jehovah the glory of his name; Worship before Jehovah in the brightness of his sanctuary.

<sup>3</sup> The voice of Jehovah is upon the waters; The God of glory thundereth; Jehovah is upon the waters great.

<sup>4</sup> The voice of Jehovah is in strength, The voice of Jehovah is in majesty.

<sup>5</sup> The voice of Jehovah breaketh the cedars; Break, I say, doth Jehovah the cedars of Lebanon.

<sup>6</sup> And he maketh Lebanon to leap like a calf, And Sirion like the son of the unicorns.

<sup>7</sup> The voice of Jehovah striketh out flames of fire.

<sup>8</sup> The voice of Jehovah maketh the desert to tremble. Jehovah maketh to tremble the desert of Kadesh.

<sup>9</sup> The voice of Jehovah maketh the hinds to bring forth, And discovereth the forests, And all *meanwhile* in his temple speak his praise.

<sup>10</sup> Jehovah over the flood presideth, Preside, I say, doth Jehovah [*as*] King for ever.

<sup>11</sup> Jehovah strength to his people will give; Jehovah will bless his people with peace.
