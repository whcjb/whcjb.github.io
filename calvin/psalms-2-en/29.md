<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 29</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*A Psalm of David*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> Ascribe to Jehovah: ye sons of the mighty — Ascribe to Jehovah glory and strength.

<!-- PAGE 879 -->

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> Ascribe to Jehovah the glory of his name; Worship before Jehovah in the brightness of his sanctuary.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> The voice of Jehovah is upon the waters; The God of glory thundereth; Jehovah is upon the waters great.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> The voice of Jehovah is in strength, The voice of Jehovah is in majesty.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> The voice of Jehovah breaketh the cedars; Break, I say, doth Jehovah the cedars of Lebanon.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> And he maketh Lebanon to leap like a calf, And Sirion like the son of the unicorns.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> The voice of Jehovah striketh out flames of fire.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> The voice of Jehovah maketh the desert to tremble. Jehovah maketh to tremble the desert of Kadesh.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> The voice of Jehovah maketh the hinds to bring forth, And discovereth the forests, And all *meanwhile* in his temple speak his praise.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> Jehovah over the flood presideth, Preside, I say, doth Jehovah [*as*] King for ever.

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> Jehovah strength to his people will give; Jehovah will bless his people with peace.
