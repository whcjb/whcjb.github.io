<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 51</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. A Psalm of David, when Xathan the prophet came to*</span> <span style="color:#000080">*him, after he had gone in to Bathsheba*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> Have pity upon me, O God! according to thy mercy; According to the multitude of thy compassions blot out my iniquities.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> Multiply to wash me from my sin, And from my wickedness do thou cleanse me.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> For my sins I know, And my wickedness is before me continually.

<!-- PAGE 910 -->

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Against thee, against thee only, have I sinned, And that which was displeasing in thy sight have I done; That thou mayest be justified when thou speakest, And be pure in giving judgment.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> Behold in iniquity I was born, And in sin conceive me did my mother.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> Behold, truth thou hast loved in the inmost parts, And in secret, wisdom thou hast made me know.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> Thou shalt purge me with hyssop, and I shall be clean; Thou shalt wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> Make me to hear joy and gladness; And exult shall the bones which thou hast broken.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> Hide thy face from my sins, And all my iniquities blot out.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> A heart [*that is*] clean create in me, O God! And a spirit [*that is*] right renew in my inward parts.

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> Cast me not away from thy face, And the Spirit of thy holiness take not from me.

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> Restore to me the joy of thy salvation, And with a free spirit uphold me.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">13</span> I will teach transgressors thy ways, the ungodly to time shall be converted.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">14</span> Deliver me from bloods, O God! O God of my salvation! And sing aloud shall my tongue of thy righteousness.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">15</span> O Lord! my lips do thou open, And my mouth shall show forth thy praise.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">16</span> For thou wilt not accept a sacrifice; Though I should give a burnt-offering, it would not please thee.

<span style="color:#800000">17</span> The sacrifices of God are an afflicted spirit: A heart afflicted and contrite, O God! thou wilt not despise.

<!-- PAGE 911 -->

<span style="color:#800000">18</span> Do good in thy good pleasure to Zion; Build thou the walls of Jerusalem.

<span style="color:#800000">19</span> Then shalt thou accept the sacrifices of righteousness, The burnt-offering and oblation; <span style="color:#800000">ff80</span> Then shall come upon thy altar calves.
