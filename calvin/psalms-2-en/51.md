<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 51</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. A Psalm of David, when Xathan the prophet came to*</span> <span style="color:#000080">*him, after he had gone in to Bathsheba*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> Have pity upon me, O God! according to thy mercy; According to the multitude of thy compassions blot out my iniquities.

<sup>2</sup> Multiply to wash me from my sin, And from my wickedness do thou cleanse me.

<sup>3</sup> For my sins I know, And my wickedness is before me continually.

<!-- PAGE 910 -->

<sup>4</sup> Against thee, against thee only, have I sinned, And that which was displeasing in thy sight have I done; That thou mayest be justified when thou speakest, And be pure in giving judgment.

<sup>5</sup> Behold in iniquity I was born, And in sin conceive me did my mother.

<sup>6</sup> Behold, truth thou hast loved in the inmost parts, And in secret, wisdom thou hast made me know.

<sup>7</sup> Thou shalt purge me with hyssop, and I shall be clean; Thou shalt wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow.

<sup>8</sup> Make me to hear joy and gladness; And exult shall the bones which thou hast broken.

<sup>9</sup> Hide thy face from my sins, And all my iniquities blot out.

<sup>10</sup> A heart [*that is*] clean create in me, O God! And a spirit [*that is*] right renew in my inward parts.

<sup>11</sup> Cast me not away from thy face, And the Spirit of thy holiness take not from me.

<sup>12</sup> Restore to me the joy of thy salvation, And with a free spirit uphold me.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>13</sup> I will teach transgressors thy ways, the ungodly to time shall be converted.</p>

<sup>14</sup> Deliver me from bloods, O God! O God of my salvation! And sing aloud shall my tongue of thy righteousness.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>15</sup> O Lord! my lips do thou open, And my mouth shall show forth thy praise.</p>

<sup>16</sup> For thou wilt not accept a sacrifice; Though I should give a burnt-offering, it would not please thee.

<sup>17</sup> The sacrifices of God are an afflicted spirit: A heart afflicted and contrite, O God! thou wilt not despise.

<!-- PAGE 911 -->

<sup>18</sup> Do good in thy good pleasure to Zion; Build thou the walls of Jerusalem.

<sup>19</sup> Then shalt thou accept the sacrifices of righteousness, The burnt-offering and oblation; <span style="color:#800000">ff80</span> Then shall come upon thy altar calves.
