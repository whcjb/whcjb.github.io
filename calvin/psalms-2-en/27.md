<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 27</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*Of David*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> Jehovah is my light and my salvation, Whom shall I fear? Jehovah *is* the strength of my life, Of whom shall I be afraid?

<sup>2</sup> When approach against me did the wicked, To devour my flesh — My oppressors and my enemies against me — They stumbled and fell.

<!-- PAGE 877 -->

<sup>3</sup> If there should encamp against me a camp, My heart shall not fear: If there should rise against me war, In this shall I have confidence.

<sup>4</sup> One thing have I requested from Jehovah — This will I follow after — That I may dwell in the house of Jehovah all the days of my life, To behold the beauty of Jehovah, To survey his temple.

<sup>5</sup> For he shall hide me in his pavilion in the day of evil; He shall conceal me in the secret [*recess*] of his tent, Upon a rock he will set me.

<sup>6</sup> And now he shall exalt my head Above my enemies who surround me: And I will sacrifice in his tabernacle sacrifices of triumph; I will sing and will celebrate in psalms Jehovah.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>7</sup> Hear, O Jehovah! my voice with which I cry; Have mercy upon me and answer me.</p>

<sup>8</sup> To thee my heart hath said, “Seek ye my face; “ Therefore thy face, O Jehovah! will I seek.

<sup>9</sup> Hide not thy face from me; Cast not away in wrath thy servant: My strength thou hast been, do not desert me, And do not forsake me, O God of my salvation!

<sup>10</sup> When my father and my mother shall forsake me, Jehovah shall receive me.

<sup>11</sup> Teach me, O Jehovah! thy way, And lead me in the path of rectitude because of my enemies.

<sup>12</sup> Do not deliver me to the desire of my oppressors, For risen up against me have false witnesses, And he who uttereth violence.

<sup>13</sup> Unless I had believed to see the goodness of Jehovah, in the land of the living —.

<sup>14</sup> Wait thou on Jehovah; Be strong, and he will comfort thy heart, And wait thou on Jehovah.
