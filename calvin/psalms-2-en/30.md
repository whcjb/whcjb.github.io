<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 30</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*A Psalm of a Song at the dedication of the house of David*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> I will exalt thee, O Jehovah! for thou hast raised me up, And hast not made glad my enemies over me.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> O Jehovah, my God! I have cried to thee, And thou hast healed me.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> O Jehovah! thou hast brought up from the grove my soul; Thou hast quickened me from among those who descend into the pit.

<!-- PAGE 880 -->

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Sing to Jehovah, O ye his meek ones! And acknowledge the memorial of his holiness.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> For *there is only* a moment in his anger, *But* life in his favor: In the evening will lodge weeping, And in the morning shall come exultation.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> But as for me I had said in my tranquillity, “I shall not be moved for ever.”

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> O Jehovah! in thy good pleasure Thou hast established strength to my mountain; Thou didst hide thy face, I was confounded.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> O Jehovah! to thee I cried, And to my Lord I made supplication.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> What profit shall there be in my blood, When I descend into the pit? Celebrate thee shall the dust? Shall it proclaim thy truth?

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">10</span> Hear, O Jehovah! and have mercy upon me; O Jehovah! be thou a helper to me.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> Thou hast turned my mourning into dancing for me; Thou hast loosed my sackcloth, And girded me with gladness.

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> That celebrate thee in psalms may my glory, and not be silent: O Jehovah, my God! for ever will I celebrate thee.
