<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 46</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. Of the sons of Korah. Upon Alamoth. A Song*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> God is to us a protection and strength; A help in troubles he is found exceedingly.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> Therefore we will not fear when moved shall be the earth, And fall shall the mountains into the heart of the sea.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> [*When*] roar and rage tempestuously shall the waters thereof; [*When*] shake shall the mountains with the swelling thereof. Selah.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> The streams of her river shall make glad the city of God, The Sanctuary of the tabernacles of the Most High.

<!-- PAGE 904 -->

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> God *is* in the midst of her; she shall not be moved: Help her will God at the dawn of the morning.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> Rage did the peoples, moved were the kingdoms: He uttered his voice, melt did the earth.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> Jehovah of armies *is* with us; A fortress for us *is* the God of Jacob. Selah.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> Come ye, consider the works of Jehovah, What desolations he hath wrought in the earth.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> He maketh to cease battles, even to the ends of the earth; He breaketh the bow, he shattereth in pieces the arms; The chariot he burneth with fire.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> Be still and know that I am God: I will be exalted among the heathen; I will be exalted in the earth.

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> Jehovah of armies is with us; A fortress for us *is* the God of Jacob. Selah.
