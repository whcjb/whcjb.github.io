<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 4</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician on Neginoth. A Psalm of David*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> When I cry, answer me, O God of my righteousness! In distress thou hast enlarged me; Have pity upon me, and hear my prayer.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> O ye sons of men! how long my glory will ye try to put to shame? Will ye love vanity, will ye seek after lying? Selah.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> But know that chosen hath Jehovah the merciful man for himself: Jehovah will hear when I cry unto him.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Tremble and ye shall not sin; Discourse in your heart upon your bed, and be silent. Selah.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> Sacrifice the sacrifices of righteousness, And trust in the Lord.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> Many say, “Who will show us good? “ Lift thou up on us the light of thy countenance, O Jehovah!

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> Thou hast given joy to my heart More than *theirs* in the time that their corn and their wine are increased.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> In peace [*as if*] with a multitude I will sleep and take rest, <span style="color:#800000">ff65</span>

For thou, O Jehovah! in safety hast placed me, [*though*] alone.
