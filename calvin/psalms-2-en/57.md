<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 57</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. Destroy not. Michtam of David. When he fled from*</span> <span style="color:#000080">*the face of Saul in the cave*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> Have mercy upon me, O God! have mercy upon me; For in thee my soul doth trust; And in the shadow of thy wings will I hope, Until iniquity pass away.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> I will cry to God Most High, Who perfecteth [*his work*] towards me.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> He shall send from Heaven, And shall save me from the reproach of him who swalloweth me up: Send forth shall God his mercy and his truth.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> My soul is in the midst of lions; I lie among those who are set on fire, — Among the sons of men, whose teeth are spears and arrows, And their tongue a sword [*that is*] sharp.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> Be thou exalted above the heavens, O God! Above all the earth thy glory.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> A net they have prepared for my steps: Bowed down is my soul: They have digged before me a pit, *But* they have fallen into it. Selah <span style="color:#800000">7</span> Prepared *is* my heart, O God! prepared *is* my heart: I will sing, and chant psalms.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> Awake my tongue, awake nablum  <span style="color:#800000">ff85</span> and harp: I will awake at dawn of day.

<!-- PAGE 917 -->

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> I will celebrate thee, among the peoples, O Lord! I will sing psalms to thee among the nation:

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">10</span> For great unto the heavens is thy goodness, And unto the clouds thy truth.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> Be thou exalted, above the heavens, O God! Above all the earth thy glory.
