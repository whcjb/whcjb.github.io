<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 56</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician upon, the silent dove in distant places. Michtam of*</span> <span style="color:#000080">*David, when the Philistines took him in Gath*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> Have mercy upon me, O God, For swallow me up doth man; He daily assaulting doth oppress me.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> Swallow me up do *my* enemies daily: Truly many *strive* to oppress me, *O Jehovah*, Most High!

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> In the day when I was afraid, I in thee did trust.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> In God I will praise his word; In God have I trusted; I will not fear what flesh can do to me.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> Every day my words disquiet me; Against me *are* all their thoughts for evil.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> They assemble together, they hide themselves, My heels they watch, Because they desire my soul.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> In *their* iniquity *they think* there is escape for them: In *thy* wrath the peoples thou wilt cast down, O God!

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> My wanderings thou numbereth, even thou: Put my tears into thy bottle; Are they not in thy register?

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> Then my enemies shall be turned back in the day when I cry: This I know, because God is with me.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> In God will I praise [*his*] word; In Jehovah will I praise [*his*] word.

<!-- PAGE 916 -->

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> In God have I trusted; I will not fear what man can do to me.

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> Upon me, O God! are thy vows, I will render praises to thee.

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> For thou hast delivered my soul from death: Hast thou not *also delivered* my feet from falling? That I may walk before God in the light of the living.
