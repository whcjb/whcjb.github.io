<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 47</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. Of the sons of Korah. Upon Alamoth. A Song*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> O all ye peoples! clap the hand; Shout unto God with the voice of exultation;

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> For Jehovah *is* high, terrible, A great king over all the earth.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">3</span> He hath put in order the people under us, And the nations under our feet.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> He hath chosen for us our inheritance, The glory of Jacob, whom he hath loved. Selah.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> Gone up is God with a shout, Jehovah with the sound of a trumpet.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> Sing psalms to God, sing psalms; Sing psalms to our King, sing psalms;

<!-- PAGE 905 -->

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> For king of all the earth is God; Sing praises, all ye who understand!

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> The kingdom he hath obtained over the heathen; God sitteth upon the throne of his holiness.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> The princes of the peoples are assembled together To the people of the God of Abraham: For to God belong the shields of the earth: He is greatly exalted.
