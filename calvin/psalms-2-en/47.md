<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 47</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. Of the sons of Korah. Upon Alamoth. A Song*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> O all ye peoples! clap the hand; Shout unto God with the voice of exultation;

<sup>2</sup> For Jehovah *is* high, terrible, A great king over all the earth.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>3</sup> He hath put in order the people under us, And the nations under our feet.</p>

<sup>4</sup> He hath chosen for us our inheritance, The glory of Jacob, whom he hath loved. Selah.

<sup>5</sup> Gone up is God with a shout, Jehovah with the sound of a trumpet.

<sup>6</sup> Sing psalms to God, sing psalms; Sing psalms to our King, sing psalms;

<!-- PAGE 905 -->

<sup>7</sup> For king of all the earth is God; Sing praises, all ye who understand!

<sup>8</sup> The kingdom he hath obtained over the heathen; God sitteth upon the throne of his holiness.

<sup>9</sup> The princes of the peoples are assembled together To the people of the God of Abraham: For to God belong the shields of the earth: He is greatly exalted.
