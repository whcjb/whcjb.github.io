<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 32</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*Of David. Giving instruction*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> Blessed are those to whom is remitted iniquity, And whose transgression is covered.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> Blessed is the man to whom Jehovah doth not impute iniquity, And in whose spirit there is not guile.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> When I kept silence, waste away did my bones, And when I wailed all the day.

<!-- PAGE 883 -->

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> For by day and night heavy upon me was thy hand; Turned into the drought of summer was my greenness. Selah.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> My sin I have acknowledged to thee, And my iniquity I have not hid. I said, “I will confess against myself my wickedness to Jehovah;” And thou didst remit the guilt of my sin. Selah.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> On this account, pray to thee shall every *man*, that is meek, In the time of finding thee: So that in a flood of many waters, To him they shall not come near.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> Thou art a hiding-place to me; From trouble thou shalt preserve me; With songs of deliverance thou shalt encompass me. Selah.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> I will instruct thee, And teach thee the way wherein thou shouldest walk: I will counsel thee with mine eye.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> Be ye not like a horse, *or* like a mule, Which have not understanding: With bit and bridle his jaws thou shalt bind, Lest they kick against thee.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> Many sorrows *shall be* to the wicked: But the man who hopeth in Jehovah goodness shall encompass.

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> Rejoice in Jehovah, and exult, ye righteous! Sing all ye *who are* upright in heart.
