<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 6</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician on Neginoth, upon the Eighth. A Psalm of David*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> O Jehovah! do not in thy anger rebuke me, And do not in thy wrath chastise me.

<sup>2</sup> Have mercy upon me, O Jehovah! for I am weak; Heal me, O Jehovah! for affrighted are my bones.

<sup>3</sup> And my soul is exceedingly afraid: And thou, O Jehovah! — how long? — <sup>4</sup> Return, O Lord! deliver my soul; Save me for the sake of thy mercy.

<sup>5</sup> For there is not in death any remembrance of thee; In the grave who shall acknowledge thee?

<sup>6</sup> I have become wearied with my groaning; I make to swim every night my bed; With my tears my couch I water.

<sup>7</sup> Waxed dim for vexation hath mine eye; It hath become old among all my persecutors.

<sup>8</sup> Depart from me, all ye workers of iniquity; For heard hath the Lord the voice of my weeping.

<sup>9</sup> Heard hath the Lord my supplication; The Lord my prayer will receive.

<sup>10</sup> Put to shame and confounded greatly be all my enemies; Let them turn back, and be ashamed suddenly.
