<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 44</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. Of the sons of Korah. Giving instruction*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> O God! with our ears we have heard, Our fathers have told us, The work which thou hast done in their days, In the days of old.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> Thou with thy hand hast expelled the heathen, and planted them: Thou hast wasted the peoples and multiplied them:

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> For not by their own sword acquired they the land, And their own arm did not save them; But thy right hand and thy arm, and the light of thy countenance, Because thou wast favorable to them.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Thou, even thou, *art* my King; O God! command salvations for Jacob.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> Through thee our adversaries with the horn we have pushed; In thy name we have trampled under foot those who rise up against us.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">6</span> For not in my bow will I trust, And my sword will not save me.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> Surely thou hast saved us from our enemies, And our haters thou hast put to shame.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> In God we will boast all the day, And thy name for ever shall we praise. Selah.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> Nevertheless thou hast abhorred us and put us to shame, And thou goest not forth *any more* with our armies.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> Thou hast made us to turn back from him that afflicteth us, And our haters have spoiled *us* for themselves.

<!-- PAGE 901 -->

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> Thou hast given us as sheep for food; And among the heathen thou hast scattered us.

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> Thou hast sold thy people, and not become rich, And thou hast not increased the price of them.

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> Thou hast made us a reproach to our neighbors, A scorn and derision to those who are round about us.

<span style="color:#800000">14</span> Thou hast made us a byword among the heathen, A shaking of the head among the peoples.

<span style="color:#800000">15</span> Daily *is* my reproach before me, And the shame of my face hath *quite* covered me, <span style="color:#800000">16</span> Because of the voice of him who reproacheth and revileth, Because of the face of the adversary and avenger.

<span style="color:#800000">17</span> All this is come upon us, and we have not forgotten thee, Nor dealt perfidiously in thy covenant;

<span style="color:#800000">18</span> Not turned back hath our heart, Nor declined have our steps from thy path.

<span style="color:#800000">19</span> Although thou hast crushed us in the place of dragons,  And covered us with the shadow of death.

<span style="color:#800000">20</span> If we have forgotten the name of our God, And have spread forth our hands to a God that is strange;

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">21</span> Shall not God search out this? For he knoweth the secrets of the heart.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">22</span> Surely for thy sake we are killed daily; We are accounted as sheep [*appointed*] for slaughter.

<span style="color:#800000">23</span> Arise, why sleepest thou, O Lord? Awake, do not forget *us* for ever.

<span style="color:#800000">24</span> Wherefore thy face dost thou hide? Wilt thou forget our misery and our affliction?

<!-- PAGE 902 -->

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">25</span> For humbled to the dust is our soul; Cleave to the earth doth our belly.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">26</span> Arise for our help, And redeem us for the sake of thy lovingkindness.
