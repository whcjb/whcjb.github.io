<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 44</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. Of the sons of Korah. Giving instruction*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> O God! with our ears we have heard, Our fathers have told us, The work which thou hast done in their days, In the days of old.

<sup>2</sup> Thou with thy hand hast expelled the heathen, and planted them: Thou hast wasted the peoples and multiplied them:

<sup>3</sup> For not by their own sword acquired they the land, And their own arm did not save them; But thy right hand and thy arm, and the light of thy countenance, Because thou wast favorable to them.

<sup>4</sup> Thou, even thou, *art* my King; O God! command salvations for Jacob.

<sup>5</sup> Through thee our adversaries with the horn we have pushed; In thy name we have trampled under foot those who rise up against us.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>6</sup> For not in my bow will I trust, And my sword will not save me.</p>

<sup>7</sup> Surely thou hast saved us from our enemies, And our haters thou hast put to shame.

<sup>8</sup> In God we will boast all the day, And thy name for ever shall we praise. Selah.

<sup>9</sup> Nevertheless thou hast abhorred us and put us to shame, And thou goest not forth *any more* with our armies.

<sup>10</sup> Thou hast made us to turn back from him that afflicteth us, And our haters have spoiled *us* for themselves.

<!-- PAGE 901 -->

<sup>11</sup> Thou hast given us as sheep for food; And among the heathen thou hast scattered us.

<sup>12</sup> Thou hast sold thy people, and not become rich, And thou hast not increased the price of them.

<sup>13</sup> Thou hast made us a reproach to our neighbors, A scorn and derision to those who are round about us.

<sup>14</sup> Thou hast made us a byword among the heathen, A shaking of the head among the peoples.

<sup>15</sup> Daily *is* my reproach before me, And the shame of my face hath *quite* covered me, <sup>16</sup> Because of the voice of him who reproacheth and revileth, Because of the face of the adversary and avenger.

<sup>17</sup> All this is come upon us, and we have not forgotten thee, Nor dealt perfidiously in thy covenant;

<sup>18</sup> Not turned back hath our heart, Nor declined have our steps from thy path.

<sup>19</sup> Although thou hast crushed us in the place of dragons,  And covered us with the shadow of death.

<sup>20</sup> If we have forgotten the name of our God, And have spread forth our hands to a God that is strange;

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>21</sup> Shall not God search out this? For he knoweth the secrets of the heart.</p>

<sup>22</sup> Surely for thy sake we are killed daily; We are accounted as sheep [*appointed*] for slaughter.

<sup>23</sup> Arise, why sleepest thou, O Lord? Awake, do not forget *us* for ever.

<sup>24</sup> Wherefore thy face dost thou hide? Wilt thou forget our misery and our affliction?

<!-- PAGE 902 -->

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>25</sup> For humbled to the dust is our soul; Cleave to the earth doth our belly.</p>

<sup>26</sup> Arise for our help, And redeem us for the sake of thy lovingkindness.
