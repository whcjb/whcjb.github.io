<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 13</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the chief Musician. A Psalm of David*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> How long, O Jehovah! wilt thou forget me for ever? How long wilt thou hide thy face from me?

<sup>2</sup> How long shall I take counsel in my soul? And have sorrow in my heart daily? How long exalted shall be my enemy over me?

<sup>3</sup> Look upon me, answer me, O Jehovah, my God! Enlighten my eyes, lest I sleep in death;

<sup>4</sup> Lest my enemy say, “I have prevailed against him; “ And those who afflict me rejoice if I should fall.

<sup>5</sup> But as for me, in thy goodness I trust; Exult shall my heart in thy salvation. I will sing to the Lord because he hath dealt bountifully with me.
