<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 49</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. Of the sons of Korah. A Psalm*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> Hear this all ye peoples; Give ear all ye inhabitants of the world:

<sup>2</sup> Both ye sons of Adam, and ye sons of men, Rich and poor together.

<sup>3</sup> My mouth shall speak of wisdoms, And the meditation of my heart is of understanding.

<sup>4</sup> I will incline to a parable my ear: I will open upon the harp my enigma.

<sup>5</sup> Wherefore should I fear in the days of evil? The iniquity of my heel shall compass me about.

<sup>6</sup> They trust in their wealth, And in the multitude of their riches do glory.

<sup>7</sup> The brother shall not be able to redeem; None shall give to God the price of his redemption.

<sup>8</sup> And precious shall be the redemption of their soul, And *their* continuance for ever:

<sup>9</sup> That he should still live for ever, And not see the grave.

<!-- PAGE 907 -->

<sup>10</sup> For he shall see that wise men die, Together the fool and the brutish person shall perish, And leave to strangers their wealth.

<sup>11</sup> Their inward thought is [*how to make*] their houses [*to continue*] for ever, Their dwelling-places from generation to generation: They have called out their names upon the earth.

<sup>12</sup> And man in honor shall not abide; He hath become like the beasts: they perish.

<sup>13</sup> This their way *is* foolishness in them, And their posterity in their saying will acquiesce. Selah.

<sup>14</sup> Like sleep in the grave they are laid; Death shall feed them; And have dominion over them shall the upright in the morning, And their strength shall wax old; The grave *shall receive* them from their dwelling.

<sup>15</sup> But God shall redeem my soul from the hand of the grave; For he hath taken me up. Selah.

<sup>16</sup> Be not thou afraid when a man shall become rich, When increased shall be the glory of his house;

<sup>17</sup> For he shall not at his death carry all away: Descend not after him shall his glory:

<sup>18</sup> For his soul in his lifetime he will bless, And they shall praise thee when thou doest well to thyself.

<sup>19</sup> He shall come to the age of his fathers, Even for ever he shall not see the light.

<sup>20</sup> Man *is* in honor, and will not understand: He hath become like the beasts: they shall perish.
