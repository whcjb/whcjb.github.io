<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 18</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. Of the servant of Jehovah, David, who spake to*</span> <span style="color:#000080">*Jehovah the words of this song, in the day that Jehovah delivered him from*</span> <span style="color:#000080">*the hand of all his enemies, and from the hand of Saul. And he said,*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> I will love thee, O Jehovah! my strength;

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> Jehovah my rock, my fortress, and my deliverer; My God, my rock, I will hope in him: My shield and the horn of my salvation, my refuge.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> Upon the praised Jehovah will I call; And from my enemies I shall be saved.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Encompassed me had the cords of death. The torrents of ungodliness had affrighted me.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> The cords of the grave had encompassed me; Prevented me had the snares of death.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> In my distress I called upon Jehovah, And to my God I cried: And he heard from his temple my voice, And my cry before him came, [*even*] into his ears.

<!-- PAGE 864 -->

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> Then shake and tremble did the earth; And the foundations of the mountains were troubled and shaken, Because he was wroth.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> There ascended smoke out of his nostrils, And fire *proceeding* from his mouth consumed; Coals were kindled by it.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> And he bowed the heavens and descended; And thick darkness *was* under his feet.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> And he rode upon a cherub and flew; And was carried upon the wings of the wind.

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> He made darkness his hiding place; Round about him his pavilion was dark waters, And the clouds of the skies.

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> At the brightness *which was* before him his clouds passed away, [*There were*] hail-storm, and coals of fire.

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> And thunder in the heavens did Jehovah, And the Most High sent forth his voice; [*There were*] hailstorm and coals of fire.

<span style="color:#800000">14</span> And he sent out his army and scattered them; And lightnings he multiplied, and put them into confusion.

<span style="color:#800000">15</span> And seen were the abysses of the waters, And disclosed were the foundations of the world, At thy rebuke, O Jehovah! At the blast of the breath of thy nostrils.

<span style="color:#800000">16</span> He sent from on high, he took me; He drew me from waters great.

<span style="color:#800000">17</span> He delivered me from my enemy [*that was*] strong, And from my adversary; Because they were stronger than I.

<span style="color:#800000">18</span> They had prevented me in the day of my calamity; And Jehovah was a support for me.

<span style="color:#800000">19</span> And he brought me forth into a wide place; He rescued me because he wished well to me.

<span style="color:#800000">20</span> Rewarded me hath Jehovah according to my righteousness; According to the purity of my hands he hath repaid me;

<!-- PAGE 865 -->

<span style="color:#800000">21</span> Because I have kept the ways of Jehovah, And have not impiously departed from my God;

<span style="color:#800000">22</span> Because all his judgments *I have had* before me, And his statutes I have not removed from me.

<span style="color:#800000">23</span> And I have been upright with him, And have kept me from my iniquity.

<span style="color:#800000">24</span> And repaid me hath Jehovah according to my righteousness According to the purity of my hands before his eyes.

<span style="color:#800000">25</span> With the merciful thou wilt deal mercifully, With a man of uprightness thou wilt show thyself upright.

<span style="color:#800000">26</span> With the pure thou wilt be pure, And with the perverse thou wilt deal perversely.

<span style="color:#800000">27</span> For thou the afflicted people wilt save, And the haughty eyes thou wilt east down.

<span style="color:#800000">28</span> For thou shalt light my lamp, O Jehovah! My God shall enlighten my darkness.

<span style="color:#800000">29</span> For by thee I shall break through the wedge of a troop, And by my God I shall leap over a wall.

<span style="color:#800000">30</span> As for God perfect is his way; The word of Jehovah [*is*] refined; A shield is He to all who confide in him.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">31</span> For who is God besides Jehovah? And who is strong except our God?</p>

<span style="color:#800000">32</span> It is God who hath girded me with strength, And hath made perfect my way.

<span style="color:#800000">33</span> He maketh my feet like *those* of hinds, And upon my high places he hath set me.

<span style="color:#800000">34</span> He traineth my hands to the battle, And broken will be a bow of steel by my arms.

<!-- PAGE 866 -->

<span style="color:#800000">35</span> And thou hast given me the shield of thy salvation, And thy right hand hath sustained me, And thy clemency hath increased me.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">36</span> Thou hast enlarged my steps under me, And my ankles shall not totter.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">37</span> I will pursue my enemies and will seize them; Nor will I return until I have consumed them.

<span style="color:#800000">38</span> I have afflicted them, and they were not able to rise; They have fallen under my feet.

<span style="color:#800000">39</span> Thou hast girded me with might for the battle; Thou hast bowed down my enemies under me.

<span style="color:#800000">40</span> And of my enemies thou hast given me the neck, And [*as for*] my haters I will destroy them.

<span style="color:#800000">41</span> They shall cry aloud, and *there shall be* no Savior *for them*; To Jehovah, but he shall not answer them.

<span style="color:#800000">42</span> And I will bruise them to powder as dust before the wind; As the mire of the streets I will tread upon them.

<span style="color:#800000">43</span> Thou shalt deliver me from the contentions of the people; Thou shalt make me head of the nations; A people whom I have not known shall serve me.

<span style="color:#800000">44</span> At the hearing of the ear they shall obey me; The children of strangers shall lie to me;

<span style="color:#800000">45</span> The children of strangers shall lose courage, And tremble from within their places of concealment.

<span style="color:#800000">46</span> Let Jehovah live and blessed *be* my strength, And let the God of my salvation be exalted:

<span style="color:#800000">47</span> The God who giveth vengeance for me, And subdueth peoples under me.

<!-- PAGE 867 -->

<span style="color:#800000">48</span> My deliverer from my enemies: Yea, from those who had risen up against me thou hast lifted me up: From the man of violence thou hast rescued me.

<span style="color:#800000">49</span> Therefore will I praise thee, O Jehovah! among the Gentiles, And to thy name will I sing.

<span style="color:#800000">50</span> Who worketh great deliverances for his king, And showeth mercy to his anointed, [even τό David, And to his seed for ever.
