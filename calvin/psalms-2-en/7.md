<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 7</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*Shiggaion of David, which he sung to Jehovah, upon the words*</span> <span style="color:#000080">*of Cush the Benjamite*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> O Jehovah, my God! in thee do I trust: Save me from all them that persecute me, and deliver me:

<!-- PAGE 853 -->

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> Lest He seize, as a lion, upon my soul, And tear it in pieces while there is none to deliver it.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> O Jehovah, my God! if I have done this, If there be iniquity in my hands, <span style="color:#800000">4</span> If I have rewarded to him that was at peace with me evil, And have not delivered him that afflicted me without cause;

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> Pursue let the enemy my soul and take it, And let him cast down to the earth my life, And my glory in the dust let him hold down. Selah <span style="color:#800000">6</span> Arise, O Jehovah! in thy anger; Lift up thyself against the fury of my enemies; And awake thou for me to the judgment which thou hast ordained.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> And the congregation of peoples shall be round about thee: And on account of this, do thou on high return.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> Jehovah shall judge the peoples: Judge me, O Jehovah! according to my righteousness, And according to the integrity which is in me.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> Let come to an end, I pray, the malice of the wicked; And direct thou the righteous man: For he proves the hearts and the reins, the righteous God.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> My shield is in God, Who saves the upright in heart.

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> God judgeth the righteous man, And him who despiseth God, daily.

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> If he turn not, his sword he will whet; His bow he hath bent, he hath made it ready.

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> And for it he hath prepared the instruments of death; He shall make fit his arrows for the persecutors.

<span style="color:#800000">14</span> Behold! he shall travail to bring forth iniquity, And he hath conceived wickedness, And he shall bring forth falsehood.

<!-- PAGE 854 -->

<span style="color:#800000">15</span> A pit he hath digged, and hollowed it out; And he hath fallen into the ditch which he hath made.

<span style="color:#800000">16</span> Return shall his wickedness upon his own head, And upon his own crown his violence shall descend.

<span style="color:#800000">17</span> I will praise Jehovah, according to his righteousness; And I will sing to the name of Jehovah, Most High.
