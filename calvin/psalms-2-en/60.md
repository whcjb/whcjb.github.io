<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 60</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician upon Shushan Eduth. Michtam of David to teach.*</span> <span style="color:#000080">*When he fought against the Syrians of Mesopotamia and against the Syrians*</span> <span style="color:#000080">*of Zobah; and when Joab, having returned, smote of the Edomites in the*</span> <span style="color:#000080">*Valley of Salt twelve thousand*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> O God! thou hast cast us off, thou hast scattered us; Thou hast been angry, O return to us!

<sup>2</sup> To tremble thou hast made the earth; Thou hast caused it to open wide; Heal the breaches thereof for it shaketh.

<sup>3</sup> Thou hast showed to thy people a hard thing, Thou hast made us drunk with the wine of stupefaction.

<sup>4</sup> Thou hast given to those who fear thee a banner, That it may be displayed before *thy* truth. Selah.

<!-- PAGE 920 -->

<sup>5</sup> That thy beloved ones may be delivered, Save with thy right hand and hear me.

<sup>6</sup> God hath spoken in his holiness; I will rejoice: I will divide Shechem, And the valley of Succoth I will measure.

<sup>7</sup> Mine *shall be* Gilead, and mine *shall be* Manasseh; And Ephraim *shall be* the strength of my head; Judah, my lawgiver.

<sup>8</sup> Moab *shall be* the pot for my washing; Over Edom will I cast my shoe; O Palestina! triumph thou over me.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>9</sup> Who will bring me into the city fortified? Who will lead me into Edom?</p>

<sup>10</sup> Wilt not thou, O God! who hadst cast us off?  [*thou*] O God! [*who*] didst not go forth with our armies?

<sup>11</sup> Grant us relief from trouble, For vain *is* the help of man.

<sup>12</sup> Through God we shall do valiantly: And he it is who shall tread down our adversaries.
