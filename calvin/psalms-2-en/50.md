<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 50</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#000080">*A Song, of Asaph*</span></p>

<!-- PAGE 908 -->

<sup>1</sup> The God of gods, Jehovah hath spoken, And called the earth From the rising of the sun unto the going down thereof.

<sup>2</sup> Out of Zion, the Perfection of beauty, God hath shined.

<sup>3</sup> Come shall our God, and shall not keep silence; A fire before him shall devour, And round about him a tempest shall rage exceedingly.

<sup>4</sup> He shall call to the heavens from above, And to the earth, to judge his people.

<sup>5</sup> “Gather together to me my meek ones, (*will he say*),  “Those who strike a covenant with me over sacrifices.”

<sup>6</sup> And declare shall the heavens his righteousness: For God is judge himself. Selah.

<sup>7</sup> “Hear, O my people! and I will speak; “O Israel! and I will announce to thee:  “God, [*even*] thy God am I.

<sup>8</sup> “Not for thy sacrifices will I reprove thee; “And thy burntofferings *are* before me continually.

<sup>9</sup> “I will not take out of thy house a calf, “*Nor* out of thy folds he-goats:

<sup>10</sup> “For mine are all the beasts of the forest, “The cattle upon a thousand hills.

<sup>11</sup> “I know all the birds of the mountains; “And the wild beasts of the field are at my command.

<sup>12</sup> “If I am hungry, I will not tell thee: “For mine is the world and the fullness thereof.

<sup>13</sup> “Will I eat the flesh of bulls, “And the blood of goats will I drink?

<sup>14</sup> “Sacrifice unto God praise, “And pay unto the Most High thy vows.

<!-- PAGE 909 -->

<sup>15</sup> “Call upon me in the day of trouble; “I will deliver thee, and thou shalt glorify me.

<sup>16</sup> “But unto the wicked said hath God, “What hast thou to do to declare my statutes, “[*Or*] that thou shouldest take my covenant into thy lips?

<sup>17</sup> “Also thou hatest correction, “And castest my words behind thee.

<sup>18</sup> “If thou seest a thief, thou wilt run with him, “And with adulterers is thy portion.

<sup>19</sup> “Thy mouth thou puttest forth to evil, “And thy tongue frameth deceit, <sup>20</sup> “Thou wilt sit against thy brother; “Thou wilt speak against the sons of thy mother; “Thou wilt set forth slander.

<sup>21</sup> “These things thou hast done, and I kept silence; “Thou thoughtest that I would be like thyself’: “I will reprove thee, and set them in order before thine eyes.

<sup>22</sup> “Now consider this, ye who forget God, “Lest I seize upon you and there be none to deliver.

<sup>23</sup> “He who sacrificeth praise will glorify me: “And he who ordereth his way to him will I show the salvation of God.”
