<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 65</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. A Psalm of David. A Song*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> For thee waiteth praise, O God! in Zion; And to thee performed shall be the vow.

<sup>2</sup> O thou who hearest prayer! To thee shall all flesh come.

<sup>3</sup> Words of iniquity  <span style="color:#800000">ff89</span> have prevailed against me: Our crimes thou shalt expiate.

<sup>4</sup> Blessed is he whom thou wilt choose, and cause to approach to thee; He shall dwell in thy courts: We shall be satisfied with the goodness of thy house — Of the sanctuary of thy palace.

<sup>5</sup> Terrible things in righteousness wilt thou answer us, O God of our salvation! The hope of all the ends of the earth, And of the far off places of the sea.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>6</sup> Establishing the mountains by his power,  <span style="color:#800000">ff90</span> Being girded with might.</p>

<sup>7</sup> Stilling the noise of the seas, And the noise of their waves, the tumults of the nations.

<sup>8</sup> And afraid shall be the dwellers in the ends *of the earth* at thy signs; The outgoings of the morning and evening thou shalt make to shout for joy.

<sup>9</sup> Thou hast visited the earth, and watered her; Thou hast abundantly enriched her: The river of God is full of waters: Thou wilt prepare their corn, For so thou hast prepared her.

<sup>10</sup> Her furrows thou dost saturate, Thou makest the rain to fall into her ridges, With showers thou dost moisten her, Her buddings thou dost bless.

<sup>11</sup> Thou crownest the year with thy beneficence, And thy paths will drop fatness.

<!-- PAGE 925 -->

<sup>12</sup> They drop upon the dwellings of the wilderness; And with gladness the hills shall be girded.

<sup>13</sup> Clothed are the pastures with flocks; And the valleys are covered with corn; They shout for joy, they also sing.
