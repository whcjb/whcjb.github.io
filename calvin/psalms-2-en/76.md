<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 76</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician on Neginoth. A Psalm of Asaph. A Song*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> Known in Judah is God; In Israel great is his name.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> And in Salem was his tabernacle, And his dwelling-place in Zion.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> There he broke the arrows of the bow, The shield, and the sword, and the battle. Selah.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> More glorious, more terrible *art* thou Than the mountains of prey.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> Spoiled were the stout in heart, They slept their sleep: And none of the men of might have found their hands.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> At thy rebuke, O God of Jacob! Cast into a deep sleep were the chariot and the horse.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> Thou *art* terrible [*even*] thou; And who shall stand before thy face when thou art angry?

<!-- PAGE 942 -->

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> From the heavens thou hast made judgment to be heard: The earth was afraid and was still,

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">9</span> When God arose to judgment, To save all the meek of the earth. Selah.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> Surely the wrath of men shall praise thee, The remainder of wrath thou wilt restrain.

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> Vow and pay to Jehovah your God: Let all who are round about him bring a present to the terrible one.

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> He will cut off the spirit of princes: *He is* terrible to the kings of the earth.
