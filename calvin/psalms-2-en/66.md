<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 66</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Justice,. A Song of a Psalm*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> Shout joyfully to God all the earth, <sup>2</sup> Sing the glory of his name, Make glorious his praise.

<sup>3</sup> Say unto God: “How terrible art thou in thy works! “Through the greatness of thy power shall thy enemies feign submission unto thee.

<sup>4</sup> “All the earth shall worship thee, and shall sing to thee, “They shall hymn thy name.” Selah.

<sup>5</sup> Come ye, and see the works of God: He is terrible in his doing towards the sons of men.

<sup>6</sup> He turned the sea into dry *land*; Through the river  <span style="color:#800000">ff91</span> they passed on foot; There we were glad in him.

<sup>7</sup> He ruleth by his might over the world;  <span style="color:#800000">ff92</span> His eyes upon the nations are fixed: The rebels shall not exalt themselves. Selah.

<sup>8</sup> Bless, O ye people! our God, And resound the voice of his praise.

<sup>9</sup> Who hath brought our souls into life, And hath not suffered our feet to fall.

<sup>10</sup> For thou hast proved us, O God! Thou hast tried us as silver is tried:

<sup>11</sup> Thou hast brought us into the net Thou hast laid restraint upon our loins;

<!-- PAGE 926 -->

<sup>12</sup> Thou hast made man to ride over our heads; We have come into fire and water; And thou hast brought us into a place of abundance.

<sup>13</sup> I will come into thy house with burnt-offerings; I will pay thee my vows,

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>14</sup> Which my lips have uttered, And my mouth hath spoken in my affliction.</p>

<sup>15</sup> Burnt-offerings of fat *lambs* I will offer to thee with incense of rams; I will bring bullocks with goats. Selah.

<sup>16</sup> Come, hear, and I will tell all you who fear God, What he hath done for my soul.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>17</sup> To him with my mouth I cried, And I have exalted him with my tongue.</p>

<sup>18</sup> If I have regarded iniquity in my heart, The Lord will not hear me.

<sup>19</sup> But truly heard me hath God; He hath attended to the voice of my prayer.

<sup>20</sup> Blessed be God who hath not turned away my prayer, Nor his mercy from me.
