<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 34</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*Of David when he changed his countenance before Abimelech, who expelled*</span> <span style="color:#000080">*him, and he departed*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> I will bless Jehovah at all times: Continually his praise *shall be* in my mouth.

<sup>2</sup> In Jehovah make her boast shall my soul: Hear shall the humble and be glad.

<sup>3</sup> Magnify? Jehovah with me, And let us exalt his name together.

<sup>4</sup> I sought. Jehovah, and he answered me, And from all my fears he delivered me.

<sup>5</sup> They shall look to him, and shall flow to him, And their faces shall not he ashamed.

<sup>6</sup> This poor man cried,: and Jehovah heard him, And from all his distresses saved him.

<sup>7</sup> Encamp doth the angel of Jehovah about those who fear him, And will deliver them.

<sup>8</sup> Taste ye and see that good is Jehovah: Blessed is the man who trusteth in him.

<sup>9</sup> Fear Jehovah, ye saints of his; For nothing is lacking to those who fear him.

<sup>10</sup> The young lions suffer want and are famished: But those who fear Jehovah shall not want any good thing.

<sup>11</sup> Come ye children, hearken to me, The fear of Jehovah I will teach you.

<!-- PAGE 886 -->

<sup>12</sup> Who is the man that desireth life, Loving days in which he may see good?

<sup>13</sup> Keep thy tongue from evil, And thy lips from speaking deceit.

<sup>14</sup> Depart from evil, and do good; Seek peace, and pursue it.

<sup>15</sup> The eyes of Jehovah *are* upon the righteous, And his ears [*are open*] to their cry.

<sup>16</sup> The face of Jehovah is upon those who do evil, To blot out from the earth their remembrance.

<sup>17</sup> They cried, and Jehovah heard *them*, And from all their distresses he delivered them.

<sup>18</sup> Near is Jehovah to the broken in heart; The contrite in spirit he will save.

<sup>19</sup> Many *are* the afflictions of the righteous; But from them all deliver him will Jehovah.

<sup>20</sup> He keepeth all his bones: One of them is not broken.

<sup>21</sup> That malice shall slay the wicked, And those who hate the righteous shall be destroyed.

<sup>22</sup> Redeem doth, Jehovah the soul of his servants, And they shall not perish who confide in him.
