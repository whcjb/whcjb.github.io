<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 34</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*Of David when he changed his countenance before Abimelech, who expelled*</span> <span style="color:#000080">*him, and he departed*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> I will bless Jehovah at all times: Continually his praise *shall be* in my mouth.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> In Jehovah make her boast shall my soul: Hear shall the humble and be glad.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> Magnify? Jehovah with me, And let us exalt his name together.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> I sought. Jehovah, and he answered me, And from all my fears he delivered me.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> They shall look to him, and shall flow to him, And their faces shall not he ashamed.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> This poor man cried,: and Jehovah heard him, And from all his distresses saved him.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> Encamp doth the angel of Jehovah about those who fear him, And will deliver them.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> Taste ye and see that good is Jehovah: Blessed is the man who trusteth in him.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> Fear Jehovah, ye saints of his; For nothing is lacking to those who fear him.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> The young lions suffer want and are famished: But those who fear Jehovah shall not want any good thing.

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> Come ye children, hearken to me, The fear of Jehovah I will teach you.

<!-- PAGE 886 -->

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> Who is the man that desireth life, Loving days in which he may see good?

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> Keep thy tongue from evil, And thy lips from speaking deceit.

<span style="color:#800000">14</span> Depart from evil, and do good; Seek peace, and pursue it.

<span style="color:#800000">15</span> The eyes of Jehovah *are* upon the righteous, And his ears [*are open*] to their cry.

<span style="color:#800000">16</span> The face of Jehovah is upon those who do evil, To blot out from the earth their remembrance.

<span style="color:#800000">17</span> They cried, and Jehovah heard *them*, And from all their distresses he delivered them.

<span style="color:#800000">18</span> Near is Jehovah to the broken in heart; The contrite in spirit he will save.

<span style="color:#800000">19</span> Many *are* the afflictions of the righteous; But from them all deliver him will Jehovah.

<span style="color:#800000">20</span> He keepeth all his bones: One of them is not broken.

<span style="color:#800000">21</span> That malice shall slay the wicked, And those who hate the righteous shall be destroyed.

<span style="color:#800000">22</span> Redeem doth, Jehovah the soul of his servants, And they shall not perish who confide in him.
