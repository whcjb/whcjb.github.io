<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 70</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. Of David. To call to remembrance*</span></p>

<!-- PAGE 933 -->

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> O God! to deliver me, O Jehovah! to my aid, hasten.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> Let those be ashamed and confounded who seek after my soul; Let those be turned backward and put to confusion who desire my hurt.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> Let those be destroyed for a reward of their shame, Who say to me, “Aha! Aha!”

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Exult and rejoice in thee let all who seek thee; And let those say, “For ever may God be magnified” — Those who love thy salvation.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> But as for me I am poor and needy: O God! hasten to me: My help and my deliverer art thou: O Jehovah! delay not.
