<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 62</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician upon Jeduthun. A Psalm of David*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> Nevertheless towards God silent is my soul: From him is my salvation.

<sup>2</sup> Nevertheless he is my reek and my salvation, My fortress: *therefore* I shall not be moved greatly.

<sup>3</sup> How long will ye continue to lay snares against a man? Ye shall be slain all of you: As an inclining wall shall ye be, and a fence that is shaken.

<sup>4</sup> Yet from his elevation they consult to east him down:  They delight in falsehood: With their mouth they bless, And in their hearts they curse. Selah. Nevertheless towards God be thou silent O my soul: For from him *is* my expectation.

<sup>6</sup> Nevertheless he *only is* my rock, and my salvation: My fortress; I shall not fall.

<sup>7</sup> In God *is* my salvation and my glory; The rock of my strength; my hope *is* in God.

<sup>8</sup> Hope in him at all times, O ye people! Pour out before his face your hearts: God *is* our hope. Selah.

<!-- PAGE 922 -->
<sup>9</sup> Nevertheless vanity are the sons of Adam; A lie the sons of men: When put all together in a balance they are found lighter than vanity itself. <span style="color:#800000">ff88</span> <sup>10</sup> Trust not in oppression and robbery; be not vain: [*Upon*] riches if they abound set not your heart.

<sup>11</sup> Once God hath spoken; Twice this I have heard, “That power belongeth to God; “ And to thee, O Lord! *belongeth* mercy; Truly thou wilt render to every man according to his work.
