<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 45</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. Upon the lilies. Of the sons of Korah. Giving*</span> <span style="color:#000080">*instruction. A Song of loves*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> Boiling over is my heart to speak a goodly theme, Speak shall I myself of my works concerning the King: My tongue is as the pen of a swift writer.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> Thou art fair above the sons of men; Diffused is grace on thy lips; Because God hath blessed thee for ever.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> Gird thy sword upon thy thigh, O mighty one! With majesty and glory.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> And in thy majesty prosper thou; Ride forth upon the word of truth, and meekness and righteousness; And thy right hand shall teach thee terrible things:

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> Thy arrows *are* sharp (so that the people fall under thee) In the hearts of the enemies of the king.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> Thy throne, O God! *is* for ever and ever; The scepter of rectitude *is* the scepter of thy kingdom.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> Thou lovest righteousness and hatest impiety; Because anointed thee hath God, thy God, With the oil of gladness above thy fellows.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> Myrrh, and aloes and cassia [*are*] *in* all thy garments, Out of the ivory palaces, whence they have made thee glad.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> The daughters of kings [*were*] among thy maids of honor; Stand did thy consort at thy right hand in gold of Ophir.

<!-- PAGE 903 -->

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> Hearken, O daughter! and consider and incline thy ear; And forget thy people, and the house of thy father.

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> And enamored shall be the King with thy beauty; For he *is* thy Lord, and thou shalt worship him.

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> And the daughter of Tyre [*shall come*] with a present; Thy favor entreat shall the rich among the people.

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> All glorious is the daughter of the King within; Of garments embroidered with gold *is* her clothing.

<span style="color:#800000">14</span> In raiment of needle-work she shall be brought to the King; Her virgins that follow her, her companions shall be brought to thee.

<span style="color:#800000">15</span> They shall be brought with joy and exultation; They shall enter into the palace of the King.

<span style="color:#800000">16</span> Instead of thy fathers shall be thy children; Thou shalt make them princes in all the earth.

<span style="color:#800000">17</span> I will make thy name to be remembered throughout all generation; Therefore celebrate thee shall the peoples for ever and ever.
