<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 25</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#000080">*Of David*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> To thee, O Jehovah! my soul have I lifted up.

<sup>2</sup> O my God! in thee have I hoped: Let me not be put to shame, Let not my enemies rejoice over me.

<sup>3</sup> Yea none who wait on thee shall be put to shame: They shall be ashamed who deal perfidiously without cause.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>4</sup> Thy ways, O Jehovah! make me to know, Thy paths teach thou me.</p>

<sup>15</sup> Direct me in thy truth, and teach me; For thou art the God of my salvation; For thee I have waited all the day.

<sup>6</sup> Remember thy compassions, O Jehovah! And thy lovingkindnesses; for they have been from everlasting.

<sup>7</sup> The transgressions of my youth, and my iniquities do not thou remember According to thy clemency remember thou me, For the sake of thy goodness, O Jehovah!

<sup>8</sup> Good and upright *is* Jehovah, Therefore he will teach sinners in the way.

<sup>9</sup> He will guide the poor in judgment, And will teach the poor his way.

<sup>10</sup> All the ways of Jehovah are mercy and truth, To those who keep his covenant and his testimony.

<!-- PAGE 875 -->

<sup>11</sup> For the sake of thy name, O Jehovah! Be merciful to my iniquity, for great is it.

<sup>12</sup> Who is the man that feareth Jehovah? He will teach him in the way which he should choose.

<sup>13</sup> His soul in good shall dwell, And his seed shall inherit the land.

<sup>14</sup> The counsel of Jehovah is to those who fear him, That he may make known to them his covenant.

<sup>15</sup> My eyes are continually towards Jehovah, For he will bring out of the net my feet.

<sup>16</sup> Have respect to me, take pity upon me, For solitary and poor am I.

<sup>17</sup> The afflictions of my heart are enlarged; Out of my distresses do thou bring me.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>18</sup> Behold my affliction and my travail, And take away all my sins.</p>

<sup>19</sup> Behold my enemies, for they are multiplied; And with a violent hatred they hate me.

<sup>20</sup> Guard my soul, and rescue me, That I may not be ashamed; For I have trusted in thee.

<sup>21</sup> Let integrity and rectitude preserve me; Because I have waited for thee.

<sup>22</sup> Redeem O God! Israel from all his troubles.
