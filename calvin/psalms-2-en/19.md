<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 19</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. A Psalm of David*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> The heavens recount the glory of God; And the works of his hands proclaim doth the expanse.

<sup>2</sup> Day unto day poureth forth speech; And night unto night publisheth knowledge.

<sup>3</sup> There is no speech and no language, Where is not heard their voice.

<sup>4</sup> Through all the earth hath gone forth their writing, And to the extremity of the world their words: For the sun he hath set a tabernacle in them, <sup>5</sup> And he as a bridegroom goeth forth from his chamber; He exulteth as a strong man to run *his* course.

<sup>6</sup> From *one* extremity of the heavens *is* his going forth, And his circuit to the utmost limits thereof, And no *person* is hidden from his heat.

<sup>7</sup> The law of the Lord is perfect, restoring the soul; The testimony of Jehovah is faithful, instructing in wisdom the babes;

<sup>8</sup> The statutes of Jehovah *are* right, rejoicing the heart; The commandment of the Lord *is* pure, enlightening the eyes;

<!-- PAGE 868 -->

<sup>9</sup> The fear of Jehovah is clean, enduring for ever; The judgments of Jehovah are truth, they are justified together.

<sup>10</sup> More desirable [*are they*] than gold, and much fine gold; And sweeter than honey, and the dropping of honeycombs.

<sup>11</sup> Moreover, thy servant is made circumspect by them; And in their observance the reward *is* great.

<sup>12</sup> His errors who can understand? From my hidden *sins* cleanse thou me.

<sup>13</sup> Likewise from presumptuous sins restrain thou thy servant, That they may not have dominion over me; Then upright shall I be and clean from much wickedness.

<sup>14</sup> Let the words of my mouth, and the meditation of my heart be acceptable Before thee, O Jehovah! my strength and my Redeemer.
