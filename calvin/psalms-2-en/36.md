<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 36</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. Of the servant of Jehovah, [εςεν of David*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> Ungodliness saith to the wicked man in the midst of my heart, There is no fear of God before his eyes.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> For he flattereth himself in his own eyes, Until his iniquity be found to be hateful.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> The words of his mouth are iniquity and deceit, He teaseth to understand that he may do good.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Iniquity he meditates upon his bed; He setteth himself in a way not good; Evil he doth not abhor.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">5</span> O Jehovah! unto the heavens is thy mercy, Thy truth even unto the clouds.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> Thy justice is like the great mountains, Thy judgments are a great deep. Man and beast, thou preservest, O Jehovah!

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> How precious is thy loving-kindness, O God! Therefore the sons of men in the shadow of thy wings shall trust.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> They shall be fully satisfied with the fatness of thy house; And of the river of thy pleasures thou shalt cause them to drink.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> For with thee is the fountain of life, And in thy light shall we see light.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> Extend thy mercy to those who know thee, And thy righteousness to the upright in heart.

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> Let not the foot of pride come upon me; And the hand of the ungodly let it not remove me.

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> There fallen are the workers of iniquity; They are thrust down and shall not be able to stand. <span style="color:#800000">ff75</span>
