<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 71</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> In thee, O Jehovah! do I put my trust, Let me not be put to shame for ever.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> In thy righteousness deliver me, and rescue me; Incline to me thy ear, and save me.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> Be thou to me for a rock of strength, Into which I may enter at all times: Thou hast given commandment to save me; For my tower and my fortress art thou.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> O my God! deliver me from the hand of the ungodly man, — From the hand of the wicked and violent men.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> For thou art my expectation, O Lord Jehovah! My trust from my youth.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> Upon thee have I been sustained from the womb; Out of my mother’s bowels thou art he who took me: Of thee is my praise continually.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> As a prodigy I have been to the great ones, And yet thou art my strong confidence.

<!-- PAGE 934 -->

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> Filled shall be my mouth with thy praise, Daily with thy glory.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> Cast me not off in the time of my old age: In the declining of my strength forsake me not.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> For my enemies have spoken concerning me, And those who watch for my soul have consulted together, <span style="color:#800000">11</span> Saying, “God hath forsaken him; “Follow after him, and ye shall take him; “For there is none to deliver *him*.”

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> O God! be not far from me: O my God! to my aid hasten.

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> Let those be put to shame, let those fail who are the adversaries of my soul: Let those be covered with reproach mid shame who seek my hurt.

<span style="color:#800000">14</span> But as for me continually will I hope, And will add to all thy praise.

<span style="color:#800000">15</span> My mouth shall tell of thy righteousness, Daily of thy salvation; For I know not the number *thereof*.

<span style="color:#800000">16</span> I will go in the strength of the Lord Jehovah! I will remember thy righteousness alone.

<span style="color:#800000">17</span> O God! thou hast taught me from my youth; And unto this time will I announce thy wonders.

<span style="color:#800000">18</span> And therefore  <span style="color:#800000">ff93</span> in old age and gray hairs, O God! forsake me not, Until I have declared thy strength to the generation, To all who are to come thy might.

<span style="color:#800000">19</span> And thy righteousness, O God! is very high: For thou hast done great things: O God! who is like to thee?

<span style="color:#800000">20</span> Thou hast made me to see troubles many and grievous, *But* being turned thou wilt quicken me, And from the depths of the earth, being turned thou wilt raise me up.

<span style="color:#800000">21</span> Thou wilt multiply my greatness; And being turned thou wilt comfort me.

<!-- PAGE 935 -->

<span style="color:#800000">22</span> Moreover, I, even I, will sing to thee on instruments of music; <span style="color:#800000">ff94</span> For thy truth, O my God! I will sing psalms to thee upon the harp, O Holy One of Israel!

<span style="color:#800000">23</span> Exult shall my lips, when I sing psalms to thee; And my soul which thou hast redeemed.

<span style="color:#800000">24</span> Also my tongue daily shall declare thy righteousness; For they are put to shame, for they are disgraced, who seek my hurt.
