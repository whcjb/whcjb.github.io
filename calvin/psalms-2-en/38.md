<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 38</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*A Psalm of David: to bring to remembrance*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> O Jehovah! do not in thy fury rebuke me, And in thy wrath do not chasten me.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> For thy arrows go down in me, And descend upon me doth thy hand.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> There is no soundness in my flesh, because of thy wrath, Nor peace in my bones because of my sin.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> For my iniquities have passed over my head, Like a burden that is weighty, they are too heavy for me.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> Putrid [*and*] corrupt have my wounds become, Because of my foolishness.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> I am bowed down, I am brought low exceedingly; Day by day a mourner I do walk:

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> For my reins are filled with a burning heat; Nor is there soundness in my flesh.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> I am enfeebled and broken exceedingly: I have roared through the disquietude of my heart.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">9</span> O Lord! before thee is all my desire, And my groaning from thee is not hidden,</p>

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> My heart hath throbbed, forsaken me hath my strength; And the light of my eyes, it also is gone from me.

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> My friends and my companions aloof from my stroke have stood, And my kinsmen afar off have stood.

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> And they have laid snares who sought for my soul; Arid those who sought my hurt have threatened mischiefs; And deceits daily do they meditate.

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> But as for me, like a deaf man I hear not; And *I am* like a dumb man who openeth not his mouth.

<!-- PAGE 894 -->

<span style="color:#800000">14</span> And I was as a man who doth not hear, And in whose mouth there are no reproofs.

<span style="color:#800000">15</span> For on thee, O Jehovah! do I wait: Thou wilt answer *me*, O Lord, my God!

<span style="color:#800000">16</span> For I said, “Lest they should rejoice over me: “ At the slipping of my foot against me they magnified themselves, <span style="color:#800000">17</span> Surely as for me I am ready to halt; And my sorrow is before me continually.

<span style="color:#800000">18</span> Surely my iniquity I declare; I am in consternation because of my sin.

<span style="color:#800000">19</span> But my enemies are living; <span style="color:#800000">ff77</span> they are strong; And those are become mighty who hate me wrongfully.

<span style="color:#800000">20</span> And they who requite me evil for good are against me, Because I follow [*what is*] good.

<span style="color:#800000">21</span> Forsake me not, O Jehovah, my God! Be not far from me.

<span style="color:#800000">22</span> Hasten to my help, O Lord, my salvation!
