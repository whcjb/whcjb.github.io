<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 15</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*A Psalm of David*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> O Jehovah! who shall dwell in thy tabernacle? Who shall rest in the mountain of thy holiness?

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> He who walketh in integrity and doeth righteousness, And who speaketh truth in his heart, <span style="color:#800000">3</span> He who detracteth not with his tongue, Who doeth not to his companion evil, And a calumnious report doth not raise up against his neighbor.

<!-- PAGE 861 -->

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Despised in his eyes is the off cast; But those who fear the Lord he honoreth; When he hath sworn to his own hurt he doth not change.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> His money he doth not put out to usury; And a present upon the innocent he doth not accept: He who doeth these things shall not be moved for ever.
