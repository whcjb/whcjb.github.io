<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 59</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. Destroy not. Michtam of David. When Saul sent, and*</span> <span style="color:#000080">*they watched the house to kill him*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> Deliver me from my enemies, O my God! from those who rise up against me set me on high.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> Deliver me from the workers of iniquity, And from men of bloods save me:

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> For lo! they have laid snares for my soul; Gathered together against me have the strong men; Not [*for*] my sin, nor [*for*] my wickedness, O Jehovah!

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Without *any* iniquity *of mine* they have run and prepared themselves. Awake and come to meet me, and behold.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> And thou, O Jehovah, God of armies! the God of Israel! Awake to visit all the nations; Do not have compassion upon any who transgress wickedly. Selah.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> They will return at evening; They will make a noise like a dog, And go round about the city.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> Behold they will prate with their mouth; Swords are in their lips; “For who,” *say they*, “will hear?”

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> But thou, O Jehovah! shalt laugh at them; Thou shalt hold in derision all the nations.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> His strength with thee I will put in trust; For God is my fortress.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> The God of my mercy shall prevent me; God will make me see my desire upon my enemies.

<!-- PAGE 919 -->

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> Slay them not, lest my people should forget; Make them to wander by thy power; And bring them down, O Lord, our shield!

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> The sin of their mouth, the words of their lips: Let them be taken in their pride: of cursing and lying let them speak.

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> Consume, in thy fury consume them, that they may be no more; And let men know that God ruleth in Jacob, Even to the ends of the earth. Selah. <span style="color:#800000">ff87</span> <span style="color:#800000">14</span> And they will return at evening; They will bark like a dog, And go round about the city.

<span style="color:#800000">15</span> They will wander up and down for food; If they be not satisfied they will even stay all night.

<span style="color:#800000">16</span> But as for me, I will sing of thy strength, I will praise in the morning thy mercy; For thou hast been a fortress to me, And a refuge in the day of my trouble.

<span style="color:#800000">17</span> My strength is with thee, I will sing psalms; For God *is* my fortress, the God of my mercy.
