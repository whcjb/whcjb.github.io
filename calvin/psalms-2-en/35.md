<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 35</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*Of David*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> Plead, O Jehovah! with those who plead with me, Fight against those who fight against me.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> Lay hold on shield and buckler, And rise up to my aid.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> Draw out the spear, and stop my ears against my persecutors: Say to my soul, “Thy salvation am I.”

<!-- PAGE 887 -->

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Let those be confounded and put to shame Who seek my soul, Let those be turned back and be abashed Who devise my hurt.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> Let them be as chaff before the wind? And let the angel of Jehovah drive them.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> Let their way be darkness and slipperiness; And let the angel of Jehovah pursue them:

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> For without a cause they have hid for me the pitfall of their net, <span style="color:#800000">ff74</span> Without a cause they have digged a pit for my soul.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> Let there come upon him confusion of which he is not aware; And let the net which he hath hidden catch him; With confusion let him fall into it.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> And my soul hath exulted in Jehovah, It shall rejoice in his salvation.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> All my bones shall say, “O Jehovah! who is like to thee, “Rescuing the poor from him who is too strong for him, “The poor and wretched from his spoiler?”

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> Rise up do witnesses [*that are*] violent; Things which I have not known they ask me.

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> They repay me evil for good, To the bereaving of my soul.

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> But as for me, in their sickness my clothing was sackcloth: I afflicted by fasting my soul; And my prayer upon my own bosom was turned.

<span style="color:#800000">14</span> As if he had been a friend, as if a brother to me, I behaved myself *towards him*: As he who mourneth heavily for his mother I humbled myself.

<span style="color:#800000">15</span> But they at my halting rejoiced, they were assembled together: Assembled together, I say, against me, were the abjects whom I had not known: They have torn with their lips and have not been silent.

<!-- PAGE 888 -->

<span style="color:#800000">16</span> Among perfidious jesters at feasts, They gnash upon me with their teeth.

<span style="color:#800000">17</span> O Lord! how long wilt thou behold it? Rescue my soul from their violence, From the lions my only one.

<span style="color:#800000">18</span> I will celebrate thee in the congregation great: Among people many I will praise thee.

<span style="color:#800000">19</span> Let not those rejoice over me, who unjustly are my enemies; Nor let those who hate me without a cause wink with the eye.

<span style="color:#800000">20</span> For peace they speak not;

<span style="color:#800000">21</span> They have opened against me their mouth; They have said, “Aha! aha! seen *it* hath our eye.”

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">22</span> Thou hast seen *it also*, O Jehovah: Be not silent, O Lord! be not far from me.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">23</span> Arouse thyself, and awake for my judgment, My God! and my Lord! to my cause.

<span style="color:#800000">24</span> Judge me according to thy righteousness, O Jehovah, my God! And let them not rejoice over me.

<span style="color:#800000">25</span> Let them not say in their heart, “Aha! our soul!” Let them not say “We have destroyed him.”

<span style="color:#800000">26</span> Let those be ashamed and confounded together, Who rejoice at my calamity; Let those be clothed with shame and ignominy, Who magnify themselves against me.

<span style="color:#800000">27</span> *But* let those shout and rejoice, who favor my righteousness: And let them say continually, “Magnified be Jehovah! “Who loveth the peace of his servant.”

<span style="color:#800000">28</span> And my tongue shall speak of thy righteousness; All the day of thy praise.
