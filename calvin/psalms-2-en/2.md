<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 2</span></p>

<sup>1</sup>  Why tumultuously rise do the Gentiles, And the peoples murmur in vain?

<sup>2</sup>  Confederated have the kings of the earth, And the princes have assembled together, Against Jehovah and against his Christ.

<!-- PAGE 849 -->

<sup>3</sup> “Let us break asunder their bonds, “And cast away from us their yoke.”

<sup>4</sup> He who dwelleth in heaven shall laugh; The Lord shall deride them.

<sup>5</sup> Then shall he speak to them in his wrath, And in his hot displeasure he shall vex them.

<sup>6</sup> “I have anointed my king, “Upon Zion, the mountain of my holiness.”

<sup>7</sup> I will declare the decree: Jehovah hath said to me, “My Son art thou; I, this day, have begotten thee.

<sup>8</sup> “Ask of me, and I will give thee the Gentiles for thy inheritance, “And for thy possession the uttermost parts of the earth.

<sup>9</sup> “Thou shalt break them with a rod of iron;” As the vessel of a potter thou shalt dash them in pieces.”

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>10</sup> And now, O ye kings! understand; Be instructed, O ye judges of the earth!</p>

<sup>11</sup> Serve Jehovah with fear, Rejoice with trembling.

<sup>12</sup> Kiss the Son, lest he be angry, And ye perish from the way, When shall be kindled in a short time his wrath. Blessed are all who trust in him.
