<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 73</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*A Psalm of Asaph*</span></p>

<!-- PAGE 937 -->

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> Yet God is good to Israel, To those who are right in heart.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">2</span> As for me, wellnigh slipped had my feet, My steps were almost gone:</p>

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">3</span> For I was envious at the foolish, On seeing the prosperity of the ungodly.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> For there are no bands to their death, And vigorous is their strength.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> In the [*common*] trouble of man they are not; And with other men they are not scourged.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> Therefore encompass them as a chain doth pride; Covered them hath the raiment of violence.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> Their eye projecteth through fatness; They have passed beyond the thoughts of their heart.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> They break forth, and talk wickedly of extortion: From on high do they speak.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> They have set against the heaven their mouth, And their tongue walketh through the earth.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> On this account his people will return hither, And waters of a full cup will be wrung out to them.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">11</span> And they say, “How doth God know? “And is there knowledge in the Most High? “</p>

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> Behold these are the ungodly, and [*yet*]. they are in quiet for ever: They heap up riches.

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> Surely in vain I have purified my heart, And washed every day my hands:

<span style="color:#800000">14</span> And I have been scourged every day, And my chastisement [*has been*] every morning.

<span style="color:#800000">15</span> If I have said, “I will speak thus,” Behold! the generation of thy children: I have transgressed.

<!-- PAGE 938 -->

<span style="color:#800000">16</span> And although I applied my mind to know this, A pain fill thing it was in mine eyes, <span style="color:#800000">17</span> Until I entered into the sanctuaries of God, *And* understood their end.

<span style="color:#800000">18</span> Surely in slippery places thou hast set them; Thou shalt east them down into destructions.

<span style="color:#800000">19</span> How have they been destroyed as in a moment! They have perished, they have been consumed with terrors.

<span style="color:#800000">20</span> As it were a dream after a man is awakened; O Lord! in awaking, their image thou wilt render contemptible.

<span style="color:#800000">21</span> For in a ferment was my heart, And in my reins I was pierced.

<span style="color:#800000">22</span> And I myself [*was*] foolish and knew not *any thing*; As the beasts have I been before thee.

<span style="color:#800000">23</span> Nevertheless *I have been* continually with thee; Thou hast held my right hand.

<span style="color:#800000">24</span> With thy counsel thou shalt guide me; And at length to glory thou shalt take me.

<span style="color:#800000">25</span> Whom have I in heaven *but thee*? And another with time I have not desired on the earth.

<span style="color:#800000">26</span> Failed have my flesh and my heart; *But* the strength of my heart and my portion *is* God for ever.

<span style="color:#800000">27</span> For lo! those who depart from thee shall perish: Thou hast destroyed every one who goeth a whoring from thee.

<span style="color:#800000">28</span> As for me, to draw near to God is good for me: I have put in the Lord Jehovah my trust, That I may tell of all thy works.
