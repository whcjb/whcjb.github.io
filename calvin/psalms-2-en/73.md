<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 73</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*A Psalm of Asaph*</span></p>

<!-- PAGE 937 -->

<sup>1</sup> Yet God is good to Israel, To those who are right in heart.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>2</sup> As for me, wellnigh slipped had my feet, My steps were almost gone:</p>

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>3</sup> For I was envious at the foolish, On seeing the prosperity of the ungodly.</p>

<sup>4</sup> For there are no bands to their death, And vigorous is their strength.

<sup>5</sup> In the [*common*] trouble of man they are not; And with other men they are not scourged.

<sup>6</sup> Therefore encompass them as a chain doth pride; Covered them hath the raiment of violence.

<sup>7</sup> Their eye projecteth through fatness; They have passed beyond the thoughts of their heart.

<sup>8</sup> They break forth, and talk wickedly of extortion: From on high do they speak.

<sup>9</sup> They have set against the heaven their mouth, And their tongue walketh through the earth.

<sup>10</sup> On this account his people will return hither, And waters of a full cup will be wrung out to them.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>11</sup> And they say, “How doth God know? “And is there knowledge in the Most High? “</p>

<sup>12</sup> Behold these are the ungodly, and [*yet*]. they are in quiet for ever: They heap up riches.

<sup>13</sup> Surely in vain I have purified my heart, And washed every day my hands:

<sup>14</sup> And I have been scourged every day, And my chastisement [*has been*] every morning.

<sup>15</sup> If I have said, “I will speak thus,” Behold! the generation of thy children: I have transgressed.

<!-- PAGE 938 -->

<sup>16</sup> And although I applied my mind to know this, A pain fill thing it was in mine eyes, <sup>17</sup> Until I entered into the sanctuaries of God, *And* understood their end.

<sup>18</sup> Surely in slippery places thou hast set them; Thou shalt east them down into destructions.

<sup>19</sup> How have they been destroyed as in a moment! They have perished, they have been consumed with terrors.

<sup>20</sup> As it were a dream after a man is awakened; O Lord! in awaking, their image thou wilt render contemptible.

<sup>21</sup> For in a ferment was my heart, And in my reins I was pierced.

<sup>22</sup> And I myself [*was*] foolish and knew not *any thing*; As the beasts have I been before thee.

<sup>23</sup> Nevertheless *I have been* continually with thee; Thou hast held my right hand.

<sup>24</sup> With thy counsel thou shalt guide me; And at length to glory thou shalt take me.

<sup>25</sup> Whom have I in heaven *but thee*? And another with time I have not desired on the earth.

<sup>26</sup> Failed have my flesh and my heart; *But* the strength of my heart and my portion *is* God for ever.

<sup>27</sup> For lo! those who depart from thee shall perish: Thou hast destroyed every one who goeth a whoring from thee.

<sup>28</sup> As for me, to draw near to God is good for me: I have put in the Lord Jehovah my trust, That I may tell of all thy works.
