<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 3</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*A Psalm of David, when he fled from the face of Absalom his son*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> O Lord! how are my oppressors multiplied! Many rise up against me,

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">2</span> Many say to my soul, “There is no help for him in God.” Selah.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> And thou, O Jehovah! art a shield for me; My glory, and he that exalteth my head.

<!-- PAGE 850 -->

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> With my voice to the Lord have I cried, And he heard me from the mountain of his holiness. Selah.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> I lay down and slept; I awaked, because the Lord sustains me.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> I will not be afraid of ten thousands of people, Who on all sides have set their camps against me.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> Arise, O Lord l Save me, O my God! For thou hast smitten all my enemies upon the cheekbone; The teeth of the ungodly thou hast broken.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> To the Lord belongeth salvation; Upon thy people *is* thy blessing. Selah.
