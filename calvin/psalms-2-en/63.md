<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 63</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*A Psalm of David, when he was in the wilderness of Judea*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> O God! my God art thou; Early will I seek thee: Thirsted for thee hath my soul, Longed for thee hath my flesh, In this land [*that is*] inhospitable and dry, without water.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">2</span> Thus in the sanctuary have I beheld thee, To see thy power and thy glory.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> Because better *is* thy mercy than life, My lips shall praise thee.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Thus will I bless thee in my life; In thy name I will lift up my hands.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> As with marrow and farness satisfied shall be my soul, And with lips of rejoicing shall my mouth praise *thee*.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> Surely I will remember thee upon my bed; In the watches *of the night* I will meditate upon thee;

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> Because thou hast been a help to me: And in the shadow of thy wings I will shout for joy.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> Cleaved hath my soul to [*literally after*] thee; Sustain me shall thy right hand.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> And they whilst they seek to destroy my soul, Shall go into the lowest parts of the earth.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> They shall east him down to the edge of the sword: The portion of foxes they shall be.

<!-- PAGE 923 -->

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> But the king shall rejoice in God, And glory shall every one who sweareth by him: For stopped shall be the mouth of those who speak falsehood.
