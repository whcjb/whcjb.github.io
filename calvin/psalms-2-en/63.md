<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 63</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#000080">*A Psalm of David, when he was in the wilderness of Judea*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> O God! my God art thou; Early will I seek thee: Thirsted for thee hath my soul, Longed for thee hath my flesh, In this land [*that is*] inhospitable and dry, without water.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>2</sup> Thus in the sanctuary have I beheld thee, To see thy power and thy glory.</p>

<sup>3</sup> Because better *is* thy mercy than life, My lips shall praise thee.

<sup>4</sup> Thus will I bless thee in my life; In thy name I will lift up my hands.

<sup>5</sup> As with marrow and farness satisfied shall be my soul, And with lips of rejoicing shall my mouth praise *thee*.

<sup>6</sup> Surely I will remember thee upon my bed; In the watches *of the night* I will meditate upon thee;

<sup>7</sup> Because thou hast been a help to me: And in the shadow of thy wings I will shout for joy.

<sup>8</sup> Cleaved hath my soul to [*literally after*] thee; Sustain me shall thy right hand.

<sup>9</sup> And they whilst they seek to destroy my soul, Shall go into the lowest parts of the earth.

<sup>10</sup> They shall east him down to the edge of the sword: The portion of foxes they shall be.

<!-- PAGE 923 -->

<sup>11</sup> But the king shall rejoice in God, And glory shall every one who sweareth by him: For stopped shall be the mouth of those who speak falsehood.
