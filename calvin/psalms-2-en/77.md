<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 77</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician upon Jeduthun. A Psalm of Asaph*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> My voice [*I lifted up*] to God, and I cried; My voice [*I lifted up*] to God, and he heard me.

<sup>2</sup> In the day of my affliction the Lord I sought: My hand in the night was stretched out and relaxed not; Refuse consolation did my soul.

<sup>3</sup> I will remember God, and will be disquieted; I will meditate, and oppressed with sorrow shall be my spirit. Selah.

<sup>4</sup> Thou hast held the watches of my eyes: I am troubled, and will not speak.

<sup>5</sup> I have recounted the days of old, The years of ages past.

<sup>6</sup> I will call to remembrance my song in the night: With my heart I will meditate, And search diligently shall my spirit.

<sup>7</sup> For ever will the Lord east off? And will he be favorable no more?

<sup>8</sup> Gone for ever is his mercy? Fail doth his oracle from generation to generation?

<!-- PAGE 943 -->

<sup>9</sup> Forgotten to be merciful hath God? Hath he shut up in anger his compassions? Selah.

<sup>10</sup> And I said my disease [*is only for a season*;] The years of the right hand of the Most High [*I will wait for*.] <sup>11</sup> I will remember the works of God; Surely I will remember thy wonders from the beginning.

<sup>12</sup> And I will meditate on all thy works, And on thy doings I will muse.

<sup>13</sup> O God! in the sanctuary *are* thy ways: What god *is* so great as God?

<sup>14</sup> Thou art the God who doest wonders; Thou hast made known among the peoples thy strength.

<sup>15</sup> Thou hast redeemed by [*thy*] arm thy people, The sons of Jacob and Joseph. Selah.

<sup>16</sup> See thee did the waters, O God! see thee did the waters; They were afraid; also tremble did the deeps.

<sup>17</sup> The clouds poured out waters; The heavens gave forth a voice: Also thine arrows went abroad.

<sup>18</sup> The voice of thy thunder was in the circuit, The lightnings illumined the world; The earth trembled and was shaken.

<sup>19</sup> In the sea are thy ways, And thy paths in the great waters; And thy footsteps are not known.

<sup>20</sup> Thou leadest like sheep thy people By the hand of Moses and Aaron.
