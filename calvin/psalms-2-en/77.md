<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 77</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician upon Jeduthun. A Psalm of Asaph*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> My voice [*I lifted up*] to God, and I cried; My voice [*I lifted up*] to God, and he heard me.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> In the day of my affliction the Lord I sought: My hand in the night was stretched out and relaxed not; Refuse consolation did my soul.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> I will remember God, and will be disquieted; I will meditate, and oppressed with sorrow shall be my spirit. Selah.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Thou hast held the watches of my eyes: I am troubled, and will not speak.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> I have recounted the days of old, The years of ages past.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> I will call to remembrance my song in the night: With my heart I will meditate, And search diligently shall my spirit.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> For ever will the Lord east off? And will he be favorable no more?

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> Gone for ever is his mercy? Fail doth his oracle from generation to generation?

<!-- PAGE 943 -->

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> Forgotten to be merciful hath God? Hath he shut up in anger his compassions? Selah.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> And I said my disease [*is only for a season*;] The years of the right hand of the Most High [*I will wait for*.] <span style="color:#800000">11</span> I will remember the works of God; Surely I will remember thy wonders from the beginning.

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> And I will meditate on all thy works, And on thy doings I will muse.

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> O God! in the sanctuary *are* thy ways: What god *is* so great as God?

<span style="color:#800000">14</span> Thou art the God who doest wonders; Thou hast made known among the peoples thy strength.

<span style="color:#800000">15</span> Thou hast redeemed by [*thy*] arm thy people, The sons of Jacob and Joseph. Selah.

<span style="color:#800000">16</span> See thee did the waters, O God! see thee did the waters; They were afraid; also tremble did the deeps.

<span style="color:#800000">17</span> The clouds poured out waters; The heavens gave forth a voice: Also thine arrows went abroad.

<span style="color:#800000">18</span> The voice of thy thunder was in the circuit, The lightnings illumined the world; The earth trembled and was shaken.

<span style="color:#800000">19</span> In the sea are thy ways, And thy paths in the great waters; And thy footsteps are not known.

<span style="color:#800000">20</span> Thou leadest like sheep thy people By the hand of Moses and Aaron.
