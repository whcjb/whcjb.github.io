<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 58</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. Destroy not. Michtam of David*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> Do ye indeed, O ye congregation! speak righteousness? Uprightly do ye judge? O ye sons of men!

<sup>2</sup> Yea rather in *your* heart wickedness ye plot, On the earth violence your hands weigh out.

<sup>3</sup> Estranged are the ungodly from the womb, They have gone astray from their birth speaking falsehood.

<sup>4</sup> They have poison like the poison of a serpent, [*They are*] like the deaf adder which stoppeth her ear;

<sup>5</sup> Which listeneth not to the voice of the enchanter, — Of him who exerciseth enchantment skillfully.

<sup>6</sup> O God! break their teeth in their mouth: The jawbones of the lions break, O Jehovah!

<sup>7</sup> Let them melt away like water, let them be gone: Let them bend their bow, and let their arrows be as if broken.

<sup>8</sup> Like a snail which melteth away let them vanish; Like the untimely birth of a woman which doth not see the sun.

<sup>9</sup> Before your pots can feel the fire of the bramble, *Like* flesh yet raw as a whirlwind he shall carry him away.

<sup>10</sup> Rejoice shall the righteous when he seeth the vengeance; His hands he shall wash in the blood of the ungodly.

<!-- PAGE 918 -->

<sup>11</sup> And men  <span style="color:#800000">ff86</span> shall say, “Truly there is fruit for the righteous, “Truly there is a God who judgeth in the earth.”
