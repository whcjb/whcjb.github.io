<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 8</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the chief Musician upon Hagittith. A Psalm of David*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> O Jehovah, our Lord! How wonderful is thy name in all the earth, To set thy glory above the heavens!

<sup>2</sup> Out of the mouths of babes and sucklings Thou hast founded *thy* strength, because of thy adversaries, To put to flight the enemy and the avenger.

<sup>3</sup> When! see thy heavens, the works of thy fingers; The moon and the stars which thou hast arranged:

<sup>4</sup> What is man that thou art mindful of him? And the son of man, that thou visitest him?

<sup>5</sup> For thou hast made him lower a little than God, And with glory and honor thou hast crowned him.

<sup>6</sup> Thou hast set him over the works of thy hands: All things thou hast put under his feet.

<sup>7</sup> Sheep and oxen, all of them, And also the beasts of the fields;

<sup>8</sup> The fowl of the heavens, and the fish of the sea, And whatsoever passeth through the paths of the seas.

<sup>9</sup> O Jehovah, our Lord! How wonderful is thy name in all the earth!
