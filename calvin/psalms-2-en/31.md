<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 31</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician,. A Psalm of David*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> In thee, O Jehovah! have I trusted, Let me not be put to shame for ever: In thy righteousness deliver me.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> Incline to me thy ear, Speedily do thou rescue me; Be to me for a rock of strength, For a house of defense to save me.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> For my rock, and my fortress *art* thou: And for the sake of thy name thou wilt direct and guide me.

<!-- PAGE 881 -->

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Extricate me from the net, which they have hidden for me, For thou art my strength.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> Into thy hand I will commend my spirit; For thou hast redeemed me, O Jehovah! God of truth.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> I hate all those who regard lying vanities; But as for me in Jehovah I have trusted.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> I will exult and rejoice in thy goodness; Because thou hast looked upon my affliction: Thou hast known in distresses my soul:

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> And thou hast not shut me up in the hand of the enemy: *But* thou hast set at large my feet.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> Have pity upon me, O Jehovah! for trouble is to me: My eye hath consumed away for vexation; my soul and my belly.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> My life hath riffled through grid; And my years with groaning: My strength hath sunk through my sorrow, And my bones have moldered away.

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> On account of all my enemies I was a reproach, Yea to my neighbors exceedingly, And a terror to my acquaintances; And those who saw me abroad fled from me.

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> I was forgotten as a dead man from the heart; I became like a vessel [*that is*] broken.

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> For I have heard the slander of many: Fear *hath seized* me on every side, While they consult together against me, And to take away my life do devise.

<span style="color:#800000">14</span> But as for me, in thee have I trusted, O Jehovah! I have said, “My God art thou.”

<span style="color:#800000">15</span> In thy hand are my times; Rescue me from the hand of my enemies, And from those who persecute me.

<span style="color:#800000">16</span> Cause to shine upon thy servant thy face, Save me in thy goodness.

<!-- PAGE 882 -->

<span style="color:#800000">17</span> O Jehovah! let me not be put to shame, For I have called upon thee: Let the wicked be put to shame, Let Gem be silent in the grave.

<span style="color:#800000">18</span> Let the lips of lying be put to shame, Which speak against the righteous a hard thing in pride and scorn.

<span style="color:#800000">19</span> How great is thy goodness, Which thou hast hidden for those who fear thee! Which thou hast performed for those who confide in thee, Before the sons of men!

<span style="color:#800000">20</span> Thou shalt hide them in the hidden place of thy presence, From the prides of man; Thou shalt conceal them *as* in a tent, From the strife of tongues.

<span style="color:#800000">21</span> Blessed be Jehovah! For he hath made wonderful his goodness towards me, As in a city fortified.

<span style="color:#800000">22</span> But as for me, I had said in my fear, “I am cast away from before thy eyes: “ Yet truly thou hast heard the voice of my supplications, When I cried unto thee.

<span style="color:#800000">23</span> Love Jehovah, all ye meek ones of his! The faithful preserve doth Jehovah, And he repayeth plentifully him who behaveth proudly. 2d. Be of good courage, and he shall strengthen your heart, All ye who hope in Jehovah.
