<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 48</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*A Song of a Psalm. Of the sons of Korah*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> Great is Jehovah and to be praised greatly In the city of our God, in the mountain of his holiness.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> Beautiful for situation, the joy of the whole earth, The mountain of Zion, on the sides of the north, The city of the great King.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> God in her palaces is known for a defense.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> For behold! the kings assembled, They passed away together.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> They themselves saw, so they marveled; They were frightened, they fled precipitately, <span style="color:#800000">6</span> Fear seized upon them there, Pain as of one in travail.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> By the east wind thou breakest in pieces the ships of Tarshish.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> As we have heard, so have we seen In the city of Jehovah of Hosts, In the city of our God: God will establish it for ever. Selah <span style="color:#800000">9</span> We have waited, O God! for thy mercy In the midst of thy temple, <span style="color:#800000">10</span> As *is* thy name, O God! So *is* thy praise unto the ends of the earth: Of righteousness full is thy right hand.

<!-- PAGE 906 -->

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> Rejoice shall Mount Zion, Exult shall the daughters of Judah, Because of thy judgments.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">12</span> Encompass Zion, and walk round about her. Number the towers thereof,</p>

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> Set your heart to her walls, Exalt her towers, That ye may make report to the generations to come.

<span style="color:#800000">14</span> For this God is our God for ever and ever: He will be our guide even unto death.
