<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 48</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#000080">*A Song of a Psalm. Of the sons of Korah*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> Great is Jehovah and to be praised greatly In the city of our God, in the mountain of his holiness.

<sup>2</sup> Beautiful for situation, the joy of the whole earth, The mountain of Zion, on the sides of the north, The city of the great King.

<sup>3</sup> God in her palaces is known for a defense.

<sup>4</sup> For behold! the kings assembled, They passed away together.

<sup>5</sup> They themselves saw, so they marveled; They were frightened, they fled precipitately, <sup>6</sup> Fear seized upon them there, Pain as of one in travail.

<sup>7</sup> By the east wind thou breakest in pieces the ships of Tarshish.

<sup>8</sup> As we have heard, so have we seen In the city of Jehovah of Hosts, In the city of our God: God will establish it for ever. Selah <sup>9</sup> We have waited, O God! for thy mercy In the midst of thy temple, <sup>10</sup> As *is* thy name, O God! So *is* thy praise unto the ends of the earth: Of righteousness full is thy right hand.

<!-- PAGE 906 -->

<sup>11</sup> Rejoice shall Mount Zion, Exult shall the daughters of Judah, Because of thy judgments.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>12</sup> Encompass Zion, and walk round about her. Number the towers thereof,</p>

<sup>13</sup> Set your heart to her walls, Exalt her towers, That ye may make report to the generations to come.

<sup>14</sup> For this God is our God for ever and ever: He will be our guide even unto death.
