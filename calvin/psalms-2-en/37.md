<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 37</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#000080">*Of David*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> Vex not thyself because of the wicked, Nor be envious on account of the workers of iniquity.

<sup>2</sup> For like the grass quickly shall they be cut down; And as the green herb they shall wither.

<sup>3</sup> Trust thou in Jehovah, and do good; Dwell in the land, and be fed in truth.

<sup>4</sup> And delight in Jehovah, And he will grant thee the request of thy heart.

<sup>5</sup> Devolve upon Jehovah thy ways, And trust in him, and he will bring it to pass.

<sup>6</sup> And he will bring forth as the light thy righteousness, And thy judgments as the noonday.

<sup>7</sup> Be silent to Jehovah, and wait for him; Vex not thyself at him who prospereth in his way, At the man who committeth wickedness.

<sup>8</sup> Cease from anger, forsake wrath, Vex not thyself so as to commit sin.

<sup>9</sup> For the wicked shall be cut off; But those who wait for Jehovah shall inherit the earth.

<sup>10</sup> Yet a little while, and the ungodly shall not be; And thou shalt give attention to his place, and shalt not find *him*.

<sup>11</sup> But the meek ones shall inherit the earth, And shall be delighted in the abundance of peace.

<sup>12</sup> Plot doth the ungodly against the righteous, And gnasheth against him with his teeth.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>13</sup> The Lord shall laugh at him, For he seeth that coming is his day.</p>

<!-- PAGE 891 -->

<sup>14</sup> *Their* sword draw do the ungodly, And their bow they bend, To lay prostrate the poor and needy, To slay those who are upright in the way.

<sup>15</sup> *But* their sword shall enter into their own heart, And their bow shall be broken.

<sup>16</sup> Better is a little to the righteous man, Than the wealth of the ungodly who are great.

<sup>17</sup> For the arms of the ungodly shall be broken; But Jehovah sustaineth the righteous.

<sup>18</sup> Know doth Jehovah the days of the upright, And their inheritance for ever shall be.

<sup>19</sup> They shall not be put to shame in the time of adversity; And in the days of famine they shall be satisfied.

<sup>20</sup> For the ungodly shall perish, And the enemies of Jehovah like the fattest of lambs shall be consumed; Into smoke they shall be consumed.;

<sup>21</sup> Borrow doth the ungodly man, and doth not repay; But the righteous man is compassionate and giveth.

<sup>22</sup> For those who are blessed of him shall inherit the earth, And those who are cursed of him shall be cut off.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>23</sup> By Jehovah the steps of a man are directed, And his way he will love.</p>

<sup>24</sup> When he shall fall he shall not be bruised, For Jehovah putteth under [*him*] his hand.

<sup>25</sup> Young I have been, I have also grown old; And yet I have not seen the righteous man forsaken, Nor his seed begging for bread.

<sup>26</sup> Daily he is compassionate and lendeth, And his seed is for blessing.

<sup>27</sup> Depart from evil and do good, And dwell for ever.

<!-- PAGE 892 -->

<sup>28</sup> For Jehovah loveth judgment, And doth not forsake his meek ones; For ever shall they be preserved: And the seed of the ungodly shall be cut off.

<sup>29</sup> The righteous shall inherit the earth, And shall dwell for ever upon it.

<sup>30</sup> The mouth of the righteous shall speak wisdom;

<sup>31</sup> The law of his God is in his heart; Slide not shall his steps.

<sup>32</sup> Watch doth the ungodly the righteous man, And seeketh to put him to death.

<sup>33</sup> Jehovah will not leave him in his hand, Nor condemn him when he is judged.

<sup>34</sup> Wait for Jehovah, and keep his way, And he shall exalt thee to inherit the earth: When the ungodly shall be cut off thou shalt see it.

<sup>35</sup> I have seen the ungodly man robust, <span style="color:#800000">ff76</span> And spreading himself like a green bay tree;

<sup>36</sup> And he passed away, and to I he was not; And I sought for his place, and he was not found.

<sup>37</sup> Mark the perfect man, and consider the upright; For the end of such a man is peace.

<sup>38</sup> But transgressors shall be destroyed together: The end of the ungodly shall be cut off.

<sup>39</sup> But the salvation of the righteous is from Jehovah: *He will* be their strength in the time of trouble.

<sup>40</sup> And help them shall Jehovah, and deliver them: He shall deliver them from the wicked: He shall save them, because they trust in him.
