<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 11</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the chief Musician. A Psalm of David*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> In Jehovah do I put my trust: How then say ye to my soul, “Flee ye into your mountain as a bird? “ <span style="color:#800000">2</span> Surely behold! the ungodly shall bend the bow, They have made ready their arrows upon the string, shoot in secret at the upright in heart.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> Truly, the foundations are destroyed: The righteous, what hath he done?

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Jehovah is in the palace of his holiness; Jehovah in heaven *hath* his throne; His eyes behold, his eyelids consider the children of men.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> Jehovah will approve the righteous man; But the ungodly and him who loveth iniquity, hate doth his soul.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> He will rain upon the ungodly snares, Fire and brimstone, and a storm of whirlwinds: This is the portion of their cup.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> For the righteous Jehovah loveth righteousness; His countenance approveth the upright.
