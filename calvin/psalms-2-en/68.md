<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 68</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. A Psalm [ορ Song of David*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> Arise shall God: scattered shall be his enemies; And flee from before his face shall those who hate him.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> As smoke is driven away thou shalt drive them away; As wax melteth before the fire, perish shall the ungodly from before the face of God.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">3</span> But the righteous shall rejoice, They shell exult before God, And leap for joy.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Sing ye unto God, sing psalms to his name; Exalt him who rideth upon the clouds whose name is Jah, And exult before him.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> The father of the orphans, and the judge of the widows, *Is* God in the habitation of his holiness.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> God who causeth the solitary to dwell in a family, Who bringeth forth those who are bound with chains: But rebels shall dwell in a dry land.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> O God! when thou well test forth before thy people, When thou marchedst through the wilderness; Selah:

<!-- PAGE 928 -->

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> The earth was moved, Also the heavens dropped at the presence of this God: Sinai at the presence of God, the God of Israel.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> A liberal rain thou shalt make to fall, O God! upon thy inheritance, And when it is weary thou refreshest it.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> Thy congregation shall dwell therein: Thou wilt provide in thy goodness for the poor, O God!

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> The Lord shall give the word To the women who announce the great army.

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> Kings of armies shall flee — shall flee; And she who dwelleth within the house shall divide the spoils.

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> Though ye should lie among the pots, *in the ashes*, *Yet shall ye be as* the wings of the dove covered with silver, And *which* behind is *as* fine yellow gold.

<span style="color:#800000">14</span> When the Almighty scattered kings in it, It was white in Salmon.

<span style="color:#800000">15</span> The mountain of God, the mountain of Bashan, The mountain of heights, the mountain of Bashan.

<span style="color:#800000">16</span> Why leap ye, ye mountains of heights? The mountain in which it hath pleased God to dwell: Yea dwell *in it* will Jehovah for ever.

<span style="color:#800000">17</span> The chariots of God [*are*] twenty thousand thousands of angels: The Lord *is* among them, In the sanctuary as in Sinai.

<span style="color:#800000">18</span> Thou hast ascended on high; Thou hast led captive captivity; Thou hast received gifts among men; Yea even [*among*] rebels, That Jehovah God might dwell *in the midst of his people*.

<span style="color:#800000">19</span> Blessed *be* God day by day: *This* God will load us with deliverances. Selah.

<span style="color:#800000">20</span> Our God *is* the God of salvations; And to Jehovah Lord *belong* the issues of death.

<!-- PAGE 929 -->

<span style="color:#800000">21</span> Surely God shall wound the head of his enemies, The crown of the hair of him who walketh in his wickedness.

<span style="color:#800000">22</span> The Lord hath said: “From Bashan I will bring back; “I will bring back from the depths of the sea:

<span style="color:#800000">23</span> “That thy foot may be stained with blood, “The tongue of thy dogs even in that of thy enemies.”

<span style="color:#800000">24</span> They have seen thy goings, O God!— The goings of my God, my King, in the sanctuary.

<span style="color:#800000">25</span> Before went the singers; [*Then*] followed the players on instruments; In the midst [*were*] the damsels striking the timbrels.

<span style="color:#800000">26</span> In the congregations bless ye God, [*Even*] the Lord, *ye who* are of the fountain of Israel!

<span style="color:#800000">27</span> There *was* little Benjamin their ruler, The princes of Judah in their assembly, The princes of Zebulun, the princes of Naphthali.

<span style="color:#800000">28</span> Commanded hath thy God thy strength; Strengthen, O God! what thou hast wrought in us.

<span style="color:#800000">29</span> From thy temple upon Jerusalem To thee shall kings bring presents.

<span style="color:#800000">30</span> Destroy the company of spearmen, The assembly of bulls, with the calves of the peoples, Treading with their feet upon pieces of silver: Scatter thou the peoples that in wars delight.

<span style="color:#800000">31</span> Princes shall come out of Egypt; Ethiopia shall speedily stretch forth her hands to God.

<span style="color:#800000">32</span> O ye kingdoms of the earth! sing unto God: Sing psalms to the Lord. Selah.

<!-- PAGE 930 -->
<span style="color:#800000">33</span> To him who rideth upon the heavens of heavens *which are* of old: Lo! he shall send forth in his voice a voice of strength, <span style="color:#800000">34</span> Ascribe ye strength to God over Israel; His majesty and his strength [*are*] in the clouds.

<span style="color:#800000">35</span> Terrible art thou, O God! out of thy holy places: The God of Israel himself shall give strength and might to his people. Blessed *be* God!
