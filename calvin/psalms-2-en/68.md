<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 68</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. A Psalm [ορ Song of David*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> Arise shall God: scattered shall be his enemies; And flee from before his face shall those who hate him.

<sup>2</sup> As smoke is driven away thou shalt drive them away; As wax melteth before the fire, perish shall the ungodly from before the face of God.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>3</sup> But the righteous shall rejoice, They shell exult before God, And leap for joy.</p>

<sup>4</sup> Sing ye unto God, sing psalms to his name; Exalt him who rideth upon the clouds whose name is Jah, And exult before him.

<sup>5</sup> The father of the orphans, and the judge of the widows, *Is* God in the habitation of his holiness.

<sup>6</sup> God who causeth the solitary to dwell in a family, Who bringeth forth those who are bound with chains: But rebels shall dwell in a dry land.

<sup>7</sup> O God! when thou well test forth before thy people, When thou marchedst through the wilderness; Selah:

<!-- PAGE 928 -->

<sup>8</sup> The earth was moved, Also the heavens dropped at the presence of this God: Sinai at the presence of God, the God of Israel.

<sup>9</sup> A liberal rain thou shalt make to fall, O God! upon thy inheritance, And when it is weary thou refreshest it.

<sup>10</sup> Thy congregation shall dwell therein: Thou wilt provide in thy goodness for the poor, O God!

<sup>11</sup> The Lord shall give the word To the women who announce the great army.

<sup>12</sup> Kings of armies shall flee — shall flee; And she who dwelleth within the house shall divide the spoils.

<sup>13</sup> Though ye should lie among the pots, *in the ashes*, *Yet shall ye be as* the wings of the dove covered with silver, And *which* behind is *as* fine yellow gold.

<sup>14</sup> When the Almighty scattered kings in it, It was white in Salmon.

<sup>15</sup> The mountain of God, the mountain of Bashan, The mountain of heights, the mountain of Bashan.

<sup>16</sup> Why leap ye, ye mountains of heights? The mountain in which it hath pleased God to dwell: Yea dwell *in it* will Jehovah for ever.

<sup>17</sup> The chariots of God [*are*] twenty thousand thousands of angels: The Lord *is* among them, In the sanctuary as in Sinai.

<sup>18</sup> Thou hast ascended on high; Thou hast led captive captivity; Thou hast received gifts among men; Yea even [*among*] rebels, That Jehovah God might dwell *in the midst of his people*.

<sup>19</sup> Blessed *be* God day by day: *This* God will load us with deliverances. Selah.

<sup>20</sup> Our God *is* the God of salvations; And to Jehovah Lord *belong* the issues of death.

<!-- PAGE 929 -->

<sup>21</sup> Surely God shall wound the head of his enemies, The crown of the hair of him who walketh in his wickedness.

<sup>22</sup> The Lord hath said: “From Bashan I will bring back; “I will bring back from the depths of the sea:

<sup>23</sup> “That thy foot may be stained with blood, “The tongue of thy dogs even in that of thy enemies.”

<sup>24</sup> They have seen thy goings, O God!— The goings of my God, my King, in the sanctuary.

<sup>25</sup> Before went the singers; [*Then*] followed the players on instruments; In the midst [*were*] the damsels striking the timbrels.

<sup>26</sup> In the congregations bless ye God, [*Even*] the Lord, *ye who* are of the fountain of Israel!

<sup>27</sup> There *was* little Benjamin their ruler, The princes of Judah in their assembly, The princes of Zebulun, the princes of Naphthali.

<sup>28</sup> Commanded hath thy God thy strength; Strengthen, O God! what thou hast wrought in us.

<sup>29</sup> From thy temple upon Jerusalem To thee shall kings bring presents.

<sup>30</sup> Destroy the company of spearmen, The assembly of bulls, with the calves of the peoples, Treading with their feet upon pieces of silver: Scatter thou the peoples that in wars delight.

<sup>31</sup> Princes shall come out of Egypt; Ethiopia shall speedily stretch forth her hands to God.

<sup>32</sup> O ye kingdoms of the earth! sing unto God: Sing psalms to the Lord. Selah.

<!-- PAGE 930 -->
<sup>33</sup> To him who rideth upon the heavens of heavens *which are* of old: Lo! he shall send forth in his voice a voice of strength, <sup>34</sup> Ascribe ye strength to God over Israel; His majesty and his strength [*are*] in the clouds.

<sup>35</sup> Terrible art thou, O God! out of thy holy places: The God of Israel himself shall give strength and might to his people. Blessed *be* God!
