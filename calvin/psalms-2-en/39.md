<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 39</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician, Jeduthun. A Psalm of David*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> I said “I will take heed to my ways, “That I sin not with my tongue: “I will keep my mouth with a muzzle, “While the ungodly man standeth before me.”

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> I was dumb not uttering a word; I held my peace [*even*] from good; And my sorrow was stirred.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> My heart became hot within me; In my musing a fire did burn; I spake with my tongue.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Cause me to know, O Jehovah! my end, And the number of my days, That I may know of what duration I am.

<!-- PAGE 895 -->

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> Behold! as an handbreadth thou hast made my days, And my duration is as nothing before thee: Surely altogether vanity is every mart while he standeth.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> Surely in a shadow man walketh; [Selah. Surely in vain is he disquieted: They heap together, and know not who shall gather.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">7</span> And now what do I wait for, O Lord? My hope is towards thee.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> From all my sins deliver thou me: The reproach of the foolish make me not.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">9</span> I was dumb; I will not open my mouth, Because thou hast done it.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> Remove from me thy stroke: By the blow of thy hand I have failed.

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> With rebukes for iniquity thou chastisest man; And causest to waste away as a moth his beauty: Surely vanity *is* every man. Selah.

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> Hear my prayer, O Jehovah! And to my cry give ear; At my tears be not silent; For a stranger am I before thee, A sojourner like all my fathers.

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> Let me alone, that I may recover strength, Before I depart and be no more.
