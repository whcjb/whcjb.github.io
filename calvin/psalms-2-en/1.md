<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 1</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">l</span>  BLESSED *is* the man who walketh not in the counsel of the ungodly; And in the way of sinners doth not stand; And in the seat of scoffers doth not sit:

<sup>2</sup>  But in the law of the Lord *is* his delight; And in his law doth he meditate day and night.

<sup>3</sup>  He shall be like a tree planted near rivers of waters, Which shall yield its fruit in its season, And whose leaf shall not fade; And all that he doeth shall prosper.

<sup>4</sup>  Not so the ungodly: But they are like the chaff, which scatter doth the wind.

<sup>5</sup>  Therefore shall not stand the ungodly in the judgment, Nor sinners in the assembly of the righteous.

<sup>6</sup>  For know doth Jehovah the way of the righteous; But the way of the ungodly shall perish.
