<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 33</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> Exult, ye righteous! in Jehovah: For to the upright comely is praise.

<sup>2</sup> Celebrate Jehovah upon the harp; Upon the nablure, <span style="color:#800000">ff73</span> and instrument of ten strings, sing psalms to him <sup>3</sup> Sing to him a song that is new; Ardently sing with shouting.

<sup>4</sup> For right *is* the word of Jehovah, And all his works are in faithfulness.

<!-- PAGE 884 -->

<sup>5</sup> He loveth righteousness and judgment: Of the goodness of Jehovah fill is the earth.

<sup>6</sup> By the word of Jehovah the heavens were established, And by the breath of his mouth all their host.

<sup>7</sup> He gathered together as an heap the waters of the sea; He laid up as in treasure houses the deeps.

<sup>8</sup> Let all the earth fear Jehovah; Of him let all the inhabitants of the world stand in awe.

<sup>9</sup> For he spake, and it was; He commanded, and it stood.

<sup>10</sup> Jehovah scattereth the counsel of the Gentiles, He rendereth fruitless the imaginations of the peoples.

<sup>11</sup> The counsel of Jehovah for ever shall stand, The thoughts of his heart from age to age.

<sup>12</sup> Blessed is the people to whom Jehovah is their God, The people whom he hath chosen for: in inheritance to himself.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>13</sup> From heavens looked down hath Jehovah, He hath beheld all the sons of Adam.</p>

<sup>14</sup> From the dwelling place of His throne, He hath looked on all the inhabitants of the earth.

<sup>15</sup> He who hath fashioned together their hearts, Who understandeth all their works.

<sup>16</sup> There is not a king saved by the multitude of an host, Nor a giant rescued by greatness of strength.

<sup>17</sup> Fallacious is a horse for safety, And by the greatness of his strength he will not deliver.

<sup>18</sup> Behold! the eye of Jehovah is upon those who fear him, [*Upon those*] who hope in his mercy;

<sup>19</sup> To rescue from death their souls, To keep them alive in famine.

<sup>20</sup> Our soul waiteth for Jehovah: Our help and our shield is he.

<!-- PAGE 885 -->

<sup>21</sup> Surely in him rejoice shall our heart, Because in his holy name we will trust.

<sup>22</sup> Let thy mercy be upon us, O Jehovah! According as we have trusted in thee.
