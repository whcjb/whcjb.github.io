<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 69</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician upon Shoshannim. Of David*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> Save me, O God! For entered have the waters even to my soul.

<sup>2</sup> I am sunk into deep mire where there is no standing place; I am come into the depths of waters, And the floods of water have overflowed me.

<sup>3</sup> I am weary with crying, Hoarse is my throat become therewith; Failed have my eyes with waiting for my God.

<sup>4</sup> More in number than the hairs of my head are those who hate me without cause: Increased are those who desire to destroy me, [*even*] my lying adversaries: That which I took not by violence, then I restored it.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>5</sup> O God! thou knowest my foolishness, And my faults from thee are not hidden.</p>

<sup>6</sup> Let not those be ashamed in me that wait for thee, O Lord, Jehovah of armies! Let not those be put to shame in me who seek thee, O God of Israel!

<sup>7</sup> For on thy account I have suffered reproach; Shame hath covered my face.

<sup>8</sup> A stranger I have been to my brethren, And an alien I am become to the children of my mother.

<sup>9</sup> For the zeal of thy house hath eaten me up; And the reproaches of those who reproached thee have fallen upon me.

<!-- PAGE 931 -->

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>10</sup> And I wept, my soul fasted; And that was for a reproach to me.</p>

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>11</sup> And I made my garment sackcloth; And I was to them for a jest.</p>

<sup>12</sup> Defame me do those who sit in the gate; And *I am* the songs of those who drink intoxicating liquor.

<sup>13</sup> But as for me my prayer [*is*] to thee, O Jehovah! *In the* time of *thy* favor, O God! In the multitude of thy mercy answer me, In the truth of thy salvation.

<sup>14</sup> Rescue me from the mire that I may not sink; That I may be delivered from my adversaries, And from the depths of waters.

<sup>15</sup> Let not the flood of waters overflow me; And let not the deep swallow me up; And let not the pit dose its mouth upon me.

<sup>16</sup> Answer me, O Jehovah! for good *is* thy mercy: In the multitude of thy compassions look upon me.

<sup>17</sup> And hide not thy face from thy servant, for I am afflicted: Hasten! answer me!

<sup>18</sup> Draw near to my soul, redeem it: On account of my adversaries deliver me.

<sup>19</sup> Thou knowest my reproach, and my confusion, and my ignominy: Before thee *are* all my adversaries.

<sup>20</sup> Reproach hath broken my heart, and I am afflicted; And I looked for some one to show compassion, but there was none; For comforters, but I found *them* not.

<sup>21</sup> And they put into my meat gall, And in my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink.

<sup>22</sup> Let their table be before them for a snare; And their prosperity for a net;

<sup>23</sup> Darkened let be their eyes, that they may not see; And cause their loins continually to tremble.

<!-- PAGE 932 -->

<sup>24</sup> Pour out upon them thy wrath; And let the fury of thy indignation take hold of them.

<sup>25</sup> Let their palace be desolate; In their tents let there not be a dweller.

<sup>26</sup> For him whom thou hast smitten they have persecuted; And to the grief of those whom thou hast wounded they have added.

<sup>27</sup> Add iniquity to their iniquity; And let them not enter into thy righteousness.

<sup>28</sup> Let them be blotted out of the book of the living; And with the righteous let them not be written.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>29</sup> But as for me I am poor and sorrowful: Thy salvation shall exalt me.</p>

<sup>30</sup> I will praise the name of God in a song, And I will magnify him with thanksgiving.

<sup>31</sup> And this will please Jehovah More than a young bullock that hath horns and hoofs.

<sup>32</sup> Seen it have the afflicted, And they shall rejoice at it who seek God; And live shall your heart.

<sup>33</sup> For hearkened hath Jehovah to the afflicted; And his prisoners he hath not despised.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>34</sup> Praise him let heaven and earth; The seas and whatever creepeth in them.</p>

<sup>35</sup> For God will save Zion, And will build the cities of Judah; And they shall dwell there, And possess it by inheritance.

<sup>36</sup> And the seed of his servants shall inherit it; And they who love his name shall dwell in it.
