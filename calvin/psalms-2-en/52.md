<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 52</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. A lesson of instruction of David: when Doeg the*</span> <span style="color:#000080">*Edomite came and told Saul, and said to him, that David had come into the*</span> <span style="color:#000080">*house of Abimelech*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> Why dost thou glory in thy wickedness, O mighty man? The goodness of God *continueth* daily.

<sup>2</sup> Thy tongue reckoneth up mischiefs, Like a razor [*that is*] sharp working deceitfully.

<sup>3</sup> Thou lovest wickedness more than goodness; Falsehood more than to speak righteousness. Selah.

<sup>4</sup> Thou lovest all words of deceit, O thou tongue of guilefulness!

<sup>5</sup> Likewise God shall destroy thee for ever: He shall lay hold on thee and pluck thee out of thy tabernacle, And root thee out of the land of the living. Selah.

<sup>6</sup> See it also shall the righteous ones, and shall fear, And at him they shall laugh.

<sup>7</sup> Behold! the man who made not God his strength; And trusted in the abundance of his riches, And was strong in his wickedness.

<sup>8</sup> But as for me *I shall be* like an olive-tree [*that is*] green in the house of God: I have trusted in the goodness of God for ever and ever.

<sup>9</sup> I will celebrate thee for ever, because thou hast done [*it*:]  <span style="color:#800000">ff81</span> I will wait on thy name, For it is good in the presence of thy meek ones.
