<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 26</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*Of David*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> Judge me, O Jehovah: Because in my integrity I have walked, And in Jehovah have trusted, I shall not stumble.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> Prove me, O Jehovah! and try me, Examine my reins and my heart.

<!-- PAGE 876 -->

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">3</span> For thy goodness *is* before my eyes; Therefore I have walked in thy truth.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> I have not sat with men of vanity, And with crafty men I will not go in.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">5</span> I hate the assembly of transgressors, And with the wicked I will not sit.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> I will wash in purity my hands, And will encompass thy altar, O Jehovah!

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> To cause to be heard the voice of praise, And to tell of all thy marvelous deeds.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> O Jehovah! I have loved the habitation of thy house, And the place of the dwelling of thy glory.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">9</span> Gather not with ungodly men, my soul, Nor with men of bloods my life.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> For in their hands *is* maliciousness, And their right hand is full of bribes.

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> But as for me in my integrity I will walk: Redeem me, and have mercy upon me.

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> My foot hath stood in uprightness: In the congregations will I bless thee, O Jehovah!
