<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 55</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician on Neginoth. A lesson of instruction of David*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> Give ear, O God! to my prayer, And hide not thyself from my supplication.

<sup>2</sup> Attend to me, and answer me: I will wail in my address, and will become tumultuous.

<sup>3</sup> By reason of the voice of the enemy — Under the oppression of the ungodly; For they cast upon me iniquity, In wrath they set themselves against me.

<sup>4</sup> My heart trembleth within me, And the terrors of death have fallen upon me.

<sup>5</sup> Fear and trembling are come upon me, And horror hath overwhelmed me.

<sup>6</sup> And I said, “Who will give me wings like a dove? “I will fly away and be at rest.

<sup>7</sup> “Lo! I will flee far away, “I will repose in the desert. Selah.

<sup>8</sup> “I will hasten my escape from the tempestuous whirlwind.”

<sup>9</sup> Destroy, O Lord! divide their tongue: For I have seen oppression and strife in the city.

<!-- PAGE 914 -->

<sup>10</sup> By day and night they go round it upon the walls thereof: And labor and sorrow are in the midst of it.

<sup>11</sup> Wickedness  <span style="color:#800000">ff82</span> is in the midst of it; And fraud and deceit depart not from the streets thereof.

<sup>12</sup> Truly it was not an enemy who reproached me, For [*then*] I could have borne [*it*:] *It was* not an adversary who magnified himself against me, For [*then*] I would have hid himself from him.

<sup>13</sup> But thou, a man according to my own rank, My guide, and my familiar friend.

<sup>14</sup> We sweetly exchanged our secret thoughts; Into the house of God we walked in company.

<sup>15</sup> Let death seize upon them, Let them descend into the grave alive; For wickedness  <span style="color:#800000">ff83</span> *is* I, their dwelling, in the midst of them.

<sup>16</sup> As for me, to God; will I cry, And Jehovah shall save me.

<sup>17</sup> In the evening, and the morning, and at noonday Will I pray, and cry aloud; And he shall hear my voice.

<sup>18</sup> He hath redeemed into peace my soul From the battle which was against me: For many were with me.

<sup>19</sup> Hear shall God, and shall afflict them, Even he who sitteth from ancient time. Selah. Because they have no changes, And fear not God.

<sup>20</sup> He hath set his hands against those who were at peace with him: He hath broken his covenant.

<sup>21</sup> Smoother than butter are the words of his mouth, And *in* his heart *is* war; Softer are his words than oil, And [*yet*] they *are* darts.

<!-- PAGE 915 -->

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>22</sup> Cast upon Jehovah whatever blessings thou askest from him, <span style="color:#800000">ff84</span></p>

And he shall feed thee; He shall not suffer for ever the righteous man to stagger.

<sup>23</sup> Thou, O God! shalt cast them into the pit of corruption: The men of blood and deceit shall not live out half their days: But as for me, I will hope in thee.
