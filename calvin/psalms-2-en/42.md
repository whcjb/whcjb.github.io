<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 42</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. A lesson of instruction to the Sons of Korah*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> As the hart crieth for the fountains of waters, So my soul crieth for thee, O God!

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> Thirsted hath my soul for God, for the living God: When shall I come to appear before the face of God?

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> To me have my tears been *for* bread, by day and night, While it is said to me daily, “Where is thy God? “ <span style="color:#800000">4</span> When these things I remember, I pour out within me my soul, Because I had gone with the procession, Leading them even to the house of God, With the voice of exultation and praise, — The multitude dancing for joy.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> ‘Why art thou cast down, O my soul! And why art thou disquieted within me? Wait thou for God; for I shall yet give him thanks, [*For*] the salvations of his countenance.

<!-- PAGE 899 -->

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> O my God! my soul within me is cast down, When I remember thee from the land of Jordan and of Hermonim, From the mountain Mizar.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> Deep unto deep calleth at the noise of thy waterspouts: All thy waves and all thy billows over me have gone.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> By day command will Jehovah his loving-kindness, And by night his song *shall be* with me, And prayer to the God of my life.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> I will say to God, my Rock, “Why hast thou forgotten me? “Why mourning do I go because of the oppression of *my* enemy?”

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> [*It is as*] a wound in my bones when my enemies reproach me, Saying to me daily, “Where is thy God? “ <span style="color:#800000">11</span> ‘Why art thou cast down, O my soul? And why art thou disquieted within me? Hope in God; for I shall yet give thanks to him, The salvations of my countenance, and my God.
