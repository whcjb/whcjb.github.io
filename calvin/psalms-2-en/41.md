<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 41</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. A Psalm of David*</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> Blessed is he who judgeth wisely of the poor: In the day of evil Jehovah will deliver him.

<sup>2</sup> Jehovah will keep him, and preserve him alive: He shall be blessed upon the earth: And thou will not give him up to the desire of his enemies.

<sup>3</sup> Jehovah will support him upon the bed of sorrow: All his bed thou hast turned in his sickness.

<sup>4</sup> I have said, “O Jehovah! have mercy upon me; “Heal my soul, for I have sinned against thee.”

<!-- PAGE 898 -->
<sup>5</sup> My enemies have spoken evil of me, “When will he die, and perish shall his name? “ <sup>6</sup> And if he come to see me falsely doth he speak; His heart gathereth iniquity to itself’: When he goeth abroad he speaketh [*it*.] <sup>7</sup> Together against me do all my haters whisper; Against me do they devise evil for me, [*saying*,] <sup>8</sup> “An evil deed of Belial cleaveth fast to him; “And he who lieth shall not rise again.”

<sup>9</sup> Even the man of my peace in whom I trusted, Who eateth of my bread, hath lifted up against me the heel.

<sup>10</sup> Do thou *then*, O Jehovah! have mercy upon me; Raise me up, and I will recompense them.

<sup>11</sup> By this I have known that I have been acceptable to thee, Because my enemy shall not triumph over me.

<sup>12</sup> And as for me, in my integrity thou wilt uphold me, And wilt establish me before thy face for ever.

<sup>13</sup> Blessed *be* Jehovah, the God of Israel, For ever and ever. Amen and Amen.
