<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 10</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> Why, O Jehovah! standest thou afar off? And winkest at seasonable times in trouble?

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> In his pride the ungodly doth persecute the poor; Let them be caught in the devices which they imagine.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> For praise himself doth the ungodly on account of the desire of his own soul; And the violent man blesseth himself: He despiseth Jehovah.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> The ungodly, in the pride of his countenance doth not inquire; All his devices say, “There is not a God.”

<!-- PAGE 857 -->

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> Prosperous are his ways at all times; High are thy judgments before him; At all his enemies he puffeth.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> He saith in his heart, “I shall not be moved from generation to generation,” Because he is not in adversity, <span style="color:#800000">7</span> Of cursing his mouth is full, and of deceit, and of malice: Under his tongue are mischief and iniquity.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> He will sit in the ensnaring places of the villages;  In his lurking places will he murder the innocent: His eyes against the poor will take their aim.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> He will lie in wait in secret, as a lion in his den; He will lie in wait to catch the poor; He will catch the poor by drawing him into his net.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> He will crouch low, he will cast himself down; Then shall fall by his strengths an army of the afflicted.

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> He hath said in his heart, “Forgotten it hath God; “He hideth his face, that he may not see it for ever.”

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> Arise, O Jehovah, God! lift up thy hand: Do not forget the poor.

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> Why do the wicked despise God? He saith in his heart, “Thou wilt not require it.”

<span style="color:#800000">14</span> Thou hast seen *it*; For mischief and oppression thou considerest, That thou mayest take them into thy own hand: Upon thee shall the poor leave; To the orphan thou wilt be an helper.

<span style="color:#800000">15</span> Break thou the arm of the wicked and the evil man; Thou shalt seek his wickedness, and shalt not find it.

<span style="color:#800000">16</span> Jehovah is King for ever and ever; Perished are the heathen out of his land.

<span style="color:#800000">17</span> The desire of the needy thou hast heard, O Jehovah! Thou wilt direct their heart; Hear shall thine car:

<!-- PAGE 858 -->

<span style="color:#800000">18</span> That thou mayest judge the fatherless and the poor, That the man *who* is of earth may terrify no more.
