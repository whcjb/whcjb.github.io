<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 40</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the Chief Musician. A Psalm of David*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> I have patiently waited for Jehovah, And he inclined to me, and heard my cry.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> And he drew me out of the roaring pit, Out of the mire of clay, And set upon a rock my feet; He hath established my steps.

<!-- PAGE 896 -->

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> And he hath put in my mouth a song [that ισ new, *Even* praise to our God: See shall many and shall fear, And shall trust in Jehovah.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Blessed is the man who maketh Jehovah his confidence, And hath not respected the proud, Nor those who turn aside to lying.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> Many, O Jehovah, my God! are thy wonderful works which thou hast done; And thy thoughts towards us it is impossible to reckon up in order to thee: I will declare and speak [*of them;*] [Βυτ they are more than can be told.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> Sacrifice and oblation thou hast not taken pleasure in: *But* my ears hast thou made fit:  <span style="color:#800000">ff78</span> Burnt-offering and sin-offering thou hast not required.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> Then I said, “Behold! I come; “In the volume of the book it is written of me, <span style="color:#800000">8</span> “That I may do thy pleasure, O my God! “I have desired *to do it*, “And thy law is in the midst of my bowels.”

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> I have proclaimed thy righteousness in the assembly great: Behold my lips I will not restrain; O Jehovah! thou knowest it.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> Thy righteousness I have not hidden within my heart; Thy truth and thy salvation I have declared: I have not concealed thy goodness and thy truth in the assembly great.

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> O thou, Jehovah! withhold not thy compassions from me; Let thy goodness and thy truth continually preserve me.

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> For encompassed me have evils innumerable; Laid hold upon me have my iniquities, *yea in so great number* that I cannot see them: They are more in number than the hairs of my head; *And* my heart hath failed me.

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> Let it please thee, O Jehovah! to deliver me; O Jehovah! to my aid make haste.

<!-- PAGE 897 -->

<span style="color:#800000">14</span> Let them be put to shame and confounded together Who seek after my soul to destroy it; Let them be turned backward and put to shame Who seek after my hurt.

<span style="color:#800000">15</span> Let them be destroyed for a reward of their shame, Who have said to me, “Aha! aha!”

<span style="color:#800000">16</span> Let all those exult and rejoice in thee who seek thee; And let those say continually, “May Jehovah be magnified” — “Those who love thy salvation.

<span style="color:#800000">17</span> But as for me *I am* poor and needy. Jehovah hath regarded me; My help and my deliverer *art thou*: O thou, my God! delay not.
