<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 16</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*Michtam. A Psalm Of David*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> Keep me, O God! *For* in thee do I trust.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> Thou shalt say to Jehovah, “My Lord thou art; “My well doing *extendeth* not to thee.”

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> To the saints who are on the earth, And to the excellent; all my delight is in them.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> Multiplied shall be their sorrows who offer to a stranger; I will not taste their libations of blood, Nor will I take their names in my lips.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> Jehovah is the portion of my inheritance, and of my cup; Thou maintainest my lot.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> The lines have fallen to me in pleasant *places*; Yea, an inheritance that is goodly hath fallen to me.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> I will magnify Jehovah, who giveth me counsel; Even in the nights instruct me do my reins.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> I have set Jehovah before me continually; Since he is at my right hand, I shall not be moved.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> Therefore glad is my heart, rejoice doth my tongue; Also my flesh dwelleth in confidence.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> For thou wilt not leave my soul in the grave; Nor wilt thou make thy Holy One to see the pit.

<!-- PAGE 862 -->

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> Thou wilt make known to me the way of life; Fullness of joy *is* in thy countenance; Pleasures *are* at thy right hand for evermore.
