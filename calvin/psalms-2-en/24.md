<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 24</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*A Psalm of David*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> Jehovah’s is the earth, and the fullness thereof; The world, and those who dwell therein.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> For he upon the seas hath founded it, And upon the floods he hath arranged it.

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> Who shall ascend into the mountain of Jehovah? Who shall stand in the place of his holiness?

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> He who is clean in hands and pure in heart, Who hath not lifted up to vanity his soul, And hath not sworn deceitfully.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> He shall receive blessing from Jehovah, And righteousness from the God of his salvation.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> This is the generation of those who seek him, Of those who seek thy face, O Jacob! Selah.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> Lift up, O ye gates! your heads, And be ye lifted up, ye doors everlasting! And enter shall the King of Glory.
