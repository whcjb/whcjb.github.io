<p class="title-block-h2" style="text-align:center; font-size:16px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#006411">PSALM 9</span></p>

<p style="text-align:center; color:#000080; margin:14px 2em;"><span style="color:#000080">*To the chief Musician upon Hagittith. Almuth Laben. A Psalm of David*</span></p>

<span style="color:#800000">1</span> I will praise Jehovah with my whole heart; will recount all thy marvelous works.

<span style="color:#800000">2</span> I will rejoice and exult in thee; I will celebrate in Psalms thy name, O thou Most high!

<span style="color:#800000">3</span> When turned are my enemies backward, They fall and are put to flight at thy presence.

<span style="color:#800000">4</span> For thou hast maintained my judgment and my cause; Thou hast sat upon the throne a righteous judge.

<span style="color:#800000">5</span> Thou hast rebuked the heathen; Thou hast destroyed the ungodly; Their name thou hast blotted out for ever and ever.

<span style="color:#800000">6</span> O thou enemy! come to an end are desolations for ever; And cities thou hast destroyed; Perished has their memory with them.

<span style="color:#800000">7</span> And Jehovah for ever sitteth: His throne for judgment lie hath prepared.

<span style="color:#800000">8</span> And he shall judge the world in righteousness; He shall judge the peoples in rectitude.

<span style="color:#800000">9</span> And Jehovah will be a place of defense to the poor, And a protection in seasonable times in trouble.

<span style="color:#800000">10</span> And trust in thee shall those who know thy name: For thou forsakest not those who seek thee, O Jehovah!

<span style="color:#800000">11</span> Sing psalms to Jehovah, who dwelleth in Zion, Proclaim among the peoples his doings;

<span style="color:#800000">12</span> For in requiring blood, it lie hath remembered: He hath not forgotten the cry of the poor.

<!-- PAGE 856 -->

<span style="color:#800000">13</span> Have mercy upon me, O Jehovah! See my affliction from those who persecute me, O thou that liftest me up from the gates of death;

<span style="color:#800000">14</span> That I may recount all thy praises in the gates of the daughter of Zion; That I may exult in thy salvation.

<span style="color:#800000">15</span> Sunk are the heathen into the pit which they have made; In the net which they have hid taken are their own feet.

<span style="color:#800000">16</span> Known is Jehovah by executing judgment: In the work of his own hands the wicked is snared. Higgaion Selah.

<span style="color:#800000">17</span> Turned shall be the wicked into hell: All the nations that forget God.

<span style="color:#800000">18</span> For not for ever shall be forgotten the poor; The hope of the humble shall not perish for ever.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><span style="color:#800000">19</span> Arise, O Jehovah! let not man prevail: Let the heathen be judged in thy sight.</p>

<span style="color:#800000">20</span> Put, O Jehovah! fear in them, That the heathen may know that mortal men they are. Selah.
