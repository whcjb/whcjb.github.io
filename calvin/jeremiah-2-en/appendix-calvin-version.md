<p class="title-block-h1" style="text-align:center; font-size:22px; font-weight:bold; margin:18px 0 12px;" markdown="1"><span style="color:#0000d4">JEREMIAH</span></p>

<sup>1</sup> The words of Jeremiah, the son of Hilkiah, *one* of the priests who were in Anathoth, in the land of Benjamin:

<sup>2</sup> Even the word of Jehovah came to him in the days of Josiah, the son of Amon, The king of Judah, in the tenth year of his reign;

<sup>3</sup> And it came in the days of Jehoiakim, the son of Josiah, the king of Judah, to the end of the eleventh year of Zedekiah, the son of Josiah, the king of Judah, to the transmigration of Jerusalem in the fifth month.

<sup>4</sup> And the word of Jehovah came to me, saying, — <sup>5</sup> Before I formed thee in the womb, I knew thee; Before thou camest forth from the womb, I sanctified thee; A prophet to the nations have I made thee.

<sup>6</sup> And I said, — Ah! Lord Jehovah, Behold, I know not how to speak, for I am a child.

<sup>7</sup> And Jehovah said to me, — Say not, I am a child; For wheresoever I send thee, thou shalt go; And whatsoever I command thee, thou shalt say:

<sup>8</sup> Fear not their face, for I am with thee, To deliver thee, saith Jehovah.

<sup>9</sup> And Jehovah extended his hand and touched my mouth; and Jehovah said to me, — Behold, I have put my words in thy mouth;

<!-- PAGE 1133 -->

<sup>10</sup> See, I have set thee today Over nations and over kingdoms, To pull down and to destroy, To root up and to demolish, To build and to plant. (1:47)

<sup>11</sup> Then the word of Jehovah came to me saying, What seest thou, Jeremiah? And I said, The rod of a watcher *is what* I see.

<sup>12</sup> Then Jehovah said to me, Thou hast rightly seen, for I watch over my word to do it.

<sup>13</sup> And the word of Jehovah came to me again, saying, What seest thou? And I said, A pot boiling *is what* I see; its face is towards the north.

<sup>14</sup> And Jehovah said to me, — From the north shall break forth an evil On all the inhabitants of the land:

<sup>15</sup> For behold, I am calling all the families Of the kingdoms of the north, saith Jehovah; And they shall come, *and* set shall each his throne, At the entrance of the gates of Jerusalem, And on all its walls around, And on all the cities of Judah:

<sup>16</sup> And I will execute my judgments on them For all their wickedness; Because they have forsaken me, And have burnt incense to strange gods, And bowed down to the works of their own hands. (1:58)

<sup>17</sup> Thou then, gird thy loins and arise, And speak to them whatsoever I command thee; Fear not their face, Lest I dismay thee before them.

<sup>18</sup> And I, behold I have made thee this day A fortified city, an iron pillar, And a brazen wall, as to the whole land, Against the kings of Judah, Against his princes, against his priests, Against the people of the land:

<sup>19</sup> And they shall fight with thee, But shall not prevail over thee, For with thee am I, saith Jehovah, to deliver thee.

<!-- PAGE 1134 -->

<sup>1</sup> And the word of Jehovah came to me, saying, — <sup>2</sup> Go and cry in the ears of Jerusalem, Saying, Thus saith Jehovah, — I remember thee for *my* kindness to thine youth And *my* love at thy espousal, When thou didst follow me in the desert, In a land not sown. (1:70)

<sup>3</sup> Holiness *was* Israel to Jehovah, The first-fruits of his increase: Whosoever devour him shall be punished, Evil shall come upon them, saith Jehovah.

<sup>4</sup> Hear the word of Jehovah, ye house of Jacob, And all the families of the house of Israel:

<sup>5</sup> Thus saith Jehovah, — What iniquity did your fathers find in me? For they alienated themselves from me, And walked after vanity, and became vain; (1:75)

<sup>6</sup> And they said not, “Where is Jehovah, Who brought us out of the land of Egypt, And led us through the wilderness, In a land waste and rugged, In a land horrible and deadly, In a land through which none passed, And in which no man dwelt?” (1:79)

<sup>7</sup> And I brought you into a fertile land, To eat its fruit and its abundance; But ye entered and polluted my land, And my heritage have ye made an abomination:

<sup>8</sup> The priests said not, “Where is Jehovah?” And they who handled the law, knew not me; And the pastors dealt treacherously with me, And the prophets prophesied by Baal, And after things which did not profit, they walked.

<sup>9</sup> Therefore still will I contend with you, saith Jehovah; And with your children’s children will I contend.

<!-- PAGE 1135 -->
<sup>10</sup> For pass over to the isles of Chittim, and see; And to Kedar send, and consider diligently; And see whether such a thing as this has been done — <sup>11</sup> Has a nation changed its gods, Though they are no gods? Yet my people have changed their glory Unto that which does not profit.

<sup>12</sup> Be astonished, ye heavens, at this, and terrified, Be ye wholly desolated, saith Jehovah: (1:92)

<sup>13</sup> Surely, two evils have my people done, — Me have they forsaken, the fountain of living waters, And dug have they for themselves cisterns, Broken cisterns, which hold no waters!

<sup>14</sup> Is Israel a servant? Is he one born in the house? Why is he become a prey?

<sup>15</sup> Over him roar the lions, They have raised their voice; They have made his land waste; His cities are burnt up, Without an inhabitant.

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>16</sup> Even the children of Noph and Thaphanes Do break thy crown.</p>

<sup>17</sup> Hast thou not done this for thyself, By forsaking Jehovah thy God, While he was leading thee in the way?

<sup>18</sup> And now what hast thou to do in the way to Egypt, That thou mightest drink the waters of the Nile? And what hast thou to do in the way to Assyria, That thou mightest drink of the water of the river?

<sup>19</sup> Chastise thee shall thine own wickedness, And thy apostasies, they shall punish thee; And thou shalt understand and know, That it is an evil and a bitter thing for thee To have forsaken Jehovah thy God, And that my fear has not been in thee, Saith the Lord, Jehovah of hosts.

<sup>20</sup> For of old have I broken thy yoke, Have I burst thy bands; But thou hast said, “I will not serve;” For on every high hill and under every shady tree Hast thou rambled like a harlot. (1:107)

<sup>21</sup> I indeed planted thee a choice vine, Altogether a good seed; How then art thou turned to me A degenerated foreign vine!

<!-- PAGE 1136 -->

<sup>22</sup> Even though thou washest thyself with nitre, And multipliest to thee the herb of the fuller; *Yet* imprinted is thine iniquity Before my face, saith the Lord Jehovah.

<sup>23</sup> How canst thou say, “I am not polluted, After Baalim have I not gone?” See thy ways in the valley, Know what thou hast done, — *Thou* swift dromedary, traversing her ways, — <sup>24</sup> A wild she-ass, used to the desert, In her own lust snuffing up the wind she meets with: Who can thence bring her back? Whosoever seeks her, needs not weary himself; In her month will he find her. (1:118)

<sup>25</sup> Keep thy foot from being unshod, And thy throat from thirst: Yet thou hast said, “It is all over, No, for I have loved strangers, And after them will I go.”

<sup>26</sup> As there is shame to a thief when caught, So ashamed shall be the house of Israel, Their kings and their princes, Their priests and their prophets, — <sup>27</sup> Who say to the wood, “My father art thou;” And to the stone, “Thou hast begotten me:” For they have turned to me the back, not the face; But in the time of their calamity they say, “Arise and save us.”

<sup>28</sup> But where are thy gods, Which thou hast made for thyself? Let them arise, if they can save thee In the time of thy calamity; For according to the number of thy cities Have been thy gods, O Judah!

<sup>29</sup> Why do ye contend with me? Ye have all dealt perfidiously with me, saith Jehovah.

<sup>30</sup> In vain have I chastised your children; Correction they received not; Devoured has the sword your prophets, As a destroying lion.

<sup>31</sup> O generation! see ye the word of Jehovah; Have I been a desert to Israel, or a land of darkness? Wherefore have my people said, — “We have ruled, we will come no more to thee.” (1:135)

<!-- PAGE 1137 -->

<sup>32</sup> Can a maid forget her ornaments, A spouse her attire? But my people have forgotten me, Days without number.

<sup>33</sup> Why trimmest thou thy ways to seek love? Thou hast even thus taught wickedness by thy ways.

<sup>34</sup> Even in thy skirts is found The blood of the souls of the poor innocents; Not in digging under have they been found, But on account of all these things: (1:143)

<sup>35</sup> Yet thou hast said, “Surely I am clean; Only let his fury depart from me.” Behold I will contend with thee in judgment, Because thou hast said, “I have not sinned.”

<sup>36</sup> Why ramblest thou so much to change thy ways? Even of Egypt shalt thou be ashamed, As thou hast been ashamed of Assyria.

<sup>37</sup> Even now for this thou shalt go forth, And thine hands on thine head; For abhorred hath Jehovah thy confidences, And in them thou shalt not prosper. (1:151)

<sup>1</sup> It is said, when a man puts away his wife, And she goes from him to another man, Shall he return to her again? Would not the land be thus greatly polluted? But thou hast played the harlot with many friends; Yet return to me, saith Jehovah.

<sup>2</sup> Raise thine eyes to the high places, And see where thou hast played the harlot: By the ways thou didst sit for them, As the Arabian in the desert; And polluted hast thou the land With thy whoredoms and thy wickedness.

<sup>3</sup> Restrained therefore have been the showers, And the late rain has not been; Yet the front of a strumpet has been thine, Thou hast refused to be ashamed.

<sup>4</sup> Wilt thou not hereafter cry to me, — “My Father, the guide of my youth art thou?

<!-- PAGE 1138 -->

<sup>5</sup> Will he keep *wrath* for ever? Will he reserve it perpetually?” Behold, thou hast spoken, And hast done evils with all thy might. (1:162)

<sup>6</sup> And Jehovah said to me in the days of Josiah the king, — Hast thou seen what the apostate Israel has done? She went on every high mountain And under every shady tree, And played there the harlot:

<sup>7</sup> And I said, after she had done all these things, “Return to me; “ but she returned not; And see this did her perfidious sister Judah.

<sup>8</sup> And I saw, that when for all these things, Because rebellious Israel had played the harlot, I had dismissed her and given her a bill of divorce, Yet fear did not her perfidious sister Judah, But went and played also the harlot. (1:166)

<sup>9</sup> And it happened through the levity of her whoredom, That she polluted the land, And played the harlot with stone and with wood.

<sup>10</sup> And yet after all this, returned to me Has not her perfidious sister Judah, With her whole heart, but feignedly, saith Jehovah.

<sup>11</sup> And Jehovah said to me, — Justified herself has apostate Israel Rather than perfidious Judah:

<sup>12</sup> Go and publish these words towards the north, And say, Return, rebellious Israel, saith Jehovah; I will not let fall my wrath upon you, For I am merciful, saith Jehovah; I will not keep it for ever:

<sup>13</sup> But know thine iniquity, That against Jehovah thy God thou hast acted wickedly, And prostituted thy ways to strangers Under every shady tree; (1:176) And to my voice thou didst not hearken, saith Jehovah.

<sup>14</sup> Return, ye rebellious children, saith Jehovah; For I am your husband; And I will take you, one from a city, And two from a family, and bring you to Sion;

<!-- PAGE 1139 -->

<sup>15</sup> And will give you pastors according to my heart, And they shall feed you with knowledge and understanding.

<sup>16</sup> And it shall be, when ye shall multiply and increase In the land, in those days, saith Jehovah, That they will no more say, — “The ark of the covenant of Jehovah;” And it shall not come to mind, And they shall not remember nor visit it; Even *this* shall not be done any more. (1:185)

<sup>17</sup> At that time they shall call Jerusalem, The throne of Jehovah; And assemble to it shall all nations, For the name of Jehovah, even to Jerusalem; And walk shall they no more After the evil hardness of their own hearts. (1:186)

<sup>18</sup> In those days shall come The house of Judah with the house of Israel; Together shall they come from the land of the north, To the land which I have given For an inheritance to your fathers.

<sup>19</sup> But I said, How shall I put thee among the children, And give thee the desirable land, The heritage coveted by hosts of nations? And I said, “My Father,” shalt thou call me, And from me thou wilt not depart. (1:189)

<sup>20</sup> Surely as a woman deals perfidiously with her partner, So hast thou dealt perfidiously with me, O house of Israel, saith Jehovah.

<sup>21</sup> A voice on high places was heard, The weeping of the prayers of the children of Israel; Because they had perverted their way, *And* forgotten Jehovah their God. (1:192)

<sup>22</sup> “Return, ye rebellious children, I will heal your transgressions.” “Behold we come to thee, For thou art Jehovah our God:

<sup>23</sup> Surely deceit *is* from the hills, *From* the multitude of mountains; Surely, in Jehovah our God Is the salvation of Israel. (1:194)

<sup>24</sup> Even shame hath devoured the labor Of our fathers, from our youth, Even their sheep and their cattle, Their sons and their daughters.

<!-- PAGE 1140 -->

<sup>25</sup> We have lain down in our shame, And our reproach hath covered us, Because with Jehovah our God We have dealt wickedly, we and our fathers, From our childhood even to this day, (1:196) And have not attended to the voice of Jehovah our God.”

<sup>1</sup> If thou wilt return, Israel, saith Jehovah, Return to me; Even if thou wilt take away Thine abominations from my sight, And wilt not wander: (1:199)

<sup>2</sup> And thou shalt swear, “Live does Jehovah, In truth, in judgment, and in righteousness;” Then bless themselves in him shall nations, And in him shall they glory. (1:202)

<sup>3</sup> For thus saith Jehovah To the men of Judah and to Jerusalem, — Plough again the first ploughing, And sow not among thorns:

<sup>4</sup> Be ye circumcised to Jehovah, And take away the foreskin of your heart, Ye men of Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem; Lest my fury go forth like fire, And burn that none may quench it; On account of the evil of your doings.

<sup>5</sup> Proclaim ye in Judah, And publish in Jerusalem, and say, “Sound the trumpet in the land;” Call, assemble, yea, say, — “Be assembled, and let us enter into fortified cities;”

<sup>6</sup> Raise the standard in Sion; Flee, stay not, for an evil do I bring From the north, even a great ruin. (1:208)

<sup>7</sup> Ascended has the lion from his thicket, And the waster of nations is gone forth; He is come forth from his place, To make thy land a waste; Thy cities shall be destroyed, So as to be without an inhabitant.

<sup>8</sup> For this gird yourselves with sackcloth, Lament and howl; for turned away from us Is not the fury of Jehovah’s wrath.

<sup>9</sup> And it shall be in that day, saith Jehovah, That perish shall the heart of the king, And the heart of the princes; And amazed shall be the priests, And the prophets shall be astonished. (1:212)

<!-- PAGE 1141 -->

<sup>10</sup> Then I said, Ah! Lord Jehovah! Surely, deceiving thou hast deceived This people and Jerusalem, by saying, “Peace shall be to you;” Yet reached has the sword to the soul. (1:214)

<sup>11</sup> At that time it shall be said To this people and to Jerusalem, — A dry wind from the heights of the desert *Shall be* towards the way of the daughter of my people, Not to fan nor to cleanse;

<sup>12</sup> A wind stronger than this shall come for me; Now also will I pronounce judgments on them. (1:217)

<sup>13</sup> Behold as clouds shall he ascend, And as a whirlwind his chariots; Swifter than eagles his horses: Woe to us! for we are lost.

<sup>14</sup> Cleanse from evil the heart, Jerusalem; That thou mayest be saved: How long will remain within thee The thoughts of vanity! (1:221)

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>15</sup> For a voice proclaims from Dan And publishes ruin from Mount Ephraim.</p>

<sup>16</sup> Rehearse it to the nations; Behold, publish against Jerusalem, — Besiegers come from a remote land, And raise over the cities of Judah their voice;

<sup>17</sup> As keepers of the field they shall be over her around; Because she hath provoked me, saith Jehovah.

<sup>18</sup> Thy way and thy doings have done this for thee; This is thy wickedness, though it be bitter, Though it reaches to thy heart. (1:227)

<sup>19</sup> My bowels! my bowels! I am in pain; The walls of my heart! My heart is in a tumult within me; I will not be silent, for the sound of the trumpet Has my soul heard, And the clamor of war has it heard. (1:229)

<sup>20</sup> Calamity on calamity is cried; For destroyed is the whole land; Suddenly destroyed are my tents, In an instant my curtains.

<sup>21</sup> How long shall I see the standard — Shall I hear the sound of the trumpet?

<!-- PAGE 1142 -->

<sup>22</sup> Because foolish are my people, Me have they not known; Sottish children are they, And they are not intelligent; Acute are they for evil, But how to do good they know not.

<sup>23</sup> I beheld the land, and lo, it was waste and without form; And the heavens, and they had no light:

<sup>24</sup> I beheld the mountains, and lo, they trembled; And all the hills were shaking:

<sup>25</sup> I beheld, and lo, there was no man, And every bird of the heavens was fled:

<sup>26</sup> I beheld, and lo, Carmel was a desert; And all its cities were destroyed, At the presence of Jehovah, At the presence of the burning of his wrath.

<sup>27</sup> For thus saith Jehovah, Laid waste shall be the whole land; But an end will I not make.

<sup>28</sup> For this mourn shall the land, And black shall become the heavens above; For I have spoken, I have purposed, And will not repent nor be turned from this.

<sup>29</sup> At the voice of the horseman and of the bowmen, Flee shall the whole city; They shall penetrate into thick clouds, They shall ascend into rocks; Every city shall be forsaken, And no man shall dwell in them. (1:245)

<sup>30</sup> And thou, wretched one, what wilt thou do? Though thou puttest on crimson, Though thou deckest thyself with ornaments of gold, Though thou adornest with paint thine eyes, In vain wilt thou decorate thyself; Hate thee will thy lovers, Thy life will they seek.

<sup>31</sup> Surely, the voice of one in travail have I heard, The distress as of one giving birth to a first-begotten, The voice of the daughter of Sion; Who mourns, who spreads her hands, — “Wo to me now! for fainted has my soul On account of murderers.” (1:251)

<!-- PAGE 1143 -->

<sup>1</sup> Go round through the streets of Jerusalem, And see, I pray, and know, Inquire also in its cross-ways, Whether ye shall find a man, Whether there be any, who doeth judgment, Who seeketh the truth, And I will spare it. (1:252)

<sup>2</sup> Though they say, “Live does Jehovah;” Yet in this they swear falsely.

<sup>3</sup> Jehovah! are not thine eyes on the truth? Thou hast smitten them, But they have not grieved; Thou hast consumed them, But they have not received correction: They have made their faces harder than a rock, They have refused to return.

<sup>4</sup> But I said, Surely the poor are these; They have acted foolishly, Because they knew not the way of Jehovah, The judgment of their God:

<sup>5</sup> I will go to the great and speak to them, For they know the way of Jehovah, The judgment of their God: But these have altogether broken the yoke, They have burst the bonds.

<sup>6</sup> Therefore smite them shall the lion from the forest, The wolf of the desert shall spoil them, The leopard shall watch over their cities; Whosoever goeth out shall be torn: For manifold are their transgressions, Increased are their defections.

<sup>7</sup> How for this shall I spare thee? Thy children have forsaken me, And have sworn by one that is not a god; When I fully satisfied them, they committed adultery, And at the house of the harlot they assembled:

<sup>8</sup> Fed horses! rising early in the morning, Every one at his neighbor’s wife neigheth.

<sup>9</sup> Should I not for these things visit? saith Jehovah, And on such a nation as this Shall not my soul be avenged?

<sup>10</sup> Ascend her walls and demolish, But an end make not; Take away her foundations, For they are not Jehovah’s:

<!-- PAGE 1144 -->

<sup>11</sup> For by transgressing they have transgressed against me, The house of Israel and the house of Judah saith Jehovah.

<sup>12</sup> They have denied Jehovah and said, “He is not, and come on us shall no evil; Yea, the sword and the famine we shall not see:

<sup>13</sup> And the prophets shall be wind, For the word is not in them: Thus shall it be done to them.”

<sup>14</sup> Therefore, thus saith Jehovah, the God of hosts, — Because you have uttered this word, Behold, I will make my words in thy mouth fire, And this people wood, And it shall devour them:

<sup>15</sup> Behold I will bring on you a nation from far, O house of Israel, saith Jehovah, A strong nation, an ancient nation, A nation whose language thou knowest not, And understandest not what it says: (1:286)

<sup>16</sup> Their quiver *is* like an open sepulcher, All of them *are* valiant;

<sup>17</sup> And they will devour thy harvest and thy bread; They will devour thy sons and thy daughters, They will devour thy flocks and thy herds, They will devour thy vine and thy fig-tree; To want shall they reduce thy fortified cities, To which thou trustest, by the sword:

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>18</sup> But even in those days, saith Jehovah, I will not make with you an end.</p>

<sup>19</sup> And it shall be, when ye say, “Why hath Jehovah our God done to us All these things?” that thou wilt say to them, — As ye have forsaken me, And served foreign gods in your land, So shall ye serve foreigners in a land not your own.

<sup>20</sup> Declare this in the house of Jacob, And publish it in Judah, saying, — <sup>21</sup> Hear this, I pray, Ye foolish people and void of heart, Who have eyes and see not, Who have ears and hear not:

<!-- PAGE 1145 -->

<sup>22</sup> Will ye not fear me? saith Jehovah; Will ye not at my presence tremble? Who have set the sand a bound to the sea, By a perpetual decree that it cannot pass it; Though its waves rage, yet they cannot prevail; Though they roar, yet they cannot pass over it.

<sup>23</sup> But this people hath a perverse and rebellious heart; They have turned aside and departed:

<sup>24</sup> And they have not said in their heart, — Let us now fear Jehovah our God, Who giveth rain, both the early And the latter shower in its season, Who keeps to us the appointed weeks of harvest.

<sup>25</sup> Your iniquities have prevented these things, And your sins have restrained good from you.

<sup>26</sup> For found among my people are the wicked; They look, as though they would set snares; A trap they set, in which they catch men. (1:303)

<sup>27</sup> As a cage is full of birds So their houses are full of fraud: Therefore they are increased and become rich;

<sup>28</sup> They are become fat, they shine; They even surpass the deeds of the wicked: The cause they judge not, The cause of the fatherless, yet they prosper; And the judgment of the poor they judge not. (1:306)

<sup>29</sup> Shall I not for this visit, saith Jehovah? On such a nation as this Shall not my soul be avenged?

<sup>30</sup> A monstrosity and baseness is in the land!

<sup>31</sup> The prophets prophesy falsely, And the priests rule by their means; (1:309) And my people wish it to be so: But what will ye do at the end of it?

<!-- PAGE 1146 -->

<sup>1</sup> Be assembled, ye children of Benjamin, From the midst of Jerusalem, And in Tekoa sound the trumpet; In Beth-haccerem also set up a sign; For evil is seen from the north, And great distress.

<sup>2</sup> To a quiet and delicate *woman* Have I likened the daughter of Sion:

<sup>3</sup> To her shall come shepherds and their flocks; They shall pitch their tents near her around, Feed shall each in his own place.

<sup>4</sup> Prepare ye war against her: “Arise ye, and let us ascend at midday; Alas for us! for declined has the day, For extended are the evening shadows:

<sup>5</sup> Arise, and let us ascend in the night, And let us demolish her palaces.” (1:319)

<sup>6</sup> For thus saith Jehovah of hosts, — Cut ye down wood, And form against. Jerusalem a mound; It is a city of visitation, Entire oppression is in the midst of her:

<sup>7</sup> As a fountain casts out its waters, So she casts out her wickedness; Violence and plunder is heard of in her; Before me continually is grief and smiting.

<sup>8</sup> Be thou instructed, O Jerusalem, Lest my soul be torn from thee, Lest I make thee a desert, A land not inhabited.

<sup>9</sup> Thus saith Jehovah of hosts, — By gleaning they shall glean, as a vine, The remnant of Israel: Turn back thine hand, As a grapegatherer into the baskets. (1:326)

<sup>10</sup> To whom shall I speak and protest, That they may hear? Behold, uncircumcised is their ear, And they cannot hear; Behold, the word of Jehovah *is* to them a reproach; They delight not in it.

<!-- PAGE 1147 -->

<sup>11</sup> Of Jehovah’s indignation therefore am I full, I am wearied with refraining To pour it on the children in the streets, And on the assembly of young men also; For the husband with his wife shall be taken, The aged with the full of days:

<sup>12</sup> And turned shall be their houses to aliens, Their fields and their wives in like manner; For I will stretch out my hand On the inhabitants of the land, saith Jehovah:

<sup>13</sup> For from the least to the greatest of them, Every one is given to covetousness; And from the prophet to the priest, Every one acts deceitfully;

<sup>14</sup> And healed have they the wound Of the daughter of my people slightly By saying, “Peace, peace,” when there was no peace. (1:336)

<sup>15</sup> Were they ashamed, that they had done abomination? Even of shame they were not ashamed, And how to blush they knew not: Fall therefore shall they among the fallen; At the time of their visitation They shall perish, saith Jehovah. (1:339)

<sup>16</sup> Thus said Jehovah, — Stand in the ways and see, Inquire also concerning the old paths, Which is the right way, and walk ye in it, That ye may find rest to your soul: And they said, “We will not walk *in it*.”

<sup>17</sup> I also set over them watchmen, That they might, attend to the sound of the trumpet; But they said, “We will not attend.”

<sup>44</sup> Behold, as a lion from the swelling of Jordan Will he ascend to the strong habitation: After having made him to rest, I will make him flee from her; And who is the chosen one whom I shall set over her? For who is as I am? And who will protest against me? And who is the shepherd that before me will stand?

<sup>45</sup> Therefore hear ye the counsel of Jehovah, Which he hath counseled against Babylon; And his thoughts which he has thought Against the land of the Chaldeans: Surely draw them forth shall the least of the flock, Surely destroyed over them shall be their dwellings.

<sup>46</sup> At the sound of Babylon being taken, tremble shall the earth, And a cry among the nations shall be heard.

<sup>1</sup> Thus saith Jehovah, — Behold, I will raise a destroying wind against Babylon, And against boasters of wisdom, my adversaries;

<sup>2</sup> And I will send against Babylon winnowers, And they shall winnow her and empty her land; For they shall be against her around in the day of evil.

<sup>3</sup> As to him who bends the bow, And him who raises himself up in his coat of mail, — Spare ye not her young men, Destroy all her army

<p style="text-align:center" markdown="1"><sup>4</sup> And fall shall they wounded, in her land, And pierced through in her streets.</p>

<sup>5</sup> For not widowed is Israel, nor Judah By his God, by Jehovah of hosts: But rather their land is filled with sin On account of the Holy One of Israel.

<sup>6</sup> Flee ye from the midst of Babylon, And save ye, every one his life, Lest ye perish in her iniquity; For it is the time of Jehovah’s vengeance, A reward will he render to her <sup>7</sup> A golden cup has Babylon been In Jehovah’s hand, inebriating the whole earth; From her wine have the nations drunk, Therefore have the nations become mad.

<sup>8</sup> Suddenly has Babylon fallen, and is broken; Howl ye for her, take rosin for her wound, It may be that she can be healed!

<sup>9</sup> We have tried to heal Babylon, But she was not healed; Leave her, and let us depart, Every one to his own land; For to the heavens has reached her judgment And has risen up to the clouds.

<sup>10</sup> Brought forth has Jehovah our righteousness; Come ye and let us declare in Sion The work of Jehovah our God.

<sup>11</sup> Polish the arrows, prepare the shields, Rouse will Jehovah the spirit of the kings of Media; For as to Babylon his thought is to destroy her. For it is Jehovah’s vengeance, the vengeance of his temple.

<sup>12</sup> On the wails of Babylon raise the standard, Increase the watch, set the watchmen, Set in order the ambushes: For as Jehovah has thought, so will he do What he hath spoken concerning the inhabitants of Babylon.

<sup>13</sup> Thou that dwellest among great waters, Who aboundest in treasures, Come is thine end, the measure of thy cupidity.

<sup>14</sup> Sworn hath Jehovah by himself, — Surely I will fill thee with men as with locusts, Who will shout over thee with the vintageshouting, — <sup>15</sup> *(Even)* he who made the earth by his power, Who hath constituted the world by his wisdom, And by his knowledge extended the heavens, <sup>16</sup> At whose voice there is abundance of waters in the heavens, Who raises vapors from the extremity of the earth, Who makes the lightnings and the rain, And brings out the wind from his treasures.

<sup>17</sup> Infatuated is every man become by his knowledge, Put to shame shall be every founder by the graven image, For a lie is the molten image, And there is no breath in them:

<sup>18</sup> Vanity are they, the work of illusions; At the time of their visitation they shall perish.

<sup>19</sup> Not like these is the portion of Jacob; For the former of all things is He, And the rod of his inheritance is Israel; Jehovah of hosts is his name.

<sup>20</sup> A hammer hast thou been to me, weapons of war; And by thee have I broken nations in pieces, And by thee destroyed kingdoms:

<sup>21</sup> Yea, by thee have I broken in pieces The horses and their riders, And by thee have I broken in pieces The chariots and their riders<sup>2</sup> <sup>22</sup> And by thee have I broken in pieces Men and women, And by thee have I broken in pieces Old men and children, And by thee have I broken in pieces Young men and maidens, <sup>23</sup> And by thee have I broken in pieces The shepherds and their flocks, And by thee have I broken in pieces The husbandmen and their yokes of oxen, And by thee have I broken in pieces The captains and the rulers.

<sup>24</sup> But I will render to Babylon And to all the inhabitants of Chaldea, All the evils which they have done in Sion, Before your eyes, saith Jehovah.

<sup>25</sup> Behold, I am against thee, O destroying mountain, saith Jehovah, Which destroyest all the earth; And I will extend my hand over thee, And will roll thee down from the rocks, And will make thee a burnt mountain:

<sup>26</sup> And they shall not take of thee a stone for a corner, Nor a stone for foundations; For perpetual wastes shalt thou be, saith Jehovah.

<sup>27</sup> Raise a banner in the land, Sound a trumpet among the nations, Prepare the nations against her, Assemble against her the kingdoms of Ararat, Of Minni and of Ashkenaz; Set up against her a leader, Bring forth the horse as the horrible locust;

<sup>28</sup> Prepare against her the nations, The kings of Media, her captains and her princes, And all the land of its dominion.

<sup>29</sup> And tremble shall the land and be in pain, For confirmed as to Babylon shall be the thoughts of Jehovah: To set the land of Babylon a waste, So as to have no inhabitant.

<sup>30</sup> Ceased have the valiant men of Babylon to fight, They sat down in their fortresses; Fail did their valor, they became women, Burnt were her dwellings, broken her bars.

<sup>31</sup> A runner to meet a runner ran, And a messenger to meet a messenger, To announce to the king of Babylon That taken was the city at its extremity;

<sup>32</sup> And the fords were taken, And the pools were burnt with fire, And the men of war were broken in pieces.

<sup>33</sup> For thus saith Jehovah of hosts, the God of Israel, — The daughter of Babylon *shall be* like a threshing-floor, *(Come will)* the time of treading her; Yet a little while and come will her harvest.

<sup>34</sup> Devoured me, broken me in pieces, Hath Nebuchadnezzar, the king of Babylon; He hath set me an empty vessel, He hath swallowed me like a dragon, He hath filled his belly with my delicacies, He hath cast me off.

<sup>35</sup> My plunder and my flesh be on Babylon, Shall the inhabitant of Sion say; My blood be on the inhabitants of Chaldea, Shall Jerusalem say.

<sup>36</sup> Therefore thus saith Jehovah, — Behold, I will plead thy cause, And avenge thy vengeance, For I will make dry her sea, And will dry up her fountain:

<sup>37</sup> And Babylon shall become heaps An habitation of dragons, A wonder and a hissing, without an inhabitant.

<sup>38</sup> They will roar as lions, They will roar as whelps of lions.

<sup>39</sup> In their heat will I make their feasts, And make them drunk, that they may exult And sleep a perpetual sleep, And not awake, saith Jehovah.

<sup>40</sup> I will bring them as lambs to the slaughter, As rams and hegoats.

<sup>41</sup> How has Sheshach been taken! And captured, the praise of the whole land! How has Babylon become a waste among nations!

<sup>42</sup> Come up over Babylon has the sea, With the multitude of its waves is she covered.

<sup>43</sup> Her cities shall be a waste, And a land of desert, and a land of drought, Pass through it shall no man, And dwell in it shall no son of man.

<sup>44</sup> And I will visit Bel in Babylon; And I will draw what he hath swallowed out of his month: And flow to him together shall nations no more: Even the wall of Babylon hath fallen.

<sup>45</sup> Go out from the midst of her, my people, And save ye, every one his life, From the indignation of Jehovah’s wrath.

<sup>46</sup> And let not faint be your heart, And fear ye not the rumor heard in the land; Come in one year shall a rumor, And afterwards in another year, a rumor; — And violence *shall be* in the land, And a ruler after a ruler.

<sup>47</sup> Therefore, behold, the days are coming, That I will visit the images of Babylon; And her whole land shall be ashamed, And all her slain, they shall fall in the midst of her.

<sup>48</sup> And rejoice over Babylon shall heaven and earth, And all the things that are in them; When from the north shall come to her Destroyers, saith Jehovah.

<sup>49</sup> As Babylon made to fall the slain of Israel, So for Babylon they shall fall, the slain of all the land.

<sup>50</sup> Ye who have escaped from the sword, Depart, stand not still; From afar remember Jehovah, And let Jerusalem come to your minds.

<sup>51</sup> We are ashamed, because we have heard reproach; Shame hath covered our faces, because strangers came Into the sanctuaries of the house of Jehovah.

<sup>52</sup> Therefore, behold, the days are coming, saith Jehovah, That I will visit her graven images, And in the whole land groan shall the wounded.

<sup>53</sup> Though Babylon ascended into heaven, And though in the height she fortified her strength, From me would come to her wasters, saith Jehovah.

<!-- PAGE 1265 -->

<sup>54</sup> The voice of a cry from Babylon! And a great crashing from the land of the Chaldeans!

<sup>55</sup> For Jehovah will lay waste Babylon, And will destroy from her the voice of boasting: And sound shall their waves like great waters, Sent forth shall be the sound of their voice.

<sup>56</sup> For come to her, to Babylon, is a waster, And taken are her valiant men, Broken is their bow; For the God of retributions is Jehovah, Recompensing he will recompense.

<sup>57</sup> And I will inebriate her princes and her wise men, And her captains, and her rulers, and her valiant men: And they shall sleep a perpetual sleep, And shall not awake, saith the King, Whose name is Jehovah of hosts.

<sup>58</sup> Thus saith Jehovah of hosts, — The wall of Babylon, *though* wide, Shall *yet* surely be demolished; And her gates, *though* high, Shall *yet* be consumed with fire: Thus labored have the people for nothing, And the nations for the fire, and wearied themselves.
