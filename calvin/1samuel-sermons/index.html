About this text. Calvin preached on 1 Samuel in French; the sermons were taken down by a scribe, but the French text has not survived. What survives is the Latin translation by David Claudus, Homiliae in I. librum Samuelis. Ex Gallicis Latinae factae (Geneva: Gabriel Cartier, 1604), 107 homilies. The text given here is that of the reprint in Calvini Opera vols. XXIX–XXX (Corpus Reformatorum 57–58, Brunswick 1885–86), read from the scans in the University of Geneva open archive; the column numbers of that edition are marked in the margin. The editors' footnotes are given at the end of each homily. The Opera text was recognised by OCR and checked word by word against a second, independent OCR reading; 17944 words were corrected in all, 1527 of them read directly from the page images where the two readings and the lexicon could not decide; a further 951 such words were found on the page images to be printed exactly so (including misprints of the 1604 edition) and are kept. 558 words remain as the scan was read, where the image could not settle them. The indexes are not included.

文本说明。加尔文用法文讲撒母耳记上，由书记员当场记录，但法文原稿已经失传。现存的是 David Claudus 的拉丁文译本《Homiliae in I. librum Samuelis. Ex Gallicis Latinae factae》（日内瓦：Gabriel Cartier，1604），共 107 篇。本站正文据《加尔文全集》（Calvini Opera）第 29–30 卷（Corpus Reformatorum 第 57–58 卷，不伦瑞克 1885–86）的重排本，用日内瓦大学开放档案的扫描件识读；页边标的是全集本的栏码。全集编者的脚注排在各篇之末。正文由 OCR 识读，并与另一份独立的 OCR 逐词对照，共改正 17944 词，其中两份读法与词典都判不了、对照页图读定的 1527 词；另有 951 词看图确认原书就是这样印的（含 1604 年版的印错），照录；仍有 558 词看图也定不下来，暂照扫描件读出的样子录入。全集编者导言见卷首。卷末索引暂未收录。
