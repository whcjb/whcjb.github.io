About this text. Calvin preached these twenty-two sermons on Psalm 119 in Geneva, one on each eight-verse stanza of the Hebrew alphabet; they were printed in French in 1554 and put into English by Thomas Stocker (London, 1580). The text given here is the edition of Old Paths Publications (1996) as issued in the AGES Digital Library: Stocker's translation complete and unabridged, but with the spelling modernised and short explanations of obsolete words added by that edition in square brackets and italics. Names such as Esay and Moyses, the verb endings in -eth, and the capitalisation and punctuation of 1580 are kept. The marginal Scripture references of 1580 appear in parentheses in the text. The 1996 publisher's note and foreword are not included.

文本说明。这 22 篇讲章是加尔文在日内瓦讲诗篇 119 篇所留，按希伯来字母每八节讲一篇；1554 年以法文出版，1580 年由 Thomas Stocker 译成英文（伦敦）。本站正文据 AGES 数字图书馆所收 Old Paths Publications 1996 年版：Stocker 译文全本未删，但拼法改为现代拼法，古词后加了方括号斜体的释义（为该版所加）。Esay、Moyses 等人名，-eth 动词词尾，以及 1580 年的大小写与标点照旧。1580 年版页边的经文出处排在正文括号里。1996 年版的出版者说明和序言未收。
