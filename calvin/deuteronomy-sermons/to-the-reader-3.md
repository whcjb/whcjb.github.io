<p class="cs-head" markdown="1"><span class="cs-pb" data-facs="676"></span>To the Reader.</p>

<span class="cs-dropcap">T</span>Ouching the vse and vnderstanding of this table, this briefe aduertisement is to be marked; that the first number directeth thee to the page or side of the leafe. The letter (a) leadeth thee to the first columne or partition of the page; the letter (b) to the second. The other number noteth the place, where the matter is conteined within the page, as it is diuided by Decads or Tens; from 10. to 60. This I thought not vnnecessarie to recorde, hauing forgotten to place it in the title of the table.

<p class="cs-closer" markdown="1">FINIS propositi, laus Christo nescia FINIS.</p>
