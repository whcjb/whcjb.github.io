About this text. The text is the keyed transcription of the 1583 London edition (EEBO-TCP A17698, STC 4442; released under CC0), in Arthur Golding's English translation from the French. The spelling is that of the original; only the long s has been printed as s. Places the transcribers could not read on the microfilm are shown in red: 1741 letters, 288 words and 324 longer passages. The copy from which the transcription was made lacks two pages in 28 places; each is marked where it falls. Nine of these gaps fall where one sermon ends and the next begins, so that the heading and opening of the second sermon are lost and the remainder runs on in the page of the first; those sermons are listed above in grey and lead to the page that carries them. Two headings are misnumbered in the original (the second “Lxx.”, which by its date and place is Sermon 73) and are printed as they stand. The indexes at the end of the volume are not yet included.

文本说明。本书正文据 1583 年伦敦版的人工录入本（EEBO-TCP A17698，STC 4442，CC0 公共领域授权），即戈尔丁（Arthur Golding）自法文译出的英译本。拼法照录原书，只把长 s（ſ）印作 s。录入者在胶片上读不出的地方以红色记号标出：缺字母 1741 个、缺整词 288 处、缺一段以上 324 处；尚未据其他扫描本补全。录入所据的胶片本有 28 处各缺两页，均在原处注明。其中 9 处缺页正好跨在两篇讲章之间，后一篇的标题与开头随之散失，残存正文接在前一篇页内：第 39 篇（并在第 38 篇页内）、第 48 篇（并在第 47 篇页内）、第 61 篇（并在第 60 篇页内）、第 67 篇（并在第 66 篇页内）、第 84 篇（并在第 83 篇页内）、第 107 篇（并在第 106 篇页内）、第 111 篇（并在第 110 篇页内）、第 117 篇（并在第 116 篇页内）、第 157 篇（并在第 156 篇页内）；目录中以灰色标出，点开即进所在页。原书有一篇标题篇次印错（第二个「Lxx.」，按日期与次序实为第 73 篇），标题照录。卷末索引暂未收录。
