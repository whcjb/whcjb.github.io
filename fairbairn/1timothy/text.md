<h3>CHAPTER I</h3>
<div class="fb-gk" lang="grc"><sup class="fb-vn">1</sup> Παῦλος ἀπόστολος Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ κατ ʼ ἐπιταγὴν Θεοῦ σωτῆρος ἡμῶν καὶ Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ τῆς ἐλπίδος ἡμῶν <sup class="fb-vn">2</sup> Τιμοθέῳ γνησίῳ τέκνῳ ἐν πίστει , χάρις ἔλεος εἰρήνη ἀπὸ θεοῦ πατρὸς καὶ Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ τοῦ κυρίου ἡμῶν . <sup class="fb-vn">3</sup> Καθὼς παρεκάλεσά σε προσμεῖναι ἐν Ἐφέσῳ πορευόμενος εἰς Μακεδονίαν , ἵνα παραγγείλῃς τισὶν μὴ ἑτεροδιδασκαλεῖν <sup class="fb-vn">4</sup> μηδὲ προσέχειν μύθοις καὶ γενεαλογίαις ἀπεράντοις , αἵτινες ἐκζητήσεις παρέχουσιν μᾶλλον ἢ οἰκονομίαν Θεοῦ τὴν ἐν πίστει . <sup class="fb-vn">5</sup> τὸ δὲ τέλος τῆς παραγγελίας ἐστὶν ἀγάπη ἐκ καθαρᾶς καρδίας καὶ συνειδήσεως ἀγαθῆς καὶ πίστεως ἀνυποκρίτου , <sup class="fb-vn">6</sup> ὧν τινες ἀστοχήσαντες ἐξετράπησαν εἰς ματαιολογίαν <sup class="fb-vn">7</sup> θέλοντες εἶναι νομοδιδάσκαλοι , μὴ νοοῦντες μήτε ἃ λέγουσιν μήτε περὶ τίνων διαβεβαιοῦνται . <sup class="fb-vn">8</sup> οἴδαμεν δὲ ὅτι καλὸς ὁ νόμος , ἐάν τις αὐτῷ νομίμως χρῆται , <sup class="fb-vn">9</sup> εἰδὼς τοῦτο , ὅτι δικαίῳ νόμος οὐ κεῖται , ἀνόμοις δὲ καὶ ἀνυποτάκτοις , ἀσεβέσι καὶ ἁμαρτωλοῖς , ἀνοσίοις καὶ βεβήλοις , πατρολῴαις καὶ μητρολῴαις , ἀνδροφόνοις <sup class="fb-vn">10</sup> πόρνοις ἀρσενοκοίταις ἀνδραποδισταῖς ψεύσταις ἐπιόρκοις , καὶ εἴ τι ἕτερον τῇ ὑγιαινούσῃ διδασκαλίᾳ ἀντίκειται <sup class="fb-vn">11</sup> κατὰ τὸ εὐαγγέλιον τῆς δόξης τοῦ μακαρίου Θεοῦ , ὃ ἐπιστεύθην ἐγώ . <sup class="fb-vn">12</sup> χάριν ἔχω τῷ ἐνδυναμώσαντί με Χριστῷ Ἰησοῦ τῷ κυρίῳ ἡμῶν , ὅτι πιστόν με ἡγήσατο θέμενος εἰς διακονίαν <sup class="fb-vn">13</sup> τὸ πρότερον ὄντα βλάσφημον καὶ διώκτην καὶ ὑβριστήν , ἀλλὰ ἠλεήθην , ὅτι ἀγνοῶν ἐποίησα ἐν ἀπιστίᾳ · <sup class="fb-vn">14</sup> ὑπερεπλεόνασεν δὲ ἡ χάρις τοῦ κυρίου ἡμῶν μετὰ πίστεως καὶ ἀγάπης τῆς ἐν Χριστῷ Ἰησοῦ. <sup class="fb-vn">15</sup> πιστὸς ὁ λόγος καὶ πάσης ἀποδοχῆς ἄξιος, ὅτι Χριστὸς Ἰησοῦς ἦλθεν εἰς τὸν κόσμον ἁμαρτωλοὺς σῶσαι, ὧν πρῶτός εἰμι ἐγώ. <sup class="fb-vn">16</sup> ἀλλὰ διὰ τοῦτο ἠλεήθην, ἵνα ἐν ἐμοὶ πρώτῳ ἐνδείξηται Ἰησοῦς Χριστὸς τὴν ἅπασαν μακροθυμίαν πρὸς ὑποτύπωσιν τῶν μελλόντων πιστεύειν ἐπʼ αὐτῷ εἰς ζωὴν αἰώνιον. <sup class="fb-vn">17</sup> τῷ δὲ βασιλεῖ τῶν αἰώνων, ἀφθάρτῳ ἀοράτῳ μόνῳ Θεῷ, τιμὴ καὶ δόξα εἰς τοὺς αἰῶνας τῶν αἰώνων, ἀμήν. <sup class="fb-vn">18</sup> Ταύτην τὴν παραγγελίαν παρατίθεμαί σοι, τέκνον Τιμόθεε, κατὰ τὰς προαγούσας ἐπὶ σὲ προφητείας, ἵνα στρατεύῃ ἐν αὐταῖς τὴν καλὴν στρατείαν <sup class="fb-vn">19</sup> ἔχων πίστιν καὶ ἀγαθὴν συνείδησιν, ἥν τινες ἀπωσάμενοι περὶ τὴν πίστιν ἐναυάγησαν, <sup class="fb-vn">20</sup> ὧν ἐστιν Ὑμέναιος καὶ Ἀλέξανδρος, οὓς παρέδωκα τῷ σατανᾷ , ἵνα παιδευθῶσιν μὴ βλασφημεῖν.</div>
<div class="fb-en"><sup class="fb-vn">1</sup> I Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus, according to the commandment of God our Saviour, and Christ Jesus our hope; <sup class="fb-vn">2</sup> to Timothy, [my] true child in the faith: Grace, mercy, peace from God the Father, and Christ Jesus our Lord. <sup class="fb-vn">3</sup> According as I besought thee when setting out for Macedonia, [so I do now], to abide still at Ephesus, in order that thou mightest charge some not to teach any other doctrine; <sup class="fb-vn">4</sup> nor to give heed to fables, and endless genealogies, inasmuch as they minister strifes rather than God’s dispensation that is in faith. <sup class="fb-vn">5</sup> Now the end of the charge is love out of a pure heart, and a good conscience, and faith unfeigned: <sup class="fb-vn">6</sup> from which some having swerved, they turned aside into vain talk; <sup class="fb-vn">7</sup> desiring to be teachers of the law, without understanding either what things they speak, or concerning what things they affirm. <sup class="fb-vn">8</sup> We know, indeed, that the law is good, if one use it lawfully; <sup class="fb-vn">9</sup> knowing this, that the law is not made for a righteous person, but for the lawless and unruly, for the ungodly and sinful, the unholy and profane, for smiters of fathers and smiters of mothers, for murderers, <sup class="fb-vn">10</sup> for fornicators, abusers of themselves with mankind, men-stealers, liars, perjured persons, and if there be anything else that is contrary to the sound instruction; <sup class="fb-vn">11</sup> according to the gospel of the glory of the blessed God, which was committed to my trust. <sup class="fb-vn">12</sup> I give thanks to Christ Jesus our Lord, who hath strengthened me, for that he reckoned me faithful, appointing me to the service [of the ministry]; <sup class="fb-vn">13</sup> though I was formerly a blasphemer, a persecutor, and outrageous: but I obtained mercy, because I did it ignorantly in unbelief. <sup class="fb-vn">14</sup> But the grace of our Lord superabounded with faith and love that are in Christ Jesus. <sup class="fb-vn">15</sup> Faithful is the word, and worthy of all acceptation, that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am chief. <sup class="fb-vn">16</sup> Howbeit for this cause I obtained mercy, in order that in me first Jesus Christ might show forth all long-suffering, for a pattern to those who are going to believe on him to life everlasting. <sup class="fb-vn">1</sup> <sup class="fb-vn">7</sup> Now to the King of the ages, the incorruptible, invisible, only God, be honour and glory, for ages of ages (or, for ever and ever). Amen. <sup class="fb-vn">18</sup> This charge I commit to thee, child Timothy, according to the prophecies which went before on thee, in order that in them thou mayest war the good warfare; <sup class="fb-vn">19</sup> holding faith and a good conscience, which some having thrust away, concerning faith made shipwreck: <sup class="fb-vn">20</sup> of whom is Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I delivered over to Satan, that they might learn not to blaspheme.</div>
</div>

<h3>CHAPTER II</h3>
<div class="fb-gk" lang="grc"><sup class="fb-vn">1</sup> Παρακαλῶ οὖν πρῶτον πάντων ποιεῖσθαι δεήσεις προσευχὰς ἐντεύξεις εὐχαριστίας ὑπὲρ πάντων ἀνθρώπων, <sup class="fb-vn">2</sup> ὑπὲρ βασιλέων καὶ πάντων τῶν ἐν ὑπεροχῇ ὄντων, ἵνα ἤρεμον καὶ ἡσύχιον βίον διάγωμεν ἐν πάσῃ εὐσεβείᾳ καὶ σεμνότητι. <sup class="fb-vn">3</sup> τοῦτο καλὸν καὶ ἀπόδεκτον ἐνώπιον τοῦ σωτῆρος ἡμῶν Θεοῦ , <sup class="fb-vn">4</sup> ὃς πάντας ἀνθρώπους θέλει σωθῆναι καὶ εἰς ἐπίγνωσιν ἀληθείας ἐλθεῖν. <sup class="fb-vn">5</sup> εἷς γὰρ Θεός, εἷς καὶ μεσίτης Θεοῦ καὶ ἀνθρώπων, ἄνθρωπος Χριστὸς Ἰησοῦς, <sup class="fb-vn">6</sup> ὁ δοὺς ἑαυτὸν ἀντίλυτρον ὑπὲρ πάντων, τὸ μαρτύριον καιροῖς ἰδίοις. <sup class="fb-vn">7</sup> εἰς ὃ ἐτέθην ἐγὼ κῆρυξ καὶ ἀπόστολος, ἀλήθειαν λέγω οὐ ψεύδομαι, διδάσκαλος ἐθνῶν ἐν πίστει καὶ ἀληθείᾳ . <sup class="fb-vn">8</sup> Βούλομαι οὖν προσεύχεσθαι τοὺς ἄνδρας ἐν παντὶ τόπῳ ἐπαίροντας ὁσίους χεῖρας χωρὶς ὀργῆς καὶ διαλογισμοῦ · <sup class="fb-vn">9</sup> ὡσαύτως καὶ γυναῖκας ἐν καταστολῇ κοσμίῳ μετὰ αἰδοῦς καὶ σωφροσύνης κοσμεῖν ἑαυτάς, μὴ ἐν πλέγμασιν καὶ χρυσίῳ ἢ μαργαρίταις ἢ ἱματισμῷ πολυτελεῖ , <sup class="fb-vn">10</sup> ἀλλʼ ὃ πρέπει γυναιξὶν ἐπαγγελλομέναις θεοσέβειαν, διʼ ἔργων ἀγαθῶν. <sup class="fb-vn">11</sup> Γυνὴ ἐν ἡσυχίᾳ μανθανέτω ἐν πάσῃ ὑποταγῇ · <sup class="fb-vn">12</sup> διδάσκειν δὲ γυναικὶ οὐκ ἐπιτρέπω οὐδὲ αὐθεντεῖν ἀνδρός , ἀλλ ʼ εἶναι ἐν ἡσυχίᾳ . <sup class="fb-vn">13</sup> Ἀδὰμ γὰρ πρῶτος ἐπλάσθη , εἶτα Εὕα . <sup class="fb-vn">14</sup> καὶ Ἀδὰμ οὐκ ἠπατήθη , ἡ δὲ γυνὴ ἐξαπατηθεῖσα ἐν παραβάσει γέγονεν, <sup class="fb-vn">1</sup> <sup class="fb-vn">5</sup> σωθήσεται δὲ διὰ τῆς τεκνογονίας , ἐὰν μείνωσιν ἐν πίστει καὶ ἀγάπῃ καὶ ἁγιασμῷ μετὰ σωφροσύνης ·</div>
<div class="fb-en"><sup class="fb-vn">1</sup> I exhort then, first of all, that petitions, prayers, supplications, thanksgivings, be made for all men; <sup class="fb-vn">2</sup> for kings, and all that are in authority; in order that we may pass a quiet and tranquil life in all godliness and gravity. <sup class="fb-vn">3</sup> For this is good and acceptable before our Saviour God; <sup class="fb-vn">4</sup> Who willeth all men to be saved, and to come to the full knowledge of the truth. <sup class="fb-vn">5</sup> For there is one God, one Mediator also of God and men, [a] man Christ Jesus; <sup class="fb-vn">6</sup> who gave himself a ransom for all the testimony for its own seasons. <sup class="fb-vn">7</sup> Whereunto I was appointed a herald, and an apostle (I speak the truth, I lie not), a teacher of the Gentiles in faith and truth. <sup class="fb-vn">8</sup> I wish, then, that prayer be made in every place by men, lifting up holy hands, without wrath and doubting. <sup class="fb-vn">9</sup> Likewise also, that women adorn themselves in orderly apparel, with shamefastness and discretion; not in plaitings, and gold, or pearls, or costly raiment; lo but, which becometh women professing godliness, through good works. <sup class="fb-vn">11</sup> Let a woman learn in silence, in all subjection. <sup class="fb-vn">12</sup> But I permit not a woman to teach, nor to lord it over the man, but to be in silence. <sup class="fb-vn">13</sup> For Adam was first formed, then Eve. <sup class="fb-vn">14</sup> And Adam was not deceived; but the woman, being wholly deceived, fell into transgression. <sup class="fb-vn">15</sup> But she shall be saved through the child-bearing, if they abide in faith, love, and holiness, with discretion.</div>
</div>

<h3>CHAPTER III</h3>
<div class="fb-gk" lang="grc"><sup class="fb-vn">1</sup> Πιστὸς ὁ λόγος · εἴ τις ἐπισκοπῆς ὀρέγεται , καλοῦ ἔργου ἐπιθυμεῖ . <sup class="fb-vn">2</sup> δεῖ οὖν τὸν ἐπίσκοπον ἀνεπίλημπτον εἶναι , μιᾶς γυναικὸς ἄνδρα , νηφάλιον σώφρονα κόσμιον φιλόξενον διδακτικόν , <sup class="fb-vn">3</sup> μὴ πάροινον μὴ πλήκτην, ἀλλὰ ἐπιεικῆ ἄμαχον ἀφιλάργυρον, <sup class="fb-vn">4</sup> τοῦ ἰδίου οἴκου καλῶς προϊστάμενον, τέκνα ἔχοντα ἐν ὑποταγῇ, μετὰ πάσης σεμνότητος <sup class="fb-vn">5</sup> εἰ δέ τις τοῦ ἰδίου οἴκου προστῆναι οὐκ οἶδεν, πῶς ἐκκλησίας Θεοῦ ἐπιμελήσεται; <sup class="fb-vn">6</sup> μὴ νεόφυτον, ἵνα μὴ τυφωθεὶς εἰς κρίμα ἐμπέσῃ τοῦ διαβόλου. <sup class="fb-vn">7</sup> δεῖ δὲ καὶ μαρτυρίαν καλὴν ἔχειν ἀπὸ τῶν ἔξωθεν, ἵνα μὴ εἰς ὀνειδισμὸν ἐμπέσῃ καὶ παγίδα τοῦ διαβόλου. <sup class="fb-vn">8</sup> Διακόνους ὡσαύτως σεμνούς , μὴ διλόγους , μὴ οἴνῳ πολλῷ προσέχοντας , μὴ αἰσχροκερ??Deuteronomy , <sup class="fb-vn">9</sup> ἔχοντας τὸ μυστήριον τῆς πίστεως ἐν καθαρᾷ συνειδήσει . <sup class="fb-vn">10</sup> καὶ οὗτοι δὲ δοκιμαζέσθωσαν πρῶτον, εἶτα διακονείτωσαν ἀνέγκλητοι ὄντες. <sup class="fb-vn">11</sup> γυναῖκας ὡσαύτως σεμνάς, μὴ διαβόλους, νηφαλίους, πιστὰς ἐν πᾶσιν. <sup class="fb-vn">12</sup> διάκονοι ἔστωσαν μιᾶς γυναικὸς ἄνδρες, τέκνων καλῶς προϊστάμενοι καὶ τῶν ἰδίων οἴκων. <sup class="fb-vn">13</sup> οἱ γὰρ καλῶς διακονήσαντες βαθμὸν ἑαυτοῖς καλὸν περιποιοῦνται καὶ πολλὴν παρρησίαν ἐν πίστει τῇ ἐν Χριστῷ Ἰησοῦ . <sup class="fb-vn">14</sup> Ταῦτά σοι γράφω ἐλπίζων ἐλθεῖν πρὸς σὲ ἐν τάχιον· <sup class="fb-vn">15</sup> ἐὰν δὲ βραδύνω , ἵνα εἰδῇς πῶς δεῖ ἐν οἴκῳ Θεοῦ ἀναστρέφεσθαι , ἥτις ἐστὶν ἐκκλησία Θεοῦ ζῶντος , στῦλος καὶ ἑδραίωμα τῆς ἀληθείας . <sup class="fb-vn">16</sup> καὶ ὁμολογουμένως μέγα ἐστὶν τὸ τῆς εὐσεβείας μυστήριον· ὃς ἐφανερώθη ἐν σαρκί , ἐδικαιώθη ἐν πνεύματι , ὤφθη ἀγγέλοις , ἐκηρύχθη ἐν ἔθνεσιν , ἐπιστεύθη ἐν κόσμῳ , ἀνελήμφθη ἐν δόξῃ .</div>
<div class="fb-en"><sup class="fb-vn">1</sup> Faithful is the saying. If any one seeketh the office of pastor, he desireth a good work. <sup class="fb-vn">2</sup> A pastor, then, ought to be blameless, husband of one wife, sober, discreet, orderly, hospitable, apt to teach; <sup class="fb-vn">3</sup> not a brawler, not a striker, but mild, peaceable; not a lover of money; <sup class="fb-vn">4</sup> ruling well his own house, having children in subjection with all gravity. <sup class="fb-vn">5</sup> But if one knows not how to rule his own house, how shall he take charge of the church of God? <sup class="fb-vn">6</sup> Not a novice, lest, being carried with conceit, he should fall into the condemnation of the devil. <sup class="fb-vn">7</sup> But he must also have a good testimony from those that are without, lest he fall into reproach and the snare of the devil. <sup class="fb-vn">8</sup> In like manner, [ought] the deacons to be grave, not double-tongued, not addicted to much wine, not lovers of base gain; <sup class="fb-vn">9</sup> holding the mystery of the faith in a pure conscience. <sup class="fb-vn">10</sup> And these, too, let them first be proved; then let them serve as deacons, if they be without blame. <sup class="fb-vn">11</sup> Women, in like manner, [it behoves] to be grave, not slanderers, sober, faithful in all things. <sup class="fb-vn">12</sup> Let the deacons be husbands of one wife, ruling well their children and their own houses. <sup class="fb-vn">13</sup> For those who have done the office of a deacon well obtain for themselves a good degree, and much boldness in the faith which is in Christ Jesus. <sup class="fb-vn">14</sup> These things I write to thee, hoping to come to thee shortly. <sup class="fb-vn">15</sup> But if I should tarry, in order that thou mayest know how thou oughtest to conduct thyself in God’s house, which indeed is the church of the living God, the pillar and basement of the truth. i6 And confessedly great is the mystery of godliness, who was manifested in the flesh, was justified in the Spirit, was seen by angels, was preached among the Gentiles, was believed on in the world, was received up into glory.</div>
</div>

<div class="fb-par" id="ch4">
<h3>CHAPTER IV</h3>
<div class="fb-gk" lang="grc"><sup class="fb-vn">1</sup> Τὸ δὲ πνεῦμα ῥητῶς λέγει ὅτι ἐν ὑστέροις καιροῖς ἀποστήσονταί τινες τῆς πίστεως, προσέχοντες πνεύμασιν πλάνοις καὶ διδασκαλίαις δαιμονίων, <sup class="fb-vn">2</sup> ἐν ὑποκρίσει ψευδολόγων, κεκαυστηριασμένων τὴν ἰδίαν συνείδησιν, <sup class="fb-vn">3</sup> κωλυόντων γαμεῖν, ἀπέχεσθαι βρωμάτων, ἃ ὁ Θεὸς ἔκτισεν εἰς μετάλημψιν μετὰ εὐχαριστίας τοῖς πιστοῖς καὶ ἐπεγνωκόσι τὴν ἀλήθειαν. <sup class="fb-vn">4</sup> ὅτι πᾶν κτίσμα Θεοῦ καλὸν, καὶ οὐδὲν ἀπόβλητον μετὰ εὐχαριστίας λαμβανόμενον· <sup class="fb-vn">5</sup> ἁγιάζεται γὰρ διὰ λόγου Θεοῦ καὶ ἐντεύξεως. <sup class="fb-vn">6</sup> Ταῦτα ὑποτιθέμενος τοῖς ἀδελφοῖς καλὸς ἔσῃ διάκονος Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ , ἐντρεφόμενος τοῖς λόγοις τῆς πίστεως καὶ τῆς καλῆς διδασκαλίας ᾗ παρηκολούθηκας · <sup class="fb-vn">7</sup> τοὺς δὲ βεβήλους καὶ γραώδεις μύθους παραιτοῦ . γύμναζε δὲ σεαυτὸν πρὸς εὐσέβειαν. <sup class="fb-vn">8</sup> ἡ γὰρ σωματικὴ γυμνασία πρὸς ὀλίγον ἐστὶν ὠφέλιμος· ἡ δὲ εὐσέβεια πρὸς πάντα ὠφέλιμός ἐστιν, ἐπαγγελίαν ἔχουσα ζωῆς τῆς νῦν καὶ τῆς μελλούσης . <sup class="fb-vn">9</sup> πιστὸς ὁ λόγος καὶ πάσης ἀποδοχῆς ἄξιος . <sup class="fb-vn">10</sup> εἰς τοῦτο γὰρ κοπιῶμεν καὶ ἀγωνιζόμεθα , ὅτι ἠλπίκαμεν ἐπὶ Θεῷ ζῶντι , ὅς ἐστιν σωτὴρ πάντων ἀνθρώπων, μάλιστα πιστῶν . <sup class="fb-vn">11</sup> Παράγγελλε ταῦτα καὶ δίδασκε . <sup class="fb-vn">12</sup> μηδείς σου τῆς νεότητος καταφρονείτω , ἀλλὰ τύπος γίνου τῶν πιστῶν ἐν λόγῳ , ἐν ἀναστροφῇ , ἐν ἀγάπῃ , ἐν πίστει , ἐν ἁγνείᾳ . <sup class="fb-vn">13</sup> ἕως ἔρχομαι πρόσεχε τῇ ἀναγνώσει , τῇ παρακλήσει , τῇ διδασκαλίᾳ . <sup class="fb-vn">14</sup> μὴ ἀμέλει τοῦ ἐν σοὶ χαρίσματος , ὃ ἐδόθη σοι διὰ προφητείας μετὰ ἐπιθέσεως τῶν χειρῶν τοῦ πρεσβυτερίου. <sup class="fb-vn">15</sup> ταῦτα μελέτα, ἐν τούτοις ἴσθι, ἵνα σου ἡ προκοπὴ φανερὰ ᾖ πᾶσιν. <sup class="fb-vn">16</sup> ἔπεχε σεαυτῷ καὶ τῇ διδασκαλίᾳ , ἐπίμενε αὐτοῖς· τοῦτο γὰρ ποιῶν καὶ σεαυτὸν σώσεις καὶ τοὺς ἀκούοντάς σου.</div>
<div class="fb-en"><sup class="fb-vn">1</sup> But the Spirit speaks expressly, that in after times some shall depart from the faith, giving heed to seducing spirits and teachings of demons; <sup class="fb-vn">2</sup> in hypocrisy of speakers of lies, who have had their conscience scarred; <sup class="fb-vn">3</sup> forbidding to marry, [bidding] to abstain from meats, which God made to be partaken of with thanksgiving by the faithful, and those who have the full knowledge of the truth. <sup class="fb-vn">4</sup> For everything made by God is good, and nothing to be rejected, being received with thanksgiving; <sup class="fb-vn">5</sup> for it is sanctified through the word of God and prayer. <sup class="fb-vn">6</sup> By submitting these things to the brethren, thou shalt be a good servant of Christ Jesus, nourishing thyself up in the words of the faith, and of the good instruction which thou hast diligently followed. <sup class="fb-vn">7</sup> But the profane and old-wives’ fables avoid; and rather exercise thyself unto godliness. <sup class="fb-vn">8</sup> For bodily exercise is profitable unto little; but godliness is profitable unto all things, having promise of the life that now is, and of that which is to come. <sup class="fb-vn">9</sup> Faithful is the word, and worthy of all acceptation. <sup class="fb-vn">10</sup> For to this [end] we toil and strive, because we have hoped upon the living God, who is the Saviour of all men, especially of those who believe. <sup class="fb-vn">11</sup> Charge these things, and teach. <sup class="fb-vn">12</sup> Let no one despise thy youth; <h3>CHAPTER V</h3>
<div class="fb-gk" lang="grc"><sup class="fb-vn">1</sup> Πρεσβυτέρῳ μὴ ἐπιπλήξῃς ἀλλὰ παρακάλει ὡς πατέρα , νεωτέρους ὡς ἀδελφούς , <sup class="fb-vn">2</sup> πρεσβυτέρας ὡς μητέρας , νεωτέρας ὡς class="fb-vn">10</sup> ἐν ἔργοις καλοῖς μαρτυρουμένη , εἰ ἐτεκνοτρόφησεν , εἰ ἐξενοδόχησεν , εἰ ἁγίων πόδας ἔνιψεν , εἰ θλιβομένοις ἐπήρκεσεν , εἰ παντὶ ἔργῳ ἀγαθῷ ἐπηκολούθησεν . <sup class="fb-vn">13</sup> ἅμα δὲ καὶ ἀργαὶ μανθάνουσιν περιερχόμεναι τὰς οἰκίας, οὐ μόνον δὲ ἀργαὶ ἀλλὰ καὶ φλύαροι καὶ περίεργοι, λαλοῦσαι τὰ μὴ δέοντα. <sup class="fb-vn">14</sup> βούλομαι οὖν νεωτέρας κοπιῶντες ἐν λόγῳ καὶ διδασκαλίᾳ . <sup class="fb-vn">18</sup> λέγει γὰρ ἡ γραφή, βοῦν ἀλοῶντα οὐ φιμώσεις , καί ἄξιος ὁ ἐργάτης τοῦ μισθοῦ αὐτοῦ . <sup class="fb-vn">19</sup> κατὰ πρεσβυτέρου <div class="fb-en"><sup class="fb-vn">1</sup> Reprimand not an elderly person, but exhort him as a father, younger men as brothers; <sup class="fb-vn">2</sup> elderly women as mothers; the younger as sisters, with all purity. <sup class="fb-vn">3</sup> Honour widows that are widows indeed. <sup class="fb-vn">4</sup> If, however, any widow has children or grandchildren, let them first learn to show piety in their own home, and requite their parents, for this is acceptable before God. <sup class="fb-vn">5</sup> But she who is a widow indeed, and desolate, has set her hope on God, and abides in supplications and prayers night and day. <sup class="fb-vn">6</sup> But she that lives deliciously is dead while she lives. <sup class="fb-vn">7</sup> And these things enjoin, in order that they may be without reproach. <sup class="fb-vn">8</sup> But if any one provides not for his own, and especially for those of his own house, he has denied the faith, and is worse than an unbeliever. <sup class="fb-vn">9</sup> Let a widow be enrolled who has become not less than sixty years old, wife of one Man 1:10 well reported of in respect to good works; if she brought up children; if she entertained strangers; if she washed the feet of saints; if she relieved the distressed; if she followed after every good work. <sup class="fb-vn">11</sup> But younger widows decline: for when they shall become wanton against Christ, they desire to marry; <sup class="fb-vn">12</sup> having condemnation, because they made void their first faith. <sup class="fb-vn">13</sup> Moreover, they learn also to be idle, going about from house to house; and not only idle, but tattlers also, and busybodies, speaking things which they ought not. <sup class="fb-vn">14</sup> I wish, therefore, that the younger [widows] marry, bear children, manage the house, give no occasion for reproach to the adversary. <sup class="fb-vn">15</sup> For already some have turned away after Satan. <sup class="fb-vn">16</sup> If any woman that believes hath widows, let support be given to them, and let not the church be burdened, that it may relieve those who are widows indeed. <sup class="fb-vn">17</sup> Let the elders who govern well be counted worthy of double honour, especially those who labour in word and teaching. <sup class="fb-vn">18</sup> For the Scripture saith, Thou shalt not muzzle an ox while treading out [the corn]; and, The labourer is worthy of his hire, <sup class="fb-vn">19</sup> Against an elder receive not an accusation, except it be upon two or three witnesses. <sup class="fb-vn">20</sup> Those that sin rebuke before all, in order that the rest also may have fear. <sup class="fb-vn">21</sup> I solemnly charge thee before God and Christ Jesus, and the elect angels, that thou keep these things, without prejudging, doing nothing by partiality. <sup class="fb-vn">22</sup> Lay hands on no one hastily, neither be partaker in other men’s sins. <sup class="fb-vn">23</sup> No longer drink water, but use a little wine for thy stomach’s sake, and thy frequent ailments. <sup class="fb-vn">24</sup> The sins of some men are manifest, going before to judgment; <div class="fb-en"><sup class="fb-vn">1</sup> Whoever are under the yoke as bond-servants, let them reckon their own masters worthy of all honour, that the name of God and his doctrine may not be blasphemed. <sup class="fb-vn">2</sup> But such as have believing masters, let them not despise them, because they are brethren; but the rather serve them, because they who receive the benefit are faithful and beloved.
