About this text. The text follows a modern keyed transcription (the e-Sword / BibleSupport module, from a 2008 Libronix edition, as published on StudyLight.org). In it the author's footnotes have been worked into the text in parentheses, and the appendices are also repeated under the verses that refer to them. The keyed text has been checked against a scan of the 1874 Edinburgh edition (Princeton Theological Seminary copy, Internet Archive pastoralepistle00fair), used only to restore the order of the book: three comments filed under the wrong verse have been put back in their place, the repeated appendices removed from the verse comments, and five comments missing from the keyed text are marked where they stand. The wording is that of the keyed text throughout.

文本说明。本书正文据现代人工录入本（e-Sword / BibleSupport 模块，出自 2008 年 Libronix 本，经 StudyLight 刊布）。该本把作者的页脚注改作正文里的圆括号插注，又把三篇附录整篇重复嵌在引到它们的经节之下（共 112 段）。我们拿 1874 年爱丁堡初版的扫描本（普林斯顿神学院藏本，互联网档案馆 pastoralepistle00fair）作顺序证人，只用来恢复原书的次序：挂错经节的三条注释移回原处，嵌在经节下的附录副本删去（附录另有单页），录入本整节缺失的五处注释在原位注明。字句一律照录入本，未据扫描本改动；希腊文里被拆开的重音符号已按 Unicode 合并。
