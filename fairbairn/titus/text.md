<h3>CHAPTER I</h3>
<div class="fb-gk" lang="grc"><sup class="fb-vn">1</sup> Παῦλος δοῦλος Θεοῦ , ἀπόστολος δὲ Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ κατὰ πίστιν ἐκλεκτῶν Θεοῦ καὶ ἐπίγνωσιν ἀληθείας τῆς κατ ʼ εὐσέβειαν <sup class="fb-vn">2</sup> ἐπ ʼ ἐλπίδι ζωῆς αἰωνίου , ἣν ἐπηγγείλατο ὁ ἀψευδὴς Θεὸς πρὸ χρόνων αἰωνίων , <sup class="fb-vn">3</sup> ἐφανέρωσεν δὲ καιροῖς ἰδίοις τὸν λόγον αὐτοῦ ἐν κηρύγματι , ὃ ἐπιστεύθην ἐγὼ κατ ʼ ἐπιταγὴν τοῦ σωτῆρος ἡμῶν Θεοῦ , <sup class="fb-vn">4</sup> Τίτῳ γνησίῳ τέκνῳ κατὰ κοινὴν πίστιν. χάρις καὶ εἰρήνη ἀπὸ Θεοῦ πατρὸς καὶ Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ τοῦ σωτῆρος ἡμῶν . <sup class="fb-vn">5</sup> Τούτου χάριν ἀπέλιπόν σε ἐν Κρήτῃ , ἵνα τὰ λείποντα ἐπιδιορθώσῃ καὶ καταστήσῃς κατὰ πόλιν πρεσβυτέρους , ὡς ἐγώ σοι διεταξάμην , <sup class="fb-vn">6</sup> εἴ τίς ἐστιν ἀνέγκλητος , μιᾶς γυναικὸς ἀνήρ , τέκνα ἔχων πιστά , μὴ ἐν κατηγορίᾳ ἀσωτίας ἢ ἀνυπότακτα . <sup class="fb-vn">7</sup> δεῖ γὰρ τὸν ἐπίσκοπον ἀνέγκλητον εἶναι ὡς Θεοῦ οἰκονόμον , μὴ αὐθάδη , μὴ ὀργίλον , μὴ πάροινον , μὴ πλήκτην , μὴ αἰσχροκερδῆ , <sup class="fb-vn">8</sup> ἀλλὰ φιλόξενον, φιλάγαθον, σώφρονα, δίκαιον, ὅσιον, ἐγκρατῆ , <sup class="fb-vn">9</sup> ἀντεχόμενον τοῦ κατὰ τὴν διδαχὴν πιστοῦ λόγου , ἵνα δυνατὸς ᾖ καὶ παρακαλεῖν ἐν τῇ διδασκαλίᾳ τῇ ὑγιαινούσῃ καὶ τοὺς ἀντιλέγοντας ἐλέγχειν . <sup class="fb-vn">10</sup> Εἰσὶν γὰρ πολλοὶ ἀνυπότακτοι , ματαιολόγοι καὶ φρεναπάται , μάλιστα οἱ ἐκ τῆς περιτομῆς , <sup class="fb-vn">11</sup> οὓς δεῖ ἐπιστομίζειν , οἵτινες ὅλους οἴκους ἀνατρέπουσιν διδάσκοντες ἃ μὴ δεῖ αἰσχροῦ κέρδους χάριν . <sup class="fb-vn">12</sup> εἶπέν τις ἐξ αὐτῶν ἴδιος αὐτῶν προφήτης , Κρῆτες ἀεὶ ψεῦσται , κακὰ θηρία , γαστέρες ἀργαί . <sup class="fb-vn">13</sup> ἡ μαρτυρία αὕτη ἐστὶν ἀληθής . δι ʼ ἣν αἰτίαν ἔλεγχε αὐτοὺς ἀποτόμως , ἵνα ὑγιαίνωσιν ἐν τῇ πίστει , <sup class="fb-vn">14</sup> μὴ προσέχοντες Ἰουδαϊκοῖς μύθοις καὶ ἐντολαῖς ἀνθρώπων ἀποστρεφομένων τὴν ἀλήθειαν . <sup class="fb-vn">15</sup> πάντα καθαρὰ τοῖς καθαροῖς · τοῖς δὲ μεμιαμμένοις καὶ ἀπίστοις οὐδὲν καθαρόν , ἀλλὰ μεμίανται αὐτῶν καὶ ὁ νοῦς καὶ ἡ συνείδησις. <sup class="fb-vn">16</sup> Θεὸν ὁμολογοῦσιν εἰδέναι , τοῖς δὲ ἔργοις ἀρνοῦνται , βδελυκτοὶ ὄντες καὶ ἀπειθεῖς καὶ πρὸς πᾶν ἔργον ἀγαθὸν ἀδόκιμοι .</div>
<div class="fb-en"><sup class="fb-vn">1</sup> I Paul, a servant of God, also an apostle of Jesus Christ for the faith of God’s elect, and the full knowledge of the truth that is according to godliness; <sup class="fb-vn">2</sup> in hope of eternal life, which God, that cannot lie, promised before eternal times; <sup class="fb-vn">3</sup> but in its own seasons manifested his word in preaching, which was entrusted to me, according to the commandment of our Saviour God; <sup class="fb-vn">4</sup> to Titus, [my] true son in respect to the common faith: Grace and peace from God our Father, and Christ Jesus our Saviour. <sup class="fb-vn">5</sup> For this cause I left thee behind in Crete, that thou shouldest further set in order the things which are wanting, and mightest appoint elders in every city, as I directed thee: <sup class="fb-vn">6</sup> If any one is blameless, husband of one wife, having faithful children, not accused of profligacy, or unruly. <sup class="fb-vn">7</sup> For a pastor must be blameless, as God’s steward; not self-willed, not soon angry, not a brawler, not a striker, not greedy of gain; <sup class="fb-vn">8</sup> but hospitable, a lover of good, discreet, righteous, holy, temperate; <sup class="fb-vn">9</sup> holding fast the faithful word according to the teaching, in order that he may be able both to exhort with the sound doctrine and to convince the gainsayers. <sup class="fb-vn">10</sup> For there are many unruly vain-talkers and deceivers, especially they of the circumcision: <sup class="fb-vn">11</sup> whose mouths must be stopped; who for the sake of base gain subverted whole houses, teaching things which they ought not. <sup class="fb-vn">12</sup> One of them, their own prophet, has said, The Cretans are alway liars, evil beasts, idle bellies. <sup class="fb-vn">13</sup> This testimony is true: wherefore reprove them sharply, that they may be sound in the faith; <sup class="fb-vn">14</sup> not giving heed to Jewish fables and commandments of men, who turn away from the truth. <sup class="fb-vn">15</sup> To the pure all things are pure: but to them that are defiled and unbelieving is nothing pure; but both their mind and conscience are defiled. <sup class="fb-vn">16</sup> They profess that they know God; but in works they deny [him], being abominable, and disobedient, and unto every good work reprobate.</div>
</div>

<div class="fb-par" id="ch2">
<h3>CHAPTER II</h3>
<div class="fb-gk" lang="grc"><sup class="fb-vn">1</sup> Σὺ δὲ λάλει ἃ πρέπει τῇ ὑγιαινούσῃ διδασκαλίᾳ . <sup class="fb-vn">2</sup> πρεσβύτας νηφαλίους εἶναι , σεμνούς , σώφρονας , ὑγιαίνοντας τῇ πίστει , τῇ ἀγάπῃ , τῇ ὑπομονῇ · <sup class="fb-vn">3</sup> πρεσβύτιδας ὡσαύτως ἐν καταστήματι ἱεροπρεπεῖς , μὴ διαβόλους μὴ οἴνῳ πολλῷ δεδουλωμένοις , καλοδιδασκάλους , <sup class="fb-vn">4</sup> ἵνα σωφρονίζουσιv τὰς νέας φιλάνδρους εἶναι , φιλοτέκνους <sup class="fb-vn">5</sup> σώφρονας, ἁγνὰς, οἰκουργοὺς, ἀγαθάς , ὑποτασσομένας τοῖς ἰδίοις ἀνδράσιν , ἵνα μὴ ὁ λόγος τοῦ Θεοῦ βλασφημῆται. <sup class="fb-vn">6</sup> Τοὺς νεωτέρους ὡσαύτως παρακάλει σωφρονεῖν <sup class="fb-vn">7</sup> περὶ πάντα , σεαυτὸν παρεχόμενος τύπον καλῶν ἔργων , ἐν τῇ διδασκαλίᾳ ἀφθορίαν , σεμνότητα , <sup class="fb-vn">8</sup> λόγον ὑγιῆ ἀκατάγνωστον , ἵνα ὁ ἐξ ἐναντίας ἐντραπῇ μηδὲν ἔχων λέγειν περὶ ἡμῶν φαῦλον. <sup class="fb-vn">9</sup> Δούλους ἰδίοις δεσπόταις ὑποτάσσεσθαι, ἐν πᾶσιν εὐαρέστους εἶναι , μὴ ἀντιλέγοντας , <sup class="fb-vn">10</sup> μὴ νοσφιζομένους , ἀλλὰ πᾶσαν πίστιν ἐνδεικνυμένους ἀγαθήν , ἵνα τὴν διδασκαλίαν τὴν τοῦ σωτῆρος ἡμῶν Θεοῦ κοσμῶσιν ἐν πᾶσιν . <sup class="fb-vn">11</sup> Ἐπεφάνη γὰρ ἡ χάρις τοῦ Θεοῦ σωτήριος πᾶσιν ἀνθρώποις, <sup class="fb-vn">12</sup> παιδεύουσα ἡμᾶς , ἵνα ἀρνησάμενοι τὴν ἀσέβειαν καὶ τὰς κοσμικὰς ἐπιθυμίας σωφρόνως καὶ δικαίως καὶ εὐσεβῶς ζήσωμεν ἐν τῷ νῦν αἰῶνι , <sup class="fb-vn">13</sup> προσδεχόμενοι τὴν μακαρίαν ἐλπίδα καὶ ἐπιφάνειαν τῆς δόξης τοῦ μεγάλου Θεοῦ καὶ σωτῆρος ἡμῶν Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ , <sup class="fb-vn">14</sup> ὃς ἔδωκεν ἑαυτὸν ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν , ἵνα λυτρώσηται ἡμᾶς ἀπὸ πάσης ἀνομίας καὶ καθαρίσῃ ἑαυτῷ λαὸν περιούσιον , ζηλωτὴν καλῶν ἔργων . <sup class="fb-vn">15</sup> Ταῦτα λάλει καὶ παρακάλει καὶ ἔλεγχε μετὰ πάσης ἐπιταγῆς· μηδείς σου περιφρονείτω.</div>
<div class="fb-en"><sup class="fb-vn">1</sup> But speak thou the things which become the sound instruction [of the gospel]: <sup class="fb-vn">2</sup> that the aged men be sober, grave, discreet, sound in faith, in love, in patience. <sup class="fb-vn">3</sup> In like manner the aged women, that they demean themselves as becomes holiness, not slanderers, not enslaved to much wine, teachers of what is good; <sup class="fb-vn">4</sup> that they school the young women to be lovers of their husbands, lovers of their children, <sup class="fb-vn">5</sup> discreet, chaste, workers at home, good, submitting themselves to their own husbands, that the word of God may not be blasphemed. <sup class="fb-vn">6</sup> The younger men, in like manner, exhort to be sober-minded. <sup class="fb-vn">7</sup> In all things showing thyself a pattern of good works: in thy teaching [showing] incorruption, gravity, <sup class="fb-vn">8</sup> sound discourse that cannot be condemned; in order that he who is of the contrary part may be ashamed, having no evil thing to say of us. <sup class="fb-vn">9</sup> Bondmen [exhort to be] in subjection to their own masters, in all things to be well-pleasing; not gainsaying; <sup class="fb-vn">10</sup> not purloining, but showing all good fidelity; in order that in all things they may adorn the doctrine of our Saviour God. <sup class="fb-vn">11</sup> For the grace of God, having salvation for all men, was manifested; <sup class="fb-vn">12</sup> disciplining us to the end that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we might live soberly, justly, and godlily in this present world; <sup class="fb-vn">13</sup> looking for the blessed hope and appearing of the glory of the great God, and of our Saviour Jesus Christ; <sup class="fb-vn">14</sup> who gave himself for us, in order that he might redeem us from all iniquity, and purify to himself a peculiar people, zealous of good works. <sup class="fb-vn">15</sup> These things speak, and exhort, and reprove with all authority. Let no one despise thee.</div>
</div>

<h3>CHAPTER III</h3>
<div class="fb-gk" lang="grc"><sup class="fb-vn">1</sup> Ὑπομίμνῃσκε αὐτοὺς ἀρχαῖς ἐξουσίαις ὑποτάσσεσθαι , πειθαρχεῖν , πρὸς πᾶν ἔργον ἀγαθὸν ἑτοίμους εἶναι , <sup class="fb-vn">2</sup> μηδένα βλασφημεῖν , ἀμάχους εἶναι , ἐπιεικεῖς , πᾶσαν ἐνδεικνυμένους πραΰτητα πρὸς πάντας ἀνθρώπους. <sup class="fb-vn">3</sup> ἦμεν γάρ ποτε καὶ ἡμεῖς ἀνόητοι , ἀπειθεῖς , πλανώμενοι , δουλεύοντες ἐπιθυμίαις καὶ ἡδοναῖς ποικίλαις , ἐν κακίᾳ καὶ φθόνῳ διάγοντες , στυγητοί , μισοῦντες ἀλλήλους· <sup class="fb-vn">4</sup> ὅτε δὲ ἡ χρηστότης καὶ ἡ φιλανθρωπία ἐπεφάνη τοῦ σωτῆρος ἡμῶν Θεοῦ , <sup class="fb-vn">5</sup> οὐκ ἐξ ἔργων τῶν ἐν δικαιοσύνῃ ἃ ἐποιήσαμεν ἡμεῖς ἀλλὰ κατὰ τὸ αὐτοῦ ἔλεος ἔσωσεν ἡμᾶς διὰ λουτροῦ παλιγγενεσίας καὶ ἀνακαινώσεως πνεύματος ἁγίου , <sup class="fb-vn">6</sup> οὗ ἐξέχεεν ἐφ ʼ ἡμᾶς πλουσίως διὰ Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ τοῦ σωτῆρος ἡμῶν , <sup class="fb-vn">7</sup> ἵνα δικαιωθέντες τῇ ἐκείνου χάριτι κληρονόμοι γενηθῶμεν κατ ʼ ἐλπίδα ζωῆς αἰωνίου. <sup class="fb-vn">8</sup> πιστὸς ὁ λόγος, καὶ περὶ τούτων βούλομαί σε διαβεβαιοῦσθαι , ἵνα φροντίζωσιν καλῶν ἔργων προΐστασθαι οἱ πεπιστευκότες Θεῷ . ταῦτά ἐστιν καλὰ καὶ ὠφέλιμα τοῖς ἀνθρώποις· <sup class="fb-vn">9</sup> μωρὰς δὲ ζητήσεις καὶ γενεαλογίας καὶ ἔριν καὶ μάχας νομικὰς περιΐστασο · εἰσὶν γὰρ ἀνωφελεῖς καὶ μάταιοι . <sup class="fb-vn">10</sup> αἱρετικὸν ἄνθρωπον μετὰ μίαν καὶ δευτέραν νουθεσίαν παραιτοῦ , <sup class="fb-vn">11</sup> εἰδὼς ὅτι ἐξέστραπται ὁ τοιοῦτος καὶ ἁμαρτάνει ὢν αὐτοκατάκριτος . <sup class="fb-vn">12</sup> Ὅταν πέμψω Ἀρτεμᾶν πρὸς σὲ ἢ Τυχικόν , σπούδασον ἐλθεῖν πρός με εἰς Νικόπολιν , ἐκεῖ γὰρ κέκρικα παραχειμάσαι . <sup class="fb-vn">13</sup> Ζηνᾶν τὸν νομικὸν καὶ Ἀπολλῶν σπουδαίως πρόπεμψον , ἵνα μηδὲν αὐτοῖς λείπῃ . <sup class="fb-vn">14</sup> μανθανέτωσαν δὲ καὶ οἱ ἡμέτεροι καλῶν ἔργων προΐστασθαι εἰς τὰς ἀναγκαίας χρείας , ἵνα μὴ ὦσιν ἄκαρποι . <sup class="fb-vn">15</sup> Ἀσπάζονταί σε οἱ μετʼ ἐμοῦ πάντες. ἄσπασαι τοὺς φιλοῦντας ἡμᾶς ἐν πίστει. Ἡ χάρις μετὰ πάντων ὑμῶν.</div>
<div class="fb-en"><sup class="fb-vn">1</sup> Put them in mind to submit themselves to magistrates, to authorities, to obey rulers, to be ready to every good work, <sup class="fb-vn">2</sup> to revile no man, to be no brawlers, forbearing, showing all meekness unto all men. <sup class="fb-vn">3</sup> For we also were once foolish, disobedient, going astray, serving divers lusts and pleasures, living in malice and envy, hateful, and hating one another. <sup class="fb-vn">4</sup> But when the kindness and the love toward man of our Saviour God was manifested, <sup class="fb-vn">5</sup> not of works works in righteousness which we did, but according to his mercy he saved us, through the laver of regeneration, and [through] renewing of the Holy Ghost; <sup class="fb-vn">6</sup> which he poured out on us richly through Jesus Christ our Saviour; <sup class="fb-vn">7</sup> in order that, being justified by his grace, we might become heirs according to the hope of eternal life. <sup class="fb-vn">8</sup> Faithful is the saying, and concerning these things I would have thee strenuously affirm, to the end that they who have believed God may be careful to practise good works. These things are good and profitable to men. <sup class="fb-vn">9</sup> But foolish questionings, and genealogies, and strifes, and contentions about the law, avoid; for they are unprofitable and vain. <sup class="fb-vn">10</sup> A heretical man, after one and a second admonition, shun; <sup class="fb-vn">11</sup> knowing that such an one is perverted and sinneth, being self-condemned. <sup class="fb-vn">12</sup> When I shall send Artemas to thee, or Tychicus, make haste to come to me to Nicopolis; for I have determined to spend the winter there. <sup class="fb-vn">13</sup> Zealously forward on their journey Zenas the lawyer and Apollos, that nothing may be wanting to them. <sup class="fb-vn">14</sup> But let ours also learn to practise good deeds for necessary uses, in order that they may not be unfruitful. <sup class="fb-vn">15</sup> All that are with me salute thee. Salute those that love us in the faith. The grace [of God] be with you all.</div>
