<p class="hc-v" markdown="1">**29** Out of whose womb came the Ice? and the houry frost of heaven, who hath gendred it?</p>
<p class="hc-v" markdown="1">**30** The waters are hid at with a stone, and the face of the deep is frozen.</p>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="job-38-29"></div>

<div class="commentary-anchor" id="job-38-30"></div>

In the last place, he produceth the *hoary frost,* and *Ice,* whose generation is of God, *v.* <span class="hc-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>9. And the effect of *Ice* is, that it hides and freezeth (or, taketh, as a captive) the waters, as if they were covered with a stone; and that not only the lesser, but the deepest waters, *v* 30. See. *Psal.* 147.16, 17. Those, considered with an eye to the s<span class="hc-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">••</span>p:, may *teach,*

1\. <span class="hc-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>old Meteors (such as these are) are full of warm beams and rayes of the glory of God; so that even a cold day may preach somewhat of him unto us.

2\. The womb of Omnipotency can produce whatsoever it pleaseth; not only rain, and drops of dew, *v.* 28. but *Ice* and *hoary frost of heaven.*

3\. God in not to be limited by our unbelief; who can make the Dew, or Vapours, in falling, turn in hoary frost, and turn the liquid waters into strong and firm Ice, and can cause men, in some places, travel upon depths and seas, as upon firm stones.

4\. As men do not mistake the hiding of the waters by Ice, nor fret at these winter storms, but do with patience attend the warm Spring; so men should not stumble at their present sad Lots, but expect the end of the Lord.
