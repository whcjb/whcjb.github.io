About this text. The text is EEBO-TCP A45241, keyed by hand from microfilm images of the 1657 London edition (Wing H3826; Cambridge University Library copy; CC0). Original spelling and italics are retained; the long s is printed as s. Page numbers of the 1657 edition are kept as in-page anchors.

Illegible places. The keyers could not read 838 places on the microfilm. They have been restored in three ways, in this order of evidence: (1) where this book’s own vocabulary admits only one reading; (2) by comparing, word by word, the page images of the 1841 London reprint (T. Ward & Co., “Reprinted from the Edition of 1657”; Google Books, British Library copy), keeping the 1657 spelling; (3) where neither applies, from the context or the cited source. Readings of the third kind that the evidence does not fix are shown [in brackets]: they are editorial conjectures, not the words of the 1657 edition (22 places). Four illegible punctuation marks absent from the 1841 reprint, and one apparently stray letter, have been dropped. The EarlyPrint machine-filled readings were checked and not used.

文本说明。本书据 EEBO-TCP A45241，即 1657 年伦敦初版（剑桥大学图书馆藏本缩微胶片）的人工录入本，保留原书拼写与斜体，长 ſ 作 s。录入本有 838 处胶片上读不出的字，依证据强弱分三步补出：① 本书自身用词只容一种读法的；② 逐处对照 1841 年伦敦 T. Ward 重印本（扉页注明「据 1657 年版重印」，大英图书馆藏本）的页面影像判读，拼写仍按 1657 年；③ 以上都无从判定的，据上下文或所引出处推补。凡用 [方括号] 另色标出的，都是编者推补，不是 1657 年原书的文字，共 22 处，鼠标移上去可见说明；另有 4 处录入本标为「认不清的标点」、而 1841 年本该处并无标点的，按 1841 年本删去，另 1 处疑为衍文的单字亦删去，均记录在案；其中卡拉米序里一处希腊文无从推断，只注明「原书希腊字不可辨」。EarlyPrint 项目的机器补字经比对错误较多，未采用。逐条依据见 hutcheson_raw/john/PROVENANCE.md。
