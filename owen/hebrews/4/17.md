# 希伯来书 4:17

<div class="owen-vg-sub">约翰·欧文注释 · Ver. 17</div>

<div class="owen-scr" markdown="0"><p class="owen-scr-grk">Τίσι δὲ προσώχθισε; Beza, "quibus infensus fuit?"—"with whom was he angry," or "provoked?" Vulg. Lat., "infensus est," in the present tense; which is blamed by Erasmus, and corrected by Vatablus and Arias, as that which regards what was long since past. Arabic, as before, "whom did he curse?" Syr., "who were a weariness to him?" Of the ground of which variety we spake before, on verse 10.</p></div>

<p class="owen-d0">Ὡν τὰ κῶλα ἔπεσον. Beza, &quot;quorum artus conciderant,&quot;—&quot;whose members fell;&quot; Vulg. Lat., &quot;quorum cadavera prostrata sunt,&quot;—&quot;whose carcasses were cast down;&quot; Erasmus, &quot;quorum membra;&quot; Syr., וְגַרְמַיְהיּן, —&quot;and their bones:&quot; whose members, bodies, bones, carcasses, fell in the wilderness. Of the proper signification of the word I shall speak afterwards.</p>
