# 希伯来书 10:12-14

<div class="owen-vg-sub">约翰·欧文注释 · Ver. 12–14</div>

<div class="owen-scr" markdown="0"><p class="owen-scr-en">In these verses the apostle opposeth that one sacrifice of Christ unto the legal offerings that the priests attended unto; and that in three things: 1. In the nature of it, and its perfection, ver. 12. 2. The consequence on the part of Christ, by whom it was offered, ver. 12, 13. 3. In the effect of it towards the church, ver. 14.</p></div>
