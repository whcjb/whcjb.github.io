# 希伯来书 2:2

<div class="owen-vg-sub">约翰·欧文注释 · Ver. 2</div>

<div class="owen-scr" markdown="0"><p class="owen-scr-grk">Εἰ γὰρ ὁ διʼ ἀγγέλων λαληθεὶς λόγος ἐγένετο βέβαιος, καὶ πᾶσα παράβασις καὶ παρακοὴ ἔλαβεν ἔνδικον μισθαποδοσίαν·</p><p class="owen-scr-en">For if the word spoken [pronounced] by angels was sure [steadfast], and every transgression and [stubborn] disobedience received a just [meet, equal] retribution [or, recompence of reward];</p></div>

<p class="owen-d0">Εἰ γάρ, &quot;si enim,&quot; &quot;etenim,&quot; &quot;and if,&quot; &quot;for if.&quot; Ὁ λόγος λαληθεὶς, &quot;sermo dictus;&quot; דֵּאתְמֵלַלֵת נֶילְתָא, Syr., &quot;sermo qui dictus est,&quot; or &quot;pronuntiatus,&quot; &quot;the word which was spoken or pronounced,&quot;—properly, as we shall see. Διʼ ἀγγέλων, Syr., מַלָאכֵא כְּיַד &quot;by the hand of angels;&quot; a Hebraism for their ministry. &quot;The word pronounced by the ministry of angels.&quot; The Arabic refers these words to the testimonies before insisted on about angels, and renders them, &quot;If that which is spoken concerning the angels be approved,&quot; or confirmed to be true; that is, περὶ ἀγγέλων, not διʼ ἀγγέλων. Ἐγένετο βέβαιος, &quot;factus est firmus,&quot; Ar., V. L., &quot;was made firm&quot; or &quot;stable,&quot; &quot;became sure;&quot; &quot;fuit firmus,&quot; Eras., Beza, &quot;was firm;&quot; or, as ours, &quot;steadfast;&quot; אֶשְׁתַּיְרַת, Syr., &quot;confirmatus fuit,&quot; &quot;was confirmed or established.&quot; Καὶ πᾶσα παράβασις καὶ παρακοή, &quot;et omnis prevaricatio et inobedientia,&quot; V. L., Ar., &quot;prevarication and disobedience;&quot; Rhem., &quot;omnisque transgressio et contumacia;&quot; Beza, &quot;every transgression and stubborn disobedience;&quot; the Syriac, a little otherwise, וְכֻל עֲלַיְהַ וַעֲבַי דְּשַׁמְעָחּ, &quot;and every one that heard it and transgressed it,&quot;—with peculiar respect, as it should seem, to παρακοή, which includes a disobedience to that which is heard. Ἔλαβεν ἔνδικον μισθαποδοσίαν, &quot;accepit justam mercedis retributionem,&quot; V. L., Bez.; &quot;retulit, praemii,&quot; Eras.,—all to the same purpose, &quot;received a just recompence,&quot; &quot;reward,&quot; &quot;a just compensation;&quot; Syr., &quot;received a retribution in righteousness.&quot;</p>
