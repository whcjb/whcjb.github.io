# 希伯来书 2:3

<div class="owen-vg-sub">约翰·欧文注释 · Ver. 3</div>

<div class="owen-scr" markdown="0"><p class="owen-scr-grk">Πῶς ἡμεῖς ἐκφευξόμεθα τηλικαύτης ἀμελήσαντες σωτηρίας; ἥτις ἀρχὴν λαβοῦσα λαλεῖσθαι διὰ τοῦ Κυρίου, ὑπὸ τῶν ἀκουσάντων εἰς ἡμᾶς ἐβεβαιώθη,</p><p class="owen-scr-en">How shall we escape [fly or avoid], if we neglect [not taking care about] so great salvation, which began to be [was first of all] spoken [declared] by the Lord, and was confirmed [assured, established] unto us by them that heard [it of him],</p></div>

Ἀμελήσαντες, "si neglexerimus," V. L., Eras., Beza, "if we neglect;" נֶכְסֵא אִן, Syr., "si contemnamus," "if we despise," "if we care not about," "if we take no care of." Τηλικαύτης σωτηρίας, "tantam salutem," "so great salvation;" the Syriac, a little otherwise, דַדַין אִנוּן דְּהָנון אִילֵין עַל, "super ea ipsa quae sunt vitae," "those things which are our life;" or, as others render the words, "eos sermones qui vivi sunt," "those words which are living." The former translation, taking the pronoun in the neuter gender, and חַיַּין substantively, with respect unto the effects of the gospel, most suits the place. ἽΙτις ἀρχὴν λαβοῦσα λαλεὶσθαι‚ "quae cum primum enarrari coepit," Eras., Bez., "which when it was begun to be declared;" and so the Syriac, "which began to be declared," which was first, at first spoken, declared, pronounced.
