# 希伯来书 2:3

<div class="owen-vg-sub">约翰·欧文注释 · Ver. 3</div>

<div class="owen-scr" markdown="0"><p class="owen-scr-grk">Πῶς ἡμεῖς ἐκφευξόμεθα τηλικαύτης ἀμελήσαντες σωτηρίας; ἥτις ἀρχὴν λαβοῦσα λαλεῖσθαι διὰ τοῦ Κυρίου, ὑπὸ τῶν ἀκουσάντων εἰς ἡμᾶς ἐβεβαιώθη,</p><p class="owen-scr-en">How shall we escape [fly or avoid], if we neglect [not taking care about] so great salvation, which began to be [was first of all] spoken [declared] by the Lord, and was confirmed [assured, established] unto us by them that heard [it of him],</p></div>

<p class="owen-d0">Ἀμελήσαντες, &quot;si neglexerimus,&quot; V. L., Eras., Beza, &quot;if we neglect;&quot; נֶכְסֵא אִן, Syr., &quot;si contemnamus,&quot; &quot;if we despise,&quot; &quot;if we care not about,&quot; &quot;if we take no care of.&quot; Τηλικαύτης σωτηρίας, &quot;tantam salutem,&quot; &quot;so great salvation;&quot; the Syriac, a little otherwise, דַדַין אִנוּן דְּהָנון אִילֵין עַל, &quot;super ea ipsa quae sunt vitae,&quot; &quot;those things which are our life;&quot; or, as others render the words, &quot;eos sermones qui vivi sunt,&quot; &quot;those words which are living.&quot; The former translation, taking the pronoun in the neuter gender, and חַיַּין substantively, with respect unto the effects of the gospel, most suits the place. ἽΙτις ἀρχὴν λαβοῦσα λαλεὶσθαι‚ &quot;quae cum primum enarrari coepit,&quot; Eras., Bez., &quot;which when it was begun to be declared;&quot; and so the Syriac, &quot;which began to be declared,&quot; which was first, at first spoken, declared, pronounced.</p>
