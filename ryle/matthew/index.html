About this text. The text follows the first American edition (New York: Robert Carter, 1857; the preface is dated Helmingham Rectory, December 1856), with the text of St. Matthew printed before each exposition as in the original. It was prepared from the e-Sword module of 2009 (a keyed text with the original wording and italics) collated word by word against the Internet Archive scan of the 1857 volume. Where the module departs from the 1857 page, the 1857 reading is restored after inspection of the page image: modernised quotations of Scripture, altered words and word order, Americanised spellings (Saviour, fulfil, fulness, offence, marvellous and the like), the omitted “St.”, and references in the original form (roman chapter numbers). The Scripture text of the boxes is the King James Version as printed in the 1857 volume, which in a few places differs from the Oxford text (laborers, honor, cloak, often …).

文本说明。本书依 1857 年纽约 Robert Carter 首个美国版（序言落款 1856 年 12 月，Helmingham 教区），每段前照原书排出马太福音经文。底本为 2009 年 e-Sword 模块（人工录入，措辞与斜体保留原书），与 Internet Archive 所藏 1857 年版扫描件逐词对齐：凡模块与原书不同之处，逐处看原书影像后照原书回改——包括被改成现代译本的引经、改过的词与词序、被美式化的拼写（Saviour、fulfil、fulness、offence、marvellous 等）、被删去的「St.」，以及出处的原书写法（章号用罗马数字）。经文框是 1857 年版所印的钦定本，个别拼写与牛津本不同（laborers、honor、cloak、often 等），照原书。原书显系误植之处（如 Due Veil、「is this world」）沿用录入本的改正；出处误植见下表。
