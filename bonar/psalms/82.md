<p><span class="bn-vr">1–8</span>ASAPH'S name is at Psalm 50, in which the solemn scenes and expostulations of the Great Day are given; and here, too, is his name, prefixed to this awfully authoritative rebuke and warning—</p>

<p>We see, verse 1, the Judge surveying earth's rulers.</p>

<p>"God hath placed himself in the assembly of the mighty." (Isa. 3:13.)</p>

<p>"He judges in the midst of the gods," (i. e., earth's judges, Exodus 22:28, John 10:34.)</p>

<p>We hear his voice (ver. 2), and we recognise in it him who speaks to us, "How long, ye simple ones" in Proverbs 1:26. The "Selah" completes it, giving a solemn seal to the words.</p>

<p>We listen again (verses 3, 4); he is declaring the rules that should guide them—rules on which the Judge himself has ever acted— defending the poor; interposing where no help of man was on the side of the oppressed; maintaining equity; doing acts of disinterested grace and favour.</p>

<p>We are told of the contempt poured upon Him (ver. 5), for it is said, "They take no notice" (they disregard God and his Christ), &amp;c.; and as a consequence, "The foundations of earth begin to totter," (comp. Isa. 24:20); and we hear the voice of the Great Judge (ver. 6)—</p>

<p>"I have said! (<span class="bn-he" lang="he" dir="rtl">אֲנִי</span>, i. e., This is now your doom—I solemnly proclaim it)—</p>

<p>Ye are judges (bearing the name of God, as verse 2);</p>

<p>All of you are sons of the Most High!"</p>

<p>This is your great name, in which you rest secure. But</p>

<p>"Surely ye shall die, as other men have died;</p>

<p>Ye shall fall, as other men of rank have fallen."<sup class="bn-fn"><a href="#fn-1" id="fnref-1">1</a></sup></p>

<p>Your day is coming! The saints are raising the loud cry of verse 8, inviting Messiah, the true God, the Son of the Most High (John 10:34), the Mighty One, the Judge and Ruler, to arise and take his inheritance, for he is the "heir of all things;" and to be the true Othniel, Ehud, Shamgar, Barak, Gideon, Tola, Jair, Jephthah, Samson, and Samuel, who will judge, or govern and rule, a mismanaged earth. We sing this song of Zion in his ears, urging him to come quickly; and we sing it to one another in joyful hope, while the foundations of earth seem out of course, because here we find</p>

<p class="bn-sum">Messiah the true Judge of a misgoverned world.</p>
