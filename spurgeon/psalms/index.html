About this text. The text is the transcription of The Treasury of David made for The Spurgeon Archive (archive.spurgeon.org, now administered by Midwestern Baptist Theological Seminary). Each psalm has Spurgeon’s own Exposition, the Explanatory Notes and Quaint Sayings he collected from earlier writers, and his Hints to the Village Preacher. Hebrew words in that transcription are encoded in a Latin-letter font (e.g. “hwq” for קוה) and are reproduced as found.

文本说明。正文据 Spurgeon Archive（司布真文献库，现由中西部浸信会神学院维护）的人工录入本。 每篇含司布真本人的讲解、他汇集的历代注释与古人语录、给乡村传道人的讲章提示。 录入本里的希伯来文是按字体编码的拉丁字母（如 “hwq” 即 קוה），暂照录未改。中译见中文版（逐篇推进中）；另有麦种传道会出版的正式中译本。
