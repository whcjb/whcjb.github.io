About this text. EEBO-TCP A45335 (London: E. Tyler for John Starkey, 1658; Wing H436), keyed by hand from the EEBO microfilm; CC0. Hall expounds only the last two chapters of 2 Timothy. Original spelling and italics are retained; the long s is printed as s. Page numbers of the original are kept as in-page anchors.

Illegible places. The keyers marked 1,611 places they could not read from the film images. 275 were read from the grayscale scan of the same film (Internet Archive), place by place against the page image; 0 are editorial conjectures shown [in brackets]. 7 places still unread remain marked in red; all are listed below.
