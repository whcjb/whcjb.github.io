About this text. James Foote preached these 137 lectures to his congregation in Aberdeen and afterwards expanded them for the press; they expound the whole of Luke's Gospel, each lecture opening with the passage in the Authorised Version. The text here is taken from the OCR of the second-edition volumes scanned by Google from the Bodleian copy (Internet Archive lecturesongospe03footgoog, …02footgoog, …00footgoog). It is a working text: systematic misreadings (ligatures, broken letters) have been corrected by rule, but scattered OCR errors remain, especially in Greek, Latin and footnote references, and are being corrected against the page images.

文本说明。福特在阿伯丁向会众逐段宣讲路加福音，后扩写成书，共 137 讲，每讲先列钦定本经文。本页正文取自 Google 扫描的牛津大学藏第 2 版三卷 OCR。目前是校勘中的工作本：成套的连字误读、残字已按规则改正，零星 OCR 错字仍在，希腊文、拉丁文与脚注出处尤多，正对照页面影像逐步校正。
