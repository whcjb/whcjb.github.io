About this text. Hildersham preached these lectures at Ashby-de-la-Zouch, Leicestershire, on Wednesdays from 28 September 1625 to 1631, reaching the seventh verse of the psalm before his death in 1632; his son Samuel published them in 1635. The text is the keyed transcription of that edition (EEBO-TCP A03343, STC 13459; released under CC0), with the spelling of the original; only the long s is printed as s. Each lecture is headed by the author's own summary of it from the table at the front of the book. Places the transcribers could not read are shown in red (1132 letters, 108 words, 55 longer passages, and 62 Greek or Hebrew words left untranscribed). The headings of four lectures are misnumbered in the original (two are printed LXXI and two CXVI, the second of each being 70 and 117) and Lecture 57 is printed after Lecture 60; the lectures are here numbered and ordered by their dates and the author's table. The index of Scripture places and the general index are not included.

文本说明。这些讲义是希尔德沙姆 1625 年 9 月 28 日起在莱斯特郡阿什比-德拉祖什每周三所讲，讲到 1631 年， 讲至本篇第 7 节；他 1632 年去世，1635 年由儿子塞缪尔出版。本站正文据该版的人工录入本（EEBO-TCP A03343，STC 13459， CC0 公共领域授权），拼法照录原书，只把长 s（ſ）印作 s。各讲页首是作者自编的逐讲提要（原书卷首）。录入者读不出的地方以红色标出： 缺字母 1132 个、缺整词 108 处、缺一段以上 55 处，另有希腊文、希伯来文 62 处未录。 原书有四讲标题讲次印错（两个 LXXI、两个 CXVI，后一个分别实为第 70、117 讲），第 57 讲印在第 60 讲之后；本站按日期与作者提要定讲次、排次序。 卷末经文索引与总索引未收。
