*Vers. 22.* He also that receiveth seed among the thornes, is he that heareth the word: and the care of this world, and the deceitfulnesse of riches choak the word, and he becometh unfruitfull.

</div>

From the exposition of the seed sown in thorny ground, learn, 1. That however the word seem to be received, if it do not bring forth fruits of amendment of life, if it beget not Faith working by Love, it is like seed choaked with thorns, and the word is lost in such an heart. 2. Whatsoever things pertaining to this life, do go so near a mans heart, as they take <span class="dk-pb" id="p162" data-p="162" data-facs="87" title="Page 162 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 162 页起)"></span> up the room, time, travell, and affection, which heavenly things should have, they are but thornes which choak the seed of God's Word. 3. When the care of getting, or keeping, or governing of riches, under fair pretences of a lawfull calling, of caring for our family, and not neglecting the benefits of God, do steal away the heart, and do waste a mans time and strength, which should be bestowed upon heavenly things, then all these pretences are but *the deceitfulnesse of riches.* 4. If a man's works do not proceed from the vertue of God's word, whatsoever be his works from other principles, they are nothing in God's account; for, *If the Word be choaked, the man becometh unfruitfull.*
