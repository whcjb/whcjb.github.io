*Ver. 27.* And when Iesus departed thence, two blind men followed him, crying, and saying, Thou son of David, have mercy on us.

</div>

In the healing of these two blind men, Learn, 1. That our Lord wearies not to do good; the more he is imployed, the more good he worketh. When Christ departed from the rulers house, *Two blind men follow him.* 2. When others get almes and mercy from Christ, it should allure more to come to his deal, as these blinde men hearing of many helped by Christ, do come and cry, *Have mercy on us.* 3. Such as seek good of Christ, must look on him as he is described in Scripture, as he is the promised Messiah, the native King of Israel; *Son of David,* say they, *have mercy on us.* 4. Such as beleeve to get good of Christ, will find a way to follow him, and come at him, albeit they were blinde; for these blinde men *follow* and *cry.* 5. More persons in the sense of one need, may joyn in one fute unto Christ; for these two blind men do joyn in one cry, saying, *Have mercy on us.* 6. The incarnation of the Son of God is a noble Prop to Faith, *Thou son of David,* say they, *have mercy.* 7. Mercy is our only plea with God, and Christ; *Therefore,* say they, *Have mercy on us.*
