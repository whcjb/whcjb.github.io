*Vers. 3.* And as he sate upon the mount of Olives, the disciples came unto him privately, saying, Tell us, When shall these things be? and what shal be the signes of thy coming, and of the end of the world?

</div>

The disciples understanding that the Temple should be destroyed, ask some questions. *Doct.* Men are more curious to know things hid in Gods secret counsell, then to make use of what is revealed; as the disciples here, who asked, *When shall these things be?* when a more usefull question had been more expedient.
