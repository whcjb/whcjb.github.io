*Verse 8.* Blessed *are* the pure in heart: for they shall see God.

</div>

The sixth mark of true Disciples, is *purity of heart,* whereby Christians study not only to eschew sin, and to have a blameless conversation before men; but also to be holy in their mind, in their designes, and affections, before God. *Doct.* 1. Albeit <span class="dk-pb" id="p42" data-p="42" data-facs="27" title="Page 42 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 42 页起)"></span> beleevers in Christ do find sensibly much pollution in themselves, yet if their heart love it not; if when they come short in duties, their heart is grieved for it; if the honest indeavour, and study of their heart be, that they may be pure and holy, they are notwithstanding of this felt sinfulnesse, truely blessed; for, *Blessed,* saith our Lord, *are the clean* or *pure in heart.* 2. Holinesse and purity of heart is a preparative for sensible communion with God; for it is promised they shall see God; that is, their eyes shall be opened to behold by faith the invisible God; the Lord shall make them to discerne the mysteries of salvation hid from the world; he shall make them to observe the work of his providence in justice, mercy, goodness, and power in the world about them; and withail to perceive loving kindness toward themselves, and at length they shall *see God face to face* perfectly, in the Kingdom of Heaven.
