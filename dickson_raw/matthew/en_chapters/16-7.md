*Ver. 7.* And they reasoned among themselves saying, It is because we have taken no bread.

</div>

The disciples imagine that Christ was speaking of bodily leaven. *Doct.* Readily are our Lords words mistaken, even by disciples, and upon a mistake wrong conclusions are drawn, and so the intent of Christs words are lost; for, *Because we have no bread,* is this admonition given, say the disciples.
