*Ver. 6.* Blessed *are* they which do hunger and thirst after righteousness, for they shall be filled.

</div>

The fourth mark of true Disciples, is hunger and thirst for righteousnesse, such hungry souls are they, who in the sense of their sinfulness, and want of all inherent righteousnesse, do <span class="dk-pb" id="p41" data-p="41" data-facs="26" title="Page 41 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 41 页起)"></span> heartily hunger to be more and more certified of the imputation of Christs righteousness, and do thirst to draw sap and life from him: for the changing their sinfull nature, and making them more holy and righteous by his Spirit. *Doct.* 1. Such as are heartily desirous to be justified and sanctified through Christ, they are blessed; for of such Christ prononnceth, *Blessed are they who hunger and thirst for righteousness.* 2. Kindly hunger after the righteousnesse of God in Christ shall be satisfied: albeit for the present such soules as do feel this hunger, be pained, yet they shall have all that they desire in Gods own time, the word of the Lord shall be made milk, honey, bread and marrow unto them, a Well of living Water shall spring up from the Holy Spirit unto them: now and then they shall have so large a meal, and so full satisfaction, as they shall be forced to say, *Enough, O LORD:* and at length shall be put in full, and sensible possession of all; for it is written, *They shall be satisfied.*
