*Ver. 10.* His disciples say unto him, If the case of the man be so with his wife, it is not good to marry.

</div>

The disciples think this doctrine hard, for in case divorcement were not lawful, they say it were better not to marry, then to be so straightly bound in marriage: in whom we see, 1. How impatient our nature is of all restraint, and how much we love to be at liberty, even from the bands of God; for, *If so it be, it is not good to marry,* say they. 2. Suddain resolutions and sentences are readily full of folly: for here the disciples neither do look unto their own strength, or rather inability to live in a single life, nor do they consider the incommodities of an unmarried life, nor the commodities of marriage, where God giveth a blessing.
