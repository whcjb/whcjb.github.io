*Ver. 30.* But many that he first, shal be last; and the last shall be first.

</div>

Howsoever Christ esteem much of what his servants do <span class="dk-pb" id="p226" data-p="226" data-facs="119" title="Page 226 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 226 页起)"></span> suffer for him, yet he giveth advertisement to Peter and the rest, & in them to al men, that they should not conceit of any merit in their sufferings, or worth in any other specious excellency, but to study rather to be humble and sincere; because at the time of Gods reckoning, many who in their own eyes, and before men were first, should be found to be last; that is, of no esteem with God: and such as in their own eyes, and in the eyes of the world seemed nothing, should be with God in prime estimation: many who in the conceit of their own merit were first, in the reward should be last, and of no account; and many who in the opinion of the merit were last, and worthy of nothing, should be first in the reward; this is imported in saying, *But many that are first shall be last,* &c.
