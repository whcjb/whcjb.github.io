*Ver. 9.* But what went ye out for to see? A prophet? yea, I say unto you, and more then a prophet.

</div>

The third point of Johns commendation is the clear revealing of Christ, and of the shortest course to come unto Him, wherein John was above any Prophet. Hence learn, 1. That the right reason of going to hear Sermons, is, because the man that speaketh, hath warrand to speak, and to reveal Gods will: this is it which he saith, *Went ye out to see a* <span class="dk-pb" id="p126" data-p="126" data-facs="69" title="Page 126 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 126 页起)"></span> *Prophet?* 2. John is called more then a Prophet, specially because he did point out Christ more clearly and fully, then any before him, as Christ doth shew hereafter; teaching us, that these are the greatest men in the Ministry, who do most point out Christ to the world, and do most sincerely lead men unto him.
