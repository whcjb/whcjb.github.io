Christ his greatest E•emies could find no fault in him, but were forced to make that •lau•t which was none, to wit, His foretelling of his own death and resurrection, which was the matter of his glory and our comfort, *Destroy this Temple, and I will build it in three dayes,* saith Christ; that is, Do your worst against me, in destroying the Temple of this Body of mine, and I will raise it up again the third day; which words the Witnesses do alter, and repeat them, as if he had promised to destroy the Temple of Jerusalem, and build it again in three dayes; and therefore are these men justly called here *False Witnesses.*
