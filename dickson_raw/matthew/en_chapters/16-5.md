*Ver. 5.* And when his disciples were come to the other side, they had forgotten to take bread.

</div>

This forgetting to take bread, is told us; partly, That we may know how our Lords train was maintained; to wit, As souldiers they carried their bread with them, and to shew us, that our Lord had a care that his followers should not be burdensom to any; But in speciall it is spoken of, to clear the occasion of the disciples mistaking of the speech of Christ, herafter set down in his warning of his disciples, to beware of corrupt doctrine under comparison of leaven.
