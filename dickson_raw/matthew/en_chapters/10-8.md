*Ver. 8.* Heal the sick, cleanse the Lepers, raise the dead, cast out divels; freely yee have received, freely give.

</div>

It is worthy observation, that the Lord for confirmation of mens faith hath given power to the first Preachers of his Doctrine, to deliver men from miseries, both of soul and body, from sicknesse and divels; that in all time coming the power of his Gospel might be beleeved: for, *Heal the sick,* saith he, *Cleanse the Lepers, Cast out divels.* 2. To shew the freedome and rich grace of the Gospel, (whereby, whatsoever belongeth to righteousness and salvation, is bestowed upon the unworthy, without money, and without price) he gave the first Preachers of his grace a command to take no reward for their miraculous cures, saying, *Freely have you received, freely give.*
