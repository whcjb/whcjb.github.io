*Ver. 23.* And all the people were amazed, and said, Is this the son of David?

</div>

The People are moved at the sight of this work, and do see a glimpse of his God-head, and of Divine glory in him. *Doct.* The miracles of our Lord were so demonstrative of his Divine power, that the beholders were overcome, and forced to acknowledg his works to be worthy of the promised Messiah: *Is this* (say they) *the son of David?*
