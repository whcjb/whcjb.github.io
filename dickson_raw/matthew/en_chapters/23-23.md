*Verse 23.* Wo unto you Scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites; for ye pay tithe of mint, and anise, and cummin, and have omitted the weightier matters of the law, judgment, mercy, and faith: these ought ye to have done, and not to leave the other undone.

</div>

The fifth wo is, for their ostentation of precise keeping of the Law in the least things, while in the mean tim• they despised the Law in the greatest duties. *Doct.* 1. It is no new thing for hypocrites to make a busines about smal matters, and in the mean time to reject the most weighty duties; for the Pharisees, *They tithed annise, and omitted mercy.* The discharge of greater duties, do not liberate a man from his obligation to do the smallest duties, but one authority obligeth unto both; for, *These ought ye to have done, and not left the other undone,* saith Christ.
