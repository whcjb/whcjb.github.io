*Vers. 23.* But he answered her not a word: And his disciples came and besought him, saying, Send her away, for she cryeth after us.

</div>

There are 4 means used for the triall of her faith. The first is, Christs keeping silence when she prayeth: Hence learn, That God, albeit he love the Suppliant, and do accept his petition, yet possibly he will keep silence, and not seem to take notice for a time; for, *He answered her not a word.* The next means of her triall is the small assistance she hath of the disciples prayers: wherin learn, That saith loveth the help of the prayers of the Faithfull militant, as a means prescribed of God for mutuall comfort; for, *She cryed after the disciples.* 2. When God thinketh it fit to try our faith, by delaying to hear us, he will for our tryall make other means which we use, to fail us also; as here the disciples would be rid of her importunity, and have her dispatched, that she should not trouble them; Therefore say they, *Send her away, She cryeth after us.*
