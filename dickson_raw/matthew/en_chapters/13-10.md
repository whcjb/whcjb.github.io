*Ver. 10.* And the Disciples came and said unto him, Why speakest thou unto them in Parables?

</div>

The Disciples ask the meaning of the parables, and a reason of this manner of Christs teaching. *Doct.* 1. It is a duty of love to be soliciters for the common multitude: therfore the disciples do regrate their case, saying, *Why speakest thou unto them in parables?* that is, in a way which they wil not understand. 2. Our questions about the manner of the Lords dealing with people, & manner of speaking unto them, should be proponed unto the Lord himself, and satisfaction sought from him; for, *Why speakest thou to them in parables?* say they: the question otherwise proponed by way of quarrelling, is dangerous.
