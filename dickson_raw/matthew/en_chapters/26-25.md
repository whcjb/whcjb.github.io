*Ver. 25.* Then Iudas, which betrayed him, answered, and said, Master, is it I? He said unto him, Thou hast said.

</div>

*Iudas* seigneth himself a friend, and is loath to bewray himself, by not saying as the rest did. *Doct.* 1. Hypocrites for shame will professe sincerety, as the sincere do, so saith *Iudas, Is it I?* 2. A seated conscience will make an affronted face, as here *Iudas* dares say, *Is it I?* 3. The more the hypocrite will hide his sin, the more is it brought forth, as here, *Thou hast said it,* saith Christ.
