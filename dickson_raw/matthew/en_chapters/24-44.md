*Ver. 44.* Therfore be ye also ready: for in such an hour as you think not, the Son of man cometh.

</div>

A fifth motive, Watchfulnesse maketh a man ready, albeit the Lord shall come in an hour when men thinke not; Therfore watch ye, that you may be ready. *Doct.* The solid apprehending of the certainty of the Lords coming, and of the uncertainty of the time, is a nomble means to set us on our watch, and to make our selves ready; for, *In such an hour as ye think not he shall come.*
