*Ver. 10.* Blessed *are* they which are persecuted for righteousness sake: for theirs is the kingdome of heaven.

</div>

The eighth mark of a true Disciple, is suffering persecution for righteousness sake. *Doct.* 1. Whosoever in following of Christ are troubled and persecuted by men, for doing that which God alloweth, and do chuse rather to suffer affliction, then to commit sin, are indeed blessed: for, *Blessed are they that suffer persecution for righteousness sake.* 2. Let persecuters do their utmost to rob the godly of all that they have, yet they cannot rob them of Heaven, for it is said, *the Kingdom of heaven is theirs*: that is, albeit they were banished out of their native country, and utterly spoyled, yea killed, yet Heaven belongs unto them by Christs conquest, <span class="dk-pb" id="p43" data-p="43" data-facs="27" title="Page 43 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 43 页起)"></span> and by Gods promise, and it shall certainly be given to them, for making up all their losses.
