*Ver. 31.* Insomuch that the multitude wondered when they saw the dumb to speak, the maimed to be whole, the lame to walk, and the blind to see; and they glorified the God of Israel.

</div>

Here is the effect of these miracles, They wondered and glorified God. *Doct.* 1. The miraculous works of Christ were such as forced the beholders to acknowledg divine Power in him; for here, the beholders wondred, and glorified God. 2. Very nature wil be moved, and forced to glorifie God, when it seeth Gods Power in a supernatural way manifested; Therfore it is said, *The multitude wondered*; &c.
