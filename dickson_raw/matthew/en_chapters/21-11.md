*Ver. 11.* And the multitude said, This is Iesus the Prophet of Nazareth of Galilee.

</div>

The multitude describeth Christ rudely, as he was commonly talked of. *Doct.* As men do know Christ, so do they speak of him, and according to the measure of their knowledge should they bear witnesse of him; for this was the clearest description of him which the multitude could give, *This is Iesus, the Prophet of Nazareth,* say they.
