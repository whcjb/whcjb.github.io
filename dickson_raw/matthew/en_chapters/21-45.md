*Ver. 45.* And when the chief priests and Pharisees had heard his parables, they perceived that he spake of them.

46 But when they sought to lay hands on him, they feared the multitude, because they took him for a Prophet.

</div>

These men take up Christs meaning, but do make no good use of the warning. *Doct.* 1. Threatnings profit not, but rather do irritate desperately wicked men, as here they desired *to lay hands on him.* 2. Christs most malicious adversaries (though they be set for bloud) yet can do no more than God will suffer them to do, as here is to be seen. 3. As long as the body of the people do favour Christs cause, persecutors will not vent all their designs against Christ and his followers; as here, *They sought to lay hands on Christ, but they feared the multitude.* 4. The least good opinion of Christ wil serve for some use; albeit not to the parties salvation, yet to the advantage of Christs cause, as here it served for some use, *That they took him for a Prophet.*
