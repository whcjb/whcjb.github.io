*Ver. 13.* For all the prophets, and the law prophesied untill Iohn.

</div>

The seventh point of commendation, is, that John pointed at Christ now already come, at Grace already laid open: whereas the Law and the Prophets onely did prophesie and <span class="dk-pb" id="p129" data-p="129" data-facs="70" title="Page 129 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 129 页起)"></span> foretell that it was to come; and this is a reason of the fruitfulnesse of Johns Ministry; Hence learn, 1. That the pointing forth of the fulfilling of the promises, and prophesies in Christ, is a mean to bring men in multitudes unto the Kingdome of Heaven; and this is imported in the particle *•or,* wherein a reason is rendered of what is said in the former verse; for, saith he, all the Prophets and the Law *prophesied* untill John, but John shews the *accomplishment*: And therefore multitudes did thrust themselves into the Kingdome of Heaven. 2. The Church of the Old Testament, had a dark time, in comparison of what we have now; for their light was prophesies of things to come, but ours is preaching of what is already come; for, *The Law and the Prophets prophesied untill Iohn.*
