From the history of Christs departure, Learn, 1. That Jesus hath reason to depart from the place where any of his servants are slain, and others of them are in danger; for, *When he heard this he departed.* 2. Such as have need of Christ wil find him out, and follow after him, refusing no travel nor pains to seek him; for, *The people follow him to a desert place.* 3. Christ is compassionate toward such as seek him; and of such as seek he will put none away; the needy and sick find health from him, for here it is said, *He was moved with compassion, and healed the sick which followed him.*
