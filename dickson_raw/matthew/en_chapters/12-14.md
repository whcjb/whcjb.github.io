*Ver. 14.* Then the Pharisees went out and held a councel against him, how they might destroy him.

</div>

Now the Pharisees being confounded in dispute by Scripture and experience, they resolve to kill Christ: Hence learn, 1. That Christs enemies, when arguments do fail them, do fal to violence; for, *The Pharisees seek now how to destroy Christ.* 2. Christs adversaries take help one of another, and combine against him with joint wit and power, as here, *They took counsell to destroy him.*
