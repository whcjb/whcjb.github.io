*Ver. 50.* Iesus, when he had cried again with a loud voice yeilded up the ghost.

</div>

In this close of Christ his suffering, learn, 1. That the Son of God according to his man-hood verily died, and his soul was separate from his body; for, *He yeilded up the ghost.* 2. He died not by constraint, but willingly; for of his own accord. *He yeelded up his Spirit.* 3. In dying, he was conquerour over death; for before death could come at him, as a weakling which cannot live any longer, Christ being so strong, as *To cry with a loud voice,* sets upon death, *and yeeldeth up the ghost.*
