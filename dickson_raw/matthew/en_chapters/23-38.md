*Ver. 38.* Behold, your house is left unto you desolate.

</div>

Having told the sin of the visible Kirk there, he telleth them now of their judgement. *Doct.* When mercy hath been often offered unto a visible church, and •hath been rejected or opposed, it is righteousnesse with God to remove his dwelling from that place, and to take away the face of a church from it; therefore saith he, *Thy house shall be left desolate unto thee,* even the most glorious place shall not be spared, but when mercy findeth no place, judgement shall have place.
