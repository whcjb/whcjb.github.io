*Ver. 13.* Then were there brought unto him little children, that he should put his hands on them, and pray; and the disciples rebuked them.

</div>

Some desired Christ to blesse their yong children, and to this end do present them unto him. *Doct.* 1. Albeit little children understand not the mysteries of Christ, yet it is lawful and commendable to beleeving parents to consecrate their children to Christ, and to seek his blessing, with the externall signs and seals of the blessing of them; for here, *They brought unto him little children, that he should lay his hands on them, and pray.* 2. Carnal wisdom is not fit to judg of the extent of Christ his grace, not what persons are priviledged to come unto him: for here in the disciples, it is carnal wisdom, to *thrust away children offered unto Christ.*
