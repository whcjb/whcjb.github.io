*Verse 8.* But what went ye out for to see? a man clothed in soft raiment? behold, they that wear soft clothing, are in kings houses.

</div>

The second point of the commendation of John, is, for his Holinesse and high Measure of Mortification: Hence learn, 1. That it is the commendation of a Preacher, to be mortified to the glory, ease, pleasure, and riches of this world; for on this ground Christ commendeth John, by way of interrogation, denying him to be *A man given to soft raimens.* 2. A true Preacher must be so mortified to this World, as all the Allurements of court be not able to corrupt him; for such as love to wear soft raiment, will affect to be in respect at court, for that very end; therfore saith he, *They that wear soft raiment, are in kings houses.*
