*Ver. 35.* A good man out of the treasure of his heart, bringeth forth good things: and an evil man out of the evil treasure, bringeth forth evil things.

</div>

A fourth reason proving them to be wicked, is, because they did delight to lay up wickedness in their heart, and to bring forth ill out of their heart. *Doct.* Whether a man be renewed and a good man, or unrenewed and an evil man, it may be known by the stuffe, which delibrately he layeth up in his heart, and deliberately brings forth out of his heart; for, *If it be a good treasure,* he is a good man; if it be *an evil treasure,* he is an evill man: for here such as the man is, such is his treasure; for, *A good man out of the good treasure, and an evil man of the evil treasure of his heart bringeth forth,* &c.
