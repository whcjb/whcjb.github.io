*Ver. 31.* And the multitude rebuked them, because they should hold their peace: but they cryed the more, saying, Have mercy on us, O Lord, thou Son of David.

</div>

From the discouragement off•red unto them: Learne, 1. It is no new thing that such as in appearance are following Christ, do hinder these who are following him in ea••est; for, *The multitude rebuked these poor men, and will have them to hold their peace.* 2. But it is wisdom for such as beleeve in Christ, the more they are opposed, the more to seek him, and to take no answer from any, but from himselfe: for so doth these blind men, who do not forsake their petition, till it be granted.
