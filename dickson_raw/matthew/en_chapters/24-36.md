*Ver. 36.* But of that day and hour knoweth no man, no, not the Angels in heaven, but my Father only.

</div>

Concerning the precise time of his second coming, he saith, that neither man nor Angel knoweth, but only the Father, wherby he doth not exclude the rest of the persons of the God-head, but only the creatures. *Doct.* 1. The peremptory time of the day of judgement •nd keepeth it to himself secret, and will not have it particularly known, and therefore it should not be narrowly pryed into; for, *Of this day and hour knoweth no man,* &c. 2. All things are not revealed, neither to the Angels nor to men, but so much at may edifie the Church, and as concerneth us to know; for, *My Father only knoweth,* saith he.
