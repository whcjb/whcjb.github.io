*Verse 6.* And blessed is he whosoever shall not be offended in me.

</div>

Our Lord obviats any temptation which might arise from his outward humiliation. *Doct.* 1. Many stumbling blocks are to be met within a mans way toward Jesus Christ, such as were taking on the shape of a servant, and his outward humbling of himselfe unto the crosse: This is imported *In his mentioning of mens being offended, or stumbling at him.* 2. Such as do not stumble at Christ, nor start back from beleeving and professing of him, for whatsoever crosse or impediment laid in their way, shall surely be <span class="dk-pb" id="p125" data-p="125" data-facs="68" title="Page 125 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 125 页起)"></span> saved; for, *Blessed is he whosoever shall not be offended in me,* saith he.
