*Vers. 3.* And why beholdest thou the mote that is in thy brothers eye, but considerest not the beame that is in thine owne eye?

</div>

A third reason: It is unreasonable, that having grosser faults your self then these which are in others, ye should passe by your own faults, grosse like beams, without observation, and go pry in upon other mens infirmities, small like motes in comparison: Therfore ye should not judge rashly. Hence learn, that Self-love so blindeth us, that of our own great faults we are not sensible, but the smallest infirmities of others we narrowly pry in upon, and observe them; therefore saith he, *Why beholdest thou the mote,* &c.
