*Ver. 7.* Iesus said unto him, It is written again, Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God.

</div>

Christ answereth the second temptation by Scripture also. *Doct.* 1. We must not esteem the lesse of Scripture, albeit Satan, and his instruments do abuse it; but with the same weapons must we fight against Satan still, and oppose a clear place of Scripture unto such places as the Tempter blowes mist upon; for, *It is written again,* saith our Lord. 2. We should not desire God to give any extraordinary proofs of his care towards us, when he hath after an ordinary manner provided means for our safety; neither should we limit the Lord in any thing: for this is the meaning of *Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God.* 3. What the Scripture speaks indifferently to all, it is to be esteemed as spoken to every singular person, and the singular persons are to be accounted as written in the writing of the generall• for upon this ground, Christ saith, *It is written, Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God,* Because *Deut.* 6.16. it is written, *Ye shall not tempt your God.* 4. Christ as our surety did subject himselfe unto the law, and therefore he doth apply the prcepts <span class="dk-pb" id="p34" data-p="34" data-facs="23" title="Page 34 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 34 页起)"></span> to himself no less then to us; for he saith of himself, Thou shalt not tempt the Lord.
