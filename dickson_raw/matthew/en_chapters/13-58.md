*Ver. 58.* And he did not many mighty works there, because of their unbelief.

</div>

The despising of Christ turneth to the disadvantage of the despiser. *Doct.* 1. Despising of the Ministers prejugeth men of the blessing of the Ministry, and misbelief is punished by the Lords hiding his power from the misbeliever; fortherfore *Christ did not many mighty works among them, because of unbelief*: for as unto the Believer God manifesteth himself, so from the misbeliever God doth hide himself.
