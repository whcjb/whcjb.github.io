*Ver. 29.* Iesus answered, and said unto them, Ye do erre, not knowing the Scriptures, nor the Power of God.

30. For in the resurrection, they neither marry, nor are given in marriage; but are as the angels of God in heaven.

</div>

Christ confuteth them by Scripture. *Doct.* 1. If the Scriptures be not understood and believed, it cannot misse but <span class="dk-pb" id="p255" data-p="255" data-facs="133" title="Page 255 of the 1651 edition begins here (原书第 255 页起)"></span> errours will arise: for nothing else but this light can prevent or remove errours; for, *They erred, not knowing the Scriptures.* 2. It is necessary for quieting our minds in the truth of Gods Word, that we look onely to the promise of God, and to his ability, to performe all what he hath promised; Therefore saith he, *Ye erre, not knowing the Scriptures, nor the Power of God.* 3. After the resurrection, we shall be set free from the infirmities whereunto now we are subject; and shall neither need meat, nor drink, nor marriage, but shall be upholden immediately of God, without means, as Angels are, and shall be imployed onely in the immediate service of God, *as the Angels of Heaven are.*
