*Ver. 12.* And his disciples came, and took up his body and buried it, and went and told Iesus.

</div>

From the history of Johns buriall, Learn, 1. That the faithful must not be ashamed at the suffering of the Saints, but testifie their respect to the living and to the dead; for; *Iohns disciples came and buried Iohns body.* 2. When Pastors are cut off, men must resort to the chiefe shepherd so much the more, at the Disciples of John came to Christ, and *told him of Iohns death.*
