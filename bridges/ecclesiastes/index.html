About this text. The text follows the public-domain transcription of the 1860 New York edition (Robert Carter & Brothers) published by Precept Austin. The transcription does not preserve the author’s footnotes; the stray reference figures it left in the text have been removed. Where the printed book has “Ib.” or “Chap.” in Scripture references, the transcription gives the name of the book. The index at the end of the volume is not reproduced.
