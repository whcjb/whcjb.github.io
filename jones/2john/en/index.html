About this text. The second and third Epistles of St John are expounded at the end of William Jones's Commentary upon the Epistles of Saint Paul to Philemon, and to the Hebrewes (London, 1635; STC 14739.5); see also Jones on 3 John. Text from EEBO-TCP A04619, keyed by hand from the EEBO microfilm (Phase 1, public domain). Original spelling and italics are retained; the long s is printed as s. Page numbers of the original are kept as in-page anchors.

Gaps. The keyers did not transcribe Greek: 38 Greek words or phrases in these pages are marked 〈Greek〉, and 15 other places they could not read are marked in red. They have not yet been checked against the page images.

文本说明。约翰二书、三书的讲解附在琼斯《腓利门书、希伯来书注释》卷末（伦敦 1635 年，STC 14739.5），本站只收这两篇，两卷分开上线（另一卷见 Jones on 3 John）。底本是 EEBO-TCP 人工录入本 A04619（据 EEBO 缩微胶片录入，第一期，已进入公有领域）。保留原书拼写与斜体，长 ſ 作 s；印本页码保留为页内锚点。录入者没有录希腊文，本卷正文 38 处以 〈Greek〉 标出；另有 15 处读不出的字标红。这些都还没有对照原书影像补出，判读做完后再更新本页。
