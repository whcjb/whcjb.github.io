About this text. The second and third Epistles of St John are expounded at the end of William Jones's Commentary upon the Epistles of Saint Paul to Philemon, and to the Hebrewes (London, 1635; STC 14739.5); see also Jones on 3 John. Text from EEBO-TCP A04619, keyed by hand from the EEBO microfilm (Phase 1, public domain). Original spelling and italics are retained; the long s is printed as s. Page numbers of the original are kept as in-page anchors.

Gaps. The keyers left 58 places blank: Greek, which they did not transcribe, and letters they could not read from the film. 50 have been read from the grayscale scan of the same microfilm (Internet Archive), place by place against the page image, with the 1636 issue as a second witness for doubtful places; 7 are editorial conjectures shown [in brackets], each explained in the table below. 1 place remains marked in red (1 unreadable); all are listed below.

  • 残缺补（4 处）：两本同一处字母都残缺或墨淡，按看得见的笔画与上下文补出。
