About this text. The second and third Epistles of St John are expounded at the end of William Jones's Commentary upon the Epistles of Saint Paul to Philemon, and to the Hebrewes (London, 1635; STC 14739.5); see also Jones on 2 John. Text from EEBO-TCP A04619, keyed by hand from the EEBO microfilm (Phase 1, public domain). Original spelling and italics are retained; the long s is printed as s. Page numbers of the original are kept as in-page anchors.

Gaps. The keyers left 64 places blank: Greek, which they did not transcribe, and letters they could not read from the film. 55 have been read from the grayscale scan of the same microfilm (Internet Archive), place by place against the page image, with the 1636 issue as a second witness for doubtful places; 8 are editorial conjectures shown [in brackets], each explained in the table below. 1 place remains marked in red (1 unreadable); all are listed below.

编者推补逐条说明（8 处）

以下各处录入本空着，原书在 1635 年胶片影像上又读不定；编者再拿 1636 年印本（Internet Archive 另一份扫描）逐处看图对照，仍须编者下判断，经本站同意后发布。正文中以 [另一种字体加方括号] 标出，不是原书确定的文字。分三类：

  • 残缺补（2 处）：两本同一处字母都残缺或墨淡，按看得见的笔画与上下文补出。
  • 误植改（4 处）：两本字形一致、看得清楚，是原书印错了；按文意改正，原书印作什么见「原书所见」一栏。
  • 照录（2 处）：两本字形一致，照印本录，但词形可疑，存疑待考。
