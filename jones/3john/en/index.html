About this text. The second and third Epistles of St John are expounded at the end of William Jones's Commentary upon the Epistles of Saint Paul to Philemon, and to the Hebrewes (London, 1635; STC 14739.5); see also Jones on 2 John. Text from EEBO-TCP A04619, keyed by hand from the EEBO microfilm (Phase 1, public domain). Original spelling and italics are retained; the long s is printed as s. Page numbers of the original are kept as in-page anchors.

Gaps. The keyers left 64 places blank: Greek, which they did not transcribe, and letters they could not read from the film. 55 have been read from the grayscale scan of the same microfilm (Internet Archive), place by place against the page image. 9 places remain marked in red (8 proposed readings awaiting approval, 1 unreadable); all are listed below.

文本说明。约翰二书、三书的讲解附在琼斯《腓利门书、希伯来书注释》卷末（伦敦 1635 年，STC 14739.5），本站只收这两篇，两卷分开上线（另一卷见 Jones on 2 John）。底本是 EEBO-TCP 人工录入本 A04619（据 EEBO 缩微胶片录入，第一期，已进入公有领域）。保留原书拼写与斜体，长 ſ 作 s；印本页码保留为页内锚点。录入本空着的 64 处（录入者不录的希腊文与读不出的字），55 处已对照同一卷缩微胶片的灰度扫描（Internet Archive）逐处看图读出；其余 9 处（推断待同意 8、读不出 1）标红留在明面，逐条列在下表。判读记录见 jones_raw/3john/gap_review.tsv。
