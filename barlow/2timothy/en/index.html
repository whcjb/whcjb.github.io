About this text. EEBO-TCP A04220 (London: I. D[awson] for Iohn Bellamie, 1625; STC 1434), keyed by hand from the EEBO microfilm; CC0. This edition covers the first chapter of 2 Timothy only. Original spelling and italics are retained; the long s is printed as s. Page numbers of the original are kept as in-page anchors. The film used by the keyers lacked sixteen openings (two pages each); those pages are supplied from the grayscale scan of the 1625 edition and marked with a dashed rule.

Illegible places. The keyers marked 461 places they could not read. 7 were read from the grayscale scan of the 1625 edition (Internet Archive), place by place against the page image; 0 are editorial conjectures shown [in brackets]. 0 places still unread remain marked in red; all are listed below.
