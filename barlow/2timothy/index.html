About this text. EEBO-TCP A04220 (London: I. D[awson] for Iohn Bellamie, 1625; STC 1434; the engraved title is dated 1624), keyed by hand from the EEBO microfilm; CC0. This edition covers the first chapter of 2 Timothy only. Original spelling and italics are retained; the long s is printed as s. Page numbers of the original are kept as in-page anchors. The film used by the keyers lacked sixteen openings (two pages each); those pages are supplied from the grayscale scan of the 1625 edition and marked with a dashed rule.

Illegible places. The keyers marked 461 places they could not read. 5 were read from the grayscale scan of the 1625 edition (Internet Archive), place by place against the page image; 0 are editorial conjectures shown [in brackets]. 0 places still unread remain marked in red; all are listed below.

文本说明。本书据 EEBO-TCP 人工录入本 A04220（伦敦 1625 年，STC 1434；刻版扉页作 1624 年）。这一版只讲提摩太后书第一章。保留原书拼写与斜体，长 ſ 作 s；印本页码保留为页内锚点。录入所据的胶片漏拍了十六个对开（每处两页），这些页据 1625 年本的胶片灰度扫描补录，正文中以左侧虚线标出。录入本有 461 处读不出的字，5 处对照胶片灰度扫描（Internet Archive）逐处看图读出；0 处为编者推补，以 [方括号] 另色标出；仍读不出的 0 处标红留在明面。推补与读不出的都逐条列在下表，判读记录见 barlow_raw/2timothy/。
