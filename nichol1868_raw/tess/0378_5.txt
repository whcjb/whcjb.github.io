Sec. 77. Of the property of gratefulness to make men
inquisitive.

I. Gratefulness makes men inquisitive.’ All manner
of gratefulness, whether to God or man, hath this
property. When David thought on Jonathan’s
kindness to him, though Jonathan were dead, yet
he inquireth whether ‘any were left of the house of
Saul, that he might shew him kindness for Jona-
than’s sake,’ 2 Sam. ix. 1. And when he considered
how God had established peace to his people, first he
inquireth and consulteth about bringing the ark to
the tabernacle, 1 Chron. xiii. 2, and then adviseth
about building a temple for the Lord, chap. xvii. 1.
