Sec. 75. Of the miature of faith and fear.

X. Faith and fear may be mized together. After
Abraham had so believed as ‘ his faith was counted to
him for righteousness,’ Gen. xv. 6; he said, ‘They
will slay me for my wife’s sake,’ Gen. xx. 11, which
argued much fear. Of faithful Jacob it is said, ‘He
was greatly afraid,’ Gen. xxxii. 7. He that said,
‘T will call upon the Lord and he shall save me,’
Ps. ly. 5, 16, an undoubted evidence of faith, said
also in the very same psalm, ‘ Fearfulness and trem-
bling are come upon me, and horror hath ovyer-
whelmed me,’ an apparent sign of fear. Oft doth
Christ upbraid fear even to his believing disciples,
Mat. viii. 26, xiv. 31; Luke xxiy. 37. Of Peter
it is noted, after that he was endued with an ex-
traordinary measure of faith and other gifts, that
he feared them which were of the circumcision,
Gal. ii. 12.

xii. 9, is true of faith, and of all other graces for
the time of this life, ‘We believe in part.’ So much
