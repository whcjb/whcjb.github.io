general description of a wicked man in these words,
‘The wicked are not so.’

But as the chaff which the wind driveth away.

The prophet David having shewed the difference
betwixt the godly and the wicked by a general
introduction, Jt is not so, cometh now to set out
their estate by a similitude and comparison, where
he compares the wicked to chaff. And it is all one
as if he should say, The wicked and ungodly man is
not like a tree well planted and watered, that bears
good fruit and always flourisheth, but like unto
chaff, which hath no root at all in the earth, no
Juice nor sap, but wants all kind of good fruit and
greenness, so as it is easily scattered and dispersed
