Sec. 114. Of vowing praise to God.

Ver. 18. J will pay my vows unto the Lord now in
the presence of all his people.

The immediate inference of this verse upon the
former, sheweth the principal matter of the prophet’s
vow, for he maketh mention of his vow as of a
motive, the more to provoke him to offer up a sacri-
fice of thanksgiving.? But if he had not vowed such
a sacrifice, what motive could his vow have been
thereto? This approved pattern of the prophet
| giveth eyidence that—

Praise to God is a meet matter to be vowed.

will render praises unto thee,’ Ps. lvi. 12 ;-‘I will
sing praise to thy name for ever, that I may daily
perform my vows,’ Ps. lxi. 8.8 The praises which he
