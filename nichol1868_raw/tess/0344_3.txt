To infer any matter of merit on man’s part, be-
cause a word that sometimes signifieth to repay is
attributed to God in relation to man, is too sandy a
foundation for such a lofty Babel.

To take occasion from hence to set out the bounty
of the Lord would be too impertinent. The word
doth indefinitely set down the grace, favour, mercy, or
goodness of the Lord, without any particular or dis-
tinct respect to extraordinary liberality and bounty.

The principal point here to be noted is, the altera-
tion of the prophet’s disposition. Where before he
was restless, now he returneth to rest.

In expressing hereof three points are observable :

1. The matter.

2. The manner.

3. The motive.

1. The action, return.
