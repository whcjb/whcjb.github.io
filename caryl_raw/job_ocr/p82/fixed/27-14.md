They who having justly gotten, duely divide their Estates, shall hold them; but they who get them unjustly, or hold them basely; shall have others to share and divide them. He that gets honestly, yet keeps covetously, <span class="cy-ocr" title="OCR 存疑：两份扫描都判不了">endangere</span> his wealth to the division of strangers. Solomon speaks this expresly (Pro. 11. 24.) There is that scattereth (he doth not mean it of a prodigal scattering upon our lusts, but of a prudent scattering to good and pious uses; a man may give so much to these that it may be <span class="cy-margin">corrodendo edificat, corrodedo diruit.</span> said he scattereth) and yet encreaseth; and there is that withholdeth more then is meet, but it tendeth te poverty, that is, it tendeth to his own poverty, and may possibly, be a relief to the poverty of others. Now, I say, if he that keeps covetously, what he hath got honestly, endangers it to a division among strangers, how much more he that keeps covetously, what he hath gotten difhonestly? The carnal man in gathering the Riches of this World is but like the beast, he hath the least of it himself, he workes for others; he is like <span class="cy-ocr" title="OCR 存疑：两份扫描都判不了">theAss</span> <span class="cy-ocr" title="OCR 存疑：两份扫描都判不了">orCamel</span> that carries a great burthen of excellent things, of precious Jewels and Rich Commodities, yet never shares of them, but is put off with a little Way or Provender. So <span class="cy-ocr" title="OCR 存疑：据上下文推定，原读 inthis">in this</span> case, while covetous Worldly men burthen and tire themselves out in gathering and heaping up the things of this World, the best of them, the sweet of them is enjoyed by such as they suspected not, much less wished should enjoy them. God provides heirs for them, and puts their wealth into hands that will make better use of it then ever themselves would. This is the Portion of a <span class="cy-ocr" title="OCR 存疑：据上下文推定，原读 wiched">wicked</span> man : the Portion which he hath prepared, neither he nor his shall inherit.
