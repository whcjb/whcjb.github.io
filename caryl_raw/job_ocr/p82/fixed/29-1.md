And that's it which the Lord threatened in very doleful language by the Prophet (Jer. 13. 16.) Give glory to the Lord your God, before he cause darkness, and before your feet stumble upon the dark mountains, and while ye look for light, he turn it into the shadow of death, and make it gross darkness.
