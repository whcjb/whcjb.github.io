The Verbe signifieth to unite or joyn together, the Adverbe which is here used, hath four significations in Scripture. <span class="cy-margin">Verbum univit, unum fecit, in adverbio, simul pariter.</span>

To cleare this a little further, Man may be considered in a threefold state.

First, In the ftate of this life: here, they are both alike, and unlike, We see some good men prosper, & so do many wicked, We see unjust men somtimes troubled, and so are the righteons often. Thus the Lord made a difference to the eye between his people and the Ægyptians by the death of their first born, and by drowning their huge Army in the Sea.
