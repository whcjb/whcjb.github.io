What longing is there for his comming, who having been prà yed to come, promiseth to come quickly, and is yet againe prayed to come. The prepurations and prognostickes of the comming of Christ, are dreadfully set downe, to the amazement of the world, (Luk. 21.25, 26.) There shall be signes in the Sunne, and in the Moene, and in the Starres, and upon the earth, distresse of Nations, &c. mens hearts ( that is, the hearts of the men of the earth, or of earthly minded men) failing them for feare, and fer loohing after those things mhich are comming on the earth, &c. And then spall they see the Sonne of man coming in a Cloud with power and great Glory. This glory and terror of the coming of Christ, which shall dazle the eyes and astonish the hearts of all that know not God, shall be delight to the eyes, and rejoyce the hearts of all that obey him. These Christ bespeakes in the 28h Verse, IVhen these things begin to come te passe, then looke up and lift up your heads ( looking up and listingup the head, is a posture of confidence <span class="cy-pb" id="p356" data-p="356" data-leaf="365" title="Page 356 of the printed part begins here (原书第 356 页起)"></span>and of joy) for your Redemption draweth nigh; That is, you fhall soone receive all the fruits of your recemption, or redemption in full. Our Redemption was wrought when Christ came first into the world, but we reape not all the benefits of our redemption, till Christ comes the second time into the world.

Then they who are dead shall be redeemed from the power of the grave, and they who shall be found alive, shall be redeemed from all the troubles and sorrowes of this life. Then Christ will wipe all teares from the eyes of every afflicted Job, then he will heale all the sores of his Pob’s, then he will supply all the wants, and restore the broken estates of his Tob's; then he will repaire the credit and honour of his peb’s; then he will right ly interpret the actions and speeches, the workes and words of his fob's; then he will give his fob's a full estate, a double estate, a seaven-fold estate to all that they had before; then he will make his Job's like himselfe; they that lay on the dunghill, scraping their fores with a Potsherd, when Christ appeares, spall appeare xith him in glory ( Col. 3. 4.) The discaled and leprous bodies of his Jeb's fhall be fashioned like the glorious body of Iesus Christ, by that mighty pouer by which he is able to subdue all things to himselfe; Yea, when the Redeemer fhall stand upon the earth in the latter day, he will marry all his sob's to himselfe, he will take them from the dust, and lay them in his bosome for ever.
