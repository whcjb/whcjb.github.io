About this text. Cowper, minister at Perth and from 1612 Bishop of Galloway, published this verse-by-verse commentary on the whole of Psalm 119 in 1613 (its running title is The Anatomie of a Christian); each of the twenty-two letters of the Hebrew alphabet is one page here. Verse 80 is passed over in the original edition (verse 79 ends on p. 197, Caph begins on p. 198). The marginal summaries of the original are given beside the text. The text is the keyed transcription of the 1613 edition (EEBO-TCP A19498; released under CC0), with the spelling of the original; only the long s is printed as s. Places the transcribers could not read are shown in red (322 letters, 80 words, 0 longer passages, 14 Greek or Hebrew passages left untranscribed). The author's two prayers before his evening doctrine are given after the exposition; the table of principal points is not included.

文本说明。考珀先任珀斯牧师，1612 年起任加洛韦主教；这部逐节注解全篇诗篇 119 篇的书 1613 年出版（书眉题作「基督徒剖析」）；本站按希伯来字母每八节一页，共 22 页。原书漏讲第 80 节（第 79 节讲到第 197 页，第 198 页即接下一字母）。原书页边的提要排在正文旁。本站正文据 1613 年版的人工录入本（EEBO-TCP A19498，CC0 公共领域授权），拼法照录原书，只把长 s（ſ）印作 s。录入者读不出的地方以红色标出：缺字母 322 个、缺整词 80 处、缺一段以上 0 处，另有希腊文、希伯来文 14 处未录。作者晚间讲道前常用的两篇祷文附在讲解之后；卷末要点索引未收。
