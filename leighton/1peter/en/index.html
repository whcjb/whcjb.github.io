About this text. EEBO-TCP A47642 (Vol. I, York: J. White, 1693, Wing L1028A) and A47643 (Vol. II, London: B.G. for Sam. Keble, 1694), keyed by hand from microfilm; CC0. Original spelling and italics are retained; the long s is printed as s. Page numbers of the original are kept as in-page anchors (each volume numbered separately).

Illegible places. Of the 1,095 places the keyers could not read, 1,054 have been read directly from page images of the same edition (Internet Archive: Princeton Theological Seminary copies), using the 1864 Philadelphia reprint only to locate the passage; the original spelling is kept, including the printer’s own misprints. The rest are listed for approval; any conjecture adopted is shown [in brackets], and places still undecided remain marked in red.

文本说明。本书据 EEBO-TCP 人工录入本：卷一 A47642（约克 1693）、卷二 A47643（伦敦 1694），保留原书拼写与斜体，长 ſ 作 s；两卷印本页码各自从 1 起，保留为页内锚点。录入本有 1,095 处读不出的字，其中 1,054 处已对照同一版原书（普林斯顿神学院藏本扫描）逐处看图判读，1864 年费城重印本只用来定位；原书拼写照录，连原书自己的排印错也照录。其余几十处列清单待定：经同意采用的推补以 [方括号] 另色标出，仍未定的标红留在明面。逐条依据见 leighton_raw/1peter/PROVENANCE.md。
