<p class="op-verse">Verse 1. Out of the depths have I cried unto thee, O Lord.<br>2. Lord, hear my voice; let thine ears be attentive to the voice of my supplications.<br>3. If thou, Lord, shouldest mark iniquities, O Lord, who shall stand<br>4. But <em>there is</em> forgiveness with thee, that thou mayest be feared.<br>5. I wait for the Lord, my soul doth wait, and in his word do I hope.<br>6. My soul <em>waiteth</em> for the Lord more than they that watch for the morning: <em>I say, more than</em> they that watch for the morning.<br>7. Let Israel hope in the Lord: for with the Lord <em>there is</em> mercy, and with him <em>is</em> plenteous redemption.<br>8. And he shall redeem Israel from all his iniquities.</p>
