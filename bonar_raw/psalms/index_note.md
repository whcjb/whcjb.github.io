**About this text.** The text follows the Monergism e-text of Andrew A. Bonar's *Christ and His Church in the Book of Psalms*. That e-text drops the text of the author's footnotes (the reference marks survive). All 154 notes have been restored from the first edition (London: James Nisbet, 1859; Internet Archive scan *christhischurchi00bona*). Each note was transcribed from the page images and matched to its mark in order. Italics, Hebrew and Greek follow the 1859 page. One mark in Psalm 32 is doubled in the e-text; the note is attached to the second mark, where the 1859 edition prints it. A few slips in the 1859 edition itself have been corrected. Every editorial change is listed below. Not yet restored from the 1859 edition: the italics of the main text, the full text of each psalm printed at its head (the e-text keeps only the verse range, e.g. “1–6”), and the marginal headings.

**文本说明。** 正文字句据 Monergism 网页录入本。该本没有作者的页脚注注文（注号还在），全部 154 条脚注据 1859 年伦敦初版（James Nisbet 出版；IA 扫描本 *christhischurchi00bona*）逐条看原图转录，并按次序与录入本注号配对。斜体、希伯来文、希腊文照初版。录入本第 32 篇有一个注号重复了，注文挂在初版印注号的第二处。初版本身的几处排印错误也已改正。初版里另有三样尚未补回：正文斜体、每篇开头所印的整篇经文（录入本只留「1–6」这样的节号范围）、页边小标题。初版排印错误的改正如下：

- 第 19 条脚注引奥古斯丁："et am potest dicere"，初版脱一字母，改作 "et jam potest dicere"（奥古斯丁《诗篇讲解》原句作 *et iam potest dicere*）。
- 第 30 条脚注论诗 22:16 "他们扎了我的手、我的脚"：初版把 Keri、Ketibh 两个名目印反了。据该注下文所引 Jacob ben Haiim 的玛索拉注，"有些抄本"写作 כארו（Ketibh），读作 כארי（Keri），因此对调这两个名目；注里"前者／后者"的讲法不受影响。
- 第 51 条脚注："objeets"改作 "objects"（排印错字）。
