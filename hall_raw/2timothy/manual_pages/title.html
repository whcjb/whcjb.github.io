A Practical and Polemical
COMMENTARY
Or,
EXPOSITION
UPON
The Third and Fourth Chapters of the latter
Epistle of Saint Paul to
TIMOTHY.
WHEREIN
The Text is explained, Some Controversies dis-
cussed, Sundry Cases of Conscience are cleared,
Many Common Places are Succinctly handled,
and Divers Usefull, and Seasonable
Observations raised,
By THOMAS HALL, B. D. and
Minister of Kings-Norton in Worcester-shire.
