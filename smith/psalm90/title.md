Wherein

<p class="sx-item" markdown="1">1. The Sum and Scope.</p>

<p class="sx-item" markdown="1">2. The Doctrines.</p>

<p class="sx-item" markdown="1">3. The Reasons.</p>

<p class="sx-item" markdown="1">4. The uses of most Texts are observed.</p>
