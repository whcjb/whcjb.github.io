About this text. Smith published this exposition of Psalm 90 late in life (the title page calls him "author of Davids Repentance and the Great Assize, and yet living"). For the title and each verse he sets out the sum and scope, the doctrines, the reasons and the uses; each is one page here. The text is the keyed transcription of the 1656 edition (EEBO-TCP A93404; released under CC0), with the spelling of the original; only the long s is printed as s. Places the transcribers could not read are shown in red (128 letters, 8 words, 4 longer passages, 0 Greek or Hebrew passages left untranscribed). The table of principal doctrines is given after the exposition.

文本说明。史密斯晚年出版这部诗篇 90 篇讲解（扉页称他为「《大卫的悔改》《大审判》的作者，至今健在」）。诗题与每节都先讲大意与要旨，再列教义、理由、应用；本站诗题与各节各一页。本站正文据 1656 年版的人工录入本（EEBO-TCP A93404，CC0 公共领域授权），拼法照录原书，只把长 s（ſ）印作 s。录入者读不出的地方以红色标出：缺字母 128 个、缺整词 8 处、缺一段以上 4 处，另有希腊文、希伯来文 0 处未录。卷末「要义一览」附在讲解之后。
