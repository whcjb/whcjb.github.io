About this text. Smith's exposition of the first Psalm, "directing a man to true happinesse", was first published in 1614 and went through many editions; this is the second edition of 1616. The opening discourse on the Book of Psalms and each verse are one page here (verses 1 and 3, being long, are divided by the printed pages), and the morning and evening prayers follow. The copy that was transcribed lacks 34 pages in six places; five of these are supplied, in framed blocks, from the 1868 Edinburgh reprint (James Nichol), which follows a later enlarged edition in modern spelling, read by OCR and checked against its page images. The sixth, two pages of the morning prayer, cannot be supplied, because the later edition rewrote the prayer. The text is the keyed transcription of the 1616 edition (EEBO-TCP B00721; released under CC0), with the spelling of the original; only the long s is printed as s. Places the transcribers could not read are shown in red (2 letters, 0 words, 0 longer passages, 0 Greek or Hebrew passages left untranscribed).

文本说明。史密斯讲诗篇第一篇「引人到真幸福」的这部书 1614 年初版，此后多次重印，这是 1616 年的第二版。本站开头论诗篇一卷书的总论与各节各一页（第 1、3 节讲得长，按原书页码分页），晨祷、晚祷附后。录入所据那一册有六处共缺 34 页：五处据 1868 年爱丁堡 Nichol 重印本补入，在页面上加框标出——该本据后来的增订版、拼法已现代化，由 OCR 识读并对照影像核过；第六处是晨祷中间两页，后来的版次把祷文改写了，无从补入。本站正文据 1616 年版的人工录入本（EEBO-TCP B00721，CC0 公共领域授权），拼法照录原书，只把长 s（ſ）印作 s。录入者读不出的地方以红色标出：缺字母 2 个、缺整词 0 处、缺一段以上 0 处，另有希腊文、希伯来文 0 处未录。
