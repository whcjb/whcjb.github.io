About this text. Smith, minister of Roxwell in Essex, first preached this exposition of Psalm 51 to his congregation and published it, as he says, in the spare hours of a long sickness; this second edition of 1614 was "profitably amplified by the Authour". Each part of the book, from the title of the psalm to the last verse, is one page here. The text is the keyed transcription of the 1614 edition (EEBO-TCP A12516; released under CC0), with the spelling of the original; only the long s is printed as s. Places the transcribers could not read are shown in red (176 letters, 3 words, 2 longer passages, 0 Greek or Hebrew passages left untranscribed).
