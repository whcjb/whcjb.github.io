About this text. Greenhill delivered this exposition as weekly lectures in London and published it in five volumes between 1645 and 1662. The text here is the keyed transcription of the first editions made by the Text Creation Partnership from the EEBO microfilms (Vol. 1 (ch. 1–5, 1645): A85666; Vol. 2 (ch. 6–13, 1649): A85667; Vol. 3 (ch. 14–19, 1651): A42015; Vol. 4 (ch. 20–29, 1658): A85668; Vol. 5 (ch. 30–48, 1662): A42016), released under CC0. Original spelling and italics are retained; the long s is printed as s. Page numbers of the original are kept as in-page anchors; each volume is paged from 1. The commentary is divided here into passages of a few verses each, following the sections of the original where the transcription marks them; the fourth volume (chapters 20–29), which has no such divisions in the transcription, is divided at the verses it quotes. The marginal heads of the original (Note 1, Observ. 1 …) are shown as small tags. The errata lists, the tables of matters and of Scripture places, and the booksellers’ advertisements are not included.

Illegible places. The transcription is incomplete at 3,286 places: letters or words the keyers could not read from the film images, and the Greek and Hebrew, which they did not transcribe. These are being checked against the EEBO film images of the same editions (Internet Archive). 1,759 have been read from the page images so far; the rest remain marked in red.
