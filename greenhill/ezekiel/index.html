About this text. Greenhill delivered this exposition as weekly lectures in London and published it in five volumes between 1645 and 1662. The text here is the keyed transcription of the first editions made by the Text Creation Partnership from the EEBO microfilms (Vol. 1 (ch. 1–5, 1645): A85666; Vol. 2 (ch. 6–13, 1649): A85667; Vol. 3 (ch. 14–19, 1651): A42015; Vol. 4 (ch. 20–29, 1658): A85668; Vol. 5 (ch. 30–48, 1662): A42016), released under CC0. Original spelling and italics are retained; the long s is printed as s. Page numbers of the original are kept as in-page anchors; each volume is paged from 1. The commentary is divided here into passages of a few verses each, following the sections of the original where the transcription marks them; the fourth volume (chapters 20–29), which has no such divisions in the transcription, is divided at the verses it quotes. The marginal heads of the original (Note 1, Observ. 1 …) are shown as small tags. The errata lists, the tables of matters and of Scripture places, and the booksellers’ advertisements are not included.

Illegible places. The transcription is incomplete at 3,286 places: letters or words the keyers could not read from the film images, and the Greek and Hebrew, which they did not transcribe. They have been filled in two steps.

(1) Read from the film. Each place was checked against the EEBO film images of the same editions (Internet Archive): 1,758 could be read there and are printed as plain text.

(2) Checked against a second copy. At 199 places the film itself is blotted or show-through covers the letters, and only a conjecture was possible. The film, and the TCP keyers who worked from it, are one and the same copy, so each conjecture was checked against a copy printed from a different original: the Edinburgh reprint of 1863 (J. Nichol, ed. James Sherman; Internet Archive anexpositionofth00greeuoft). 159 places are filled from that check and shown in [brackets]: 144 where the reprint has the same word as the conjecture (the original spelling is kept); 6 where the reprint differs and is followed (chapter and verse numbers restored to the original style); 4 where the reprint drops the passage but its English rendering or verse range confirms the conjecture. At 5 places a second look at the film settled the reading where the reprint had altered it; there the film is followed, including an evident misprint of the original (fifteen thousand miles), which is kept. The reprint modernises spelling, so it confirms the word, not the original spelling.

(3) Still open. The remaining places stay in red. They include 37 where the reprint omits the Latin, Greek or marginal note altogether (every one checked on the reprint’s page images), 1 where the film and the reprint disagree, and the places in Volumes I–II, for which no film of the same edition is available.
