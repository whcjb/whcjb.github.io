### <span class="gh-pb" data-facs="2"></span><span class="gh-pb" data-facs="2"></span>TO THE Christian Reader. AND Especially to those, who helped on and countenanced THE EXPOSITORIE-WOORK.

<span class="gh-dropcap">T</span>His is the fift and last part upon *Ezekiel;* which was finished in the latter end of the year *1654.* and hath been latent full seven years, like *Josiah* in the Temple: but now through the instigation of learned and godly Ministers, some others, and considerations of mine own cometh to publick view. *Ezekiel* prophesied unto the *Jews,* who were then the *only people* God <span class="gh-pb" data-facs="3"></span> had in the world, and honoured with singular Titles, *Sons* of the *living God* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Hos. 1.10</span> *his first born* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Exod. 4.22.</span> *his inheritance* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Isa. 19.29.</span> *his peculiar Treasure* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Ps. 134 4.</span> *his pleasant Portion* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Jer. 12.10.</span> *a noble Vine* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Jer. 2.21.</span> *the holy flock* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Ezek. 36 38.</span> *a chosen generation* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Isa 41.11.9. 1 Pet. 2 8</span> *the Lords glory* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Ps. 78.61.</span> *the dearly beloved of his Soul* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Ier. 12.7.</span> & indued with great *Priviledges* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Rom. 9 45.</span> yet were they not exempted from afflictive *and severe sufferings;* they were a *Reproach* to the Heathen, a *mocking* to all Countries, and much *vexed* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Ezek. 22.15.</span> The Pestilence, Sword and Famine *consumed them,* so that the *pitiful women* did *eat their own children* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Lam 4.10.</span> and *pluckt* off their own *breasts* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Ezek. 23.3<span class="gh-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>.</span> the *Priests* and *Elders* gave up the *ghost,* while they sought for *meat,* to releive their *souls* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Lam. 1.19.</span> those brought up in *Scarlet* imbraced *dunghils* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Lam. 4.5.</span> the *women* were *ravished* in Sion, the *maids* in the Cities of *Judah,* and *Princes* were *hanged* up by their *hands* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Lam. 5.11.12.</span> they could not walk safely in the *Streets* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Lam. 4.18.</span> their *necks* were under *persecution* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Lam. 5.5.</span> *Lyons Tiglathpileser,* and *Shalmaneser,* took away the ten Tribes, and *Nebuchadnezzer broke* the *bones* of *Judah* and *Benjamin* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Ier. 50.17.</span> He *burnt* the *Temple* and *Citie,* he ruined *Church* and *State,* and carried the *Jews* into *Captivity,* where they suffered *unspeakable* miserie. The Prophet tels us, that their *punishment* was greater then the Sodomites. <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Lam. 4 6.</span> That which *chiefly provoked* God to deal thus *severely* with them, as *Ezekiel,* and others testifie, was their *leaving* his *pure worship, Ordinances* and *Institutions,* falling to *false* wayes of *worship* and *compliance* with the *Nations.* They had the *Image* of *Jealousie,* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Ezek. 8.3.</span> the *Altar* of *Damascus,* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">2 King. 16.</span> the *Statutes* of *Omri,* <span class="gh-pb" data-facs="3"></span> <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Mic. 6.16.</span> *High places* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">2 Chron. 20.33.</span> *Traditions* and *Statutes* of their *Fathers* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Ezek. 20.18. Ier. 9.14.</span> *customes* of the Nations, they set their posts with Gods posts; worshiped with their faces toward the East: <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Ezek. 8.16.</span> they would be *as* the *Heathens* and walk after their own *Imaginations* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Ier. 18 12.</span> whereas they should have walked in Gods Laws & Statutes <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Ezek. 20.19.</span> *when* therefore the *house* of *Israel degenerated into dross,* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Ezek. 20.32.</span> God gathered them into the *Furnace,* & poured out his fury upon them: <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Ezek. 22.19.20.21.22.</span> *Apostatizing* from the *pure worship* of God, to the superstitious *Rites* and *Ceremonies* of men, *provokes bitterly;* It is a *Reproach* to God, his *Worship, Truth* and *Ordinances,* it is a *violaon* of former promises, it is high *Ingratitude* and *Rebellion* against God, it is a great *scandal* to all the godly, especially the *weak,* it *encourages, justifies* and *hardens* the wicked, it *ruines souls, gratifies Satan,* it *exposeth* men to *visible,* and *unavoidable* judgements. When the *Jews* purposed to leave the *Lords Tents,* and fall to *Babylonish Temples,* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Ezek. 20.22.23.</span> Then did he *swear,* that he would *rule* over them with *Furie.* This should caution us to keep *distance* from *inward approbation* of, <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">χαράγματος appellatione, omnia ea intelliguntur quae homines: colendo, aut representando numini solent comminisci, *Bez.* vid. *Leigh* in Verbo</span> and *outward compliance* with, either *Jewish Ceremonies,* or *Romish Rites;* The first are *abrogated* by the *Lord:* and it may be *Jericho* work to *raise* them up again; The other are the *Mark* of the *Beast,* and its not safe to bear that *Mark,* Rev. *14.9, 16.2, God* is a *jealous* God, and would not have his to *Symbolize* with Idolatrous: or superstitious practices; He forbad his people to take, or desire in their hearts the *silver* and *gold* was upon *Idols;* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">χάραγμα</span> they might not *touch* with their *hearts* or *hands,* any thing belonged to them, they might not do as other *Nations,* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Deut. 7.25.</span> <span class="gh-pb" data-facs="4"></span> nor enquire after their *gods,* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Levit. 18.2.</span> and *mode* of worship, *Deut. 12.30.* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">*In Treatise of Idolatr.* chap. 11. §. 1.</span> They might not be like the Nations in *Apparrel* and *Hair,* saith Maymonie, much more not in the service, and Worship of God; we should not be as other Nations, but keep Gods *Ordinances,* and *walk* therein. <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Levit. 18.</span> *Symbolizing* with Idolatrous worship, persons or things, <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Isa. 52.11.</span> God *allows* not. In Babylon they might not *touch* any *unclean* thing, and shall we in *Sion,* where all is to be pure according to the mind of the Holy one of Sion? This made that great Apostle *Paul* say, <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">2 Cor. 6 17.</span> *touch* not the *unclean* thing (whatsoever is from man in the worship of God hath *aliquid faecis & terrae,* like the man whence it comes and is an unclean thing) & if *non-touching* do cause *Suffering, casting* out, or off, God wil *receive,* be a Father & *recompense sufferings.* God would not have his *touch* or *countenance* any corruption in worship. When the *Damascene Altar* was in the Temple, <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1"><span class="gh-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>ings 16.14. Ezek. 9.2.</span> and Gods *Altar* removed out of its place, our *Saviour* and those with him did not *appear* at *that Altar,* lest they should seem to countenance it, but at the *Brason Altar,* though deserted and despised. As Christ will own nothing but what is *appointed* of the Lord; so those are with him will not own the *susuperstitious appointments* of men, but cleave to the *Institutions* of God, though out of place, and despised; The *Papists* say of the *Protestants,* that in matters of *Religion,* in *praying, reading* their books, hearing their *Sermons,* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Rehmist. on epist of *Iohn v. 10,*</span> presence at their *service,* partaking of their *Sacraments,* and all other *communicating* with them in *spiritual things,* is a great, *damnable sin.* If they hold it so sinfull, to *communicate* with us in the *Institutions* of Christ, shall we *Symbolize* with them in the *Superstitions* <span class="gh-pb" data-facs="4"></span> of *Antichrist.* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Fidel bus fas <span class="gh-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">〈◊〉</span> est ullo symbolo ostendere, sibi cumsu<span class="gh-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>e stitio<span class="gh-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>s esse con sensum, in *Ps. 16.*</span> Judicious *Calvin* saith it is unlawful for Beleevers, to manifest by any *Rite,* that they *Symbolize* with *superstitious ones. Mr. Paget* tels us, that *Temples* wherein *Idols,* and Idolatrous Service are still retained, and dayly practiced; cannot *lawfully* be *frequented,* nor *walked* in, <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">In his arrow against separat. pag. 319.</span> because of of the *wo,* incurred by reason of *scandal,* Mat. *18.7. Marcus* Bishop of *Arethusa,* having pul'd down an Idolatrous *Temple,* at the command of *Constantine,* and being put upon it by *Julian,* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">*Theodor.* l. 3. cap. 6. *juxta finem.*</span> either to build it up again, or pay for building of it; he denied both, a *small summe* was demanded, his answer was, it is as great wickedness to give a *half-penny* towards such a work, as to give the whole; he *suffered great tortures,* rather then he would contribute ὀβολὸν one half-penny to *promote Idolatry,* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Arrow against seperat pag. 253.</span> and the forenamed *Paget* holds it to be the dutie of a Christian man rather to die then give any thing, for the furtherance of Idolatry, though it were but a half-penny. <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">De cerona militis.</span> *Tertullian* defends and commends the *Christian Souldiers* that would not wear a *Crown* of *Laurel,* like the Heathens: and *Rhenanus* in his argument before that Booke of *Tertul.* saith *curandum fuit Christianis ne profanis, Ethnicorum ritibus subscribere viderentur;* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Mor. same quam Idol<span class="gh-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>thritist esci August. de <span class="gh-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>ono conjug<span class="gh-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>l. *c. 16.*</span> It concerned the Christians to take heed of *complying* with their *Rites.* Another of the Fathers saith it were better to be *starved* to *death* than to *eat* things *offered to Idols:* Those take part in the ceremonies of Idolaters offer to *Devils,* and joyn themselves to the service of Idols, *1 Cor. 10.*

*Reader,* it is *dangerous* to *Symbolize* with the *Superstitions Rites* and *inventions* of men. Do not *Judaize,* <span class="gh-pb" data-facs="5"></span> do not *Gentilize,* do not *Romanize* but see you *Christianize. Nothing* in Worship pleaseth God but what is *his own;* what man brings is *spurious, pollutes* his Ordinances, and *frustrates* his commands, *Math. 15.6.* That *comes* from *God,* is *set up* by him, *carries to him,* is *pure,* and *approved* of by him. *False* Worship, and *mixtures* with the pure Ordinances of God, are as *smoak* to his eyes, *Vineger* to his teeth, <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Ezek. 8.6.</span> an *abomination* to his spirit, and when they come into his *Sanctuary,* he goeth out of it and far from it. God had chosen *Sion* to dwell in, *Psal. 132.13.14.* <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Ezek. 9.3.11.23.</span> There was the glory, but when the *people* grew prophane, the *Princes,* and *Priests corrupted his worship,* then the *Glorious Lord,* and *Glory* of the Lord, left them. Gods *Worship* is his *Name;* and that is so dear unto him, that he will not endure any humane mixtures therewith, but shew *vehement Indignation* against them, <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">*Hos. 4.15. Ier 23.16.* Per ministeristerium verbale, *saith. Boderianus* λογικὴν λατρείαν id est κατὰ λόγον Leigh in critica. sacr. service of the word id. in Rom. *12.1.* if <span class="gh-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">〈Greek〉</span> be the milke of the word <span class="gh-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">〈Greek〉</span> it the service of the Word.</span> and had rather mens *souls* and *bodies* should perish, then his *Name* be polluted; go ye serve every man his Idols, but pollute ye my holy Name no more, &c. *Ezek. 20.39.*

Keep off therefore from false Worship, and mixtures of men; you are not to come *there,* you are not to *hear those teach* such things, but to decline them as *Serpents* in the way, and as *poyson* in your meats, you are to feed on nothing, but the *sincere* milk of the Word, and to Worship God only with &#42; *Word-service* Rom. *12.1.* Such as is appointed and ordered by the Word, not contrarie to, or besides it. The jealousie of the Lord about matters of his worship appeared eminently in *Ezekiels Temple* (which represented the Church under the Gospel) where <span class="gh-pb" data-facs="5"></span> nothing was to be of the *Priests devising;* but the *Rule* was, they *shall keep my Laws* and my *Statutes* in *all my Assemblies,* and they shall hallow my *Sabbaths,* Ezek. *44.24.* The *Assembiles* are the Lords, in them must be, not *mens,* but *his Lawes,* and *Statutes,* otherwise his *Assemblies* are robbed, and wronged, his *Sabbaths polluted,* and *profaned.* Some say *Imperium sequitur Ecclesia; the false Church* may, but the True *Church, sequitur Christum,* It follows Christ; His *sheep* hear his *voice,* and not *Strangers.* They say Christ we know, and *Paul* we know, but who are *you?* They will not *comply with false* wayes, and *mixtures,* but *stand* for pure worship, and pure Ordinances, <span class="gh-margin" markdown="1">Vt caelum terrae m sceri contingu, merito tat<span class="gh-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•</span>m plu<span class="gh-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">••</span>s esse nobis debet sincerus Dei cultas & sacro sancta ejus veritas, in quo nobis reposita est <span class="gh-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">〈◊〉</span> <span class="gh-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">•••</span>u<span class="gh-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">▪</span> quam centum mundi. *Calv. Epist. Com. in Tot. Col. 2 <span class="gh-gap" title="TCP: illegible in source">••</span>.*</span> saying Let heaven and *earth* be *confounded,* yet the *sincere worship of God,* and his holy *Truth* in which eternal life is laid up for us, *deservedly* ought to be, and shall be more dear unto us then an *hundred worlds.* Happie be the *souls* are so *resolved,* they will *buy* the *Truth* what ever it cost them, and it will be no *Symonie;* they shall find in Gods pure wayes where nothing of man is, Gods gracious presence, large discoveries of himself, *transformation* into his *Image, intimate communion* with him, sensation of his *goodness* strong *consolations,* and *increasments* with the *increases of God,* which will make all *losses* and *sufferings,* and fit men for the *New heavens,* new *earth,* wherein shall dwell *righteousness,* which the Lord *hasten,* and for which let us *beleevingly,* and *patiently wait,* giving diligence that we may be found of him in *peace,* without *spot,* and *blameless.*

Reader, if thou hast found any *benefit* by the four <span class="gh-pb" data-facs="6"></span> former *parts* uppon *Ezekiel,* or shalt *gain* any by this, give God all the *Glory,* who hath *led* me through the *difficulties* and *depths* of this Prophet, and vouchsafe him an *interest* in thy prayers, who hath *spent* his *strength* for thee, and be at some *pains* to read what he hath taken *much,* and *long paines* to *write.* The faithful God, who makes good his promises, put *his spirit* into *thee,* and cause thee to walk in *his Statutes,* to keep *his Judgements,* and do them. So prayeth.
