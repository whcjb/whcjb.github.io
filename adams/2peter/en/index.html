About this text. EEBO-TCP A00665 (London, 1633; STC 108), keyed by hand from the British Library copy on microfilm; CC0. Original spelling and italics are retained; the long s is printed as s. Page numbers of the original are kept as in-page anchors (Vol. II continues from 801).

Illegible places. The keyers marked about 11,000 places they could not read. Most were supplied from the same place in the 1862 Edinburgh reprint (ed. James Sherman), checked against page images, keeping the 1633 spelling; Latin, Greek and passages Sherman rewrote were read from the 1633 microfilm itself. Where the letters are lost in the gutter of the film, the reading is an editorial conjecture shown [in brackets] (785); places still undecided remain marked in red (428).

文本说明。本书据 EEBO-TCP 人工录入本 A00665（伦敦 1633，STC 108，大英图书馆藏本缩微胶片），保留原书拼写与斜体，长 ſ 作 s；印本页码保留为页内锚点（卷二从 801 起）。录入本约 11,000 处读不出的字：多数按 1862 年爱丁堡重印本（Sherman 修订）同一位置读出、看图核对，拼法照 1633 原书；拉丁、希腊文和 Sherman 改写过的地方对照 1633 原书胶片判读。落在胶片装订缝黑边里、原图读不出的，按上下文与引文出处推补，以 [方括号] 另色标出（785 处）；仍未定的标红留在明面（428 处）。逐条依据见 adams_raw/2peter/PROVENANCE.md。
