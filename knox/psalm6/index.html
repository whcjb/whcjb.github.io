A FORT FOR THE AFFLICTED,
IN AN
EXPOSITION UPON THE SIXTH PSALM OF DAVID,

Wherein Is Declared His Cross, Complaints, And Prayers. Most Necessary To Be Read Of All Them For Their Singular Comfort That, Under The Banner Of Christ, Are By Satan Assaulted, And Feel The Heavy Burden Of Sin With Which They Are Oppressed.

The patient abiding of the sore afflicted was never yet confounded.

Part1Letter · Argument · Verse 1
6 January 1554 Part2Verses 2–10 · Closing letter
from Dieppe, February 1554

About this text. Knox wrote this exposition in 1554 for his mother-in-law, Elizabeth Bowes, then in deep spiritual distress; the first part was finished on 6 January 1554 in England, the second at the end of February at Dieppe. The text is that of Select Practical Writings of John Knox, issued by the Free Church of Scotland's committee for the publication of the works of Scottish Reformers and Divines (Edinburgh, 1845), as given in the AGES Digital Library. That edition's six footnotes are printed at the end of each part; in the AGES text the second and following note marks are misnumbered, and they are here matched to their notes in order of occurrence.

文本说明。诺克斯 1554 年为岳母伊丽莎白·鲍斯（Elizabeth Bowes）写下这篇讲解，当时她正陷在深重的属灵忧苦里。 第一部分 1554 年 1 月 6 日在英格兰写成，第二部分同年 2 月底在法国迪耶普写完寄出。正文据 AGES 数字图书馆所收 《诺克斯实用著作选》（苏格兰自由教会大会「苏格兰改教家与神学家著作出版委员会」编，爱丁堡 1845）。 该版的 6 条脚注排在各部分之末；AGES 本从第二个注号起印错，本站按出现顺序与注文对齐。
