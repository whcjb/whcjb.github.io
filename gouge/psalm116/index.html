About this text. Gouge, for forty-five years minister of St Anne's, Blackfriars, published this commentary on Psalm 116 in 1632 and dedicated it to Robert, Lord Rich, Earl of Warwick, and to his parishioners. The text given here is that of the Edinburgh reprint of 1868 (Nichol's Series of Commentaries), read by OCR from the Princeton copy (Internet Archive davidsblessedman00smit) and checked against a second, independent OCR, against the 1632 edition where its keyed transcription survives (EEBO-TCP A01981, which lacks about a third of the book), and against the page images. The opening sections on the whole psalm and each verse are one page; Gouge's 128 numbered sections are given as headings, and the notes at the foot of the 1868 pages are collected at the end of each page. The page numbers of the 1868 edition are marked in the text. Hebrew words, which OCR cannot read, are marked in red. OCR errors still remain, chiefly in the italic passages (verse texts and section headings); they are being corrected against the page images.

文本说明。古治在伦敦黑衣修士区圣安妮堂牧会四十五年，1632 年出版这部诗篇 116 篇注释，献给沃里克伯爵罗伯特·里奇勋爵及本堂会众。本站正文据 1868 年爱丁堡 Nichol《清教徒注释丛书》重印本，用普林斯顿藏本的扫描件（IA davidsblessedman00smit）OCR 识读，并与另一份独立 OCR、1632 年原版的录入本（EEBO-TCP A01981，缺全书约三分之一）及页面影像对照校过。开头论全篇的几节一页，每节经文一页；古治全书编号的 128 小节作小标题，1868 年版页脚的注收在各页之末；文中标出 1868 年版页码。希伯来文 OCR 读不出，以红字标出。目前仍有 OCR 错误，主要在斜体段落（经文与小节标题），正在对照页面影像逐页校正。
