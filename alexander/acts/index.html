About this text. The wording follows the Monergism Books e-text (2022) of Alexander's The Acts of the Apostles Explained. That e-text drops all italics, the verse of the English Bible printed in large type before each comment, and the text of the author's footnotes. These have been restored from three scans of the first edition (New York: Charles Scribner, 1857; Internet Archive: Princeton Theological Seminary copies actsofapostlesex01alex/02alex and actsofapostlese01alex/02alex, University of California copy actsofapostlesex01alexiala/02alexiala): italics by a word-for-word majority of the three scans; the verse headings from the scans, with misread letters corrected against the King James text and doubtful places checked on the page images; the footnotes transcribed from the page images. Scripture references in the comments are in the modern form of the e-text (3:2, where the original prints 3, 2).
