About this text. The wording follows the Precept Austin e-text of Alexander's The Gospel according to Matthew Explained (from the 1864 printing; Greek and Hebrew intact). That e-text drops all italics, the verse of the English Bible printed in large type before each comment, and the text of the author's footnotes (the reference marks survive). These have been restored from three scans (Internet Archive, Princeton Theological Seminary copies: gospelaccordingt00alex and gospelaccordin00alex, New York: Charles Scribner, 1861; gospelaccordingt00alex_0, 1864): italics by a word-for-word majority of the three scans; the verse headings from the 1861 scan, with misread letters corrected against the King James text and doubtful places checked on the page images; the footnotes transcribed from the page images and matched to the marks in order. As the preface explains, the notes are complete only to the end of chapter 16; for chapters 17–28 the author left an analysis of each chapter.
