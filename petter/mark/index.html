About this text. EEBO-TCP A54583 (London: J. Streater for George Sawbridge, 1661; Wing P1888), keyed by hand from the EEBO microfilm of the British Library copy; CC0. That copy is imperfect: it lacks everything after p. 884, so the transcription ends with chapter 11 and the commentary on chapters 12–16 is wanting. Original spelling and italics are retained; the long s is printed as s. Page numbers of the original are kept as in-page anchors. Each page is one lecture, as preached at Brede from June 1618 onward; the date in the margin of each text is the author’s own. The marginal heads of the original (Observ., Use, Quest., Answ. …) are shown as small tags in the text. Two lecture headings disagree with the text they introduce (7:33–34 headed “33, 24”; 8:31 headed “Mark 7. 31.”); they are left as transcribed, and the pages are named by the true verse.

Illegible places. The keyers marked 1,581 places they could not read from the film images. 175 have been read from the page images so far; 0 are editorial conjectures shown [in brackets]. The rest remain marked in red.
