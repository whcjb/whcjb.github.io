<div class="pt-tp">
<div class="pt-tp-l s3">A Learned, Pious, and Practical</div>
<div class="pt-tp-l s0">COMMENTARY,</div>
<div class="pt-tp-l s3">Upon the</div>
<div class="pt-tp-l s00">GOSPEL</div>
<div class="pt-tp-l s3">According to</div>
<div class="pt-tp-l s0">S<sup>t.</sup> MARK:</div>
<div class="pt-tp-l s2 it ls">WHEREIN</div>
<div class="pt-tp-l s2">The Sacred Text is Logically Analysed;</div>
<div class="pt-tp-l s3">The meaning of the holy Spirit clearly and soundly opened:</div>
<div class="pt-tp-l s4">Doctrines naturally raised, strongly confirmed, vindicated from Exceptions, and excellent Inferences deduced from them:</div>
<div class="pt-tp-l s4">All seeming Differences in the History between this and the other Evangelists fairly reconciled:</div>
<div class="pt-tp-l s4">Many important Cases of Conscience, Judiciously, Succinctly, and Perspicuously solved.</div>
<hr class="pt-tp-rule">
<div class="pt-tp-l s5">By that Laborious and Faithful Servant of Christ, Mr. <i>George Petter,</i> late Minister of the Gospel at <i>Bread</i> in <i>Sussex.</i></div>
<hr class="pt-tp-rule">
<div class="pt-tp-motto c">
<p>Luke 24. 32. <i>Did not our heart burn within us, &amp;c. while he opened to us the Scriptures?</i></p>
<p>2 Tim. 4. 11. <i>Take</i> Mark <i>and bring him with thee: For he is profitable to me for the Ministry.</i></p>
</div>
<hr class="pt-tp-rule">
<div class="pt-tp-motto">
<p><i>Non potuit latere Marcum quid scriberet: Nec enim inexpers erat lectionis, qui ab infantiâ divinis literis imbutus &amp; exercitatus erat in lege, maximè cum Apostolos sit secutus.</i> Augustin. <i>Operum Tom.</i> 4. <i>in part.</i> 2. <i>Quæstionum ex Novo Testamento Col.</i> 738. <i>Edit.</i> Basil. 1569.</p>
<p><i>Habebat</i> [Paulus] <i>Titum interpretem: Sicut &amp; Beatus Petrus, Marcum. Cujus Evangelium, Petro narrante, &amp; illo scribente, compositum est.</i> Hieronym. <i>Hedibiæ quæst.</i> 11. <i>part.</i> 154. <i>Tom.</i> 3. <i>Edit.</i> Lugdun. 1530.</p>
