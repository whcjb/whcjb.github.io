<span class="pt-pb" data-facs="1"></span>A Learned, Pious, and Practical COMMENTARY, Upon the GOSPEL According to S*t.* MARK: *WHEREIN* The Sacred Text is Logically Analysed;

The meaning of the holy Spirit clearly and soundly opened: Doctrines naturally raised, strongly confirmed, vindicated from Exceptions, and excellent Inferences deduced from them: All seeming Differences in the History between this and the other Evangelists fairly reconciled: Many important Cases of Conscience, Judiciously, Succinctly, and Perspicuously solved.

By that Laborious and Faithful Servant of Christ, Mr. *George Petter,* late Minister of the Gospel at *Bread* in *Sussex.*

<p class="pt-quote" markdown="1">Luke 24. 32.</p>

<p class="pt-quote" markdown="1">Did not our heart burn within us, &c. while he opened to us the Scriptures?</p>

<p class="pt-quote" markdown="1">2 Tim. 4. 11.</p>

<p class="pt-quote" markdown="1">Take *Mark* and bring him with thee: For he is profitable to me for the Ministry.</p>

<p class="pt-quote" markdown="1">Non potuit latere Marcum quid scriberet: Nec enim inexpers erat lectionis, qui ab infantiâ divinis literis imbutus & exercitatus erat in lege, maximè cum Apostolos sit secutus.</p>

<p class="pt-quote" markdown="1">*Augustin.*Operum Tom. 4. in part. 2. Questionum ex Novo Testamento Col. 738. *Edit. Basil.* 1569.</p>

<p class="pt-quote" markdown="1">Habebat *[Paulus]* Titum interpretem: Sicut & Beatus Petrus, Marcum. Cujus Evangelium, Petro narrante, & illo scribente, compositum est.</p>

<p class="pt-quote" markdown="1">*Hieronym.* Hedibiae quaest. 11. part. 154. Tom. 3. Edit. *Lugdun.* 1530.</p>
